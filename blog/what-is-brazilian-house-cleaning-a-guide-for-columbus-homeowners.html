What is Brazilian house cleaning? A guide for Columbus homeowners

What is Brazilian house cleaning? A plain-English guide for Columbus, NC homeowners: the method, what gets cleaned by hand and how the free estimate works.

Perfect Services Team
November 25, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Brazilian house cleaning means working by hand, room by room, with corners and edges included every visit.
  • Price depends on home size, condition, bathrooms and frequency, and is set after a free in-home estimate.
  • Perfect Services drives about 35 minutes from Greer to Columbus and Polk County.

Brazilian house cleaning is a detail-first way of cleaning: every surface is wiped by hand, the corners and baseboards are done on every visit, and small things get moved so the dust underneath does not stay put. If you have ever paid for a cleaning and found the same crumbs along the wall a week later, this method is the answer to that. Here is what it looks like in practice for a home in Columbus and the rest of Polk County.

Where the method comes from

Many of the cleaners on our team learned the trade in Brazil, where a clean home is judged by the edges, not by how fast the job was finished. A floor is washed on hands and knees along the walls. A bathroom is not done until the base of the toilet and the grout lines have been scrubbed. That habit came with the team to Greer, and it is what we bring to Brazilian house cleaning in Columbus.

It is not a secret formula or a special product. It is a routine, done in the same order, with a checklist at the end. The difference is mostly time and attention, and the willingness to get low and look.

What we do differently, room by room

A typical visit follows the same path through the house, top to bottom and back to front, so that nothing already cleaned gets dirty again.

Kitchen

The stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts are degreased, because cooking residue sits on the surfaces people touch every day. Counters are wiped by hand, not misted and swept with one pass. In a foothill home with a wood stove or a busy family kitchen, the cabinet handles and the area behind the faucet are usually the spots that tell you how thorough the last cleaning was.

Bathrooms

Bathrooms are the most detailed rooms. Toilet bases, shower tracks, grout lines and the underside of the vanity edge all get hand attention. Hard water marks on glass are treated rather than skipped.

Floors, edges and the rest

Floors are washed by hand along the walls, where a mop head cannot reach. Door frames, light switches and handles are wiped, and furniture or small items are lifted and cleaned underneath, then put back where they were.

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not just sprayed
  • Baseboards and corners cleaned by hand
  • Small furniture moved and cleaned under
  • Door frames, switches and handles detailed
  • Bathrooms scrubbed, including grout and toilet base
  • Final walk-through against the room checklist

Is it right for a Columbus home?

Columbus is the Polk County seat, with foothill homes and quiet neighborhoods along NC-108 and near I-26. Many of those homes have hardwood and tile that look good when cared for and unforgiving when the grit builds up along the edges. Red clay tracked in from the yard, pollen in spring and leaf debris in fall all collect exactly where a quick clean skips.

This method suits homeowners who were disappointed by a rushed service, families who want the corners done every time, and anyone with hardwood, tile or grout that needs a gentle touch. If your home is already in good shape and you just want upkeep, a recurring visit keeps the detail work from piling up again.

You do not need to tidy first, but clearing counters and picking up loose items lets the team spend its time cleaning instead of moving things. Tell us about pets, delicate finishes or anything you want left alone.
