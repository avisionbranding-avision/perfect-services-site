Apartment cleaning in Greer: the kitchen, step by step

How an apartment kitchen in Greer, SC gets cleaned by a professional team: small-space order of work, appliances, counters, floors and a free in-home estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • A small kitchen is cleaned in a tight order so you never wipe the same spot twice.
  • The stove, the sink and the floor edge under the cabinets are where apartment kitchens go wrong first.
  • Perfect Services prices apartment cleaning only after a free in-home estimate.

Apartment kitchens in Greer come in two shapes: the galley with six feet of counter and a stove wedged between the sink and the fridge, and the open one that shares a wall with the living room. Both are small enough that a mistake in the order of work shows up fast. Here is how a professional team cleans one, start to finish.

Why order matters more in a small kitchen

With so little counter space, a team cannot spread tools around. Everything is done in passes. If you wipe the counters before cleaning the cabinet fronts, a drip lands on the counter. If you mop before cleaning the oven door, a splash undoes the floor. In a bigger kitchen you can recover; in a tiny one you start over. So the sequence below is as fixed as a recipe.

Pass one: take things off the counter

Everything goes onto the table or the bed, out of the way: the dish rack, the coffee maker, the knife block. Tell us ahead of time what you want left alone. Dirty dishes in the sink get washed or stacked. A clear counter is half the job, and in an apartment kitchen the other half is the stove.

Pass two: the stove and the hood

Grease is the hard thing here, because apartment stoves are used every day and the hood often has a thin filter that has never been washed. Grates come off, burners are wiped, and the surface under the burners is lifted and cleaned. Knobs pull off if they can, and the gap beside the stove, where crumbs and spills collect, gets cleaned with a flat tool. The hood filter is degreased if it is removable.

  • Grates soaked and scrubbed
  • Cooktop degreased and wiped dry
  • Stove knobs and panel detailed
  • Range hood and filter wiped
  • Backsplash behind the stove

Pass three: cabinets, handles and appliance fronts

Cabinet fronts, pulls and the fronts of the fridge, dishwasher and microwave are wiped with a clean damp cloth. In a small kitchen those fronts are the part everyone sees from the couch, so the finish matters. Stainless steel gets wiped in the direction of the grain. The top of the fridge, often a dust shelf, is wiped before the countertop so nothing falls onto a clean surface.

Pass four: counters, then the sink

Laminate, tile, granite and the cheap butcher-block trim each need a different treatment, so we check what you have. The counter edge and the line where the counter meets the wall get a pass with a small cloth, since crumbs hide there. Then the sink is scrubbed, the faucet is dried and the drain is checked. In many Greer rentals the faucet has limescale around the base, which a quick wipe does not shift.

Pass five: floors, from the edge in

The vacuum starts along the baseboards and the toe kick under the cabinets, where a line of crumbs sits. The floor is mopped from the far end toward the door, so there is no stepping back over wet tile. Apartments in buildings near US-29 and I-85 collect fine grit from the parking lot, and the entry side of the kitchen floor gets an extra pass. Rugs and mats are shaken or vacuumed last.

Three spots renters forget

First, the inside of the cabinet under the sink. A slow drip or a spilled bottle leaves a ring, and a damp cabinet floor is where odor starts. Second, the seam between the stove and the counter, where food falls and cooks into a hard line. Third, the door of the dishwasher, the bottom edge especially, which collects grime from every load. Property managers tend to check these on a move-out walkthrough, so cleaning them on a regular visit means no surprises later.

If you are home during the visit, it helps to know what to expect: the kitchen is usually the longest single room of an apartment visit, the bathroom second, and the living areas fastest. A small apartment is often finished in a single block, so you can plan the afternoon around it. Tell us about anything with a sentimental value or a delicate finish, such as a marble cutting board or a vintage enamel pot, and it gets handled by hand and set aside.

Keeping a small kitchen clean between visits

Wipe the stove after every few meals, not once a month. Keep a dry cloth by the sink, since water spots are what make a kitchen look dirty. Take out the trash before it smells. If you cook a lot, pull the filter and wash it every month or two. These habits cost two minutes and spare you the heavy cleaning.
