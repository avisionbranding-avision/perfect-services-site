What a regular cleaning does

A regular visit covers the surfaces you touch and walk on every day. It works well when the house was in decent shape at the last visit and you want to keep it that way.

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation

The phrase to notice is "touched up on a rotation." A regular visit does not scrub every baseboard every time. That is how the time is planned, not a shortcut. You can read the details on our house cleaning and maid service page.

What a deep cleaning adds

A deep cleaning includes everything in a regular visit, then goes where buildup collects between visits. The team takes longer and works with more hand detail.

  • Baseboards and door frames hand-wiped in every room
  • Cabinet fronts and handles degreased
  • Grout lines scrubbed in the kitchen and bathrooms
  • Ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures dusted
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds cleaned
  • Behind and around the toilet base
  • Appliance exteriors, range hood and backsplash

Our deep cleaning service in Liberty is built around those items, and the order matters: dust comes down from fans and vents first, then surfaces, then floors last.

One more way to decide is to look at who lives in the house. Pets shed into corners and onto vents, children touch door frames and cabinet handles all day, and anyone with allergies feels dust that sits on fan blades and blinds. In those homes a regular visit keeps the visible surfaces fine, but a deep cleaning is what removes the layer underneath. If you are unsure, say so at the estimate and we will tell you honestly which service fits.

Which one does your Liberty home need?

Choose a deep cleaning if the house has not had a professional clean in months, if you are getting ready for spring cleaning or holiday guests, or if you want to start recurring service from a clean baseline. It also suits allergy sufferers, since the goal is to remove dust rather than move it around. Choose a regular cleaning if the home is already kept up and you want that to continue.

Many homes do both: one deep clean first, then biweekly or monthly visits. That order keeps the regular visits shorter, because the team is not catching up on old buildup every time. Other towns use the same approach, as in our explainer on deep cleaning vs regular cleaning in Marietta.
