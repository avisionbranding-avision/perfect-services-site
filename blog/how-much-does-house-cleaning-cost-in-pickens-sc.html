What sets the price

FactorHow it changes the time
Home size and layoutMore rooms and square feet take more time, and a house with long hallways or two floors adds steps.
Bedrooms and bathroomsBathrooms are the most time-intensive rooms, so a three-bath home is a bigger job than a one-bath home of the same size.
FrequencyWeekly and biweekly visits cost less per visit than monthly, since the house never drifts far from clean.
Pets and kidsExtra hair and traffic add a little time, mostly on floors and around doors.
First visitThe first clean is usually deeper, to bring the home up to the maintenance standard.

Notice that a rate per square foot is not on the list. Square footage is only one input. A rate that ignores the others is usually wrong for either you or the cleaner, and we would rather get it right after seeing the space.

What a visit includes

Everything below is part of a regular visit, so you can compare it with any other quote you collect.

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized
  • Appliance fronts and handles wiped
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation
  • Light switches and high-touch spots wiped

The Brazilian detail-first method shows up in the small places: the handles, the switch plates, the edge where the mop meets the baseboard. We work top to bottom and finish with the floors, so nothing falls onto a surface we already cleaned.

On the walkthrough we look at things that change the plan: how many people live in the home, whether the floors are hardwood, tile or carpet, how much glass and chrome there is in the bathrooms, and where pet hair collects. A house with a sliding door, a dog bed and two kids' rooms has a different list from a quiet two-person home. Those details are why the same square footage can lead to different prices, and why a number given over the phone is a guess.

How long it takes, and booking this week

A typical recurring visit in a mid-sized home takes a few hours with a small team, and the first visit runs longer. We confirm the time during the estimate, because a four-bedroom house with two dogs is not the same as a one-level home with no pets. If you want a visit soon, tell us when you call or text and we will say what the calendar allows. We never promise a date before we have seen the home.

If your house has not been professionally cleaned in a while, a deep cleaning first can make the recurring visits lighter and cheaper to maintain. We will tell you during the walkthrough whether it makes sense.

Ways to keep the cost sensible

  • Pick a steady schedule. Biweekly is the most common choice for families, and a regular rhythm keeps each visit shorter.
  • Pick up toys, laundry and papers before the team arrives, so the time goes into cleaning and not into moving things.
  • Tell us up front about extras such as inside the oven or fridge, so they are planned instead of squeezed in.
  • Mention allergies or product preferences. If you have pets or small children, we can talk about green cleaning.
