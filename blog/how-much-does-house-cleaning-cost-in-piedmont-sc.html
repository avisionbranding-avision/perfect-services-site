The five things that set the price

Home size and layout

More rooms and more square feet take more time. Layout matters as much as size: an open plan with one large floor is quicker than a house with many small rooms, stairs and lots of trim.

Bedrooms and bathrooms

Bathrooms are the most time-intensive rooms. A three-bedroom house with one bathroom is not the same job as a three-bedroom with three, because each bathroom needs scrubbing, disinfecting and detail work.

Frequency

Weekly and biweekly visits cost less per visit than monthly ones. A house that is cleaned often stays close to standard, so each visit goes faster than one after a month of build-up.

Pets and kids

Hair, dander and heavy traffic add some time, especially along baseboards, upholstery and the entry. Tell us what lives in the house and we will plan for it.

The first visit

The first clean is usually deeper, to bring the home up to the maintenance standard. After that, regular visits are lighter and often faster.

What a regular visit includes

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized
  • Appliance fronts and handles wiped
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation
  • Light switches and high-touch spots wiped

The estimate goes faster, and the number is more accurate, if you think about a few things. Which rooms matter most to you, which can be skipped, and do you want recurring service or a single visit? Mention any surface that needs special care, such as unsealed wood or natural stone, so we plan the right method.
