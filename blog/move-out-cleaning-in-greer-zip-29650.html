What 29650 looks like on the ground

ZIP 29650 is part of Greer, which spans Greenville and Spartanburg counties and sits along I-85, SC-14, SC-101 and US-29. It includes homes near Downtown Greer and Trade Street, and it is within a short drive of GSP Airport and the BMW plant, so there is steady rental turnover alongside long-time owners. A house in the older part of town often has more trim, more window sills and sometimes tight closets. A newer build tends to have open kitchens, big pantries and larger bathrooms. Both get the same checklist, but the time is spent in different places.

The checklist, room by room

Kitchen

  • Cabinets and drawers cleaned inside and out
  • Oven interior, refrigerator and freezer interior, microwave
  • Range hood and filter area, stovetop and backsplash degreased
  • Sink, fixtures and the edge of the counter where grime collects

Bathrooms

  • Toilet base and behind the toilet
  • Grout lines, glass door tracks and shower walls
  • Vanity interiors, mirrors, fixtures

Bedrooms, closets and living areas

  • Closet shelves, rods and floors
  • Baseboards, doors, door frames and light switches
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped edge to edge

A few details people forget

The spots that cause deposit arguments are rarely the big ones. They are the inside of the microwave, the bottom of the oven, the tracks of a sliding door, the top of the refrigerator and the inside of the vanity drawers. On a Brazilian detail-first clean, those are done by hand and checked against a room list before the team leaves, rather than being left to a quick wipe.

One more: do not forget the garage entry and the laundry room. Lint behind a dryer and detergent drips on a shelf are easy to miss and easy for an inspector to find.

Furnished, empty or half-packed

The state of the house on cleaning day changes the plan more than people expect. An empty home is the easiest: the team can reach every wall, every closet corner and the whole floor in one pass. A half-packed home is the hardest, because boxes sit on the exact spots that need cleaning, such as closet floors and the bottom of a pantry. If you can, finish packing and move everything to one room or the garage before the team arrives, and tell us which room is off limits.

For renters, a useful habit is to take dated photos of each room after the clean and before you hand back the keys. It is not part of our service, but it helps if there is ever a question about the condition of the unit later. Homeowners selling a house can skip the photos and simply keep the doors closed until the showing.

What we will not promise

A clean cannot repair damage. Stained carpet, scratched floors, burned counters and wall holes are repair items, not cleaning items, and a landlord may treat them separately. What cleaning does is remove everything that is dirt, and in our experience that is most of what lands on a deposit statement. If you are unsure which category something falls into, point it out at the free estimate and the team will tell you honestly what will lift and what will not.
