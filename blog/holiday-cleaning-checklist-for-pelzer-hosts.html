Holiday cleaning checklist for Pelzer hosts

Holiday cleaning checklist for Pelzer hosts: get the house ready before guests arrive, what a one time house cleaning covers and how to book an estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 25, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Start with the rooms guests actually use: kitchen, bathrooms, entry and the guest bed.
  • A one-time cleaning before the holidays can include add-ons like the inside of the oven or fridge.
  • Holiday weeks book quickly, and the price is set after a free in-home estimate.

If you are hosting in Pelzer this season, you have maybe two weeks to turn a lived-in house into one that is ready for your family. This checklist is built for hosts: it puts the work in the order guests will notice it, and it tells you what to hand off if you would rather spend the weekend cooking than scrubbing.

Work from the front door inward

Guests see the entry first, then the living room, then the kitchen and bathroom, and finally the bed they will sleep in. Clean in that order of priority, not the order of your usual routine. In an older mill-village house near the Saluda River, the entry often has the most to do: a worn mat, scuffed baseboards and a coat closet nobody has opened since spring.

  • Entry: shake out the mat, wipe the door and handle, clear the shoe pile
  • Living room: dust surfaces and the TV stand, vacuum under cushions, wipe light switches
  • Kitchen: degrease the stovetop and hood, wipe cabinet fronts, clean the fridge shelves
  • Bathrooms: scrub the toilet and shower, wipe the mirror, set out fresh towels
  • Guest bedroom: wash the linens, dust the nightstands, vacuum under the bed
  • Floors: vacuum, then mop, finishing at the door

The kitchen deserves a day of its own

A holiday kitchen does double duty. It cooks for twenty and then becomes the place where everyone stands. Take the oven out of the equation early: if you plan to use it for a big meal, clean it a week ahead, because a baked-on oven smokes the first time it is hot. Wipe the fridge shelves before the grocery run so there is room for the extra dishes. Clear the counters completely before you wipe, and finish with the handles on drawers and cabinets, which guests touch the most.

What a one-time cleaning covers

A one-time house cleaning is a single visit, with no contract, meant for situations like this one. It usually includes the full kitchen cleaning and degreasing, bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, bedrooms and living areas dusted, floors vacuumed and mopped, baseboards and door frames wiped, mirrors and glass polished and the trash emptied. Optional add-ons like the inside of the oven, the fridge or the cabinets are worth asking about before the holidays, since those are the jobs a host rarely finds time for.

A lot of people in Anderson County use this kind of visit to try a cleaning service for the first time. It is also a quiet way to find out whether you like the result before you commit to a recurring schedule.

The week-of schedule that works

Hosts who stay calm on the day have usually spread the work out. Three days ahead, deal with the deep jobs: the oven, the fridge and the bathroom grout. Two days ahead, wash the guest linens and clean the guest room. The day before, do the kitchen counters, the floors and the bathrooms, since those get used last and show it first. On the morning itself, only do a light pass: empty the trash, wipe the bathroom sink, set out fresh towels and open the windows for a few minutes if the weather allows.

If you cannot spread the work out, hand off the heavy part. A professional visit two days before arrival gives you the deep clean, and you can handle the final touches yourself.

Timing: book before the calendar fills

The days right before a holiday disappear first, particularly the Tuesday to Wednesday window. If you want a visit this week, ask for the estimate now and let the team tell you what is open. Pelzer is about 24 miles, or 40 minutes, from our Greer base, so we plan Anderson County visits by route. A first-time clean of a full house usually takes several hours, and an extra pair of hands shortens that.

Do a few things yourself first

You do not have to tidy before the team arrives, but a little help lets them focus on cleaning. Put away the clutter on counters, pick up toys and take fragile items off low shelves. Tell us about pets, a room you want left alone and anything that needs a gentle product. Hosts with relatives who have allergies can ask about green cleaning products.

A smart extra, if a guest is coming a long way, is to book a little before arrival so the house keeps that clean smell and you are not scrubbing at midnight. After the party, many hosts book a second visit to reset the house. The same one time house cleaning in Pelzer service works for that.
