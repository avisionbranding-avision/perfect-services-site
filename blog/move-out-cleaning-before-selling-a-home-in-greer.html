Move-out cleaning before selling a home in Greer

Selling in Greer? See when to book move out cleaning for a home sale, what buyers and inspectors notice, and how the free in-home estimate works.

Perfect Services Team
November 23, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • A sale has three cleaning moments: photos, showings and the final walkthrough before closing.
  • Empty rooms show every scuff, handprint and dusty track, so buyers see more than they would in a furnished home.
  • Prices come only after a free in-home estimate, so book the visit as soon as you have a listing or closing date.

The cleanest house on the street can still lose a buyer's goodwill in the last ten minutes. The final walkthrough happens in an empty home, usually a day or two before closing, and the buyer walks every room with the light on and the furniture gone. That is when a greasy oven, a gritty shower track or a film of dust on the baseboards gets noticed. This guide explains how move out cleaning for a home sale in Greer works, and when to place it on your calendar.

The three moments when a seller's cleaning gets judged

Most sellers think about cleaning once. In practice the house is looked at three separate times, and each look is different.

MomentWho is lookingWhat stands out
Listing photosEvery online buyerWindows, counters, bathroom glass, floors catching the light
Showings and open houseBuyers and their agentsSmells, kitchen and bathrooms, cabinet interiors they open
Final walkthroughThe buyer, before closingEmpty-room detail: baseboards, closets, inside the oven and fridge, scuffs on door frames

A regular maid visit covers the first two reasonably well. The third is where a move-out clean earns its place, because it is done after the last box leaves and treats the house as empty, with nothing left to work around.

What the team does in an empty Greer home

When we walk into a vacant house in Greer, the first thing we do is open every cabinet and closet, because that is what a buyer will do. We work top to bottom so the dust falls onto surfaces we have not reached yet, and we finish with the floors, edge to edge, on the way out. Perfect Services is Brazilian-owned, and the detail-first method we use means corners and edges get cleaned by hand rather than skipped.

  • Inside and outside of kitchen cabinets and drawers, including the shelf liner marks left behind
  • Inside the refrigerator, freezer, oven and microwave, plus the range hood and backsplash
  • Bathrooms: toilet base, grout lines, shower glass and the inside of vanity drawers
  • Closets: shelves, rods, floors and the corners where hangers drop lint
  • Baseboards, doors, frames and light switches, where hand marks collect
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped edge to edge

Sliding door tracks and the gap behind the toilet are the two spots people forget most often, so we check them before we call a room done.

Timing it around your closing date

Greer sits between Greenville and Spartanburg counties, with ZIP codes 29650, 29651 and 29652, and a lot of sellers here are also buyers elsewhere, so closing dates tend to be tight. A few practical rules help.

  1. Book the free estimate as soon as you have a listing or closing date, even if the move date is weeks away.
  2. Clear the house of boxes and furniture first. Cleaners can work around some items, but an empty home is faster and gives a better result.
  3. Schedule the clean after the movers finish and before the final walkthrough, not the day before you hand over keys, so there is room to fix a missed spot.
  4. If you are still living in the home during showings, keep a lighter regular clean going and save the full move-out clean for after you leave.

One more thing the team looks at in a vacant house is smell. Pet odors, stale cooking grease and a damp bathroom fan all carry further in an empty home, so we open windows when the weather allows and clean the places that hold odor, such as the inside of the dishwasher door and the drip tray under the stove.

Things worth fixing before the cleaners arrive

Cleaning will not repair damage, so deal with these first, and the team can then finish the job properly.

  • Patch nail holes and touch up scuffed paint before we clean the walls and frames, not after.
  • Remove stickers, tape and leftover shelf paper from cabinets and closets.
  • Take out the trash, paint cans and anything stored in the garage or attic that you are not leaving.
  • Leave the water and power on so we can run the dishwasher and check the bathrooms.

Frequently asked questions

Should I clean before the listing photos or after I move out?

Both, in different ways. A regular clean before photos makes the house look its best online, and the full move-out clean happens after you leave and before the buyer's final walkthrough.

Do I have to be home during the free estimate?

It helps, because you can point out problem spots, but it is not required if someone can let the team in. The estimate is a short walk-through with no commitment.

Does move-out cleaning include inside the oven and refrigerator?

Yes. Inside the oven, refrigerator, freezer and microwave are part of the clean, though heavy grease or a very neglected appliance can add time, which the estimate takes into account.
