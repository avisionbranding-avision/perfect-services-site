Deep vs. regular cleaning in Marietta: the difference

Deep cleaning vs. regular cleaning in Marietta, SC: what each one covers, how long they take, and how to know which your home needs. Free in-home estimate.

November 11, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • A regular clean maintains a home; a deep clean resets it by reaching baseboards, grout, vents, tracks and cabinet fronts.
  • Most homes benefit from one deep clean first, then a recurring regular schedule.
  • Perfect Services gives prices only after a free in-home estimate.

Here is the short version for Marietta homeowners: a regular cleaning keeps a clean house clean, and a deep cleaning brings a house back to clean. If you can run a finger along the top of a door frame and come away gray, or the grout in the shower has turned a different color, you are past what a regular visit is designed to fix.

Marietta sits in the foothills along US-25 and US-276 in Greenville County, on the way up toward the Blue Ridge. Homes here range from older farmhouses to quiet newer builds, and in a lot of them the red clay outside finds its way onto floors and door frames all year. That mix makes the difference between the two services easy to see, so here is how we explain it when we walk through a home.

What a regular cleaning covers

A regular visit is built around the surfaces you touch and walk on every day. It is the right service when the house was in decent shape at the last visit and you want to keep it that way.

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation

Note the words "touched up on a rotation." A regular visit does not scrub every baseboard every time. That is not cutting corners, it is how the time is planned. If you want the specifics of that service, see our house cleaning and maid service page.

What a deep cleaning adds

A deep cleaning includes everything in a regular clean, then goes into the places that collect buildup between visits. The extra items are the difference:

  • Baseboards and door frames hand-wiped, every room
  • Cabinet fronts and handles degreased
  • Grout lines scrubbed in the kitchen and bathrooms
  • Ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures dusted
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds cleaned
  • Behind and around the toilet base
  • Appliance exteriors, range hood and backsplash

Our Brazilian detail-first method matters most here. The team works top to bottom, so dust from a fan blade falls before the floors are done, and floors are washed by hand along the walls instead of mopped down the middle of the room. Which one does your Marietta home need?

Choose deep cleaning if any of these sound familiar: the home has not had a professional clean in months, you are preparing for the holidays or a family visit, you are about to start recurring service, or someone in the house has allergies and you want dust removed rather than moved. In November, a lot of people book a deep clean before Thanksgiving guests arrive, and it is a good time to reset after a summer of open windows and foothill pollen season.

Choose regular cleaning if the house has been cleaned recently, you just need the routine covered, and nothing looks neglected. If you are unsure, say so at the estimate. We would rather see the house and tell you the right service than sell you the bigger one.

Many homes do best with both: one deep clean, then a recurring schedule. That first deep visit does the heavy lifting once, and the regular visits that follow are shorter and easier to keep up. For another example of how this plays out, read about deep versus regular cleaning in Mauldin.

A practical way to decide is to look at three spots before you call: the tracks of your sliding door or windows, the grout at the base of the shower, and the top of the refrigerator. If all three are fine, regular service is probably enough. If two or more are not, a deep clean will save you frustration, because a regular visit cannot spend the time those areas need without shortchanging the rest of the house.

Whichever you pick, tell us about pets, allergies and rooms that matter most. Those details shape the walkthrough more than any checklist.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I book a deep cleaning in Marietta?

Most homes do well with one deep clean to start, then recurring regular visits. Many households repeat a deep clean once or twice a year, often before the holidays or in spring.

Is deep cleaning worth it if I already have a regular cleaner?

If baseboards, grout, vents and window tracks are not covered by your current visits, a deep clean resets those areas. After that, regular visits keep them from building up again.
