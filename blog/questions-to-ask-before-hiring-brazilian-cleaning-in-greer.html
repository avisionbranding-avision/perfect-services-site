Questions to ask before hiring brazilian cleaning in Greer

Hiring Brazilian house cleaning in Greer, SC? Ask what detail-first really means, who comes, what is hand-washed and how the free in-home estimate works.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Ask to see what detail-first means room by room, not as a slogan.
  • Ask whether the same team comes each visit and who checks the work.
  • The exact price comes after a free in-home estimate.

Plenty of Greer homeowners have tried a cleaning service, been told the house was spotless and found dust on the baseboards two days later. That experience is the reason people search for Brazilian house cleaning in Greer, SC. The phrase is not a magic label, though, so here are the questions worth asking any company that uses it, ours included.

What does "detail-first" mean in your house?

Ask for specifics. In a Brazilian-style clean, surfaces are wiped by hand rather than only sprayed, corners and edges get a hand pass, small furniture and objects are moved and cleaned under, and floors are washed by hand along the walls. The Brazilian house cleaning in Greer from Perfect Services follows the same room checklist every visit, so nothing depends on how the day is going. If a company cannot describe its method in plain words, it probably is not following one.

Which spots do you actually do?

A good way to test any cleaner is to ask about the places people forget: the tops of door frames, cabinet handles, light switches, the track of a sliding door, the base of the toilet, grout lines. These are small things, and they are where a detailed clean shows. A short list to hold anyone to:

  • Baseboards cleaned by hand, not skipped
  • Door frames, switches and handles detailed
  • Toilet base and shower grout scrubbed
  • Stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts degreased
  • Floors washed along the wall edges
  • A final walk-through against the checklist

Who will be in my home each time?

Consistency is half of quality. Ask whether the same people return, whether they are employees or hired by the job, and who looks over the work before they leave. A crew that learns your home knows which stairs collect pet hair and which bathroom has the hard-water film. We are a Brazilian-owned local team based in Greer, so the same people answer the phone and show up at the door.

Can you clean my hardwood and tile properly?

Ask what they use. Many Upstate homes have a mix of hardwood in the living areas, tile in kitchens and baths and carpet upstairs. A careful team adjusts the method to each: soft touch for hardwood, scrubbing for grout, vacuum passes for carpet. If the answer is the same mop for everything, be careful. The older homes near Downtown Greer and Trade Street often have original wood floors that deserve that care.

Is it a one-time clean or a recurring service?

Brazilian cleaning works as a first deep reset followed by regular visits. The first visit is usually longer because the team brings the home to a maintenance standard. After that, weekly, biweekly or monthly visits keep it there, and recurring visits cost less per visit than one-offs. Ask any company how the first visit differs from the rest.

How much does Brazilian house cleaning cost in Greer?

It depends on the size of the home, the number of bathrooms, the level of detail you want, how often you book and the condition of the house on the first visit. Bathrooms are the most detailed rooms and the largest share of the time. We never post dollar amounts. What if I am not happy?

Ask for the answer before you hire. At Perfect Services, if something is missed, tell us and the team comes back to fix it. The point of a detail-first method is that the checklist gets followed every time, but people are people, and a good company says so.

What the first visit should feel like

You should be able to describe the first visit afterward without guessing. The team arrives, walks the home with you or with your notes, and starts at the top of each room. Kitchens and bathrooms usually take the largest part of the day. You will notice the little things: the stovetop hood no longer sticky, the baseboards no longer gray, the tracks of the sliding door clear of grit. If you walk in and see only shiny countertops, the company worked for the photo, not the house.

It also helps to ask what they do about allergies and sensitive households. Upstate spring pollen blows in through every window, and a team that damp-wipes rather than flicks a duster keeps more of it out of the air. If you have pets, say so; hair behind the couch and along the baseboards is a normal part of the job and should be on the checklist.

Finally, ask about communication. A quick text when the team is on the way and a short note when they finish tells you the company runs a tight operation.
