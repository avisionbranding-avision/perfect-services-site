Apartment cleaning in Greer: floors, baseboards and edges

If you have ever had a property manager walk through your apartment with a clipboard, you know where they look: the floor, the baseboards, the door frames and the corners behind the door. Apartment cleaning for floors, baseboards and edges is the difference between a unit that looks lived-in and one that looks cared for. This article walks through how we do it in Greer apartments and condos.

Q: What kinds of floors are in a Greer apartment?

Most units have a mix. Carpet in the bedrooms and living room, vinyl plank or tile in the kitchen and bath, and a short stretch of something else in the entry. We vacuum and mop the floors in the whole unit as part of the standard visit, and match the method to the surface.

  • Carpet: slow vacuum passes, with extra time along walls where lint builds up.
  • Vinyl plank and laminate: dry pick-up first, then a damp mop that does not flood the seams.
  • Tile: mop with a clean head, then check the grout lines near the tub and stove.

Q: Why do baseboards matter so much?

Because they collect everything that floats and falls. Dust settles on the top edge. Pet hair is pushed against the bottom. Vacuum wheels leave marks. In a small unit, the baseboards run along every wall at eye level when you sit on the sofa, so they show. Baseboards and door frames are part of our apartment checklist, and wiping them is one of the cheapest ways to make the whole place feel cleaner.

We work around the unit in one direction so we do not miss a section, and we wipe doors and frames at the same time, since marks from hands and bags are at the same height.

The edges people overlook

Here is a short list of spots we check at the end of every floor:

  • Corners behind the front door
  • The gap between the stove or refrigerator and the cabinets
  • Under the bed frame and the edge of the closet floor
  • The track of the balcony door
  • The threshold between carpet and tile
  • The floor behind the toilet, where hair collects

Keeping floors and edges clean between visits

A few small habits stretch a professional clean much further. Put a mat inside the door and take shoes off after rain. Use a dry microfiber pad on hard floors every few days, which lifts grit before it scratches. Wipe the lowest inch of the walls and the door frames when you notice a scuff, since a damp cloth takes marks off easily when they are fresh. If you have pets, brush them near a tile area instead of the carpet. Renters who set up a recurring visit through apartment cleaning in Greer find that the edges stay in good shape and the end-of-lease clean becomes a small job rather than a big one.
