Two Greer homes with the same square footage can need very different amounts of work after a move. One is lightly lived in and empty. The other has a stovetop that has not been scrubbed since the lease began and two bathrooms with grout gone gray. People searching "how much is move out cleaning" want a number, but the honest answer is a list of what pushes the job up or down. This is that list, with the order we look at things during an estimate.

The five things that decide the price

When someone from our team walks a home for a move-out cleaning quote in Greer, the same five things come up each time.

  1. Size and layout. Number of rooms and square feet set the baseline. A two-story with a bonus room has more floor, more closets and more baseboards than a ranch of the same footprint.
  2. Condition left by the last occupant. A well-kept home needs a standard clean. A home with pet hair in the vents, soap scum on glass and sticky cabinet fronts needs more passes.
  3. Appliances. The inside of the oven and fridge is part of the service, but baked-on grease or a freezer that was never defrosted adds time.
  4. Empty or furnished. Empty homes clean faster. If boxes, couches and a garage-sale pile are still in the rooms, the crew works around them.
  5. Timing. A closing or inspection date that leaves one afternoon may need a larger team, which changes how the job is staffed.

What you can do to keep the price reasonable

You do not need to scrub the house yourself, but a few small steps help. They also make the estimate faster.

  • Remove everything from cabinets, closets and the fridge
  • Take trash and unwanted furniture out before cleaning day
  • Pull rugs and loose items away from walls
  • Tell the team about stains or damage you already know about
  • Keep the water and electricity on

The point is to let the crew spend their hours cleaning rather than clearing. If you want to see our order of work for an empty house, the article on keeping a Greer home clean after move-out cleaning covers what the home looks like afterward.

Furnished, half-packed or empty: what we see in Greer

A lot of moves around the BMW plant and GSP Airport happen on a tight schedule, so we often walk into homes in the middle of the move. The kitchen is packed but the bedrooms are empty, or the garage is full and the living room is bare. Each state changes the plan. A half-packed room means we clean around the boxes and come back to the floor afterward. A fully empty room lets us work top to bottom without stopping. Tell us at the estimate which stage you expect to be in on cleaning day, and the visit will account for it.

Landlords and property managers with several units usually ask for a repeat arrangement. Once the team has seen your rental, the next turnover is easier to plan, because we already know where the grease collects and which closets get ignored.

How long does it take, and can I book this week?

A typical empty home takes a team a good part of a day, longer when the kitchen is heavy. Larger houses or tight deadlines get a bigger crew rather than a longer day. In many cases we can schedule an estimate visit within the week; the cleaning date depends on the calendar, and the end of the month fills first.

Move out cleaning compared with a regular clean

The question we hear most is whether a regular maid visit can do the job. It usually cannot. A regular visit wipes counters and mops floors; a move-out clean goes inside cabinets and appliances and into the tracks and corners, and the landlord notices the difference. If your home needs a deeper reset before listing it, deep cleaning is another service people pair with a move-out clean. Neighbors in Duncan and Lyman book the same way.
