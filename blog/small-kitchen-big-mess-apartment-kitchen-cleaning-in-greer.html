Small kitchen, big mess: apartment kitchen cleaning in Greer

How to clean a small apartment kitchen in Greer, SC: a step-by-step order, the greasy spots people miss, and when to call a studio apartment cleaning service.

Perfect Services Team
November 12, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Clean a small kitchen top to bottom and back to front, finishing with the floor, so nothing falls onto a clean surface.
  • The greasy spots people miss are cabinet handles, the stovetop edges, the microwave door and the fronts of appliances.
  • Apartment kitchen cleaning is part of our visit, with the price given after a free in-home estimate.

In a studio apartment the kitchen is also the entryway, the dining room and sometimes the desk. When a pan of grease splatters, it does not stay in one corner. If you are searching for studio apartment cleaning in Greer, SC, the kitchen is almost always where the job gets harder, because every surface does double duty and every spill lands next to something else.

This is the order our team uses in small kitchens, and you can follow it yourself on a Saturday morning.

Work in this order

  1. Clear the counters. Everything goes onto the table or the bed, so you can see the whole surface.
  2. Dishes first. Wash, dry and put away. A drying rack left full blocks the sink and hides the splash zone.
  3. Top of the fridge and cabinet tops. Dust and cooking film collect here and drop down later.
  4. Cabinet fronts and handles. Hands touch these all day, so they carry the most grease.
  5. Stovetop and range hood area. Soak the grates and burner caps while you work on other things.
  6. Counters and backsplash. Spray, wait a minute, then wipe in long strokes.
  7. Sink and faucet. Scrub the basin, then wipe the faucet base and the area behind it.
  8. Appliance fronts. Fridge door, dishwasher panel and oven handle.
  9. Floor last. Sweep or vacuum first, then mop, starting at the far corner and backing out.

The rule is simple: anything you do after the floor can drop crumbs on it, so the floor goes last.

Give each step a short window. In a studio, the whole sequence usually takes between forty-five minutes and an hour if the kitchen is in decent shape, because there is not much counter to cover. Most of that time goes to the stovetop and the cabinet fronts, not to the large surfaces. Keep two cloths going, one damp with soap for grease and one dry for finishing, so you are not spreading film from one spot to the next.

The spots that stay dirty

When we clean an apartment kitchen, the same places show up as the missed ones. They are small, but guests and landlords notice them.

  • The edge of the stovetop where the burner meets the counter
  • The microwave door seal and the handle
  • The cabinet door under the sink, which is often splashed
  • The gap between the fridge and the counter
  • The light switch and the trash can lid
  • Baseboards below the cabinets, where grease and dust mix

Use warm water with dish soap for light grease. For a heavier film, let the cleaner sit for a few minutes before you wipe. Do not use abrasive pads on a glass stovetop or stainless fronts, since they leave scratches that hold dirt later.

Small space, special problems

A tiny kitchen traps odor. Fish, onions and burnt cheese hang in the air when there is not much airflow, so run the vent fan and open a window while you cook. Take out the trash the same day if it holds food scraps, particularly in humid Upstate summers when the smell builds quickly. Wipe the fridge shelves every couple of weeks, and put a box of baking soda on a shelf if smells linger.

If you rent, remember that your cleaning affects the end of the lease. Grease on a stovetop or a sticky oven door is a common thing for inspectors to notice. If you own instead of rent, our article on cleaning a Greer townhome from top to bottom covers the rest of the unit.

A few storage habits help as well. Keep only the daily pans on the stovetop, and move rarely used appliances into a cabinet, because each thing left on the counter is one more thing to lift during every wipe. A small tray under the dish soap and the sponge keeps the sink edge from getting slimy. These habits matter most in studio layouts, where the counter you cook on is the same one you eat at.
