How long does post-construction cleaning take in Greer, SC?

How long does post construction cleaning take in Greer SC? See how rough, final and touch-up cleans fit your build schedule and what sets the timeline.

Perfect Services Team
November 13, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Timing depends on when the other trades finish more than on the cleaning itself.
  • Book the final clean after the last trade leaves, and add a touch-up before move-in.
  • Price and schedule are set together at the free in-home estimate.

If you are asking how long post construction cleaning takes in Greer, the useful answer is that the cleaning is rarely the slow part. The schedule is usually set by when the other trades finish: the painter, the flooring crew, the electrician and the final inspector. Clean too early and the dust comes back. Clean at the right moment and the job goes quickly. This guide looks at the timing from the contractor's side of the calendar.

Where cleaning fits in the build schedule

Construction cleaning tends to happen in stages rather than all at once. The service is built for that, and the rough clean, final clean and touch-up can be booked together. Here is how the stages line up with the work around them.

StageWhen it happensWhat gets done
Rough cleanAfter drywall sanding and the heavy workBulk dust off surfaces, floors vacuumed so other trades can work
Final cleanAfter paint, flooring and fixtures are inCabinets, glass, fixtures, vents, fans and floors washed in multiple passes
Touch-upRight before move-in or inspectionA last pass for anything the final trades or walk-throughs disturbed

If you book only one visit, make it the final clean, and schedule it after the last trade has left. Anything finished before that tends to need another pass.

What makes a Greer job take longer

  • Heavy drywall dust. Sanding makes the finest dust of any phase, and it settles inside cabinets and on ledges.
  • Lots of glass. Stickers, paint specks and adhesive residue take patient scraping and polishing.
  • Large square footage or many rooms affected, such as a whole-house remodel versus one bathroom.
  • Multiple floor types, which need different tools and extra passes.
  • A hard deadline. Closing or inspection dates may call for a larger team, which shortens the clock but not the standard.

A single kitchen or bathroom renovation is a smaller scope than a whole addition, and the visit reflects that. New construction in Greer, near GSP Airport and the BMW plant, often means open floor plans with many windows, and those take longer than the room count suggests.

How the final clean flows

The team works from the highest point down. Fans, light fixtures and vents go first, then windows, tracks and sills, then the insides of cabinets, drawers and closets. Bathrooms and new fixtures are polished next. Floors come last, vacuumed and washed in more than one pass, since fine dust shows up again when the first wash dries. Our post-construction cleaning in Greer follows this order on every job, and the touch-up pass at the end catches footprints and scuffs from the walkthrough.

Weather plays a part here in the Upstate. In spring, pollen blows in through open doors while crews carry materials, and on humid summer days paint and adhesive cure more slowly. Both can push the best cleaning window back a little. Red clay on boots is another reason to keep the final clean until the exterior work is done.

A realistic way to picture the timeline

Think of it in three questions you can answer before calling. How many rooms were touched by the project, and are they open to each other? How much sanding happened, and was the dust sealed off with plastic or allowed to travel? Is anything still waiting to be installed, such as hardware, trim or appliances? A small bathroom remodel with sealed doors and nothing left to install is a compact job. An addition with sanded drywall in every room, new windows and a floor still waiting for a last coat is a bigger one, and it may be better to split it into a rough clean now and a final clean later.

Walking the space with the estimator helps because we can see these answers in person. We also ask what comes next: if furniture arrives the next day, the floors are cleaned last and we leave the walkways clear so deliveries do not undo the work.

Frequently asked questions

When should post-construction cleaning be scheduled in Greer?

After the last trade has left and before move-in or inspection. A rough clean can be done earlier, but the final clean should come after paint, flooring and fixtures are finished.

Can the cleaning be done within a tight closing deadline?

Often yes. A tight deadline may call for a larger team, which is something we plan for at the free estimate.

Why are multiple floor passes needed?

Fine dust left in corners and on textured floors reappears as the first wash dries, so a second pass picks up what the first one loosened.
