Fall apartment cleaning in Greer, SC: a weekly plan

Fall apartment cleaning in Greer SC: a week-by-week plan for first-heat dust, tracked-in leaves and small-space holiday hosting, plus our free estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 24, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Fall in an apartment means first-heat dust, tracked-in leaves and a small space that has to host holiday guests.
  • A four-week plan spreads the work so nothing piles up before Thanksgiving.
  • Perfect Services prices apartment cleaning only after a free in-home estimate.

The first cool night in Greer is when people close the windows, turn on the heat and discover what has been sitting in the vents all summer. In an apartment or condo that is a smell of warm dust for a day or two, then a gray film on the sills. Fall also brings guests, leaf-covered shoes and sometimes a lease renewal or move-out. Here is a four-week plan for apartment cleaning in Greer that fits a small space and a full calendar.

Week 1: first-heat dust

Start with air and edges. Change or check the filter if your unit allows it, then wipe the vent covers, the baseboards around them and the top of every door frame. Heating vents in an apartment are often close to the floor, so lint and pet hair collect in a line right in front of them. Vacuum the slot first, then wipe it with a damp microfiber cloth. Do the window sills while you are there, since cold glass meets warm air and leaves a gray ring of moisture and dust.

Week 2: the entry and the floors

Leaves, grit and red clay arrive on shoes and spread to the carpet or vinyl in a small space very quickly. Put a boot tray by the door and a washable mat under it. Then vacuum the first six feet of floor twice as often as the rest. If the apartment has carpet, go over the traffic lane slowly in two directions. On hard floors, vacuum before mopping so you do not turn grit into a paste. A lot of apartments near US-29 and I-85 see the same pattern: the entry looks worn long before the bedroom does.

Week 3: kitchen and holiday prep

A galley kitchen has to feed a few extra people at Thanksgiving. Empty the fridge of old items and wipe the shelves, degrease the stovetop and the wall behind it, and clean the microwave. Clear the counters so you have somewhere to prep. The oven is the item people put off. If you plan to cook a turkey breast or a side dish in a rented oven, check the racks and the bottom for burned spills, which smoke when the heat comes on.

Week 4: bathrooms, bedroom and reset

Do the bathroom last because it takes the longest to dry. Scrub the tub or shower, the toilet base, the sink and the mirror, then wash the bath mat. In the bedroom, flip or rotate the mattress if it allows, wash the heavier fall bedding and vacuum under the bed. Finish with a trash and recycling run so the unit is ready for guests.

A quick checklist for a landlord walkthrough

  • Check that the stove and the oven interior are free of burnt spills.
  • Look at the bathroom caulk and the fan cover for mildew spots.
  • Wipe light switches, door handles and cabinet pulls.
  • Vacuum edges and closet floors, not just the middle of each room.
  • Remove nail-hole clutter, stickers and tape from walls and doors.

Hosting in a small space

Thanksgiving in a 900-square-foot apartment is a logistics problem before it is a cleaning one. Where do coats go, where does the extra chair sit and who uses the one bathroom? Clear the entry closet or a corner of the bedroom for coats, and decide what the guest bathroom needs: a clean towel, a new hand soap and a trash bag that is not overflowing. Wipe the bathroom last, right before guests arrive, since it is the room everyone sees.

Pets deserve a plan too. Hair settles on throws and sofa cushions faster in cooler weather, when the heat dries the air and the coat sheds. A lint roller by the door and a quick pass with a damp rubber glove across the couch picks up most of it. If a guest is allergic, wash the throw and vacuum the sofa edges the day before.

The team's method is the same in a small place as a big one: work from the top down, keep the wet work for the end and give the floor the last pass. In a small unit that order matters even more, because there is nowhere to stand while the floor dries. Our crews usually finish the kitchen and bath first, then do the bedroom and the living space, and leave the entry for the final minutes so nothing new gets tracked in.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my apartment smell like burning dust in the fall?

That smell comes from dust on the heating element or in the vents when the heat first runs. Wiping vents and sills and vacuuming near them before the first cold night helps.

Should I hire a cleaner before Thanksgiving guests?

If your kitchen and bathroom are the parts you do not have time for, yes. Book early in November, because holiday weeks fill quickly.

Do you clean for lease move-outs too?

Yes. Tell us at the free in-home estimate that it is a move-out and we plan the baseboards, appliances and bathrooms accordingly.
