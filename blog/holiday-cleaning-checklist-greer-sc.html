Seasonal cleaning

Holiday cleaning checklist: get your Greer home guest-ready

A room-by-room holiday cleaning checklist for Thanksgiving and Christmas guests in Greer, SC, with a 2-week timeline and when to book a holiday deep cleaning with Perfect Services.

Perfect Services Team
October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Start two weeks before guests arrive and work from the rooms guests see most.
  • Focus on the guest bathroom, kitchen, entry and dining room first.
  • Book a holiday deep cleaning early: November and December fill up fast.

Thanksgiving and Christmas are the busiest hosting weeks of the year in Greer. Family arrives, the oven works overtime and every room gets used. This holiday cleaning checklist from Perfect Services helps you get the home guest-ready without spending the whole week cleaning.

Two-week holiday cleaning timeline

WhenWhat to do
2 weeks beforeDeclutter, clean guest room, book your holiday deep cleaning
1 week beforeDeep clean kitchen and oven, clean inside the fridge
3 days beforeBathrooms, floors, entry, dining room
Day beforeQuick touch-up, fresh towels, empty trash

Entry and living room

  • Clean the front door, door frame, light switches and entry floor.
  • Vacuum and wash entry mats; red clay and leaves get tracked in fast in the Upstate.
  • Dust shelves, TV stand, picture frames and ceiling fan blades.
  • Vacuum under sofa cushions and furniture.

Kitchen

  • Clean inside the oven before the turkey goes in, to avoid smoke.
  • Empty and clean the refrigerator to make room for holiday food.
  • Degrease the stovetop, range hood and backsplash.
  • Wipe cabinet fronts and handles.

Dining room

  • Dust the chandelier and light fixtures.
  • Clean the table, chairs and glass surfaces.
  • Polish hardwood floors around and under the table.

Guest bathroom

  • Toilet inside and out, including the base.
  • Shower glass, mirror and chrome without spots.
  • Fresh towels, extra toilet paper and an empty trash can.

Guest bedroom

  • Wash bedding and pillows that have been stored.
  • Dust nightstands, lamps and blinds; vacuum under the bed.
  • Clear space in the closet and a drawer.

After the holidays

Once guests leave, a post-holiday cleaning resets the home: carpets, kitchen grease, bathrooms and dining room. Many Greer clients book one deep clean before and one after. Not sure which service? Compare deep cleaning vs. regular cleaning.

Book your holiday cleaning early

November and December are the busiest months for house cleaning in Greer. Request your estimate early to secure your date. See also how to prepare your home for a cleaning so the team can focus on the details.

The 30-minute guest-arriving-soon routine

Guests just called and they are on the way? Focus only on what people see and touch:

  1. Clear and wipe the kitchen counters.
  2. Load the dishwasher and wipe the sink.
  3. Guest bathroom: toilet seat and rim, sink, mirror, fresh towel.
  4. Quick vacuum of the entry and living room.
  5. Gather clutter into a basket and put it in a closet.
  6. Empty the trash and light a candle.

How to host without cleaning all week

  • Book a deep cleaning 2 to 4 days before guests arrive, so the house is fresh but still clean on the big day.
  • Clean the oven early. A dirty oven at 400°F smokes and smells.
  • Prepare for the day after. A post-holiday cleaning brings the house back to normal while you rest.
  • Use disposable or easy-wash table linens for kids’ tables.
