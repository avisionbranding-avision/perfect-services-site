The technique matters as much as the product

We wipe by hand and use microfiber cloths that capture dust instead of spreading it. That is a small difference you can see: the next day, the surface of the desk and the monitor stand is still clean and not covered in fine dust again. Our hand-wiping technique also reduces the amount of product used, which helps in a small home office where odors collect.

The areas we pay attention to in a working household:

  • The desk area, cable runs and the floor around the chair.
  • Door handles, light switches and shared surfaces in the kitchen.
  • Kitchen counters where lunches are prepared on the days you are home.
  • Bathrooms, which take the most time because soap scum needs patience with gentle products.
  • Vents and baseboards, where dust collects when the heat is running.
