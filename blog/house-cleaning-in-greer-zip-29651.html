Most homeowners in Greer 29651 who call us already know they want help. What they cannot decide is how often. Every week feels like a lot, once a month feels too far apart, and nobody wants to pay for visits they do not need. This piece compares the three schedules the way we explain them at the kitchen table, so you can pick one with your eyes open.

The three schedules at a glance

Weekly: the "never think about it" option

When a house is cleaned every week, nothing has time to harden. Soap scum stays soft, pet hair does not mat into the carpet, and the kitchen floor never gets sticky. Visits are quicker and the price per visit is lower than a monthly one, though you pay for more of them. Families with young children and dogs tend to prefer this, as do people who host often.

Biweekly: where most Greer homes land

Every two weeks is the middle ground. Bathrooms and kitchens still look good the day after, and the budget is easier on a household that does not need constant upkeep. If you travel, or only use part of the house, this is usually the sweet spot. Many customers start here and move to weekly around the holidays.

Monthly: good, with one caveat

A monthly visit works if the house is light-use and you tidy between visits. The caveat is that each visit does more catching up, so the price per visit is higher and the home slides a little between cleans. Some people use monthly visits and an occasional deep cleaning a few times a year, and that combination works for them.

A quick way to choose

Ask yourself three questions. How long can the bathrooms stay untouched before they bother you? Does anyone in the house have allergies or a pet that sheds heavily? How often do you have people over? If the first answer is "about a week," choose weekly. If it is "two weeks," go biweekly. If you rarely notice until the month is up and you live with little traffic, monthly is realistic.

Another trick is to start biweekly for two months and watch. If the team arrives and the house is still in good shape from the last visit, you could stretch it. If you are doing a mini-clean the day before each visit, move up. The estimate visit is a good time to talk it through, and the team will tell you plainly if they think a different schedule suits the house better than the one you picked.
