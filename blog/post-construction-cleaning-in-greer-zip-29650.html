The three-stage idea, in plain words

Think of the clean as stages that follow the construction schedule. The rough clean happens after the heavy work, the final clean after the last painter and trim carpenter, and the touch-up right before the buyer walks in. Booking all three means fewer surprises, because each visit starts from a house that is already mostly clean. Our post-construction cleaning service page explains the stages for contractors.

What a rushed clean misses

We have walked into houses where the floors were mopped but the tops of the doors were still white with dust. Walk your own job and run a finger over these spots: the top edge of door frames, inside the bottom drawer of the kitchen, the inside of the range hood, window tracks, and the inside lip of the bathroom vanity. If any of them leave a gray line on your finger, the house needs another pass.

Timing

Small renovations take a long day. A full new build is a multi-day, multi-person job. Booking this week is possible depending on the calendar, but contractors should call early because the final clean is almost always tied to a deadline.

Before we arrive

  1. Have the crew remove trash, scrap and packaging.
  2. Make sure water and power are on.
  3. Tell us if there are protective films on counters or floors that should stay.
  4. Point out any surfaces that are delicate, such as unsealed stone.

Homeowners versus builders

The two groups need different things. A builder wants speed and consistency across several houses, a reliable team that shows up on the day the buyer is coming, and a result that reads the same house after house. A homeowner living through a remodel usually wants something gentler: someone who understands that the family has been eating off paper plates for six weeks and just wants the kitchen to feel like a kitchen again.

For homeowners, we recommend deciding in advance which rooms you will use first. Cleaning the kitchen and a bathroom well, then the bedrooms, lets you move back in gradually while the rest of the house is finished. For builders, it helps to send the closing date and a list of the houses at the start of the week so the crew can be planned.

Surfaces that need a careful hand

New does not mean tough. Freshly installed stone, unsealed grout, brushed metal fixtures and new hardwood can all be marked by the wrong product or too much water. We ask about the finish of each surface at the visit, and we use the gentlest method that works. If a surface has not cured yet, such as freshly sealed floors, the team will tell you to wait rather than risk it.

What is the difference between post-construction cleaning and a deep clean?

Post-construction cleaning removes fine dust, paint specks, adhesive and stickers from new work and uses multiple floor passes. A deep clean targets built-up household dirt.

Yes. Contractors can ask for an estimate that covers a schedule of homes, and the team can plan the phases around each closing date.
