Office cleaning checklist for small businesses in Marietta

Office cleaning checklist for small businesses in Marietta, SC: daily, weekly and monthly tasks, restrooms and break rooms, plus a free in-home estimate.

November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Split office cleaning into daily, weekly and monthly tasks so nothing is missed.
  • Restrooms, break rooms and high-touch points matter most to staff and visitors.
  • Perfect Services quotes commercial cleaning after a free on-site estimate.

A small office does not need a janitorial contract the size of a hospital's, but it does need a routine. In a quiet foothills community like Marietta, off US-25 and US-276 on the way to the Blue Ridge, many offices are one or two rooms, a salon chair or two, or a showroom with a front counter. The cleaning work is smaller, yet customers judge the place in the first ten seconds. This checklist splits the work into what happens daily, weekly and monthly.

Daily tasks

These are the jobs that visitors and staff see and touch.

  • Wipe desks, counters and workstations
  • Disinfect high-touch points: door handles, switches, keypads
  • Clean, disinfect and restock restrooms
  • Wipe the break room sink, microwave and fridge exterior
  • Remove trash and recycling
  • Vacuum or mop the main floors
  • Clean glass doors and entry areas

Weekly tasks

Once a week, go further. Dust reception and waiting areas, including shelves and magazine racks. Wipe baseboards near entries, where grit collects. Detail the restroom floors and the base of toilets. Clean chair arms and phone handsets. If the office has carpet, vacuum edges and under desks, not just the center aisle.

Monthly tasks

These get skipped most often. Dust vents and light fixtures, clean window sills and blinds, wipe the inside of the break room fridge, and spot-treat carpet or hard-floor edges. Doing them monthly keeps them from becoming a larger project later.

Marietta offices tend to be small and personal, so the cleaning should be too. A salon needs clean floors under the chairs and sanitized handles. A small professional office needs a clear front desk and a restroom that never gets a second look from a visitor. A shop needs clean entry glass and a tidy fitting area. Share what your customers see first and we plan the checklist around it.

A few details separate a careful office clean from a quick one. Phone handsets and keyboards collect more than a desk does, and so do shared pens and the coffee pot handle. The area under the front mat catches grit from the road. Trash bins, especially in restrooms, need wiping inside and out, not only emptying. Our team works through a written list at each visit so these items are not left to memory.

Seasonal changes matter, too. Spring pollen drifts in through the front door and settles on reception counters. Wet winter weather tracks mud onto tile and carpet, and entry floors need extra passes then. A routine that adapts to the season keeps the office presentable without extra effort from your staff.

Do it yourself or hire a team?

Many owners start by cleaning after hours themselves. That works until the schedule gets tight or the restroom is the only room anyone notices. A team that comes on a fixed schedule, daily, weekly or monthly, takes the checklist off your plate. We work after hours if the business needs it, so your staff and customers are never in the way.

How long does an office clean take?

A small office often takes only an hour or two per visit, and a larger space takes proportionally longer. The estimate gives the time for your layout so you know what window to leave for the team.

One last point is access. Tell us how the team gets in, whether that is a key, a lockbox or a code, and where alarm instructions are posted. Marietta businesses often keep irregular hours, and clear access instructions keep every visit on time. We also ask which areas are off limits, such as a locked file room, so there is no confusion.

It is worth reviewing the checklist every few months. Offices change: a new employee, a new coffee machine, a busy season. A quick review keeps the checklist matched to how the space is really used, and the cleaning plan stays useful instead of becoming a routine nobody reads.

Supplies are another small detail that adds up. A restroom that runs out of soap or paper towels by noon reflects on the whole business, so our team checks stock at every visit and tells you what is running low. If you prefer to buy your own paper goods, we simply restock from what you leave. For businesses with a green preference, we can talk about gentler products at the estimate, as long as they handle the job on your floors and fixtures.

Finally, think about who is in the building when the team arrives. A salon with evening appointments needs a different schedule than a weekday office, and a retail shop that opens at nine needs the floors finished before then. Tell us your hours and we will build the visit around them.
