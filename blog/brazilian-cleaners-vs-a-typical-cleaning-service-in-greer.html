What Brazilian cleaners emphasize

Our Brazilian detail-first method is a way of working, not a product. The cleaners follow a consistent order and finish each room before moving on.

  • Start at the top: fans, light fixtures, vents and shelves
  • Work down to counters, fixtures and surfaces
  • Finish with the floors, including corners and edges
  • Check the small things: switch plates, door frames, tracks, faucet bases
