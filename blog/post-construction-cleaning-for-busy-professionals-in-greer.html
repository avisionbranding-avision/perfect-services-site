Know which clean you are booking

Construction cleaning is usually not one visit. It comes in stages, and knowing the stages lets you book only what you need:

StageWhenWhat it handles
Rough cleanAfter framing and drywallDebris and heavy dust before finish work
Final cleanAfter the contractor's last tradeDust, glass, fixtures, floors
Touch-upJust before move-in or closingWhat settled since the final clean

Perfect Services can book these together, so you deal with one schedule and one point of contact. A new build or full remodel often uses all three. A kitchen or bathroom renovation may need only the final clean.

What the final clean includes

The post-construction cleaning service covers the things that stay behind after trades leave:

  • Fine construction dust removed from surfaces, ledges and trim
  • Inside cabinets, drawers and closets
  • Windows, glass, tracks and sills
  • Paint specks, sticker residue and adhesive
  • Light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans
  • Bathrooms and new fixtures polished
  • Floors vacuumed and washed in multiple passes
  • A final touch-up pass before move-in

The place where we see the most time lost is windows. Labels, tape marks and paint on the glass and tracks take patience, and they are the first thing a buyer or inspector sees in good light.

A schedule that protects your closing date

  1. Ask the contractor for the date the last trade finishes, then book your estimate before that date.
  2. Book the final clean for the day after that last trade, not the same day.
  3. Hold the touch-up for the day before your closing or move-in.
  4. Confirm how the team will access the site and who will meet them.
  5. Walk through once yourself, or have your agent do it, after the touch-up.

Never book the final clean while carpenters or painters still have days left. Dust returns and the cleaning is wasted. A good contractor will tell you honestly when the site is ready.

How long it takes

A small bathroom or kitchen renovation is a short job. A complete house is typically a full day or more with a team, and the first pass takes the longest because of the volume of dust. Greer is our home base, so the crew is close, usually about ten minutes away, and a touch-up visit before a closing is easy to add. If you are booking this week, say so and we will tell you what is open.

What the walkthrough with the contractor looks like

If you cannot be on site, ask the contractor to meet our team for ten minutes at the start of the final clean. The purpose is simple: the contractor points out which surfaces are new and need gentle care, such as unsealed stone or finished wood, and what is still waiting on a delivery. We note what is finished so we do not clean a room that will need another visit. This kind of short handoff prevents the most common mistake on a busy project, which is cleaning on the wrong day.

It also helps to know who holds the punch list. Builders usually keep a list of small fixes after the final inspection, such as a door that sticks or a scuffed wall. Those repairs make dust again. Ask your contractor when the last of them will be done and book the touch-up after that, even if it falls a day later than you first planned.

Checking the work before you sign off

Once the final clean is done, a short inspection is worth your time. These are the places to look:

  • Run a finger along window sills and the top of the door frames
  • Open a drawer in each new cabinet and check the corners
  • Look at the glass in good daylight for streaks and paint specks
  • Check the tracks of sliding doors and the base of the shower
  • Look across the floor at an angle, where haze and dust show most

If something was missed, tell the team during the walkthrough, because that is the moment to fix it.

Frequently asked questions

Should I book the cleaning before or after the contractor finishes?

After the last trade finishes. A rough clean can happen earlier in a build, but the final clean should wait until painters, carpenters and installers are done, or dust returns.

Can the rough clean, final clean and touch-up be booked together?

Yes. They can be scheduled as one project so you handle a single schedule. The free in-home estimate is where we agree which phases your project needs.

Will you remove paint specks and stickers from windows?

Yes. Paint specks, sticker residue and adhesive on glass, tracks and sills are part of the service, along with light fixtures, vents, fans, cabinets and floors.
