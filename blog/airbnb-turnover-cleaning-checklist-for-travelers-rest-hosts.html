Airbnb turnover cleaning checklist for Travelers Rest hosts

Airbnb turnover cleaning checklist for Travelers Rest, SC hosts: kitchen, bathrooms, linens, restocking and photo reports, plus how pricing works.

Key takeaways

  • A turnover is judged by the next guest's first minute, so the order of the checklist matters as much as the list.
  • A damage and missing-item report with photos catches problems before the next check-in.
  • Pricing follows property size, turnover window, linens, restocking and how many turnovers you run, and it comes after a free in-home estimate.

The review that hurts a Travelers Rest listing is rarely about the view. It is a hair in the shower, a sticky remote or a trash can that was not reset. Guests notice small things in the first five minutes, so a turnover checklist has to be built around what a person sees and touches when they walk in with their bags.

This is the checklist we use as a starting point for Airbnb cleaning in Travelers Rest, written so you can follow it yourself or compare it with what your current cleaner does.

Before you clean: the first five minutes

Do a quick walk through before touching anything. Open the windows or turn on the fans, strip the beds, collect used towels and take out the trash. Look for anything left behind and anything broken. This walk is where a damage and missing-item report starts, and photos taken now are far more useful than a memory later. For hosts who live out of town, this report is often the only way to know what happened between check-out and the next arrival.

The turnover checklist

  • Kitchen reset: dishes done and put away, counters wiped, appliances cleaned inside and out, sink polished.
  • Bathrooms cleaned and disinfected: toilet, shower, tub, mirror, vanity and floor.
  • Beds made with fresh linens (provided by the host).
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped, including under the beds and along the edges.
  • Trash removed and bins reset with fresh liners.
  • Restocking: toilet paper, soap, coffee and other supplies refilled from the host's list.
  • Staging to match the listing photos, so the room looks like what the guest booked.
  • Final look from the doorway, the way a guest will see it.

Order of work

The sequence saves time. Linens go in first if laundry is on site, since the washer runs while everything else gets cleaned. Then bathrooms, because they need the longest contact time with cleaning products. Kitchen next, then bedrooms and living areas top to bottom, and floors last so no one walks across wet surfaces or drags dust back onto them. Restocking and staging happen at the very end, when the space is finished and you can see what is missing.

Travelers Rest details that affect turnovers

Travelers Rest is a lively small town on the Swamp Rabbit Trail, with homes that lean toward the mountains. Guests there often arrive after hiking or biking, which means muddy shoes and damp gear near the entry. Checking the entry floor, the mat and any bench or hooks is worth the extra minute. Many guests also come in through US-25 or US-276, so late arrivals are common, and a clean, staged space matters even more when someone shows up tired after a long drive.

A word on guest-ready details

Hosts who clean their own turnovers often tell us the same thing: the hardest part is not the scrubbing, it is staying consistent when a guest checks out at eleven and the next one arrives at three. A written checklist taped inside a closet helps, and so does taking the same photos after every clean. When the photos look the same each time, you can spot a change in a few seconds, such as a scuffed wall, a missing lamp shade or a stained cushion. If you use a cleaning team, ask for the same photo set. It turns the cleaning visit into a record you can use if you ever need to talk with a guest or the platform about a problem.

Restocking deserves a short list of its own. Keep a simple count of toilet paper, hand soap, dish soap, trash bags, coffee filters and anything else you promise in the listing, and check it at every turnover. Running out of toilet paper at night is the kind of detail guests remember in a review.

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Travelers Rest?

There is no single rate, and anyone who quotes one without seeing the property is guessing. These are the things that move the price:

  • Property size: bedrooms and bathrooms.
  • Turnover window: tight same-day turnovers need more careful scheduling.
  • Linens: laundry on site or off site.
  • Restocking: a supplies check and refill.
  • Frequency: how many turnovers you have per month.
