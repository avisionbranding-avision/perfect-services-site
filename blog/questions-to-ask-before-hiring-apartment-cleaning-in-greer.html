Questions to ask before hiring apartment cleaning in Greer

Before you hire apartment cleaning in Greer, SC, ask about gate codes, parking, pets, what is included and how the estimate works. A renter and owner list.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Small spaces still need a written list of what is cleaned, especially balcony glass and baseboards.
  • Gate codes, parking and building rules are part of the booking, so raise them early.
  • Price comes after a free in-home estimate, not before.

Hiring someone to clean a 900-square-foot apartment sounds simple until the cleaner cannot get through the gate, parks in a fire lane and leaves the balcony door smeared. Most of the trouble with apartment and condo cleaning in Greer comes from small logistics nobody asked about. This list is for renters, condo owners and townhome owners who want to ask the right things before booking apartment cleaning in Greer, SC.

Questions about getting into the building

Do you need a gate code, a key fob or a lockbox? Many complexes along US-29 and near I-85 have gated entries or keyed building doors. Tell the company how the crew gets in and whether a front desk or property manager needs a heads-up. If you are home for the first visit, say so, because that is the easiest way to start.

Where does the crew park? Condo and apartment lots often have guest limits or permit rules. A crew that circles the lot for twenty minutes is a crew that shows up late to the next job. Ask the company whether it plans parking in advance and whether it needs a visitor pass.

Are there elevator or move-in time rules? Some buildings restrict loud equipment or require elevator reservations for cleaning supplies. It is a short question, and it saves an awkward call from the property office.

Questions about the work

A small space leaves less room to hide missed spots. Ask for a written list. Ours for apartments and condos covers kitchen counters, sink, stovetop and appliance fronts, the bathroom (toilet, shower, tub, mirror and vanity), vacuumed and mopped floors, dusting of surfaces and shelves, beds made, trash emptied, baseboards and door frames, balcony or patio door glass and light switches and handles.

  • Is the oven inside included or an add-on?
  • Are interior windows included, or only the balcony door glass?
  • Do you bring your own vacuum and mop heads?
  • Are baseboards done every visit or on a rotation?

The baseboards question matters more than it seems. In a small unit the baseboards run the whole perimeter, and they collect the pet hair and dust that a quick vacuum skips. A company that touches them up on a rotation is fine as long as it tells you the rotation.

Questions about your household

Do you work around pets? Cats and dogs mean hair along walls, under the couch and in the corners of the bedroom. Let the cleaner know before the visit so they can bring the right attachments, and mention any pet that does not like strangers. A crate or a closed bedroom door is usually enough.

What if I am a student or on a short lease? Some residents want a single clean before a visit from family, others want a regular schedule. Ask whether the company offers both and whether the first visit is deeper than the ones after it. If you are moving, you may actually want move-out cleaning in Greer, which has its own checklist for getting a deposit back.

Questions about timing and what to do while they work

Should I be home? You do not have to be. Plenty of residents give the crew access and go to work. But for a first visit, being there for the first ten minutes helps: you can point out the bathroom fan that rattles, the drawer that sticks or the counter that should never get a harsh product. After that, most people hand over the code and let the team work.

How long will it take? Small spaces move fast, but good work still takes time. Ask for a rough window and plan around it, especially if the crew needs the whole unit and you work from home. Our team usually walks through before starting so the plan for that particular place is clear.

What should I put away? Clear the kitchen counters of loose items, take valuables off open surfaces and leave the dishwasher closed unless you want it emptied. The less on the surfaces, the more actually gets wiped. A short tidy of the floor, with shoes and laundry picked up, lets the vacuum reach the edges where the dust hides.

How much does apartment cleaning cost in Greer?

Ask the question early, and expect a straight answer about how it is decided. At Perfect Services, the price follows the unit size (a studio is not a three-bedroom townhome), the number of bathrooms, how often you book, whether you have pets and what access looks like, including gate codes and parking. Recurring visits generally cost less per visit than single ones. We do not give a figure over the phone or from photos. After a free in-home estimate, where the team sees the space, you get an exact price with no commitment. That is the easiest way to avoid a surprise.

Questions about trust and follow-through

What if something is missed? Ask for the company's policy before the first visit. A good one says to message them right away and they will return to fix it.

Who is in my home? You should know how many people are coming and who they are. Smaller crews mean fewer strangers and a more consistent result. Perfect Services is a Brazilian-owned local team in Greer, and the same people tend to return to the same homes.
