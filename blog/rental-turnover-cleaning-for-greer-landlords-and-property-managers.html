What a turnover clean covers that a regular clean does not

A weekly or biweekly visit keeps a lived-in home tidy. A turnover is different: the home is empty, nothing is hiding the baseboards, and the next person will inspect every corner. Our move-in and move-out cleaning follows a fixed list, which is exactly what a property manager needs.

  • Inside and outside of every kitchen cabinet and drawer
  • Inside the refrigerator, freezer, oven and microwave
  • Range hood, stovetop and backsplash degreased
  • Toilet base, grout, glass and vanity interiors in every bathroom
  • Closet shelves, rods and floors
  • Baseboards, doors, door frames and light switches
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped edge to edge

Because the list does not change, you can hand it to a tenant at move-in and use it again at move-out. Nobody argues about what "clean" meant.

The order that saves you a second visit

Most turnover problems are scheduling problems. A cleaner who arrives before the painter leaves will clean the same room twice. One who arrives after the next tenant has carried in boxes cannot get to the floors or the closets.

  1. Tenant moves out and returns keys.
  2. You or your maintenance contact does repairs, touch-up paint and any carpet work.
  3. The cleaning team comes in last, top to bottom, with the home still empty.
  4. You do the final inspection and photograph the unit.
  5. The next tenant gets the keys.

If a unit needs both a repaint and a full clean, tell us when you book. Fresh paint leaves specks on window glass and sills, and we would rather plan for that than discover it halfway through. For a unit that was left in rough shape, we may suggest a deep cleaning pass first, which we can discuss during the estimate.

Helping a deposit dispute before it starts

When a tenant disputes a deduction, the best defense is a record. A written checklist, dated photos before and after, and a cleaning invoice that lists what was done are far easier to stand behind than a general statement that the unit was dirty. If you manage several units, send both documents to new tenants at move-in so expectations are set early.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a rental turnover clean take in Greer?

It depends on the size of the unit and how it was left. An empty apartment moves faster than a furnished house, and heavy oven grease or bathroom buildup adds time. We confirm the schedule during the free in-home estimate.

Should the cleaning happen before or after repairs and painting?

After. Cleaners should come in once repairs, paint and carpet work are finished, so dust and paint specks are cleaned only once.

Can you clean several units for the same property manager?

Yes. Tell us how many units and the move-out dates, and we will plan the visits around your schedule. Each one still starts with a free estimate.
