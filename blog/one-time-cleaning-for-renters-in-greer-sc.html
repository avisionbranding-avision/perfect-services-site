Thanksgiving is days away, your in-laws arrive on Wednesday, and the rental you moved into last spring has never had a proper top-to-bottom clean. Is a one-time clean the right choice?

A single visit fits some situations better than others. Most renters we talk to fall into one of these:

  • Family or friends arriving for the holidays
  • A party the weekend before, and a mess to reverse
  • A first try of a cleaning service before committing to recurring visits
  • A lease inspection or renewal walk-through coming up
  • A first deep reset after months of keeping up on your own

If the home is heavily built up, say so at the estimate. A one-time visit is a full clean, and when something needs more time we tell you before we start.

What the visit covers

Our one-time house cleaning service in a rental includes the full kitchen cleaning and degreasing, bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, bedrooms and living areas dusted, floors vacuumed and mopped, baseboards and door frames, mirrors and glass, and trash emptied. Then come the extras you may want:

How to prepare in 20 minutes

You do not need to scrub before the team arrives, and please do not. Just clear what is in the way: dishes in the sink, clothes on the floor, valuables on the counter. Tell us about pets, anything fragile and any area you want us to skip. In Greer rentals, the usual surprise is how much red clay and pollen dust ends up around the entry and on windowsills, so we start there and finish at the door.

How much does one-time cleaning cost in Greer?

We do not post prices online. A one-bedroom and a four-bedroom house are different jobs, and the same home costs more after a long gap since its last professional clean. The factors:

  • Home size, meaning rooms and square feet
  • Condition, based on the time since the last professional clean
  • Add-ons such as the oven, fridge, cabinets and windows
  • Date, since holiday weeks book quickly
  • Pets, whose hair and dander add time

How long it takes and when to book

A one-time clean takes longer than a regular visit because it covers everything. The estimate gives you a time window. With Thanksgiving this week and December filling up, call early and ask what is open before your date.

A room-by-room plan for the visit

The team works from the farthest room back to the front door, and from top to bottom in each room. In the kitchen, we start with the degreasing, because it needs time to work, then move to the bathrooms while it soaks. By the time we return to the kitchen, the surfaces are ready to wipe. Bedrooms and living areas come next, and floors always come last. It is the same sequence we use in every home, and it is the reason a one-time visit can cover so much in one trip.

What renters usually forget to ask about

  • The tracks of sliding doors and windows
  • The inside of the microwave and the top of the fridge
  • Light switches and door handles
  • The vents and ceiling fans you stopped seeing
  • The area behind the toilet and under the vanity

Mention what you care about at the estimate and we build it into the visit. If your list is longer than the time allows, we tell you what to prioritize, and which items can wait for a second visit.

After the visit

A one-time clean resets the home, and what happens next is up to you. Some renters book again before the next big date, some move to recurring visits, and some are happy with a single clean each season. If you want to keep the home from sliding back, the easiest habit is to wipe the kitchen counters and bathroom sink each night, and let the team handle the rest.

One practical note for the holiday rush: if guests are staying overnight, tell us which room they will use. We give the guest bedroom and the nearest bathroom extra attention, make the bed with linens you provide and check the closet and the nightstand. Greer homes near I-85 and SC-14 also tend to collect road dust on windowsills, so we wipe those on every visit. A short list on the fridge, with the date of arrival and any rooms to skip, makes the team's work faster and the result closer to what you pictured.
