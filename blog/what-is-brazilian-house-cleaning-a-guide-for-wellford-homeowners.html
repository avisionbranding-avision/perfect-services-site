What does "Brazilian" cleaning mean?

Perfect Services is a Brazilian-owned company, and our cleaners bring a tradition where the home is treated with pride. In many Brazilian households, cleaning is a weekly ritual, and the standard is high: floors are washed, not just wiped, bathrooms are scrubbed to a shine and small details like window tracks are not forgotten. That is the idea behind the detail-first approach.

  • Top to bottom: start with ceiling fans, vents and light fixtures, finish with the floors
  • Room by room: finish one space completely before moving to the next
  • Edges and corners: baseboards, door frames, switch plates and tracks get attention
  • Hand finishing: bathrooms and kitchen surfaces are wiped by hand, not just sprayed

What is covered in a visit?

A regular visit usually includes dusting, kitchen and bathroom cleaning, vacuuming and mopping. Beyond that, the detail-first method typically adds closer work at stove tops, cabinet fronts, mirrors, sinks and faucets, and floor edges. How long does it take?

A small home may take a couple of hours with a team of two, and a larger one may take half a day. Because the cleaners work in a team, each room gets finished in sequence, and you do not need to be home.

Is it right for you?

Brazilian house cleaning is a good fit if you want a regular maid service with consistent quality, if you are tired of cleaners who skip edges, or if you want a one-time clean that feels thorough. If your home needs a bigger reset first, consider deep cleaning, then continue with recurring visits.

How it compares with a quick turnaround clean

Some cleaning services are built around speed: a short window per home and a checklist that covers the obvious surfaces. That can work for a light weekly touch-up, but it often leaves the same trouble spots untouched visit after visit, such as the base of the toilet, the tops of baseboards and the sides of cabinets. A detail-first approach spends the time on those places first, so the home stays cleaner between visits and each recurring visit gets easier.

Seasonal reasons Wellford homes need it

  • Spring: pollen settles on sills, floors and entry areas, so window tracks and floor edges matter
  • Summer: humidity makes bathrooms and kitchens need more attention to grout, drains and fixtures
  • Fall and winter: mud, red clay and holiday guests mean heavier floors and fuller kitchens

Households with pets, children or allergies usually notice the difference between a surface clean and a detailed one, because dust and dander collect at edges and in corners. If that sounds familiar, ask about green cleaning as an add-on.
