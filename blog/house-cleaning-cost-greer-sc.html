“How much does a house cleaning cost?” is the first question almost everyone asks, and the honest answer is: it depends on your home. Two houses with the same square footage can need very different amounts of work. This guide explains every factor that changes house cleaning prices in Greer, SC, so you know what to expect before you request a quote from Perfect Services.

The 6 factors that set your price

1. Size of the home

Square footage is the starting point. More floor, more baseboards, more windows. Open floor plans common in newer Greer neighborhoods can be faster to vacuum but have more visible surfaces.

2. Bedrooms and especially bathrooms

Bathrooms are the most time-consuming rooms in any house cleaning. A 3-bedroom home with 3.5 bathrooms takes noticeably longer than one with 2 bathrooms. That is why our estimate form asks for both.

3. Type of cleaning

  • Regular cleaning / maid service: the most affordable per visit.
  • Deep cleaning: more time and more surfaces. See deep vs. regular cleaning.
  • Move-in / move-out cleaning: includes inside cabinets, closets and appliances.
  • Post-construction cleaning: fine dust removal on every surface; priced by scope.

4. Frequency

Weekly and biweekly clients usually pay less per visit than one-time clients, because the home never falls far behind. Read weekly vs. biweekly vs. monthly maid service.

5. Current condition

Time since the last professional cleaning, pet hair, kids, recent construction dust or heavy kitchen grease all add time to a first visit.

6. Add-ons

Inside the oven, inside the refrigerator, interior windows, laundry or organizing can be added to any service.

Cheap vs. value: what you are really paying for

A low hourly price does not help if the team skips baseboards, rushes bathrooms or needs to come back. When you compare cleaning companies in Greer, ask what is included in each visit. With Perfect Services, the Brazilian cleaning method means the details are part of the job, not an extra.

5 ways to lower your house cleaning cost

  1. Book recurring service instead of one-time cleanings.
  2. Start with one deep clean, then switch to biweekly maintenance.
  3. Pick up clutter before the team arrives so time goes to cleaning. See how to prepare for a house cleaning.
  4. Bundle add-ons like oven and fridge into the same visit.
  5. Send a photo with your estimate request for an accurate quote with no surprises.
