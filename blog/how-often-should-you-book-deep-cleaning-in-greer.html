For most homes in Greer, a deep cleaning once or twice a year is enough, as long as regular cleaning fills the gaps. Some households need it more often, and a few can wait longer. The short answer, by household

Think of a deep cleaning as a reset. Regular maid service holds a standard, and a deep cleaning brings the house back to it when build-up has gotten ahead. This table is a starting point, not a rule.

Signs it is time, whatever the calendar says

Calendars help, but your house tells you more. Walk through it with fresh eyes and look for the things that regular wiping does not reach.

  • Grout lines in the kitchen or bathroom have turned gray or brown
  • Cabinet fronts and handles feel sticky near the stove
  • Ceiling fan blades and vents have a visible layer of dust
  • Window sills and tracks collect pollen and dead bugs
  • The area behind and around the toilet base keeps a smell
  • Baseboards and door frames have scuffs that a damp cloth will not lift

If you check three or more, it is probably time. If you have not had a professional clean in months, that is the clearest sign of all.

Seasons that matter in the Upstate

Greer has a few natural moments to book. Spring is the big one: pollen coats everything for weeks, and windows, sills and vents hold it long after the trees quiet down. Late fall is the second, before the holidays bring in guests and a lot of cooking, and before the house closes up for winter and air recirculates through the same filters and vents. Summer humidity adds a third reason for bathrooms, where moisture feeds mildew in grout and along caulk lines.

Is deep cleaning worth it between regular visits?

It is, when you pair it with the right frequency. Booking a deep cleaning every month would be a waste, since most of what it covers does not grow back that fast. Skipping it for years is the opposite problem: the grout, the cabinet fronts and the fan blades go from a ten-minute job to a long one.

The sweet spot for most households is a regular visit weekly, biweekly or monthly, with a deep cleaning once or twice a year. Our deep cleaning service includes everything in a regular house cleaning, plus baseboards and door frames hand-wiped, cabinet fronts and handles degreased, grout lines scrubbed, ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures dusted, window sills, tracks and blinds cleaned, and the areas behind the toilet and around the appliances.

If you already have a recurring plan, tell the team at the estimate. Sometimes it makes sense to rotate one deep room into each regular visit, such as the oven one month and the window tracks the next, which spreads the work out. See how that pairs with regular house cleaning in Greer.
