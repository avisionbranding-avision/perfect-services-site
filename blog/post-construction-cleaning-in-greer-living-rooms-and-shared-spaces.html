Post-construction cleaning in Greer: living and shared spaces

Post-construction cleaning in Greer living rooms and shared spaces: how fine drywall dust is removed from open floor plans, trim and glass. Free estimate.

Key takeaways

  • Open living areas collect the finest drywall dust, on every ledge, vent and trim edge, not only the floor.
  • The work goes high to low in phases, with a touch-up pass before you move furniture back.
  • Perfect Services gives prices after a free in-home estimate, because dust level and project size decide the time.

Drywall dust is the reason a freshly remodeled Greer living room can look finished and still leave a white film on your fingers. It settles on the top of the door casing, the window ledge, the TV wall and the slats of every vent, and a normal vacuum moves it into the air instead of removing it. This is how post construction cleaning handles the living room and the shared spaces around it.

We have written separate pieces on post-construction bathrooms, so this one stays in the open areas: living room, hall, entry and the dining space that usually sits next to it.

Why a shared living space is harder than a bedroom

Open floor plans, common in newer Greer neighborhoods near GSP Airport and the BMW plant, mean the dust has nowhere to stop. A kitchen island that was sanded two weeks ago can leave residue on the other side of the great room. In older homes near Downtown Greer, an added room or a taken-down wall does the same thing in a smaller footprint.

The living area also has more surface to cover: tall windows, built-ins, trim around archways, vents high on the wall and a lot of flat floor. The dust level is the biggest factor in the time it takes, and sanding drywall produces the most.

The three passes in a living area

Pass one: high to low

We start at the top. Light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans come first, since anything we disturb will fall. Next the tops of door and window frames, the ledges, shelving, and the trim. Wiping, not blowing, is the rule. Compressed air only relocates the problem.

Pass two: glass, fixtures and details

Windows and glass doors get their sills and tracks done along with the panes. Paint specks and sticker residue on glass come off carefully, without scraping where the surface could scratch. Any new fixtures, outlet covers and switch plates are wiped and checked.

Pass three: floors

Floors are last and they get multiple passes. The first vacuum lifts the loose dust, the wash picks up what is left, and a final vacuum or wipe follows once everything has dried. The same floor can look clean after the first pass and gray again after the second, so patience matters.

What to clear before the crew arrives

  • Remove tools, ladders and leftover materials. Cleaners are not there to haul a job site.
  • Cover or move furniture that stayed in the room. Anything left behind will have dust under its drop cloth.
  • Finish touch-up painting first. A wet wall and a cleaning crew do not mix.
  • Tell us where the construction ended. A paint-and-trim job needs less than a wall removal.
  • Keep the HVAC filter changed or covered if the work is still ongoing.

Rough clean, final clean and touch-up

Larger jobs are often split into phases, and the living area benefits from that. A rough clean after demolition and framing clears the heavy debris. A final clean after the trades finish removes the fine dust. A touch-up pass right before move-in catches what settled since. They can be booked together or one at a time, and our post-construction cleaning in Greer is built around that sequence. What does a Brazilian detail-first clean change here?

Some homeowners ask whether the living area really needs this much attention, since the bedrooms and kitchen are where the work happened. The honest answer is that the living room is where you will spend the first evening back in the house, with the windows open, the lights on and your eyes on the walls. A thin layer on the console table or the TV stand shows up exactly then, and it is much easier to remove before furniture and decor return than after.

Our team is Brazilian-owned and the method is hand-wiping surfaces, not spraying and moving on. In a living room that means the top of the trim, the inside of window tracks and the back edge of shelves. These are the spots a contractor's quick sweep misses, and the spots homeowners see at 4 p.m. when sunlight comes through the window.

Frequently asked questions

Why does dust come back after a post-construction clean?

Fine drywall dust settles slowly and also sits inside vents and on high ledges. That is why the final pass and a touch-up before move-in are useful, especially in open living areas.

Do you remove paint specks and stickers from windows?

Yes. Paint specks, sticker residue and adhesive are part of the checklist, and we remove them carefully so the glass is not scratched.

Can the cleaning be booked before the contractor finishes?

A rough clean can be booked earlier, and the final clean after the trades are done. Describe your schedule at the free estimate and we will line the phases up.
