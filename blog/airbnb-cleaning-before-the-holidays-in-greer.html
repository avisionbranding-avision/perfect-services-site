Airbnb cleaning before the holidays in Greer

Airbnb cleaning before the holidays in Greer: how to prepare your rental for Thanksgiving through New Year guests, with a booking timeline and free estimate.

  • Holiday bookings cluster around a few dates, so cleaning slots should be reserved before the calendar fills.

If your Greer rental has bookings for Thanksgiving week and the days around Christmas, the calendar is already telling you something: your cleaning needs to be planned now, not the morning guests arrive. Hosts who wait usually end up with back-to-back checkouts and nobody available. This piece is for those hosts, and it walks through a simple plan for the holiday stretch using Airbnb and vacation rental cleaning in Greer.

Start with a timeline, not a to-do list

A to-do list tells you what; a timeline tells you when. For a holiday season, we suggest mapping three moments:

  1. Before the first holiday guest: a thorough reset of the whole property, including the places that normally get skipped.
  2. Between holiday stays: a standard turnover, shortened only if the previous guests left it in good shape.
  3. After the last guest: a final reset before January bookings or before you close the calendar for a few weeks.

Write your check-in and checkout dates beside each of these, then send them to your cleaner. A cleaner who knows the whole calendar can group visits and avoid gaps.

What the pre-holiday reset should include

Holiday guests tend to stay longer and cook more. That means the kitchen is the first place to focus, followed by the bathrooms and the living area where people gather. During a first-visit walkthrough the team looks at:

  • Inside the oven and microwave, since holiday guests actually use them.
  • Refrigerator shelves cleared of leftovers from the last stay and wiped down.
  • Baseboards, door frames and cabinet handles, where fingerprints and grease collect.
  • Bathroom grout and shower glass, which show wear quickly in photos and reviews.
  • Guest bedding set and spare linens counted, so you know if you need another set.
  • Entry floors and mats, because November rain and Upstate clay come in with every arrival.

The service also includes the usual damage and missing-item report with photos, which is useful when many different families rotate through in a few weeks.

Staging for the listing photos

Guests book from photos, and then they compare the house to them when they arrive. For the holidays, that comparison matters more because people travel with family and expectations are higher. Staging to match your listing means the throw pillows, the table setup and the towels look the way the listing shows them. If you add seasonal decor, keep it simple and easy to move, since the team has to clean around it quickly.

Small things that protect your reviews

Most complaints from holiday stays come from small misses, not big messes: a crumb line behind the toaster, a hair in the shower drain, a sticky remote control. Ask your cleaner to check those specific places on every visit. The Brazilian detail-first approach is built around exactly that, finishing the little spots after the obvious ones are done.

It is also worth leaving a short note for guests with trash day, the thermostat limits and where extra blankets are kept. It reduces the mess caused by confusion, and it makes the next turnover faster. For more ideas on season-specific planning, read our fall Airbnb cleaning guide for Greer and the general vacation rental cleaning service overview.

Pet-friendly and family stays

Many holiday guests bring dogs, and many bring small children. Pet hair collects along the baseboards, under couch cushions and in the corners of staircases, and it takes a different pass from the regular vacuuming. Children leave sticky handprints low on doors and walls, which are easy to miss when you clean from standing height. Telling the team ahead of time that a stay includes pets or kids lets them add those passes, and it may affect the time we plan for the visit.

Hosts who allow pets should also mention it at the estimate, because it changes how the floors and soft furnishings are handled. We would rather plan for it than be surprised by it on a tight turnover morning.

After the holidays

When the last guest leaves in early January, plan a deeper reset, because the property has been used heavily. That is the best time to rotate mattress covers, wipe inside closets and check for any damage that did not show up during busy turnovers. Then your calendar is clean for the next season, and you can use our estimate form to set a regular schedule.
