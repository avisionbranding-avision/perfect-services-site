Winter apartment cleaning in Greer: indoor season tips

Winter apartment cleaning in Greer SC: condensation, dry air and closed windows change what a small space needs. Tips, a checklist and the free estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 25, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Closed windows and heat make dust, pet hair and kitchen grease build up faster in a small apartment.
  • Window tracks, balcony door glass and bathroom fans need attention in the cold months.
  • Prices come after a free in-home estimate, based on unit size, bathrooms, frequency, pets and access.

In a Greer apartment, winter means the windows stay shut from November to March. Whatever gets stirred up inside, from cooking steam to pet hair to the dust a heater blows around, has nowhere to go. A two-bedroom unit can feel noticeably dustier by January than it did in October, and it is not because anyone stopped cleaning. It is because the air never changes. This guide covers what to adjust for apartment and condo cleaning in Greer during the indoor season.

Why small spaces feel the cold season more

A house has a garage, a mudroom, a basement. An apartment has one front door and one living area that holds everything. Wet coats, boots, grocery bags and delivery boxes all end up within a few steps of each other. The carpet near the door picks up red clay, and the vinyl by the kitchen collects grease spatter that does not get ventilated away.

Condos and townhomes near I-85 and US-29 add another factor: the shared walls and hallways mean less natural airflow, so odors from cooking linger longer. Knowing that, the team looks first at the places where air and moisture sit.

A winter checklist for apartments

Use this as a monthly guide, or hand it to us so we focus on it during a visit:

  • Window tracks and sills, wiped dry, since condensation turns to gray film.
  • Balcony or patio door glass, cleaned on both sides and the track vacuumed.
  • Bathroom exhaust fan cover, because moisture holds dust there.
  • Heating vent covers and the baseboards near them.
  • Range hood filter and the cabinet tops above the stove.
  • Door handles, light switches and the thermostat panel.
  • Entry floor and the first foot of carpet near the door.

Our standard visit already covers kitchen counters, sink, stovetop and appliance fronts, bathroom fixtures, floors, dusting, baseboards and door frames, and sanitizing of handles and switches. The list above simply tells the team where winter makes the biggest difference.

Pets, hair and the heater

Pet hair is the most common reason apartment visits run longer in winter. Heat dries the air, hair floats instead of settling, and then it lands on dark furniture and under the couch. A quick vacuum with a brush attachment on upholstery before the cleaning visit helps, but the team will still work along the baseboards and under the bed. If you have a pet, tell us at the estimate. It is one of the factors that changes the time needed.

How long a visit takes

A studio or one-bedroom can usually be finished in a morning, while a three-bedroom condo with two bathrooms takes longer. If you want a rough idea of the day, the estimate visit is when we tell you. Winter does not change the visit length dramatically, but a unit that has not been cleaned since the fall can take longer on the first pass than on later ones.

What the first visit looks like

On a first visit, the team does a short walkthrough with you or with your notes. We look at the bathroom, the kitchen and the floors, ask which rooms matter most, and note anything fragile or off limits. Then the work starts in the kitchen or bathroom, whichever is heavier, and moves out toward the living area. Having the same people return on a recurring schedule means they remember your preferences, such as which products you want avoided or which shelf is not to be moved.

Recurring or one-time?

Many renters start with one visit before a holiday and then keep going every two or four weeks. A recurring schedule is usually easier on the budget per visit, and it keeps the buildup from returning. For people who work long shifts at the BMW plant or at the offices near GSP Airport, having someone handle the weekly basics frees up the weekend.
