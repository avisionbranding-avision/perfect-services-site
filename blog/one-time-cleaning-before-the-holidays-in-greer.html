One-time cleaning before the holidays in Greer

One-time cleaning before the holidays in Greer SC: how to time a single visit, what to include and how the free in-home estimate gives you an exact price.

Perfect Services Team
November 25, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Book a single holiday visit for 2 to 4 days before guests arrive, and ask early since holiday weeks fill up.
  • Decide on add-ons like the oven and refrigerator before the visit; they matter most for hosting.
  • The price comes only after a free in-home estimate, based on size, condition, add-ons, date and pets.

The week before Thanksgiving, the same thing happens in many Greer kitchens: someone opens the oven to check on it and decides it needs help. A one-time cleaning scheduled before the holidays is meant for that moment. It is a single visit, no contract, aimed at the rooms your guests will actually see. This article is about timing and planning that visit, and it is different from our winter guide because here the clock is set by your guest list.

Pick the day by working backward

Start with the day guests arrive, then count back. A visit two to four days earlier leaves time for the house to stay neat while you cook and decorate, and still gives us room to adjust if something comes up. A visit the same morning leaves no margin. For one-time cleaning in Greer, we recommend asking about dates as soon as you know the hosting schedule, since the days before Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Eve fill first.

What to prioritize in a holiday visit

Not every room matters equally for a party. In our experience the rooms guests use most are the ones to put first:

  1. Kitchen: oven, refrigerator, counters, sink and the stovetop where most of the cooking happens.
  2. Powder room or main bathroom: every guest uses it, so the sink, toilet, mirror and floor need to be finished.
  3. Dining and living areas: baseboards, dusting, floors and the glass on doors and frames.
  4. Guest bedroom: dusting, vacuuming, and the closet doors and handles.
  5. Entry: mats, floor and door, the first things people touch.

Everything else, including the rooms you will keep closed, can be done lighter or left out, which keeps the estimate in line with what you actually need.

Add-ons that matter before the holidays

The optional add-ons are worth a real decision here. Inside the oven is the obvious one, since a clean oven works better and does not smoke during a long roast. Inside the refrigerator helps when you need the space for sides and drinks. Inside cabinets matters if you are rearranging dishes for a large meal. Mention these when you book the estimate so they can be included in the plan.

How much does one-time cleaning cost in Greer?

Perfect Services does not quote a price by phone or photo. We give the exact number after a free in-home estimate, which is a short visit with no commitment. The factors:

  • Home size, counted in rooms and square feet.
  • Condition and how long since the last professional clean.
  • Add-ons, such as oven, fridge, cabinets or windows.
  • The date, since holiday weeks book quickly.
  • Pets, which add hair and dander to the work.

If the budget is tight, tell us. We can suggest which rooms to include and which to skip.

What we do in the first hour

A visit typically starts with a quick walk through the home. The team notes where the heavy areas are, such as the kitchen after a season of cooking, and sets up cleaning supplies in one place. Dusting begins at the top of each room, then the bathrooms are treated, which gives the products time to work. The kitchen follows, and the floors are always last. That order is part of the Brazilian detail-first method: you should not see footprints on a floor we just finished.

Houses with kids, pets and visitors

Holiday visitors bring luggage, shoes and sometimes pets. The first week of a stay can undo a clean house in a day. For that reason some clients schedule a second visit after the holidays, a smaller one to reset after the guests leave. If you expect that, mention it, and we can plan both visits together. For kids, we focus on low surfaces such as door handles, baseboards and stair rails, where small hands go.

Doing a bit yourself without redoing our work

Many clients ask what they can do beforehand. The most useful help is simple: clear the dishwasher, collect the laundry, and put the mail and school papers somewhere out of the way. Leave the scrubbing. Products and techniques differ by surface, and a half-cleaned shower can actually be harder to finish than an untouched one. If there is a room you do not want entered, such as a home office with loose papers, tell us and we will keep the door closed and note it on the plan.

After the visit, keeping the house fresh is easier than starting from zero. A quick daily wipe of the kitchen counters and a weekly floor pass hold the result for much longer, and it is the reason many people pick a lighter recurring plan after the first visit.

What if the holiday dates move?

Plans change: a flight is delayed, a relative decides to host instead, a child gets sick. If your dates shift, tell us as early as you can and we will move the visit to the closest open slot. Because holiday weeks are busy, a reschedule within a few days is easier to arrange than one at the last minute. We also keep a short list of clients who want an earlier time if one opens, so mention that when you book.

Finally, if you are traveling after the holidays, a visit just before you leave is a quiet gift to yourself: you come home to a clean house, with nothing sitting in the sink and no dust on the tables from three weeks away.
