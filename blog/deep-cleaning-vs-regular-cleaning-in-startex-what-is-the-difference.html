Deep cleaning vs. regular cleaning in Startex: what is the difference?

Deep cleaning vs. regular cleaning in Startex, SC: what each one covers, how to choose and when to switch. Free in-home estimate with Perfect Services.

Perfect Services Team
November 28, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Regular cleaning maintains a clean house; deep cleaning resets one that has built up months of dust, grease and grime.
  • Baseboards, grout, cabinet fronts, fans and window tracks are the usual deep-clean items.
  • Most homes do best with one deep clean first, then a recurring plan, and the free estimate tells you which you need.

Short version: a regular clean keeps a clean house clean, and a deep clean gets a house back to that starting point. If you have lived in Startex for years, maybe in one of the older homes near the mill village, you probably know the feeling of a house that is tidy every day but still has a film on the cabinet fronts and dust on top of the fans. That gap is exactly the difference between the two services, and this page lays it out side by side so you can pick the right one for deep cleaning in Startex.

Signs your Startex home needs the deep version

  • It has been several months since anyone cleaned beyond the surface
  • Baseboards are gray and the corners of the floor hold a line of dust
  • Cabinet handles feel slightly tacky near the stove
  • Shower grout looks dark even after you scrub
  • Window tracks hold dirt and dead insects
  • Someone in the house has allergies and you want dust removed, not moved around
  • You are about to start recurring service, or guests and the holidays are coming

If three or more of those sound familiar, a regular visit will not catch up. It will make the house look better for a day, but the build-up stays.

What we do differently on a deep clean

The Brazilian detail-first method is built for this kind of job. We work from the top of the room down so dust falls onto surfaces we have not cleaned yet, then finish with the floors. On a deep clean that means ceiling fans and vents first, then fixtures, then cabinets, then the baseboards, then the floor. Kitchens take longer because of grease on cabinet fronts and around the range hood. Bathrooms take longer because grout and hard water spots are the slowest work in the house.

Startex sits along US-29 close to I-85, about 7 miles east of Greer, so humid Upstate summers and red clay tracked in from outside both show up on floors and door tracks. We check those spots in the walkthrough before giving you any estimate.

How much does deep cleaning cost in Startex?

Is deep cleaning expensive? It costs more than one regular visit because it takes more hours, but it is a one-time reset, not a recurring cost. What sets the price:

  • Home size: square feet and number of rooms set the base time
  • Condition: heavy build-up takes longer to remove
  • Add-ons: inside the oven, fridge or cabinets adds time
  • Bathrooms: grout and hard water spots
  • Access: clutter-free rooms clean faster

Which one should you book?

  1. Book a deep clean first if the house has not had professional help in months, or you are preparing for the holidays or spring.
  2. Book regular cleaning if you already keep up with the house and want help with the weekly work. Our house cleaning service fits weekly, biweekly or monthly schedules.
  3. Do both in order if you want the best result: deep clean once, then recurring visits that stay short because the baseline is already high.

Pollen is the other timing factor. Upstate spring settles yellow dust into window tracks and onto sills, which is why many families schedule a reset afterward. Our article on deep cleaning during pollen season explains that in more detail, and if you are comparing nearby options, we also serve deep cleaning in Lyman.

What the walkthrough tells us

Before we quote a deep clean, we spend a few minutes in the places that show the real condition of a home. We open a kitchen cabinet near the stove, run a finger along a baseboard, look at the grout in the main bathroom and check the track of a window or sliding door. Those four checks tell us more than square footage does. A house that looks tidy can still need a full day, and a lived-in house with a good routine may need much less than the owner fears. Pets, kids and a busy kitchen all change the answer, and so does how long it has been since the last detail clean.

A note on timing

A deep clean of an average home typically takes a team most of a day, and the estimate visit tells us how long yours will run. If you want to book this week, ask early.

Frequently asked questions

Is deep cleaning worth it for a house that is cleaned regularly?

If someone already cleans it weekly, you may only need occasional deep work. If the baseboards, grout and fan blades have not been touched in months, a deep clean is worth doing first.

How often should a Startex home be deep cleaned?

Many families do it once a year or twice, often in spring and before the holidays. Homes with pets or allergies sometimes do it more often.

Does deep cleaning include the inside of the oven and fridge?

Those are add-ons that add time. Tell us during the free estimate and we will include them in the price.
