Microfiber, and why it matters with pet hair

Dander is lighter than ordinary dust. A dry feather duster pushes it into the air, and it settles back on the same surfaces an hour later. Microfiber cloths capture it instead of spreading it, and a hand-wiping technique uses less product on the surface in the first place. On bookshelves, window sills and TV stands, that makes a visible difference in how long the surface stays clean.

  • Cloths are changed between rooms, so hair from the bedroom does not travel to the kitchen
  • Hard floors are cleaned with surface-safe solutions, then dried so nobody walks on a wet area
  • Upholstery is vacuumed, not sprayed
  • Pet food and water areas are cleaned with gentle products and rinsed

Q and A with pet owners we meet in Greer

Will the house smell like cleaning products?

Low-odor is the default we aim for, and a fragrance-free option exists. A clean house should smell like nothing, not like a perfume covering a pet.

Can you remove a strong pet smell with gentle products?

Often, yes, when the smell is on hard surfaces, floors and fabric we can reach. Heavy build-up takes longer with gentle products, so tell us honestly about the condition. A smell that has soaked deep into a carpet or subfloor is another matter, and we will say so at the estimate.

What about my pet during the visit?

If your dog or cat is nervous, a closed room works. Open windows are fine for ventilation, but keep pets away from an open door when the team is carrying equipment.

One practical note on timing: after a floor is mopped, we ask pets to stay off it until it dries, and we open a window if the weather allows. For homes with a cat that likes the counters, we wipe the kitchen surfaces last and leave them dry. Those small choices matter more to a pet owner than the product name on the bottle.

A weekly routine between visits

  1. Wash pet bedding in hot water once a week, and keep a spare so the bed is never out of rotation
  2. Wipe paws at the door after wet or muddy walks; red clay carries in more than most people expect
  3. Vacuum with a filter that traps fine particles and empty the canister outside
  4. Wipe the food mat and the area under the water bowl every day
  5. Open windows for ten minutes on mild days, especially outside spring pollen season
