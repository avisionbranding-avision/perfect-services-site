Deep cleaning before guests arrive: a Greer host's checklist

Hosting soon? This Greer host checklist shows what a one time deep clean in Greer covers, what to do first and how to time it before guests arrive.

Perfect Services Team
November 12, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Guests notice bathrooms, the kitchen and the entry first, so a one-time deep clean should start there.
  • Book the deep clean two to four days before arrival, then do only a light reset yourself.
  • Perfect Services prices a deep clean only after a free in-home estimate.

A one time deep clean in Greer is a popular booking in the weeks before Thanksgiving and Christmas, and also before a wedding weekend or a visit from in-laws. The reason is simple. A house that looks fine day to day can look different when someone else spends two nights in it. They use the guest bath. They open the fridge. They notice the dust on the ceiling fan above the bed. This checklist is written for hosts who want to know where a deep clean makes the biggest difference.

What guests notice first

From years of cleaning homes around Greer, we see the same pattern: guests judge a house in about the same order every time. Entry and floors, then the bathroom they will use, then the kitchen, then the bed. Everything else is secondary. If your time or budget is limited, concentrate there.

  • The guest bathroom: toilet base, grout lines, shower glass, faucet, mirror edges.
  • The kitchen: cabinet handles, the front of the range hood, the fridge handle, the backsplash behind the stove.
  • The entry: door frame, light switches, the floor just inside the door.
  • The guest bed: dust on the headboard, nightstands, baseboards, ceiling fan.

What a deep clean covers that a regular visit does not

A regular visit keeps up the surfaces. Our deep cleaning service goes into the places that build up over months. Baseboards and door frames are wiped by hand. Cabinet fronts and handles are degreased. Grout lines in the kitchen and bathrooms are scrubbed. Ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures are dusted. Window sills, tracks and blinds are cleaned, and the team reaches behind and around the toilet base and along the range hood and backsplash.

Floors are the last step, and they matter more than you might think. In the fall, red clay on light flooring is easy to miss in a normal light and obvious in the afternoon sun, so the team vacuums first and then mops by hand, working back toward the door so no one steps on a wet floor.

It also includes everything in a regular house cleaning, so you do not need a separate visit for the basics. Anything extra, such as inside the oven or fridge, is added on request.

One detail hosts often forget is the kitchen's grease layer. It does not look dirty until a guest stands at the stove at seven in the morning, and then the haze on the backsplash and the cabinet above it is obvious. Hand-degreasing that area is one of the most visible changes a deep clean makes, and it takes a patient hand rather than a stronger product.

A timeline for the week before guests arrive

WhenWhat to do
One to two weeks outBook the deep clean. Declutter guest rooms and closets so cleaners can reach everything.
Three to four days outDeep clean day. Put away valuables and pets' items before the team arrives.
Day beforeFresh sheets and towels, empty the bathroom trash, set out soap and a clean towel.
Arrival dayQuick wipe of the kitchen counters, entry floor and guest bathroom sink.

Booking a few days ahead matters because a deep clean leaves the house bright but not guest-ready until beds are made and the kitchen is restocked. Doing the cleaning on arrival morning leaves no time for that.

If you are hosting for a holiday, think about the order of your own tasks as well. Cooking prep, grocery runs and decorating all create new mess. Have the deep clean finish before you bring in groceries or set up decorations, so you are working in a clean house instead of cleaning around your own projects. Holiday weeks fill up quickly in the Upstate, so a request made early in the month has the best chance of getting the day you want.

What to do yourself so the clean goes further

Cleaners can only work on surfaces they can reach. A little preparation before the visit means more of the time goes into scrubbing rather than moving things around.

  1. Clear the guest room closet floor and nightstands.
  2. Take down anything you do not want wet or moved near the shower and sink.
  3. Pick up pet beds and toys, and tell the team if a pet will be home.
  4. Run a load of guest bedding the day before, so it is ready right after the clean.
