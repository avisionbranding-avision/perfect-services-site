Why a deep clean matters for older homes and older residents

Slow build-up is the point. Bending, reaching and balancing are the first things to get harder, and they are exactly what high shelves, ceiling fans, window tracks and floor-level trim demand. A deep clean takes on those jobs once so the weekly routine gets lighter. It also removes dust, which matters for anyone with breathing problems.

  • Ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures dusted.
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds cleaned.
  • Behind and around the toilet base.
  • Appliance exteriors, range hood and backsplash.
