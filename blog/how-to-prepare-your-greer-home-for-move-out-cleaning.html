How to prepare your Greer home for move-out cleaning

Prepare for move out cleaning Greer homes need: a 48-hour plan so the team finishes faster and you pass the walkthrough. Free in-home estimate in Greer.

Perfect Services Team
November 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Empty the home, clear the oven racks and fridge shelves of food, and leave the water and power on before the team arrives.
  • Taking down your own nails, shelf liners and trash removes the biggest time wasters from the visit.
  • Perfect Services gives the exact price after a free in-home estimate, so you can plan your move with a real number.

The cleaners cannot start until the truck is gone. That is the first rule of preparing for move out cleaning in Greer, and the one that trips up the most people. A home that still holds boxes, a couch or a pile of bags in the garage slows the visit, because the team has to work around everything instead of through the rooms. A little preparation in the last two days makes the clean faster and the walkthrough easier.

Step 1: finish the move before the cleaning day

  1. Move every box, piece of furniture and bag out of the rooms, closets and garage.
  2. Take down curtains, hooks, shelf brackets and wall decor, and pull out the nails and anchors you can remove yourself.
  3. Collect trash, leftover paint cans and cleaning supplies you do not plan to take. The team cleans surfaces, not hauls bulky junk.
  4. Return remote controls, garage openers and mailbox keys to a spot you will remember for the handoff.

If you have furniture the buyer or landlord is keeping, tell us during the estimate so we know which surfaces are in play.

Step 2: handle the kitchen first

The kitchen decides how long the visit runs. Our crew cleans inside and outside every cabinet and drawer, inside the oven, refrigerator, freezer and microwave, and degreases the hood, stovetop and backsplash. You help the most by emptying all of it first.

  • Throw away food, spices and anything open in the pantry and fridge.
  • Unplug the refrigerator at least a few hours ahead if you can, so the freezer has time to defrost, and lay towels along the base to catch water.
  • Take out the oven racks and any loose pans or liners.
  • Empty the dishwasher and remove stray dishes from the sink.
  • Remove shelf paper and sticky drawer liners.

Heavy grease or baked-on spills are not a problem, but they do add time, and we note that during the estimate. Starting with a wipe-down helps less than you would think; it is better to leave the buildup alone and let the team handle it with the right products.

Step 3: leave bathrooms and closets empty

Bathrooms get scrubbed from the toilet base to the grout, glass and the inside of the vanity. Take your toiletries, shower curtain and bath mats with you. Shelves inside closets and the floor beneath them get cleaned as well, so pull down hangers and the last of the shoes.

Greer homes with sliding doors or a lot of window tracks collect red clay dust and spring pollen, so clear the sills and any blinds you took down. If you have a window air conditioner or box fan in a track, move it out too.

One more thing that is easy to forget in the rush: power and water. The team needs running water for mopping and scrubbing, working outlets for the vacuum, and light enough to see the corners. Do not cancel utilities until after the clean. Make sure the door code, key or lockbox is arranged, and tell us about gate codes if you live in a community with a gate.

What the team does once you leave

The visit follows a set order. We start at the top of each room with light fixtures and vents, then the cabinets, bathrooms, closets, baseboards, door frames and light switches. Window sills, tracks and blinds come next, and floors are vacuumed and mopped edge to edge at the end so nothing is stepped on twice. That order is part of the Brazilian detail-first approach Perfect Services is built on, and it is the reason we ask you to move everything out first.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to clean before the move-out cleaners arrive in Greer?

No deep cleaning is needed. You only need to remove your belongings, trash and food, and take out oven racks and fridge shelves if you want to. The team handles the scrubbing.

Should I defrost the freezer before move-out cleaning?

Yes, if you can. Unplugging the freezer a few hours before the visit lets the ice melt, and the team can wipe the interior properly. Lay towels at the base to catch the water.

What should be on during a move-out cleaning in Greer?

Keep the water and power on during the visit, since the team needs both for scrubbing, mopping and the vacuum. Cancel utilities only after the cleaning and your final walkthrough.
