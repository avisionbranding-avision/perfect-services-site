Fragrance-free cleaning for sensitive Greer households

Fragrance-free cleaning in Greer, SC for sensitive households: how chemical free cleaning works, what to ask for, and the free in-home estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 12, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Fragrance-free is a product choice we can make on request; it is not the same as no products at all.
  • Microfiber and hand-wiping do more of the work, so less product reaches the air.
  • Prices come only after a free in-home estimate, and your own products are welcome.

Some people walk into a freshly cleaned house and smile. Others get a headache within a minute. If anyone in your Greer home reacts to scented sprays, lemon cleaner or heavy air fresheners, a standard clean can leave you worse off than before. Fragrance-free cleaning solves a specific problem: the smell, not just the dirt.

People often search for chemical free cleaning, and it is worth being exact about what that can mean. Nothing a cleaner uses is truly "chemical free", since water and soap are chemicals too. What families usually want is no added fragrance, low odor and fewer harsh products. That is something we can do.

What fragrance-free means in practice

For the eco-friendly green cleaning in Greer service, fragrance-free is an option you ask for. In practice it covers four things:

  • Products without added scent for kitchens, bathrooms and floors.
  • Microfiber cloths that capture dust rather than push it around.
  • A hand-wiping technique that uses less product overall.
  • No air fresheners, scented sprays or fragranced trash bags.

We follow the same room-by-room checklist as any other visit. The difference is the product list, not the standards.

Who asks for it

Most requests come from a few kinds of households. Parents of babies and toddlers who do not want strong smells near sleeping rooms. People with asthma or allergies, especially during pollen season when windows stay closed. People with scent sensitivities who get headaches. And pet owners who notice their animals sniffing and sneezing after a clean.

It also helps to know that pollen season in the Upstate puts extra pressure on indoor air. Our post on why microfiber matters in Greer green cleaning explains the tool side of that.

A short guide to what to tell us before the first visit

Sensitive households do better when we know details ahead of time. Include as many of these as apply:

  1. What exactly triggers a reaction: fragrance, bleach, ammonia, or something else.
  2. Whether you want us to use only your own products. Many families keep a preferred set under the sink.
  3. Which rooms matter most, such as a nursery or a bedroom.
  4. Whether pets have special products or areas to avoid.
  5. Whether you want windows open during and after the visit.

The team will go over this during the free estimate walkthrough, and we write it into the visit notes so that every clean follows it.

What the first visit looks like

The first fragrance-free visit starts with a walkthrough. We look at the rooms you are most worried about, read the labels of anything you want us to use, and write down your limits. After that, the team works in the usual order: dust and high surfaces first, then kitchens and bathrooms, floors last. Windows can stay open if you want them open. Some families like to leave the house during the visit; others prefer to stay in a back room. Either works.

Afterward, the house should smell like nothing, which is the whole point. Clean has a neutral smell. If you notice a lingering odor, tell us, because it usually means a drain, a trash can or a damp spot that needs a different fix.

A few Greer households also ask about laundry rooms and mudrooms, where detergent and pet bedding scents build up. We can wipe and vacuum these with the same plain approach, and we leave your own laundry products exactly as you stored them. Small things like that are what make a sensitive household feel respected rather than managed.

Where gentle products have limits

We are straightforward about this. Gentler products can take longer on heavy build-up. Old soap scum in a shower, baked-on kitchen grease or hard-water film may need extra passes, a longer visit or a stronger product for one spot. If you have a strict limit, we will work within it, but we may tell you a specific job is not realistic in one visit. That is better than promising a result and falling short.

In older homes near Downtown Greer, we see more of this because surfaces have years of layers on them. In newer homes near GSP Airport, there is usually less build-up, and gentle products handle it easily.

Most of our sensitive-household clients settle into a rhythm after the first visit or two. We note which products worked, which rooms need extra attention and any request about timing, such as finishing before a nap or before the kids get home from school. That written note means the next visit starts where the last one stopped, instead of from scratch.

Frequently asked questions

Is fragrance-free cleaning the same as unscented cleaning?

Close, but not identical. Fragrance-free means no scent is added. Some products still have a mild natural smell. Tell us how sensitive you are so we pick products accordingly.

Can I supply my own products?

Yes. We are happy to use the products you prefer. Please have them out and labeled on the day of the visit.

Will it clean as well as regular products?

For normal upkeep, yes. For heavy build-up, gentle products may need more time or an extra visit, and we will tell you that during the estimate.
