Move-out cleaning checklist for Piedmont renters

A move out cleaning Piedmont SC checklist for renters: what landlords check, room-by-room tasks, and how a free in-home estimate sets your price.

  • Walk through your lease and landlord checklist before you start cleaning.
  • Ovens, fridge interiors, baseboards and bathroom grout are the usual deposit problems.

If your keys are due back on a Friday, the cleaning is not the part you want to be improvising on Thursday night. Before you clean: read the paperwork

Pull out your lease and any move-in condition report. Landlords usually compare the unit to that document, and normal wear is treated differently from dirt. Take dated photos of each room when you are done. If anything was already stained or damaged on day one, make sure your move-in notes say so.

The room-by-room checklist

Kitchen

  • Empty and wipe inside and outside of all cabinets and drawers.
  • Clean the oven, including racks, and the microwave inside and out.
  • Empty the refrigerator and freezer, wash shelves and bins, and leave doors propped open if it is unplugged.
  • Degrease the stovetop, range hood and backsplash.
  • Wipe the sink, faucet and the dishwasher front.

Bathrooms

  • Scrub the toilet including the base and behind it.
  • Clean shower walls, tub, glass tracks and grout lines.
  • Wipe vanity interiors, mirrors and light fixtures.

Bedrooms, closets and living areas

  • Clear closet shelves and rods, then wipe them and vacuum the floor.
  • Wipe baseboards, doors, door frames and light switches.
  • Clean window sills, tracks and blinds.
  • Vacuum carpet and mop hard floors edge to edge.

The spots that usually cost people their deposit

Based on the move-outs we see around the Upstate, the pattern repeats. The inside of the oven is first: baked-on grease does not wipe off in five minutes. The bottom of the refrigerator comes next, then the track of a sliding door, then the black line along bathroom grout. Baseboards and light switches round it out, because they are the places a landlord runs a finger. None of these are hard, just slow, and they are easy to run out of time for after carrying boxes all day.

Order matters

Clean from the top down and the back to the front. Dust the ceiling fans and vents before you vacuum, do the kitchen and bathrooms while you still have the water on, and finish with the floors as you walk out the door. If you mop first and then carry furniture across, you will do it twice.
