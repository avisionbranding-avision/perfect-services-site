What is Brazilian house cleaning? A guide for Pelzer homeowners

What is Brazilian house cleaning? Learn the detail-first method, what gets cleaned by hand and how to book a free in-home estimate in Pelzer, SC today.

Perfect Services Team
November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Brazilian house cleaning is a method, not a product: every surface hand-wiped, corners and edges included.
  • The team moves small items, cleans under them and washes floors by hand along the walls.
  • The price comes after a free in-home estimate, since detail level and condition change the time.

Most people who ask what Brazilian house cleaning is have the same story: the last cleaner came, the floors looked fine from the doorway, and a week later they noticed dust along the baseboards and a film on the stove. Brazilian cleaning is the opposite habit. It is slower, closer to the surface and built around corners. This guide explains what it is, what it is not, and what it looks like in a house in Pelzer.

The method in one sentence

Every surface is wiped by hand, in a fixed order, and the edges, corners and small items are cleaned, not skipped. The name comes from the way many Brazilian families clean at home, and Perfect Services is Brazilian-owned, so it is the way we work. There is no secret product. The difference is where the cleaner's attention goes.

What gets done by hand

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not just sprayed and swept over.
  • Corners, edges and baseboards cleaned by hand.
  • Furniture and small items moved, then cleaned under and put back.
  • Door frames, switches and handles detailed.
  • Bathrooms scrubbed, including grout and the toilet base.
  • The kitchen degreased: stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts.
  • Floors hand-washed along the walls, where a mop does not reach.
  • A final walk-through against the room checklist.

The last item matters most. Before we leave, the team goes room by room with the checklist and checks what they did against it. That is the habit that keeps visits consistent from one week to the next.

What it looks like in a Pelzer home

Pelzer is a historic mill village on the Saluda River in Anderson County, and houses here run from older mill-era homes to newer builds along SC-20 and SC-8. Older houses tend to have hardwood floors, tall baseboards, wood trim and tile with grout that has seen decades of use. Those are exactly the places a detail-first approach pays off. The team works along baseboards on a hardwood floor with a damp, well-wrung cloth, not a wet mop, and the grout in older bathrooms gets scrubbed, not only wiped.

Our Brazilian house cleaning in Pelzer follows the same checklist in a newer house, but with more open space and larger kitchens, the order changes a little to keep the team from walking over finished floors.

Who the method suits

  1. Homeowners who were disappointed by rushed cleaning services.
  2. Families who want a detailed clean each visit.
  3. Homes with hardwood, tile and grout that need careful handling.
  4. Anyone who wants the corners done, every visit.

It also pairs well with other work. A first visit can be a deep cleaning to reset the house, followed by regular Brazilian visits. And for households that prefer gentler products, green cleaning uses the same hand-wiping approach.

The first hour of a visit shows the method best. The team walks the house with the checklist, decides the order, and starts in the kitchen or bathrooms, since they need the most contact time. Small items on shelves and counters are lifted, the surface under them is wiped and the item is cleaned before it goes back. Then the team works through bedrooms and living areas with the same habit, and floors come last, so nobody walks over a finished surface. Nothing in that sequence is dramatic. It is simply done every time, in the same order, which is what keeps results steady week after week.

It is worth saying what Brazilian cleaning is not. It is not a different service category with special equipment, and it does not mean a different price structure. It is the same Brazilian house cleaning service done with more attention to edges, with a team that expects you to inspect the corners. If you see a streak or a missed spot, tell us, and we fix it. That feedback loop is part of the method.

Frequently asked questions

Is Brazilian house cleaning different from regular maid service?

The tasks overlap, but the method is more detailed. Surfaces are hand-wiped, corners and edges are cleaned and small items are moved, with a final check against a room checklist.

Do I need to buy special products?

No. The difference is the technique, not a special product. You can also ask for gentler or fragrance-free products if your household needs them.
