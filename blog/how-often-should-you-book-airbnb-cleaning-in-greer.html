How often should you book airbnb cleaning in Greer?

How often to book Airbnb cleaning in Greer, SC: after every checkout, plus when a deeper reset is due. Get a free in-home estimate for your rental.

Perfect Services Team
November 14, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Every guest checkout needs a turnover clean, no exceptions.
  • A deeper reset every so often catches what a quick turnover cannot.

The short answer for Airbnb cleaning in Greer is: after every single checkout. A guest who walks into a bathroom that was cleaned two stays ago will mention it in the review, and one review like that costs more than a year of turnovers. What changes from host to host is not if you clean between guests but how much extra work you schedule on top of the turnover.

Here is how we think about the rhythm, based on the kinds of rentals we see around Greer, from places near GSP Airport and the BMW plant to homes closer to Downtown Greer and Trade Street.

Why every checkout gets a clean

A turnover is a reset, not a deep clean. The team works through the kitchen (dishes, counters, appliances), disinfects the bathrooms, makes the beds with the fresh linens you provide, vacuums and mops the floors, and takes out the trash. That is the part guests judge first, and it has to be repeated every time the door locks behind a departing guest.

Skipping a turnover because the last stay was only one night is the most common mistake we hear about. One night in a bed still means a used shower, a used coffee maker and a trash can with food in it. The house that sat empty for ten days is its own case. Dust settles, the refrigerator smells stale, and the first guest after a gap deserves a reset, not just a quick pass.

What a quick turnover misses over time

After a dozen stays in a row, certain spots build up quietly. These are the things we look at when a host says the place feels a little tired even though every turnover was done:

  • Baseboards and door frames near the entry, where shoes and luggage rub
  • The track of a sliding door and the inside of the window sill
  • Under the beds and behind the nightstands
  • Shower glass, grout lines and the area around the drain
  • Oven door, microwave interior and the front of the refrigerator
  • Light switches, remote controls and cabinet handles

Those details are what we mean by the Brazilian detail-first method: a team that finishes the visible work and then keeps going into the places a guest only notices on day three. We usually suggest a fuller reset every so often, and a good time is the gap between two bookings. If a bigger refresh is due, a deep cleaning in an empty window does the job.

Weather, seasons and the Upstate calendar

Greer has its own rhythm of busy weeks. Spring pollen leaves a yellow film on porches, windowsills and sliding doors, and guests who arrive with allergies notice it right away. Humid summers mean bathrooms and shower corners need more attention, and red clay after a rainy week gets tracked in on shoes and pet paws. Holiday weekends and travel through GSP Airport tend to fill calendars, so it helps to lock in cleaning dates early for those weeks rather than hunting for a team the day before.
