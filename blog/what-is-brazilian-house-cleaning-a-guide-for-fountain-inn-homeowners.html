So what is Brazilian house cleaning, in one answer?

Brazilian house cleaning is a detail-first way of cleaning a home where surfaces are wiped by hand instead of only sprayed, and the edges of a room get the same attention as the middle of it. In practice, that means baseboards, door frames, switch plates, the base of the toilet and the strip of floor along the wall are part of every visit, not an add-on. If you live in Fountain Inn and want Brazilian house cleaning in Fountain Inn, SC, this guide covers what to expect and how to decide if it fits your home.

How is it different from a regular cleaning service?

A regular visit is built around speed and the open spaces: vacuum the floor, wipe the counters, clean the toilet, go. Nothing is wrong with that if you only want a basic reset. Where homeowners get frustrated is when the same dusty baseboard, sticky cabinet handle or grimy shower corner is still there after the third visit.

The Brazilian method flips the order of priority. Detail is the point of the visit, so the team works through a room the same way every time and checks it against a list before moving on. Here is how the two approaches usually compare.

What does a visit actually cover?

Every home is a little different, but these are the details that define the service:

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not just sprayed
  • Corners, edges and baseboards cleaned by hand
  • Furniture and small items moved, with the floor and surface under them cleaned
  • Door frames, light switches and handles detailed
  • Bathrooms scrubbed, including grout and the base of the toilet
  • Kitchen degreased: stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts
  • Floors hand-washed along the walls
  • A final walk-through against the room checklist

How much does Brazilian house cleaning cost in Fountain Inn?

Perfect Services does not quote a price by phone, by text or from photos. Every price comes after a free in-home estimate, which is a short visit where we walk through the space with you. There is no commitment. Here is what decides the number:

What we look at during the estimate walkthrough

When we visit, we usually start in the kitchen and the main bathroom, because those two rooms show us how much detail work the house needs. We look at the stovetop and hood, the shower and grout, the floor edges and the baseboards. We ask about pets, kids, any surfaces you want handled a certain way and anything you would like us to skip. It is a conversation, not a sales pitch.

If your home has seen heavy use or has not been cleaned in a while, we may suggest starting with a deep cleaning before moving to a recurring Brazilian schedule. If you prefer plant-based products, green cleaning can be combined with the same method.

Frequently asked questions

Is Brazilian house cleaning the same as deep cleaning?

Not exactly. Brazilian cleaning is a detail-focused method used on every visit, while a deep cleaning is a more intensive one-time or periodic reset. Many Fountain Inn homes start with a deep clean and then keep up with recurring Brazilian visits.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many homeowners give the team access and go to work. You are welcome to stay, and we do ask to be shown any areas you want treated differently during the estimate.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, the team brings what it needs. If you prefer specific products or have sensitivities, mention it at the free estimate and we will plan around it.
