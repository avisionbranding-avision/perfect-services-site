What is Brazilian house cleaning? Simpsonville guide

What is Brazilian house cleaning? A guide for Simpsonville, SC homeowners to the hand-detail method, what is cleaned, who it suits and how pricing works.

Perfect Services Team
November 10, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Brazilian house cleaning is a detail-first method: surfaces hand-wiped, corners and edges done, floors hand-washed along the walls.
  • It suits homes with hardwood, tile and grout, and homeowners disappointed by rushed cleaning.
  • Pricing comes from a free in-home estimate based on size, detail level, frequency and bathrooms.

"Brazilian house cleaning" is not a brand of product or a secret formula. It is a way of working. When you see it from the doorway, the difference is in the order and the patience: every surface wiped by hand, floors washed along the wall instead of down the middle, and furniture moved before the vacuum goes through. If you have wondered what Brazilian house cleaning in Simpsonville, SC actually means, here is what to expect.

The idea in one sentence

Do fewer things quickly and more things properly. Instead of spraying and moving on, the cleaner touches each surface with a cloth, works out the corners and finishes edges. It is how many Brazilian households clean their own homes, and Perfect Services is Brazilian-owned, so it is how our team learned to work.

What it looks like room by room

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not just sprayed.
  • Corners, edges and baseboards cleaned by hand.
  • Furniture and small items moved and cleaned under, then put back.
  • Door frames, switches and handles detailed.
  • Bathrooms scrubbed, including grout and the base of the toilet.
  • Kitchen degreased: stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts.
  • Floors hand-washed along the walls.
  • A final walk-through against the room checklist.

The final walk-through is the quiet part that matters. Before we leave a room, someone checks it against the list. That habit catches the baseboard that was skipped or the streak on the mirror.

Why Simpsonville homes suit it

Simpsonville is one of the Upstate's most popular family suburbs, and many homes are newer two-story houses off I-385 and Fairview Rd. Two-story homes mean stairs, railings, long hallways and many baseboards, and hardwood, tile and grout throughout. Those are the places a rushed clean misses: the edge where stair treads meet the wall, the spindles, the grout in a kitchen floor. A detail method pays off in exactly this kind of house.

It is also a good fit if a previous service left you disappointed. Common complaints we hear are corners left dusty, floors that look clean from the door but not along the wall, and bathrooms that were wiped but not scrubbed.

A walk through the first hour

People ask what actually happens when the team arrives, so here is the order. We start with a quick look at the house and your list of priorities. Then the crew splits: one person takes the kitchen, because it needs the most soak time, and another starts at the top of the bedrooms and works down. The kitchen gets degreased first, with the stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts, and while the product works the same person moves to the bathrooms.

Floors come last in each room. On hardwood and tile we wash by hand along the walls, then across the open area, so the edges that most cleaners skip are the first thing done. By the end of the first hour, you can usually see a difference in the kitchen and the hallway, which is why many clients ask for the same crew again.

The team brings the supplies and takes the trash out with them, so you do not need to buy anything before the visit. If you have a preference about products, say so at the estimate.

Is it for you?

It suits homeowners tired of rushed cleaning, families who want a truly detailed clean, homes with hardwood, tile and grout that need care, and anyone who wants the corners done every visit. It may be more than you need if you only want a quick surface tidy. In that case, we will say so during the estimate and suggest what makes sense.

A first visit takes longer, because we bring the home up to standard; later visits go faster. You can choose a regular level of detail or a deep Brazilian clean, which includes more scrubbing of grout, fixtures and kitchen surfaces. A deep cleaning or regular maid service can be combined with it.
