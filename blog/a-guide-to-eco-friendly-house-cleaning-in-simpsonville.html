A guide to eco-friendly house cleaning in Simpsonville

If you have a crawling baby, a dog that licks the floor or a family member who sneezes at strong cleaning smells, the question about house cleaning is not just "is it clean?" but "what was used to clean it?" That is where eco-friendly cleaning comes in. In Simpsonville, with its newer two-story family homes, it is one of the most common requests we hear. Here is what it means, what it does not mean, and how to book it.

What "green cleaning" actually means

There is no single official standard, so it helps to be specific. For eco-friendly cleaning in Simpsonville at Perfect Services, it means:

  • Low-odor, family- and pet-friendly products on request.
  • Microfiber cloths that capture dust instead of spreading it.
  • Kitchens and bathrooms cleaned with gentler products.
  • Floors cleaned with surface-safe solutions.
  • The option to use your own preferred products.
  • A fragrance-free option for sensitive households.
  • A hand-wiping technique that uses less product overall.
  • The same full room-by-room checklist as a regular visit.

What stays the same

Going green does not mean a lighter clean. The checklist is the same: kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, floors and baseboards. The difference is in the products and the technique. Because our method is Brazilian and detail-first, much of the cleaning is done with a cloth and a hand rather than with heavy spraying, so the amount of product on surfaces is already lower. A damp microfiber cloth lifts dust, and a rinsed one leaves little residue.

Who benefits most

Families with babies and young children are the most common clients. Pet owners are next, since animals spend time on floors. People with allergies or asthma often notice that strong fragrance bothers them more than dust does, so a fragrance-free option can matter more than anything else on the list. Households sensitive to scent can ask us to skip fragranced products entirely.

Choosing between our products and yours

Some Simpsonville families already have a product they trust, a fragrance-free spray or a particular floor cleaner, and prefer that we use it. That is fine. We follow your preference, and the method does the rest. Other families simply tell us they do not want strong scents and let us choose. Either way, the first conversation is about what you want in the house, and we write it into the notes for your visits so the same approach is used every time.

It also helps to be clear about what "eco-friendly" means to you. For some households it is about the health of a baby or a pet. For others it is about the environment and using less product. Both are reasonable, and both can be met. A cloth and clean water handle more than most people expect, and the hand-wiping technique we use removes dust without needing a heavy dose of spray.

If anyone at home has asthma or allergies, tell us before the first visit. We can plan the order of work so the vacuuming is finished while you are out, and we can skip scented products entirely. The goal is a clean home that does not smell like a cleaning product.
