The three phases of post-construction cleaning in Greenville

Post construction cleaning Greenville SC works best in three phases: rough clean, final clean and touch-up. See what happens in each and when to book.

Perfect Services Team
November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Post-construction cleaning runs in three phases: rough clean, final clean and a touch-up pass.
  • Drywall sanding makes the finest dust, which settles again for days after the first wipe.
  • Price comes only after a free in-home estimate, and deadlines may need a larger team.

Drywall dust is finer than flour, and it gets everywhere: inside drawers, on top of door frames, into the track of a new sliding window. That is why one pass after the contractors leave is never enough, and why post construction cleaning in Greenville, SC is better planned as three phases.

Dust from sanding also travels through ducts, so we pay attention to vents and returns, which is where the fine powder tends to hide. Greenville covers a lot of ground, from downtown condos near Main Street and Falls Park to established neighborhoods across ZIP codes like 29601, 29605, 29607, 29609 and 29615. A kitchen remodel in one and a full addition in another need the same logic: remove the heavy debris, clean everything, then come back after the dust settles.

Phase one: the rough clean

Think of this as clearing the stage before the final scene.

This happens while work is winding down, usually after the messy trades finish and before paint touch-ups and fixtures go in. The goal is not shine. It is to get the house safe and clear so the final trades can finish without working in a dust cloud.

  • Large debris, packaging and scrap swept up and bagged
  • Floors vacuumed to pull up loose dust and grit
  • Rough wipe of ledges, window sills and cabinet exteriors
  • Sticker and tape removal where it is already obvious

Downtown condos near Falls Park add their own limits, such as elevator reservations and quiet hours, so tell us early if the building has rules. Builders and general contractors often book this phase because it protects new finishes from scratching. For a homeowner doing a bathroom or kitchen, it can be a single visit before the plumber and painter return.

Phase two: the final clean

Expect this to be the longest visit, and the one where small details decide the result. Paint specks on glass, for example, come off with patience and a blade at the right angle, not with a spray.

This is the main event, done once the work is finished. The team goes room by room, top to bottom, and slows down for detail, which is where the Brazilian detail-first method shows most.

  1. Fine construction dust removed from every surface, ledge and trim line
  2. Light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans cleaned
  3. Inside cabinets, drawers and closets wiped
  4. Windows, glass, tracks and sills cleaned, with paint specks, sticker residue and adhesive removed
  5. Bathrooms and new fixtures polished
  6. Floors vacuumed and washed in multiple passes

Multiple passes matters on floors. The first one lifts the loose dust, the second picks up what the first stirred up, and a final wash takes care of the film. Skipping that leaves a haze that shows in afternoon light.

Phase three: the touch-up

The last visit is short, but it is the one the buyer or homeowner actually sees.

A lot of owners expect the final clean to be the last visit, and then notice a film on the black granite two days later. Dust does not stop settling the moment we leave. Over the next day or two, whatever was suspended in the air lands on counters and window sills. A final touch-up pass before move-in or the final walkthrough catches it, along with fingerprints and marks from the move itself. For new construction closings, this is the visit that makes the house presentable on the day the buyer walks in.

Which phases do you need?

The right mix depends on the job. Our post-construction cleaning service can be booked as one visit or all three.

ProjectLikely phases
One bathroom or kitchen renovationFinal clean, with touch-up if you are moving back in right away

New construction closingFinal clean and touch-up before the walkthrough
Builder with several unitsPhases booked together, one team across homes

If the house will also need a longer reset afterward, a deep cleaning fits after you have lived in it for a few months.

Timing and how to prepare

The best cleans happen when the schedule leaves room for them. Book the final clean as soon as the last trade has a finish date, not after, because dates fill up near closings and holidays.

Greenville is about 11 miles southwest of Greer, a 20-minute drive on I-85 or I-385, so scheduling an estimate is usually quick. Cleaning time depends on size and dust, from a few hours for a single room to several days for a large project, and we plan it at the visit.

  • Have the contractor finish all sanding and cutting before the final clean
  • Remove tools, ladders and leftover materials
  • Leave a clear path to the water and power if needed
  • Tell us the closing or inspection date as early as possible
