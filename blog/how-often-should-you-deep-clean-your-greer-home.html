How often should you deep clean your Greer home?

How often should you book a deep cleaning service in Greer, SC? A practical schedule by household type, with signs that your home is overdue.

November 12, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Most homes do well with a deep clean once or twice a year, with more often for pets, allergies, babies or a home that gets heavy use.
  • The signs are visible: greasy cabinet handles, dark grout, dusty fans and sills that are dirty again a day after wiping.
  • Perfect Services gives a price only after a free in-home estimate, so the schedule fits your actual home.

If you have a regular cleaner, you may wonder why a deep clean is even necessary. If you do not, you may wonder if you are overdue. Either way, the answer is the same: it depends on how the house is used. A deep cleaning service in Greer, SC does not follow a calendar rule. It follows the house, and the house tells you when it needs one.

The short answer, by household type

As a starting point, this is what we suggest to most clients. It is a rule of thumb from our experience, not a fixed standard, and the estimate visit adjusts it to your home.

Pets deserve a note of their own. Hair collects along baseboards, under the edges of sofas and in the corners of stairs, and dander settles into places a vacuum never reaches. A home with a dog or two can look clean on Monday and feel dusty by Thursday. Two deep cleans a year, spaced about six months apart, gives those spots a proper reset without turning it into a constant chore.

HouseholdTypical deep clean rhythm
Regular weekly or biweekly service, no petsOnce a year, often in spring
Pets, kids or someone with allergiesTwice a year, such as spring and before the holidays
Newborn in the house or a recent illnessOne deep clean when you are ready for visitors, then regular upkeep
No professional cleaning for many monthsOne deep clean now, then a regular schedule
Frequent hosting or a short-term rentalEvery few months, depending on guest traffic

The reason a regular visit does not replace this is time. A routine visit keeps surfaces clean. It does not have room in the schedule for hand-wiping every baseboard, degreasing cabinet fronts or scrubbing grout.

Signs your Greer home is overdue

You do not need to count months. Look at the house.

  • Cabinet handles and fronts near the stove feel slightly sticky.
  • Grout in the shower or kitchen has darkened and does not lighten when wiped.
  • Ceiling fan blades and vents carry a visible gray layer.
  • Window sills and tracks collect dust and pollen again within a day or two.
  • Baseboards and door frames have a film that wipes off gray.
  • Behind the toilet and around its base never quite looks clean.

In Greer, spring pollen and red clay from the yard make the sills and entry floors the first places to give it away. If you see three or four of the signs above, it is time.

Another sign is how you feel. If you wipe the kitchen every evening and it still looks tired, the build-up is probably in the places a daily wipe does not reach: the tops of cabinets, the vent over the stove, the edges of the floor. A fresh start usually fixes that, and the house feels different afterward in a way that is hard to describe until you notice it.

Is deep cleaning worth it if you already have a maid service?

Often, yes, once or twice a year. Think of it as the reset that makes regular visits easier. After a thorough deep cleaning service visit, the maintenance visits can stay shorter because there is no build-up to fight, and that can keep the long-term schedule reasonable. Many clients tell us the first regular visit after a deep clean feels like a different house, because the cleaners can spend their time on freshness instead of catching up. For homes starting recurring service, a deep clean first is almost always the right first step.

It is less necessary if the house is new, lightly used, or already deep cleaned recently. You can also ask for one section at a time. For example, kitchen and bathrooms only, if those are the rooms that matter most. The deep cleaning page for Greer lists what is included. For kitchen detail, see our kitchen deep cleaning guide.

How long does a deep clean take, and how do you plan for it?

A deep clean takes longer than a regular visit, sometimes by a lot, depending on size and condition. The team can give you a clearer time after the walk-through. Plan to be out of the way for the day, and move pets to a quiet room. The best time to book is a week or two ahead, though we can often schedule within the week.

Season matters too. Late winter and early spring are popular, because homes have been closed up for months and pollen is about to arrive. Early November is the other busy window, as people prepare for Thanksgiving and December guests. If your preferred week is full, ask for the next opening and keep the regular schedule going in the meantime.

Frequently asked questions

Is once a year enough for a deep clean in Greer?

For many homes with regular maid service and no pets, once a year is enough. Homes with pets, kids, allergies or heavy use usually do better with two deep cleans a year.

What is the difference between a deep clean and a regular clean?

A regular visit maintains surfaces. A deep clean adds baseboards, door frames, cabinet fronts, grout, fans, vents, window sills and tracks, and the areas behind and around fixtures.
