Spring deep cleaning in Greer, SC: plan it now

Spring deep cleaning Greer: plan ahead in November, line it up with holidays and pollen season, and book a free in-home estimate so March is not the scramble.

Perfect Services Team
November 23, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Booking spring deep cleaning early avoids the March rush after pollen arrives.
  • Work backward from the first heavy pollen week, and do high spots before low.
  • Prices come only after a free in-home estimate.

Ask any cleaner in the Upstate about March and you will hear the same thing: the phone starts ringing the week the yellow dust lands on the cars. By then the calendar is full, and the families who wanted a spring deep clean take whatever date is left. It is November, which is the quiet moment to plan it. This post is not the checklist of what to scrub; it is a timeline for when to do what in Greer between now and spring.

Why plan a spring clean in November

Greer sits between Greenville and Spartanburg counties, and spring pollen season there is well known: pine and oak pollen coats cars, porches and window sills, and gets inside every time a door or window opens. A deep clean timed too early will be undone by the first pollen week, and one done too late means weeks of living with it. The best plan is to deep clean once after the heavy pollen starts to settle, and to do the prep work earlier so the day itself is easier.

What to prepare ahead, in the order that saves time

  • Declutter counters, shelves and floors, since clutter-free rooms clean faster.
  • Take down curtains and wash them, and plan to rehang after the team has done the windows.
  • Change HVAC filters and ceiling fan settings so fans can be dusted cleanly.
  • Move furniture a few inches from walls so baseboards can be reached.
  • Decide on add-ons: inside the oven, refrigerator or cabinets.
  • Think about pets, and tell the team where animals will be.

How much does spring deep cleaning cost in Greer?

The price comes from the time the house needs, and the factors are the same every year:

  • Home size, with square feet and number of rooms setting the base time.
  • Condition: heavy build-up takes longer to remove.
  • Add-ons: inside the oven, fridge or cabinets adds time.
  • Bathrooms, where grout and hard water spots are the slowest work.
  • Access, because clutter-free rooms clean faster.

A note on holidays: if you are hosting in December, resist the urge to do the deep clean first. The house will be lived in hard for the next month, and the best thing to do now is a lighter pass on guest areas and the kitchen. If you want a pre-holiday reset anyway, that is a different visit, and it is fine to book one. Just do not count it as your spring clean.

What the day itself looks like

A deep clean goes beyond a regular visit: baseboards and door frames hand-wiped, cabinet fronts and handles degreased, grout lines scrubbed in the kitchen and bathrooms, ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures dusted, window sills, tracks and blinds cleaned, and the area behind and around the toilet base done. That list is why one deep clean can reset a home. Our deep cleaning in Greer works high to low, so the dust you remove from fans and vents never lands on a clean floor, and it ends with the floors and a final walk through.

For a typical home the visit takes a good part of a day, and larger homes may use a bigger team. Windows are the most common spring request in Greer because of pollen on the sills and tracks, so tell us if that is your focus.

Red clay and pine pollen follow you inside on shoes, paws and open doors. A mat on each side of the entry, shoes off at the door and a quick wipe of the sills once a week cut the load a lot. If you have a screened porch, hose it down before pollen season, so it does not become a dust source. These are small habits, but in Greer they add up by April.

Pets deserve a plan too. If a dog sheds heavily in spring, tell the team, because hair collects along baseboards, under furniture edges and in vents. Brushing the dog outside the week before and washing pet beds a day earlier makes the whole visit cleaner and a little faster.

After the deep clean: keeping it going

A deep clean is a reset, and what you do afterward keeps it. Many Greer families follow it with regular house cleaning every two weeks, so the baseline never slips. Others book the deep clean once a year and keep up by themselves. Either is fine; the point is to choose before the busy weeks, not during them.

Will pollen undo a spring deep clean?

Some will settle again, mostly on sills and porches. Keeping windows closed on high pollen days and using regular visits keeps the result.
