Apartment cleaning checklist for Lyman renters

An apartment cleaning checklist for Lyman, SC renters: weekly routine, deposit-friendly spots and how to book a free in-home estimate for apartment cleaning.

Perfect Services Team
November 23, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Small spaces get dirty in the same few places: stove, bathroom, floors and entry.
  • A weekly 30-minute routine plus a monthly detail pass keeps a deposit safe.
  • Prices come only after a free in-home estimate.

An apartment has fewer rooms than a house, but every square foot gets used, so grime builds up quickly in the same handful of places: around the stovetop, behind the toilet, along the baseboards by the front door and on the sliding glass door to the patio. The checklist below is built around those spots, written for renters in Lyman, SC, where apartments and townhomes sit alongside historic mill homes and new construction.

The weekly routine, about 30 to 45 minutes

Do these in order, top to bottom and back to front, and you will not step on anything you just cleaned.

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop wiped; appliance fronts and handles cleaned.
  • Bathroom: toilet, shower, tub, mirror and vanity.
  • Dust surfaces, shelves and furniture, starting with the highest.
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped, ending at the front door.
  • Beds made, trash emptied and bags replaced.
  • Light switches and door handles sanitized.

It sounds like a lot until you try it with a plan. Most renters we hear from say the first time takes the longest, because they are catching up on weeks of buildup.

The monthly detail pass

These are the things that rarely get done in a weekly routine, and they are what a landlord or property manager notices at move-out.

  • Baseboards and door frames wiped.
  • Balcony or patio door glass cleaned, including the track where grit collects.
  • Inside the microwave and the front of the oven door.
  • Range hood filter and the wall behind the stove.
  • Bathroom exhaust fan cover and grout lines.
  • Closet shelves and the tops of cabinets.

Where renters lose points at move-out

Deposit disputes often come down to a short list: greasy stovetops, soap scum in the tub, dirty baseboards and marks around light switches. If you rent in Lyman and think you may move within the year, doing the monthly pass keeps the final clean from becoming a weekend project. Habits that make the next clean easier, and a note on timing

Wipe the stovetop after each use. Run the bathroom fan during and after showers. Put a doormat at the entry and take shoes off if the building allows. If you have a pet, brush it away from the couch and run a rubber broom over upholstery before vacuuming. None of this takes long, and it spares the baseboards.

Timing matters as well. Lyman is a former mill town, and many of its rentals are older units with original trim and slightly uneven floors, where dust settles into grooves faster than in new construction. Newer apartments have flatter surfaces but also more shared walls and hallways, which means more door traffic. In both, a weekly pass that covers the same ten minutes of each room beats a monthly marathon. If you share the unit with a roommate, split the checklist by room and rotate it every month, so nobody always gets the bathroom. Write the schedule on the fridge. It sounds simple, but a visible plan is what actually makes people follow it. And if your lease ends in a few months, start keeping your final move-out list now, noting scuffs, marks and any damage that was already there when you moved in, so you have a record to compare against.
