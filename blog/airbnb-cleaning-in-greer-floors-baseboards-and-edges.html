Start with the floor type, not the mop

Rentals around Greer mix hardwood, laminate, tile and carpet, sometimes in the same hallway. The tool and the method change for each:

Our included floor work is vacuuming and mopping throughout the property. If a floor needs more than that, such as a stained rug, we will say so at the estimate rather than promise it and miss.

Why Upstate floors get dirty in a particular way

Greenville and Spartanburg counties sit on red clay. After a rainy week, guests walk it into the entry, and it dries into a fine orange dust that spreads when you mop too early. In spring the pollen adds a yellow layer on porch floors and door thresholds. In humid summer months, bare feet leave marks on tile. Knowing this changes the order: we vacuum or sweep first so the mop is not pushing a paste around.

The edges guests actually see

A floor is only as clean as its borders. Here is the checklist we run along the edge of each room:

  • Baseboards wiped along the full length, especially behind doors
  • Corners and the gap where the floor meets the wall, where dust lines build up
  • Under and behind the bed, nightstands and the sofa
  • The track of a sliding patio door
  • Door thresholds and the strip under the front door
  • Vent covers and the floor right below them
  • The edge of the stairs, if the house has them

Baseboards deserve a special mention. In a rental, they pick up scuffs from luggage and suitcase wheels, and they hold the hair that settles at the wall. Wiping them takes a minute per room and changes how the whole space looks in a photo.

Our floor order in a turnover

  1. Pull rugs and light furniture so nothing hides a spot.
  2. Dust baseboards and edges before the floor, so debris falls onto a floor we have not cleaned yet.
  3. Vacuum each room, working toward the door.
  4. Mop from the farthest room back toward the exit, kitchen and bathrooms last.
  5. Replace rugs and furniture, then do a final walk to catch footprints.

Doing the kitchen floor near the end matters, since cooking residue is the stickiest. Frequently asked questions

Do you wipe baseboards during every Airbnb turnover?

Baseboards and door frames are part of how we finish the edges of a room. Heavy build-up that has been there for months is better handled with a deeper clean, and we will point that out at the estimate.

Can you clean hardwood floors without damaging them?

Yes. We vacuum or dry-clean first and use a lightly damp mop, so the floor is not soaked. Tell us about any special finish ahead of time.

How do you deal with red clay tracked in by guests?

We remove the loose dirt with a vacuum or sweep before mopping, so the mop does not smear it. A mat inside the door helps cut down how much comes in.
