What a regular visit includes

Every visit follows the same list so you can check it:

  • Desks, counters and workstations wiped
  • High-touch points sanitized: handles, switches, keypads
  • Restrooms cleaned, disinfected and restocked
  • Break room sink, microwave and refrigerator exterior
  • Trash and recycling removed
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Glass doors and entry areas
  • Reception and waiting areas dusted

Daily, weekly or monthly?

Frequency is the biggest choice you make. A customer-facing business, such as a salon or clinic, often wants daily or near-daily service for the entry and restrooms. A small professional office usually does well with weekly visits. Some spaces only need a monthly deeper pass on top of staff tidying. If you are not sure, we can suggest a starting point at the estimate and adjust after a few visits, since you can see what actually needs attention.

Carpet, tile and hard floors

Floors are where office cleaning most often goes wrong. Carpet needs a slow, thorough vacuum, tile needs a mop and clean water that is changed often, and hard floors need a product that will not leave a film. We check the floor types at the walk-through so the right tools are brought the first time.

After hours and keys

Many offices prefer service after closing. That usually means a key or alarm code arrangement, which we discuss at the estimate. We set expectations about which rooms are accessible, what stays locked and who to contact if something unexpected happens. A written checklist is left or shared, so you can see what was done.

What the detail-first method looks like in an office

Perfect Services is Brazilian-owned and the team cleans by hand, the way we clean homes. In an office that means the edges of desks and the base of the monitor stands get wiped, not just the middle of the surface, the door handles and switch plates are done on every visit, and the corners of the restroom floors get attention. It is slower than a quick pass, and it is easier to see.

Questions worth asking any cleaner

Whoever you hire, ask how the checklist is shared, who inspects the work and what happens if something is missed. At Perfect Services the lead on each visit checks the rooms against the list before leaving, and if you see something wrong, you tell us and the team comes back to fix it. Ask also whether supplies are included, how keys are handled and who will actually be in your space each week. A small, steady team that learns your office is easier for everyone than a rotating crew.

It is also fair to ask about timing. A cleaner who finishes in half the expected time either has a very small space or is skipping parts of the list, and a quick look at the edges of the floor usually tells you which.

Supplies, restocking and small extras

Restocking is part of the restroom work: paper towels, toilet paper and hand soap are refilled during the visit. Tell us whether you supply them or want us to, and we will note it on the checklist. Small extras come up often, such as emptying a shred bin, wiping a conference table before a meeting or cleaning the inside of the microwave once a month. Mention them at the estimate and we will fold them into the plan rather than treating them as surprises.
