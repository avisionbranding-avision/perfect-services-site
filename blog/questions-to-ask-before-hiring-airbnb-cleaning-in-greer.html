Questions to ask before hiring Airbnb cleaning in Greer

Hiring Airbnb cleaning in Greer, SC? Ask these questions about turnover windows, linens, restocking and damage reports before you pick a cleaning team.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Ask who does the walkthrough, how the turnover is ordered and what happens when a guest leaves a mess.
  • Settle linens, restocking and the damage report before the first booking, not after.
  • Price comes after a free in-home estimate, so ask for that visit first.

A host in Greer usually finds out whether a cleaner is any good at 3 p.m. on a Sunday, when the next guest is forty minutes out and a bathroom still smells like the last stay. The questions below are meant to be asked before that Sunday. They apply to any company offering Airbnb cleaning in Greer, SC, including us, and we would rather you ask them of everyone on your list.

Start with the people and the walkthrough

Will someone see the property before the first turnover? A cleaner who quotes a listing from the bedroom count alone has not seen the stairs, the second fridge in the garage or the glass shower that shows every spot. A real estimate is an in-home visit. Ours is free, takes a short while and carries no commitment.

Is it the same crew each time? Short-term rentals are repetitive on purpose. A crew that has cleaned your place ten times knows where the spare pillows go and notices when something is missing. Ask how the company handles sick days and vacations, because the turnover still has to happen.

Who do I reach when something goes wrong? Get a name and a way to reach that person on a Saturday. Greer sits between GSP Airport and the BMW plant, so a lot of stays here overlap with travel schedules, and a message that waits until Monday is a message that arrives too late.

Questions about the turnover itself

Every vacation rental cleaning crew in Greer should be able to describe the job in order. Ours runs top to bottom and ends at the front door, and it covers:

  • A full kitchen reset: dishes, counters and appliance fronts
  • Bathrooms cleaned and disinfected
  • Beds made with the host's linens
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash removed and bins reset
  • Staging to match the listing photos

Ask the company to show you its list in writing and compare it with that one. The gaps are usually in the unglamorous spots: the oven door, the tracks of a sliding patio door, the top of the refrigerator, the drain catch in the tub. If a cleaner cannot tell you where they start in a room and where they finish, the work probably depends on the mood of the day.

What happens in a same-day turnover? Guests who check out at 11 and arrivals at 3 leave a narrow window. Ask how many people come, how long they plan to stay and what they do if checkout runs late. A tight window is workable, but it should be planned, not hoped for.

Linens, laundry and restocking

This is where hosts lose the most time without noticing. Ask directly:

  1. Does the crew wash and dry linens on site, or do they leave them for you?
  2. Who checks paper goods, soap, coffee and the other supplies against your restock list?
  3. What do they do when a supply runs low, and how fast do you hear about it?
  4. Where do stained or damaged linens go?

On our side, beds are made with host-provided sheets and the restock checklist is part of every visit. If you run a lake or mountain place and live out of town, say so up front. It changes how often you want a report and how quickly someone needs to respond when a lockbox code stops working.

Damage, missing items and the report

Do you send photos of damage? You want a cleaner who notices a cracked glass, a burn on the counter or a missing remote, writes it down and sends a photo the same day. That report helps when you need to talk to a guest or the platform, and it protects the cleaner too. Ask whether the report is part of the normal visit or an extra, and what the cleaner does when they find something unusual, such as evidence of a party the house rules did not allow.

How is a complaint handled? If a guest says the bathroom was not clean, you need a cleaner who will look at the issue and fix it, not argue about it. Read the company's reviews for how it responds, and ask for the policy before you book, not after.

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Greer?

The honest answer is that it depends on the property, and Perfect Services does not quote a number before seeing it. Price follows the bedrooms and bathrooms, how tight the turnover window is, whether the laundry happens on site, how much restocking is involved and how many turnovers you book each month. Hosts with a steady calendar often find a regular schedule easier to plan than one-off bookings. After the free in-home estimate you get an exact price for your listing. If a company gives you a firm figure by text message without seeing the place, ask what happens when the real job turns out bigger.
