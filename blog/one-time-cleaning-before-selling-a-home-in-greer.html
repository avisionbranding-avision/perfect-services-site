One-time cleaning before selling a home in Greer: Q&A

Thinking about a one-time cleaning before selling your home in Greer, SC? Answers on timing, what is included, add-ons and how to get a free in-home estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 23, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • One thorough visit before photos is usually worth more than several light touch-ups.
  • Add-ons like the inside of the oven and fridge are what buyers open first.
  • Prices are set after a free in-home estimate, never by photo.

"Do I really need to hire someone, or can I just clean it myself before the photographer comes?" It is the first thing most Greer sellers ask us. The honest answer is that you can do a lot yourself, and that a single professional visit tends to catch what a tired owner stops seeing. A one-time cleaning before selling a home in Greer is the simplest way to get that second set of eyes, and our one-time house cleaning in Greer visits are set up for exactly this.

What does a one-time pre-sale cleaning actually include?

The base visit covers the full kitchen with degreasing, bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, bedrooms and living areas dusted, floors vacuumed and mopped, baseboards and door frames, mirrors and glass polished, and the trash emptied. For a sale, most owners add the inside of the oven, the fridge or the cabinets, because those are the doors a buyer opens when the agent is not looking.

Notice what is not in that list: moving your furniture, packing boxes or repairing anything. We clean what is there. If you are still living in the house, the best plan is to tidy and put away personal items first, so the team can reach every surface.

When is the right moment to book?

For most sellers, it is the day or two before the listing photos. That way the images show the house at its best, and you keep it that way for the first weekend of showings. If the house stays on the market for a while, a lighter second visit helps, but one good clean at the start does most of the work.

Holiday weeks fill fast, and Greer homes listed around Thanksgiving and the end of the year tend to book several days out. If you want a visit this week, call and ask. We will check the calendar, but we will not promise a time before we know the size of the job.

A quick pre-listing checklist you can do the night before

  • Clear kitchen counters and the tops of the fridge and cabinets.
  • Empty the dishwasher, the sink and the trash cans.
  • Take down magnets, papers and school schedules from the fridge.
  • Pick up floors: toys, shoes, cords, pet bowls.
  • Strip bathroom counters down to one soap and one hand towel.
  • Put away laundry baskets and make the beds.
  • Tell us about pets, locked rooms and anything fragile.

Where the time goes: kitchens, baths and the places no one cleans

In most of the houses we see before a sale, the kitchen takes the longest. Grease builds on the range hood, the tops of cabinets and the handles near the stove. Bathrooms come second: soap scum on the glass, mineral spots on faucets, and the grout lines. After that come the small things people stop seeing, such as door frames near the light switches, the floor behind the toilet and the window sills in the bedrooms.

Greer has a wide range of homes. Older houses near Downtown Greer and Trade Street often have more trim, deeper window sills and older finishes that need gentler handling. Newer houses near GSP Airport and the BMW plant tend to have larger open kitchens and more glass. Either way, we plan the order the same: high to low, back of the house to the front door, floors last.

Pets deserve their own note. A house with a dog or cat has hair along baseboards, under sofas and in the corners of rooms, and a faint pet smell that the owners no longer notice. We vacuum edges and under movable furniture, wash pet bowl areas, and wipe the lower walls near the favorite napping spots. If your pet will be home during showings, a quick brush and a vacuum on the morning of each showing keeps the work from the cleaning visit lasting.

Windows are another question we hear. Buyers look out of them, so streaks and dust on the sills show in photos taken against the light. Windows can be added to the visit, and we usually suggest the main living areas and the kitchen first, where the view sells the house.

How much does one-time cleaning cost before a sale in Greer?

That depends on the home, so a flat number would be a guess. What changes the price is the home size in rooms and square feet, the condition and how long since the last professional clean, any add-ons such as the oven, fridge, cabinets or windows, how busy the date is, and whether pets live in the house.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I book a pre-sale cleaning in Greer?

A few days before the photographer or the first showing is ideal. Holiday weeks fill quickly, so call early and ask about the calendar.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not necessarily. Many sellers are out during the visit. We just need access and a note about pets, locked rooms and anything fragile.

Can I add the oven and refrigerator?

Yes. Inside the oven, fridge and cabinets are common add-ons for a sale. Mention them at the estimate so they are included in the price.
