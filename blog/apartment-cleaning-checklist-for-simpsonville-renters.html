Apartment cleaning checklist for Simpsonville renters

An apartment cleaning checklist for Simpsonville SC renters: kitchen, bath, balcony door and baseboards, plus how to get a free in-home estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Work top to bottom and finish with floors.
  • Balcony door tracks, baseboards and switch plates are easy to miss.
  • Pricing follows a free in-home estimate.

Most apartment move-ins in Simpsonville start the same way: a ground-floor unit or an upstairs condo, a small kitchen, one bathroom and a lot of boxes. The cleaning list for a space like that is shorter than for a house, but it has its own traps, and the schedule is usually tighter. Here is a checklist built for renters.

Simpsonville (ZIP 29680 and 29681, Greenville County) is a family suburb along I-385 and Fairview Road, with apartment communities mixed in with newer two-story homes. Gate codes, parking and elevator timing can matter as much as the cleaning itself.

Start with a plan, not a mop

In a small unit, order saves more time than effort. Open the windows if the weather allows, set trash and recycling by the door, and work top to bottom. Dust falls, so shelves, door frames and light switches come before counters, and floors come last.

The kitchen in a small footprint

  • Counters, sink and stovetop, with a look at the drip pans
  • Appliance fronts, including the handle on the fridge and dishwasher
  • Microwave inside, then cabinet fronts and handles
  • Check inside the oven if your lease says it must be returned clean

Bathroom and bedroom

The bathroom is small but heavy-use: toilet, shower or tub, mirror and vanity. Do the shower first, spray and wait, then scrub, so the cleaner does the work. In the bedroom, dust the nightstands, the closet shelf and the baseboards, and make the bed last so the room looks finished.

Details people skip

  • The track of a sliding balcony or patio door
  • Glass on the patio door, inside and out
  • Baseboards and door frames
  • Switch plates and handles, which are sanitized for high-touch areas

How long does an apartment take?

A one-bedroom is often a short visit for a team and an afternoon for one person. Add pets, a second bathroom or a move-out inspection and it stretches. Our team works against a room list, so a small unit does not get shortchanged because it is small.

If you are leaving, the list is heavier and includes inside the oven and fridge, which is a different service from a regular visit. See our move-in and move-out cleaning for that. If you are staying, a monthly apartment clean is usually enough.

Common mistakes in small spaces

Small units hide dirt in small places. The area behind the refrigerator door gasket, the bottom of the shower curtain track, the gap between the stove and the counter, and the top of the bathroom door all collect more than people expect. Pet hair under the bed and in the corners of the closet gets overlooked too.

Another trap is cleaning one room at a time without finishing. Tackling the bathroom, then the kitchen, then going back to dust the bedroom means walking dirt through clean areas. Work in one direction from the far end of the unit to the front door.

Things to know about Simpsonville apartment living

  • Check your lease for what the management expects at move-out, such as carpet cleaning receipts
  • Ask about quiet hours so cleaning does not run into evening limits
  • Reserve the elevator or loading area if you are moving at the same time
  • Leave the thermostat comfortable, since a team works faster when the unit is not hot

Smaller spaces also benefit from small habits between visits: a quick shower wipe, a daily counter wipe, and a weekly sweep keeps the monthly clean from turning into a full reset.

A simple monthly rhythm for renters

Most apartments stay in good shape with a light weekly routine and a deeper monthly pass. Weekly: counters, bathroom surfaces, floors and trash. Monthly: baseboards, the inside of the microwave, the shower track, window sills and the refrigerator shelves. Twice a year: behind the furniture, closet floors and the top of cabinets.

That rhythm is also what a recurring cleaning service follows, so it is easy to split the work: you handle the quick weekly items and the team handles the detail visit once a month. Renters in Simpsonville with young kids or pets often choose a visit every two weeks for the same reason.

What to tell the cleaner

A short note goes a long way. List the rooms to skip, the surfaces that need gentle products (stone, wood, glass tops), and anything you are worried about, such as a stained tub or a scratch on the floor that was there before you moved in. For pets, mention their names and whether they are friendly, so the visit goes smoothly.

If you are expecting a landlord inspection, say that too. The team can aim for the details an inspector tends to check, like baseboards, switch plates and the oven door glass.
