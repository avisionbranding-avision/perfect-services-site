Deep cleaning in Greer: bedrooms and closets

Deep cleaning bedroom Greer: mattress edges, closets, ceiling fans, baseboards and carpet. See what a deep bedroom clean includes and how to book.

You know a bedroom needs a deep clean when you sneeze while making the bed. Dust does not disappear after a regular tidy. It settles on the tops of doors, on the blades of the ceiling fan and in the corner behind the dresser, then returns when the air moves. This is a look at deep cleaning bedrooms in Greer, from the ceiling down, and how it differs from the routine visit.

How a deep clean differs from a regular one

A regular visit keeps surfaces fresh. A deep clean goes after build-up: what has collected since the last time anyone moved the furniture, took down the blinds or wiped the trim. In a bedroom, that usually means three layers: high (fans, vents, fixtures), middle (furniture, door frames, windows) and low (baseboards, floors, under the bed).

Our deep cleaning service in Greer includes everything in a regular clean plus baseboards and door frames wiped by hand, cabinet fronts degreased, light fixtures and vents dusted, and window sills, tracks and blinds cleaned.

Top to bottom: the bedroom deep-clean order

  1. Ceiling fan blades and motor housing, wiped with a damp pillowcase or cloth so dust falls in the cloth, not on the bed.
  2. Vents, light fixtures and the top edges of the door and closet frames.
  3. Window sills, tracks and blinds, slat by slat.
  4. Headboard, nightstands, dresser fronts and the backs of mirrors.
  5. Bed frame and the space around it, pulled out where safe.
  6. Baseboards, edges and corners, wiped by hand.
  7. Floors vacuumed edge to edge, then washed or carpet vacuumed slowly.

What lives under the bed and behind the dresser

Everything. A deep clean is the time to pull furniture away from the wall. We typically find lint, pet hair, loose socks and coins under the bed, and a gray line of dust on the wall behind the dresser. If you store bins or luggage under the bed, tell us if you want them moved or left in place, and we will follow your lead. For heavy furniture, we clean around it and tell you what we could not reach.

  • Under the bed and the sides of the frame
  • Behind nightstands and dressers
  • Top of the wardrobe or armoire
  • Cords and lamp bases
  • The edge of the mattress, vacuumed where safe

Allergy-minded bedrooms

Greer has a heavy pollen season in spring, and the yellow-green dust gets into window tracks and onto sills even when the windows stay shut. Anyone who wakes with a stuffy nose will notice the difference when those surfaces are wiped with a damp cloth rather than a dry duster, which tends to lift dust into the air. We work high to low and vacuum last, so the room is not stirred up after the floor is done. Pillows and bedding stay your job, but washing them the same day we visit means the room starts fresh.

Closets and carpet

For closets, a deep clean means clearing shelves enough to wipe them, cleaning the floor and the door tracks and dusting the tops of shelving units. In a walk-in, we also wipe the baseboards and light fixtures. We do not organize clothes, but we leave them neatly where they were.

Carpet in a bedroom holds dust deeper than hard floors. A slow, overlapping vacuum pass lifts more than a quick one, and the edges along the wall need a crevice tool. If the carpet needs shampooing or has set-in stains, tell us before the estimate so we can plan.

Guest rooms and kids rooms

A guest room that sits unused still needs this work, because dust settles whether or not anyone sleeps there. A child's room is different: more toys, more crumbs, more things on the floor. For those we ask that toys be put in bins before we arrive so the team can reach the edges and under the bed. In either case, we clean the closet and the baseboards on the same visit.

What to do before we arrive

Clear nightstands of cups and papers, pick clothes up off the floor and tell us about any fragile or sentimental items. Strip the bed if you would like fresh sheets put on, and leave them out. That is all the preparation a deep clean needs, and it lets the team spend the time on the work you are paying for.
