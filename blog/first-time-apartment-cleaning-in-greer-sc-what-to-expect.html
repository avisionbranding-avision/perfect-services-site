Before the appointment: access and parking

Apartments bring practical details that houses do not. Many complexes near I-85, SC-14 and US-29 have gate codes, visitor parking or a leasing office rule about vehicles, and a building with an elevator or a long walk from the lot affects how the team carries supplies. When you book, tell us:

  • The gate code or how we should be let in
  • Where we can park, and any guest pass we need
  • Whether you will be home, and if not, how we get a key or lockbox code
  • Pets in the unit, and where they will be during the visit
  • Anything that should not be touched, like a shelf of collectibles

This takes two minutes and prevents the most common first-visit delay: a team waiting outside a gate.

What the cleaning covers

For a standard visit with our apartment cleaning service in Greer, the team works through:

  • Kitchen counters, sink, stovetop and appliance fronts
  • Bathroom toilet, shower, tub, mirror and vanity
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Dusting of surfaces, shelves and furniture
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Baseboards and door frames
  • Balcony or patio door glass
  • Light switches and handles sanitized

The balcony door glass is one that renters forget. After a humid Upstate summer, the track of a sliding door collects more than it seems, and the glass shows every handprint.

What to put away and what to leave out

You do not need to clean before the cleaners come. That is a common worry, and the answer is no. What helps is clearing the surfaces we need to reach: dishes in the sink, clothes on the bed, papers on the counter. If there are items you want left exactly where they are, say so and we will work around them. Small spaces mean a few things moved can make a big difference to how fast the work goes.

Renters sometimes ask whether the visit can double as a move-out clean. A regular visit and move-out cleaning for Greer renters are separate jobs with different checklists, so mention your goal when you book. If your unit needs a reset first, a deep cleaning is a good starting point before regular visits begin.

Small spaces, specific details

Apartments have their own trouble spots, and the team knows to look for them. Window tracks collect dust and pollen in spring, when the Upstate air fills with yellow dust, so we wipe them rather than skip them. Bathroom fans in older buildings gather lint. The stovetop in a galley kitchen gets splatter on the wall behind it. Door frames and light switches near the entry get touched all day.

We work in a fixed order: kitchen and bathroom first, living areas and bedrooms next, floors last. In a small unit this prevents the team from walking over freshly done areas, and it makes the finished result easy for you to check. Perfect Services follows a Brazilian detail-first method, which means we finish one area properly before leaving it.

If you are a student or a young professional with a tight schedule, the visit can be built around your day. If you are a senior living in a smaller home, the team can go at an unhurried pace and move only what you ask us to move. Tell us what suits you at the estimate and we will write it into the plan.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to clean my apartment before the cleaners arrive?

No. Just clear the surfaces we need to reach, such as dishes in the sink or clothes on the bed, and tell us what must stay where it is.

What if my complex has a gate code or limited parking?

Give us the gate code and parking details when you book, along with a guest pass if one is needed, so the team can arrive and start on time.

Can I be away during the cleaning?

Yes. Many clients are at work. We just need a way in, such as a key or lockbox code, and a note about any pets in the unit.
