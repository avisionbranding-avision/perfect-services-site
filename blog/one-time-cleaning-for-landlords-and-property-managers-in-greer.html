One-time cleaning for landlords and property managers in Greer

One-time cleaning for landlords and property managers in Greer SC: showings, open houses and between-tenant resets, with a free in-home estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 22, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • A one-time visit resets a rental before showings or an open house.
  • Kitchens, bathrooms and entry floors carry the most weight with prospective tenants and buyers.
  • Pricing follows the property and is given after a free in-home estimate.

A vacant rental that sat three weeks with the blinds closed will show worse than the same unit cleaned the morning of the showing. That is the whole argument for a one-time cleaning if you own or manage property in Greer: you do not need a standing contract, you need the unit ready by a date. This post covers when one-time cleaning for landlords in Greer pays off and how to brief the team.

Three moments when a single visit makes sense

Before photos or a showing

Listing photos hide almost nothing. A clean kitchen, bright bathrooms and clear floors read as well maintained. We usually schedule the visit a day or two before photos, so nothing has time to settle again.

After a tenant or a repair

Once repairs are finished, the dust remains. A one-time visit after maintenance removes it from the surfaces, floors and fixtures. For heavy renovation we point you to post-construction cleaning instead.

Before an open house

Open houses invite foot traffic from Greer, Greenville and Spartanburg buyers, and the entry floor takes a beating. Ask the team to finish at the front door, so the entry is the cleanest part.

What we clean, and what landlords tend to add

  • Full kitchen cleaning and degreasing
  • Bathrooms cleaned and disinfected
  • Bedrooms and living areas dusted
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Baseboards and door frames
  • Mirrors and glass polished
  • Trash emptied
  • Optional add-ons: inside the oven, fridge or cabinets

Landlords usually add the inside of the oven and the fridge, since prospective tenants open both. If the unit has been empty, ask us to run the water in each bathroom, since a dry drain can smell.

How much does one-time cleaning cost in Greer?

There is no list price, because a small unit and a four-bedroom house cost different amounts. Our pricing depends on:

  • Home size, meaning rooms and square feet
  • Condition and time since the last professional clean
  • Add-ons such as the oven, fridge, cabinets and windows
  • The date, since holiday weeks book quickly
  • Pets that lived in the unit

How long it takes

A one-time clean of a vacant unit takes longer than a maintenance visit because everything is covered. We give you a window at the estimate. If you need it done this week, call early and we will say what we can fit before the weekend.

Before and after: how we check our own work

For landlords, the most useful part of a one-time clean is the check at the end. The team walks every room again before leaving, looking at what a prospective tenant would look at: the shower, the stovetop, the bedroom closets, the corners of the floor. If something looks off, we fix it before we go. You can also walk through with us if you are on site, and tell us about anything that needs another pass.

What to do with furniture and leftovers

Many units arrive at a one-time clean with leftover items: a lamp, a mattress, a stack of boxes. We clean around what stays and ask what to do with what does not. We do not haul away furniture, so plan removal before the visit. If the previous tenant left food in the fridge or trash in the garage, tell us in advance so we can plan for it, and we will do what is reasonable inside the visit.

Choosing between a one-time visit and a routine

If your unit turns once a year, a one-time visit is enough. If you turn units several times a year, a routine with a set checklist is easier on you and costs less per visit. We can start with one visit and move to a recurring plan if it fits. There is no obligation after the first visit, and we would rather you book what the property needs than more than that.

Whichever you choose, give us the dates early. A rental market that is slow in January can turn busy in a week, and the cleaning slot is the one thing you cannot easily move.

A last tip from the walkthroughs we do in rentals around Greer: check the small fixtures before we arrive. A bulb that is out, a toilet seat that is loose or a drawer that sticks will register with a prospective tenant even in a spotless room. We report what we notice, but we do not repair. Fix those items first, then book the cleaning for the day before the showing, so the unit is at its best when the first visitor opens the door.
