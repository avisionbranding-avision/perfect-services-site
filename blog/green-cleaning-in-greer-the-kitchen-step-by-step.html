Green cleaning in Greer: the kitchen, step by step

How a green cleaning team handles a kitchen in Greer, SC with low-odor products: food-safe surfaces, microfiber, grease, floors and a free in-home estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • In a green kitchen clean, the product matters less than the order: grease is lifted with heat, time and microfiber.
  • Food-prep surfaces and pet bowls get extra care and a clean water rinse.
  • Perfect Services quotes only after a free in-home estimate.

The kitchen is where green cleaning matters most. Food touches the counter, the baby crawls on the floor, the dog drinks from a bowl by the fridge. If you want fewer fumes there, you are not being fussy. This is how our team cleans a Greer kitchen with low-odor products, and what you can expect to see and smell afterward.

Start with what you want left alone

Before opening any bottle, the team asks a short list of questions. Does anyone in the house have allergies or asthma? Are there products you prefer, or ones you do not want near the kids? Is there a pet who eats in the kitchen? The answers shape the visit. Some households ask us to use their own spray; others want unscented only. Our green cleaning service is built around that kind of choice.

Dry work comes first

Before any liquid, we use dry microfiber on the top surfaces: the tops of cabinets, the hood, the fridge top. Dust is picked up rather than moved. A dry pass also means there is less mud on the cloth when the damp pass arrives. In Greer, where spring pollen blows through windows and settles on everything, that dust layer is thick on the window sill above the sink.

Grease with heat and time, not strong fumes

You do not need a harsh degreaser to lift cooking residue. Warm water, a gentle product and a little dwell time do most of the work, and a microfiber cloth lifts the loosened film. It is slower, and the team plans for that, working one area at a time while the product sits. The range hood, the stovetop and the backsplash get the most dwell time. Stubborn spots are worked with a soft scrub pad that will not scratch.

  • Warm water and a gentle degreasing soap
  • Dwell time of a few minutes before wiping
  • Microfiber cloths, changed often
  • No scented sprays near open food

Food surfaces, the careful part

Cutting boards, counters and the table are wiped with a mild solution and then rinsed with clean water and dried. This is the step that gives you a counter you can put bread on. Fridge shelves, if you ask for the inside of the fridge, are cleaned with warm water and soap, then dried. Nothing smells strongly afterward, and the room should feel neutral rather than perfumed.

The floor, with pets and kids in mind

Floors get vacuumed first with attention to the edges, where hair and crumbs collect. Then a damp mop with a surface-safe solution. For hardwood, the mop is barely damp, since too much water damages the finish. For tile, the grout lines are scrubbed with a brush. Bowls for pets are washed in the sink and left to dry, and the mat underneath is cleaned or shaken. In a household with a crawling baby, we skip scented products and leave the floor dry.

Common questions we hear at the walkthrough

People ask whether green means it will smell like nothing. Mostly yes: a cleaned kitchen should smell neutral, not like lemon or pine. They ask whether it is as strong as the usual bottle. For everyday soil, it is, when you give it time to work. For a stove with years of baked-on residue, we say so honestly and plan extra time rather than reaching for something harsh without asking.

Another question is about the sink drain and the garbage disposal, which are the sources of most kitchen odors. A scoop of baking soda, a splash of hot water and a cleaned strainer handle many of them, and the team does that on every visit. If you want the inside of the dishwasher or the fridge door seals cleaned, mention it at the estimate, since those details take extra time but make a difference to a household that is trying to keep fumes down. We also take out and wash the trash can lid, which is a spot where odors start.

What you will notice afterward

The first thing is what you do not notice: no sharp smell when you walk in, no film on the counter, no sticky patch near the stove. Handles feel dry and smooth. The sink is bright, and the floor has no residue that grabs at socks or paws. Households with a baby or a pet often tell us the difference is clearest on the floor, which is where they spend the most time. If something is still bothering you, tell us, since we would rather fix it than leave it.

Small habits that keep the kitchen green

Wipe spills when they happen, since fresh messes need only water. Keep a microfiber cloth near the sink, and wash it weekly with fragrance-free detergent.
