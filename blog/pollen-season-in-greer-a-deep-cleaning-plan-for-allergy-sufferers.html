Pollen season in Greer: a deep cleaning plan for allergies

Deep house cleaning Greer for pollen season: how pollen gets into a home, which spots hold it, and a dust-removal plan for Upstate allergy sufferers.

How pollen gets in and where it lands

It enters four ways: open windows and doors, on shoes and clothes, on pets, and through the HVAC system. Once inside, it settles on whatever is flat or soft.

  • Window sills, tracks and blinds
  • Ceiling fan blades and the top of door frames
  • Upholstery, curtains and rugs
  • Bedding and the pet's bed
  • Baseboards and the floor edge along walls
  • Air vents and the filter housing

A dry cloth or a feather duster pushes pollen back into the air. That is why allergy households are better served by damp microfiber and a vacuum with a good filter.

The plan, in the order that works

  1. Strip beds and wash bedding in hot water if the fabric allows.
  2. Dust ceiling fans, light fixtures and vents with a damp cloth.
  3. Wipe window sills, tracks and blinds, and wash or vacuum curtains.
  4. Wipe baseboards, door frames, shelves and furniture.
  5. Vacuum upholstery and mattresses.
  6. Vacuum floors, using slow passes along walls and under furniture, then mop hard floors.
  7. Change the HVAC filter, which you do yourself.

The order matters. Anything you do after the floors will drop dust back onto them, so start high and finish low.

What a deep clean adds beyond a regular visit

A weekly maid visit keeps surfaces tidy. A deep clean reaches the spots that hold dust for months: baseboards and door frames hand-wiped, cabinet fronts, ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures dusted, window sills and blinds cleaned, and behind and around the toilet base. It includes everything in a regular cleaning on top. For allergy sufferers it is the dust removed, not moved. Habits that keep pollen down after the clean

Keep windows closed on high-pollen days and run the air conditioner. Take shoes off at the door and change clothes after being outside. Wipe pets with a damp towel when they come in. Wash bedding weekly in spring. Put a doormat outside and a washable one inside. Vacuum twice a week during peak weeks if you can, and use damp wiping rather than dry dusting.

If anyone in the house is sensitive to strong smells, ask about gentler products. Our deep cleaning service can be paired with eco-friendly green cleaning, with low-odor products or a fragrance-free option.

What we check in the walkthrough

Before an allergy-focused deep clean, we ask where symptoms are worst. If it is the bedroom, we spend more time on the headboard, the baseboards and under the bed. If it is the living room, we focus on upholstery edges, curtains and the bottom of the vents. We also look at the filters you have and how often they are changed, because a clogged filter pushes dust back into the rooms.

In a typical house we see, the most overlooked spot is the top of the door frame and the track of a sliding patio door, where pollen and soil collect and stay until someone wipes them. The second most overlooked is the inside edge of the air return. Both take only a minute with the right cloth, but they hold more dust than many open surfaces, so they get done early in the order.
