5 one-time cleaning mistakes Greer homeowners make

One time cleaning mistakes Greer homeowners make: booking late, pre-cleaning, skipping add-ons and more. Get a free in-home estimate for your home.

Perfect Services Team
November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Book early for holiday weeks, and share what matters most so the time goes where you want it.
  • Add-ons like the oven or fridge need to be asked for, not assumed.
  • The price comes after a free in-home estimate, never from a guess over the phone.

The call usually comes three days before Thanksgiving. "Can you clean my whole house on Wednesday?" Sometimes we can, often we cannot, and the homeowner is stuck cleaning the bathrooms at 11 p.m. A one-time cleaning is a simple service, but the way people book it is where things go wrong. These are five mistakes we see in Greer, and how to avoid them.

Mistake 1: Booking the week of the event

Holiday weeks, graduation weekends and the days before a family visit fill first. The date is one of the factors in a one-time visit, and the best slots are gone early. If you know company is coming, ask for the estimate a couple of weeks ahead. Then you can pick the day that gives you room to fix anything the visit shows up.

If you are reading this and the date is close, call anyway. We can often fit a free in-home estimate this week, and sometimes the visit itself.

Mistake 2: Cleaning before the cleaners arrive

This is the most common one, and it is a kind instinct. People spend the night before scrubbing the tub so the cleaners will not see it dirty. Then they are tired, the team does the same work anyway, and nothing is gained.

What actually helps is tidying, not cleaning. Put away toys, papers and clothes so surfaces are reachable. Clear the counters. Leave the dirt. We cover this in more detail in our piece on preparing your Greer home for a one-time cleaning.

Mistake 3: Assuming every extra is included

A standard one-time cleaning covers the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas, floors, baseboards and door frames, mirrors and glass, and trash. The inside of the oven, the fridge and the cabinets are optional add-ons. Windows are another. If the oven is what you care about most and it is not on the list, you will notice.

The fix is a short list sent before the visit.

  • The three rooms that matter most to you, in order
  • Any add-on you want: oven, fridge, cabinets, windows
  • Pets in the house and whether they can be kept in one room
  • Surfaces that need gentle products, such as natural stone or unsealed wood
  • Where the supplies are, if you want us to use your own

Mistake 4: Expecting a one-time visit to solve a bigger job

A single clean is a great reset for a lived-in home. It is not the right tool for everything. If you are moving out, a move-in or move-out cleaning is built for it. If the home has not had a professional clean in a long time, a deep cleaning may fit better. Picking the wrong one leads to disappointment on both sides.

Condition and the time since the last professional clean are two of the main factors in a one-time visit. It is why we ask to see the home in person. For comparison, here is a quick way to match the need to the visit.

Mistake 5: Comparing quotes that were not seen in person

A price given over the phone for a house nobody has seen is a guess. It may be low because the guess was low, and it may change when the team arrives. We would rather not do that to you. Perfect Services quotes only after a free in-home estimate, where someone walks through the rooms, looks at the condition and the add-ons, and gives you the exact price.

It is also a good moment to ask questions: who comes, how long it takes, what is brought, what you need to provide. A walkthrough of a typical Greer home, from the newer neighborhoods near GSP Airport to the older houses near Downtown Greer, takes a short while, and it saves the surprise on the day.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I book a one-time cleaning in Greer?

Two weeks or more before a holiday or big event is a good rule. The date is one of the factors, and holiday weeks book quickly.

Do I need to clean before the cleaners come?

No. Tidy so surfaces are reachable and leave the dirt to the team. Scrubbing the night before takes your energy and saves nothing.

Is the oven included in a one-time cleaning?

The oven, fridge and cabinets are optional add-ons. Ask for them when you book the free in-home estimate.
