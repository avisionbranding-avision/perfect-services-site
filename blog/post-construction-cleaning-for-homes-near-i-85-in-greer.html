What a final walkthrough should show

Homes near I-85 in Greer include new builds and renovations, and both end the same way: a person with a clipboard or a set of keys opens the door. Use this list when you walk through.

  • Top of door frames, window sills and the ledge of every baseboard are free of dust
  • Inside cabinets and drawers, with no sawdust in the corners
  • Light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans wiped
  • Glass has no labels, paint specks or adhesive on it
  • Window tracks are clear
  • Bathroom fixtures are polished and the tub or shower has no film
  • Floors are clean at the edges and under where tools were stored

If any of those fail, the job needs another pass. Our post construction cleaning in Greer includes a touch-up pass for that reason.

Dust is not all the same

Knowing what is on the surface changes the order of work, the cloths and the tools, so we look before we start.

This is the part most people do not expect. A crew plans the work around what the construction left behind.

What was leftWhere it hidesHow we handle it
Drywall dustLedges, vents, tops of cabinets, tracksTop-down, lifted rather than pushed around
Paint specks and dripsGlass, sills, tile, hardwareCareful removal that avoids scratching the surface
Sticker and film adhesiveNew windows, appliances, fixturesSoftened and removed, then the surface is cleaned
Grit and debrisFloors, corners, stairsMultiple vacuum and wash passes

Drywall sanding creates the most fine dust, and that dust gets into return vents and onto ceiling fans, where it comes back later if nobody cleaned it.
