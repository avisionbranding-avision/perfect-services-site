Questions to ask before hiring deep cleaning in Greer

Hiring deep cleaning in Greer, SC? Ask these 10 questions about scope, products, time, insurance and pricing before you book, so there are no surprises.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Ask for the scope in writing: what is included, and what costs extra.
  • Find out whether the price is fixed after a visit or an hourly guess.
  • A good company walks the home before quoting a deep clean.

Deep cleaning is the one service where two quotes can mean two completely different jobs. One company scrubs grout and degreases cabinets; another wipes the same surfaces a regular visit would. Before you hire deep cleaning in Greer, SC, these are the ten questions that separate the two.

1. What exactly is included?

Ask for the list, not the adjective. A real deep clean should include baseboards and door frames hand-wiped, cabinet fronts and handles degreased, grout lines scrubbed in kitchen and bathrooms, ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures dusted, window sills, tracks and blinds cleaned, the area behind and around the toilet base, and appliance exteriors, range hood and backsplash. If a company cannot tell you, keep looking. Ours is on the deep cleaning service page.

2. Do you visit the home before quoting?

This is the most useful question. A deep clean depends on condition, and condition cannot be judged from the number of bedrooms. Perfect Services always does a free in-home estimate first, and the exact price comes at the visit.

3. Is the price fixed or by the hour?

Hourly sounds flexible until the job runs long. A fixed price after a visit protects you from a surprise, and it protects the team from rushing. Ask what happens if the job takes more time than expected.

4. What costs extra?

Inside the oven, fridge and cabinets are usually add-ons. Interior windows, heavily soiled grout or pet stains might be too. Ask before the visit so the quote is complete.

5. How long will it take?

A deep clean takes longer than a regular visit, sometimes close to twice as long for the same home. Ask for an expected time and how many people will come. Two or three workers finish sooner than one.

6. Who are the cleaners?

Ask whether they are employees of the company or subcontractors found that morning. A consistent local team learns your home and holds to the same standard. Ours works in Greer every week, and many of our clients see the same faces.

7. What products do you use?

Tell the company about kids, pets, fragrance sensitivity and special surfaces such as natural stone. Hard water spots and grout need real products, but you can ask for gentler options or request your own.

8. Do you have insurance and a satisfaction policy?

Ask how they handle breakage or a missed spot. A good company tells you how to report a problem and returns to fix it. Do not assume; ask the person on the phone.

9. How should I prepare?

Clutter slows deep cleaning, since every object on a counter has to be moved and replaced. Pick up toys, clear counters, and tell the team about areas you want skipped. Pets should be secured or put in one room.

10. How much does deep cleaning cost in Greer?

A fair question, and the honest answer is that it depends. The base time is set by square feet and rooms, condition adds more, add-ons and bathrooms with hard water or grout add time, and a clutter-free house goes faster. Greer-specific things to mention

Homes in Greer vary a lot. Historic houses near Downtown Greer and Trade Street often have old windows with deep tracks and original tile with grout that has never been scrubbed, while homes near GSP Airport and the BMW plant have newer fixtures but big kitchens with a lot of surface. Tell the team about both what you see and what bothers you. Spring pollen and red clay mean window sills and entry floors take the worst of it.

What a good answer sounds like

When you ask these questions, listen for specifics. "We do everything" is not an answer. "We scrub the grout, degrease the cabinet fronts and clean the window tracks, and the oven is an add-on" is. A good company explains what you get, what you do not and what you can change. They also tell you when your home does not need a deep clean yet and a regular visit would do, which is a sign they are thinking of your budget as well as theirs.

Take notes during the call, or ask for the scope by text. Having it in writing prevents confusion on the day and gives you something to point to if a spot is missed. Honest companies are glad to put it in writing. One more tip: ask how long the company has been working in your area, and whether they can name a few streets or neighborhoods in Greer they clean regularly without sharing private details.
