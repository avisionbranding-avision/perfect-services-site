What green cleaning is, and what it is not

It is a choice of products and tools, not a different set of tasks. The team still cleans the kitchen, bathrooms, floors and dusting in the same order as a regular visit. What changes is what is in the bottle and on the cloth.

  • Low-odor, family- and pet-friendly products on request
  • Microfiber cloths that lift dust instead of spreading it
  • Gentler products for kitchens and bathrooms
  • Floor solutions chosen to be safe for the surface
  • A fragrance-free option for sensitive households
  • The option to use your products instead of ours

It is not a magic label. Green cleaning does not mean no product at all, and it will not dissolve years of baked-on grease in one pass. We will tell you honestly when a heavier job needs more time.

Why the hand-wiping method helps

Our Brazilian detail-first method relies on wiping surfaces by hand with a clean cloth. That means less spray and less product on every surface, because the cloth does most of the work. A damp microfiber cloth picks up dust and a lot of residue with very little product at all. For families with a baby crawling on the floor or an allergy sufferer in the house, that matters more than the label on the bottle.

Seasonal notes for Tryon homes

Spring pollen is heavy in the foothills, and it settles on window sills, porch furniture and floors near the door. Wet days bring mud and red clay inside, which needs a floor cleaner that works on grit without harsh products. In the humid months, bathrooms and kitchens can grow mildew faster, so keep vents on and wipe wet areas between visits. Green cleaning does not change the schedule you would normally follow, and a regular house cleaning rotation fits very well with it.

Small habits that support a green home

Between professional visits, a few simple choices keep a house cleaner without more product. Take shoes off at the door, because most of the grit on a floor comes in from outside. Keep a damp microfiber cloth for quick wipes instead of reaching for spray. Open windows for ten minutes on mild days to let the air move, especially in the kitchen and bathrooms.

Watch for the things that cause trouble in sensitive households: strong fragrance, bleach in small rooms and aerosol sprays. If a product makes someone cough, switch it, even when it is labeled natural. And never mix cleaners. That is a safety rule for any home, green or not.

A word on honesty. No cleaner can promise to cure allergies, and a green label does not make a product harmless if misused. What we can promise is care: we ask what bothers you, use what you approve and keep the process simple and consistent. For households that want to go further, a fragrance-free routine plus regular dust removal is often the biggest change you can make. It also helps to cover the main dust catchers: ceiling fans, vents, curtains and the tops of bookshelves. We include those in deeper visits and can add them on request.

Ask about add-ons if your home is large. Do not forget outdoor entries. Porches and mudrooms in the mountains collect pollen, leaves and horse-country dust, and they are the last stop before the floors you want to keep clean.

Can I supply my own products?

Yes. Many Tryon households have a preferred brand. Tell us at the estimate and we will use it.

Is green cleaning good for pets and kids?

We use low-odor, family- and pet-friendly products on request, though anyone with a medical concern should check with their own doctor or vet.
