How much does move-out cleaning cost in Greer?

How much move-out cleaning costs in Greer, SC: the factors that set the price, what is included, and why the exact figure comes after a free in-home estimate.

Key takeaways

  • Move-out cleaning is priced by the home: size, condition, appliances, empty or furnished, and timing.
  • Perfect Services gives an exact price after a free in-home estimate, never from a guess.
  • Clean appliances and an empty home lower the time and the cost.

There is no flat rate for move-out cleaning, and anyone who quotes you one without seeing the house is guessing. A two-bedroom apartment left tidy and a four-bedroom house with a greasy oven are different jobs, even if both have the same move-out date. The sections below explain what actually sets the price in Greer and what you can do to bring it down.

Renters in Greer, ZIPs 29650, 29651 and 29652, often ask the same question: what will it cost me to get my deposit back? The answer starts with the checklist the landlord uses. Our move-out cleaning in Greer is built around that standard.

What the price covers

Move-out cleaning is a top-to-bottom job on an empty home. It includes the inside and outside of all kitchen cabinets and drawers, the inside of the refrigerator, freezer, oven and microwave, the range hood, stovetop and backsplash, bathrooms scrubbed down to the toilet base, grout, glass and vanity interiors, closets (shelves, rods and floors), baseboards, doors, frames and light switches, window sills, tracks and blinds, and floors vacuumed and mopped edge to edge.

That list is the reason it costs more than a regular visit. A weekly maid service maintains a clean house. A move-out clean resets one, including the places nobody has looked at for years.

Move-out checklist to reduce the work

  • Remove all furniture, boxes and trash before the cleaners arrive
  • Empty the refrigerator and freezer, and unplug them if you are told to
  • Take everything out of cabinets, drawers and closets
  • Remove nails, wall hangers and anything the lease says to take down
  • Leave the water and power on so the team can work
  • Note the walkthrough date with your landlord

How long does it take, and can it fit my move date?

Many people ask this because the truck, the keys and the cleaning all land on the same weekend.

Time depends on the size of the home and its condition. A smaller apartment moves faster than a family house, and a heavily used kitchen can take longer than the rest of the home combined. We confirm the time at the estimate. If your lease ends soon, say so at booking, because we can often add people to a visit to meet a deadline, though availability depends on the week.

What landlords and property managers tend to check

Property managers in Greer usually walk the unit with a list, and the same four places come up again and again: the oven and range hood, the inside of the refrigerator, the bathroom grout and glass, and the floors along the baseboards. Those are also the places a tenant is least likely to reach on moving day, with the truck waiting. Our post on rental turnover cleaning for Greer landlords shows the same list from the landlord's side. Knowing it in advance helps you decide where to spend your own time.

If you are choosing between services, the difference is simple.

A move-out clean assumes the home is empty, and the team works from the kitchen to the bathrooms to the bedrooms, with the floors last so nothing is walked on after it is done. If you are still living in the house and want the same attention to grout and cabinet fronts, that is a deep cleaning. If you are leaving a rental, the move-out clean is the right choice, because the inside of cabinets, closets and appliances is the part landlords inspect.
