How to prepare your Greer home for post-construction cleaning

Prepare for post construction cleaning Greer homes need: what to remove, protect and finish first so the crew can start the final clean. Free estimate.

Key takeaways

  • Finish all trade work, punch-list items and debris removal before the final clean, or dust will settle again afterward.
  • Clear work areas, protect finished floors and tell the team about problem spots such as paint on glass.

The first hour of a post-construction job in Greer is usually spent on a walkthrough, and what the team sees in that hour sets the schedule for the day. We check the windows for paint specks, run a hand along the tops of cabinets, look into vents and light fixtures, and note where the crew's debris is still stacked. Everything you handle before that walkthrough is time we can spend on the actual clean. This is how to prepare for post construction cleaning in Greer so that hour goes well.

What we check in the first hour

Knowing what the team looks for makes preparation easier. We start with the big picture: is the work actually finished, and is the dust still flying? If a painter or flooring crew is due back, the final clean should wait. Then we walk the rooms, noting which surfaces carry the most fine dust, where stickers and adhesive remain on glass and fixtures, and how the floors look. The answers decide how many passes we need and how many people to send.

You can read how a first visit usually goes in our post on how long post-construction cleaning takes in Greer.

Before the crew arrives: your checklist

  • Confirm every trade is done, including painters, flooring, plumbers and electricians. If work continues, book a rough clean now and the final clean later.
  • Have the contractor haul away debris, scrap lumber, drywall pieces and packaging. The team cleans surfaces, not job sites full of waste.
  • Remove loose tools, ladders, paint cans and supplies from the rooms.
  • Pull the protective film or paper off floors and counters only if the builder says the work is done. Otherwise leave it, and we will tell you when to remove it.
  • Clear furniture and boxes so the team can reach baseboards, window sills and corners.
  • Keep the water and power on, because we need both for mopping and vacuuming.
  • Make sure there is access: a key, a door code or someone to let us in.

New floors and countertops are the most vulnerable surfaces. If you have hardwood, tile or fresh paint, tell us about it during the estimate. Foot traffic with dirty shoes and carried debris can undo finished work, so ask the crew to stop walking on completed floors before the clean. A handful of door mats and a no-shoes rule go a long way once the work is done. Greer's red clay tracks in easily after a rainy week, and on a freshly finished floor it shows.

Protect finished surfaces and point out the problem spots

New floors and countertops are the most vulnerable surfaces, so ask the crew to stop walking on completed floors with dirty shoes before the clean. A few door mats at the entries help, since Greer red clay tracks in easily after a rainy week and shows on a fresh floor. If you have hardwood, tile or new paint, say so during the estimate.

Every project has a few spots that need more attention than the rest. Mention them when you book the estimate, so we can plan time and the right tools:

  1. Paint specks or overspray on windows and glass.
  2. Stickers on new windows, appliances and fixtures.
  3. Adhesive left by tape, labels or flooring.
  4. Heavy drywall dust near sanded walls.
  5. Grout haze on new tile.

Each of these takes a different technique, and none of them should be scraped with anything that can scratch. For a closer look at the stickier items, see our guide to removing paint, stickers and residue after construction in Greer.

What the clean includes

Once the room is ready, the team removes fine dust from every surface, ledge and trim piece, then cleans inside the cabinets, drawers and closets. Windows, glass, tracks and sills are cleaned, light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans are wiped, bathrooms and new fixtures are polished, and floors are vacuumed and washed in multiple passes. A final touch-up pass before move-in takes care of fingerprints and the last dust. Working from the top down keeps the finished floor clean until the end. Frequently asked questions

What should I do before post-construction cleaners arrive?

Make sure all trades are finished, debris and scrap are hauled away, loose tools are removed and the space is accessible. Keep water and power on, and mention problem spots such as paint on glass when you book.

Should I take the protective film off floors before the clean?

Only if your builder says the work is complete. Otherwise leave it in place and the team will advise on timing. Finished floors should stay protected until the last trade has left.

Who removes the construction debris in Greer?

Your contractor or builder should haul away debris, scrap and packaging. Perfect Services cleans the surfaces, windows, cabinets, fixtures and floors after the debris is gone.
