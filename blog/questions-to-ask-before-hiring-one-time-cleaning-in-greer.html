Questions to ask before hiring one-time cleaning in Greer

Booking a one-time cleaning in Greer, SC for guests, a party or a showing? Ask these questions about scope, add-ons, timing and the estimate before you hire.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Define the reason for the clean first: guests, a party, a showing or a first trial of a cleaner.
  • Ask what is included and which extras, such as the oven or fridge, cost more time.
  • Holiday weeks fill up, so book the free estimate early.

A one-time clean is the easiest kind of cleaning to book badly. There is no relationship with the crew, no second visit to fix anything and a date you cannot move, like Thanksgiving dinner or an open house on Saturday. If you are about to book a one-time cleaning in Greer, SC, these are the questions worth asking first.

Start with why you are cleaning

Tell the company the reason before you ask anything else. The answer changes the plan. A clean before relatives arrive focuses on the kitchen, bathrooms, guest bed and the entry. A clean after a party is about floors, the trash and whatever got spilled. A clean before a showing is about making the house look bigger and brighter, with attention to glass, mirrors and the kitchen. And a first trial clean is really an audition, so ask for the regular-service details too.

A good company asks you these same things back. If the first response is a number, with no questions about the house, be careful.

Questions about what is included

What is the standard scope? Ours covers a full kitchen cleaning and degreasing, bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, bedrooms and living areas dusted, floors vacuumed and mopped, baseboards and door frames, mirrors and glass polished and trash emptied. Get any company's list in writing and compare it line by line.

Which extras cost more time? Inside the oven, inside the refrigerator and inside the cabinets are optional add-ons. They are not part of a standard clean because they can take a good chunk of an hour or more on their own. If you plan to host Thanksgiving, the oven is usually the first thing people ask about, and it is better to book it ahead than to ask for it on the day.

  • Is the oven interior included or an add-on?
  • Are the refrigerator and cabinets inside priced separately?
  • Are interior windows part of the job?
  • Do you clean baseboards, or only wipe them where they are visible?

Questions about timing

How far ahead do I need to book? The Upstate gets busy around Thanksgiving, Christmas and the start of spring, when pollen coats everything and people decide they have had enough. Holiday weeks fill first. If your date is fixed, book the estimate visit as soon as you know it.

How long will the crew be there? Ask for a window, not just a start time. A house with four bedrooms and three baths is a longer visit than a ranch-style home near downtown Greer, and your plans after the clean may depend on it. Also ask what happens if the crew runs behind.

How much does a one-time cleaning cost in Greer?

Ask this out loud, and expect the answer to start with "it depends." Perfect Services prices a one-time clean after a free in-home estimate. The price follows the size of the home, how long it has been since the last professional clean, any add-ons such as oven or fridge, the date (holiday weeks book quickly) and pets, because hair and dander take time. We do not quote a figure by phone or photo, since the condition of a home only shows in person. The visit is short and there is no commitment.

Questions about the day itself

What do I do with the pets? If a dog is nervous or a cat slips out of an open door, tell the crew before they arrive. A closed bedroom door or a crate keeps everyone calm. Pets that are used to visitors rarely cause problems, but the team should know which doors stay closed.

Do I need to be home? Not necessarily. For a first visit, many people stay for the walkthrough and then leave. Others leave a key or a door code. Either way, ask the company how it handles access and what it does at the end of the visit, whether that is a message, a walkthrough or a quick note on the counter.

What if I am not happy with a room? Before you book, ask about the fix. A company that stands behind its work will return to a missed spot, ideally within a day or two. Test the answer: if it sounds vague, press for details.

Questions about trust

Who is coming into my home? Ask how many people, and whether they work for the company or are contractors. Perfect Services is a local Brazilian-owned team based in Greer, and the same people usually come to the same homes. Ask whether the company has a way to fix a missed spot, and how quickly you should tell them about it.

What should I do before they arrive? Put away personal items, clear the counters and let the crew know about anything fragile. The less clutter on the surfaces, the more actually gets cleaned. Tell the team about pets, a baby's nap schedule or a room that is off limits.
