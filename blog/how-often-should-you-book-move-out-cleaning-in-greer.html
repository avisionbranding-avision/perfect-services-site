How often should you book move-out cleaning in Greer?

How often move out cleaning Greer homes need: once per move for most renters, more often for landlords. Timing tips and a free in-home estimate in Greer, SC.

Perfect Services Team
November 14, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Most renters and home sellers need move-out cleaning exactly once per move, booked after the last box is out.
  • Landlords and property managers usually book it at every tenant change, and a second visit can make sense if a vacant home sits for weeks.
  • Perfect Services prices every job after a free in-home estimate, so you know the exact number before you commit.

For most people the answer is once: a single move-out cleaning at the end of a lease or just before closing. The question gets more interesting if you own a rental, move often for work, or sell a house you have lived in for years. Here is how often move out cleaning in Greer really makes sense, and the few cases where a second visit earns its place.

The short answer: once per move, right before the handoff

A move-out clean is tied to an event, not a calendar. It happens one time for each home you leave, and the best moment is after the last box is on the truck and before the keys change hands. Book it earlier and the movers undo your work. Book it too late and you are scrubbing an oven at 9 p.m. the night before the inspection.

The team works in the same order each visit: cabinets and drawers inside and out, the oven, refrigerator and microwave, the range hood and backsplash, bathrooms, closets, baseboards, door frames and light switches, window sills and tracks, and floors last so nothing falls onto a finished surface. Floors always go last, which is why the visit belongs at the very end of the move. You can see the full scope on our page for move out cleaning in Greer.

When one visit is not enough

Some situations call for more than one cleaning around the same address. These are the ones we see most in Greer and the 29650, 29651 and 29652 ZIP codes:

  • You own a rental and every tenant change needs a reset before the next showing.
  • The house sat empty for several weeks after you moved out, and dust and pollen settled back onto sills and floors before closing.
  • You are moving out of one place and into another, and the new home also needs a clean before the furniture arrives.
  • A buyer's repair request meant contractors were in and out after your first clean.
  • You are a property manager with a lease that ends mid-month and a new tenant waiting.

In every one of these cases the schedule follows the calendar of the property, not a fixed interval. A landlord with a single rental might book twice in a year, or not at all if the tenant stays. A family that is selling usually needs one visit, plus maybe a light touch-up if the closing date slips.

What the team looks at in the walkthrough

When we visit a Greer home for an estimate, we check the things that decide how long the clean will take: how the last occupant left the oven, whether the refrigerator has been defrosted, how much grease is on the hood, and whether the bathrooms have hard water buildup on glass. Red clay tracked in through a garage door can also leave a film on the floors near entries, and spring pollen collects on window tracks.

Then there are the spots that renters and sellers usually miss: the tops of door frames, the inside of the vanity, cabinet handles, the track of a sliding door and the shelves of closets. Those are the details that landlords and inspectors tend to notice first, and they are part of the Brazilian detail-first method our team uses on every move-out. Do I need move-out cleaning every time I move in Greer?

Yes, if you want to get your deposit back or hand over a clean house at closing. Each move is a separate event, so each one gets its own cleaning, ideally after the last box is out and before the final walkthrough.

How often should a Greer landlord book move-out cleaning?

Most landlords book it at every tenant change, so the next showing happens in a reset home. If a tenant stays for years, there may be no visit at all, and an occasional deep clean can help between leases.
