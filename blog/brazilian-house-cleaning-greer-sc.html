If you have ever paid for a house cleaning in Greer and still found dust on the baseboards, a gray ring behind the toilet or fingerprints on the light switches, you already understand the problem Brazilian house cleaning solves. Most cleaning services are built around speed: clean what the client sees first, then move to the next house. Brazilian house cleaning starts from the opposite idea. A home is only clean when every surface has been touched by hand.

At Perfect Services, a Brazilian cleaning team based in Greer, SC, this is the standard behind every regular cleaning, deep cleaning and move-in/move-out cleaning we do. Here is what the method really means, what it includes and how to know if it is right for your home.

Where the Brazilian cleaning standard comes from

In Brazil, keeping a home clean is treated as a daily craft, often passed down in families. It is normal to move furniture to clean underneath, scrub floors by hand, wipe every door frame and wash outdoor areas with water and soap. Cleanliness is judged by the details: the top of the refrigerator, the corners of the shower, the tracks of the sliding door.

Many Brazilian cleaning professionals who work in the United States bring that same routine with them. That is why the term Brazilian house cleaning has become a search phrase on its own: homeowners notice the difference after the first visit and want to find it again.

What a Brazilian house cleaning includes

A Brazilian-style cleaning covers everything you expect from a standard house cleaning: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen surfaces and disinfected bathrooms. The difference is the list of extra details that become part of every visit instead of an upcharge:

  • Baseboards and toe kicks wiped by hand, not just vacuumed around.
  • Door frames, light switches and handles, the high-touch spots that collect grime and germs.
  • Behind and around the toilet base, including the floor where the toilet meets the tile.
  • Cabinet fronts and backsplash grout in the kitchen.
  • Ceiling fan blades, window sills and blinds, where dust settles in Upstate homes.
  • Under cushions and under the bed when furniture allows it.
  • Shower glass and chrome finished without water spots.

Is Brazilian house cleaning right for your home?

It is a great fit for busy families, homes with kids or pets, people who work from home and anyone who wants the house guest-ready without doing the detail work themselves. It is also the best choice before a sale, after a move or when a home has not been professionally cleaned in a while.

If you only want a quick tidy-up, a standard service may be enough. If you want to walk in and see the difference in the corners, Brazilian cleaning is what you are looking for.
