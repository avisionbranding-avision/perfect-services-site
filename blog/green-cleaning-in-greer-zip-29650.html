What green cleaning means for us

It is the same service, done with different choices. The team still follows a full room-by-room checklist, but they use low-odor, family- and pet-friendly products on request, microfiber cloths that capture dust instead of spreading it, and gentler products in the kitchen and bathroom. You can ask for a fragrance-free option, and you can also supply your own preferred products. The hand-wiping technique reduces how much product goes onto each surface.

Who tends to choose it

  • Families with babies and young children
  • Pet owners
  • Allergy and asthma sufferers
  • Households sensitive to fragrance

Sometimes it is a single person. A customer who works from home may notice product smells more because they spend all day there. Others simply prefer fewer chemicals near the kitchen table.

Where the work happens

Kitchen. Counters, sink and stovetop are cleaned with gentler products. The team wipes grease with warm water and cloth first, and uses a product only where it is needed. Stainless fronts, and especially stone or marble islands, get surface-safe products so they are not etched.

Bathrooms. This is where gentler products take more patience. Soap scum and hard-water spots need dwell time, so the team starts with the bathroom, lets products work, and returns to wipe once the other tasks are underway.

Floors. Surface-safe solutions are used on hardwood, tile and laminate, with the amount kept low. That matters in homes with babies and pets that spend time on the floor.

Green cleaning in homes with kids and pets

In households with young children, the floor is the main surface. Toddlers crawl on it, eat off it and put things from it in their mouths. That is why we keep the amount of liquid on floors low and wipe with damp, not wet, cloths. For pets, the concern is paws and tongues: a dog that walks across a freshly cleaned floor and licks its paws is a good reason to use gentler products and to rinse well.

We also look at the places where children and pets spend time: playroom shelves, couch edges, dog beds near the back door. These get dusted and wiped, and anything that needs washing, such as a removable cover, you can set aside for yourself. If your home is in a quiet 29650 cul-de-sac or on a busy road like US-29, the dust looks different, but the method is the same.

A green cleaning visit is not about using fewer steps. It is about choosing materials with care and using technique so the result is still a home that looks and feels clean.

Smell, residue and what you should expect

After a green cleaning visit, the home should smell like very little. Some customers expect a strong clean scent as proof of work, and we explain at the first visit that a plain smell is the point. Surfaces should feel smooth, not sticky or filmy, because the team rinses and wipes rather than leaving product on the surface. If you notice any odor or residue, tell us and we will fix it at the next visit.

Some households start with green cleaning only in certain rooms, such as the kitchen, the nursery or the room where a family member with sensitivities sleeps. We can do that. Just say so at the estimate so the team plans the products room by room. Over time many people move the whole home over once they see how it feels.

If you have a seasonal reason, such as spring pollen, a new baby coming home or a pet with sensitive skin, mention the date and we will try to schedule the first visit before it.
