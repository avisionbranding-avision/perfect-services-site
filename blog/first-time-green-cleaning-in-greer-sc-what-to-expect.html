A mom in Greer asks the same thing nearly every time: "If you are using gentler products, will it actually get clean?" It is the right question, and the honest answer is yes, with a few differences in how the work is done. Here is what eco friendly cleaning in Greer, SC looks like the first time you book it.

What "green" means at Perfect Services

Terms like natural or non-toxic are used loosely, so we will be specific. On a green cleaning visit, the team uses low-odor, family- and pet-friendly products on request, microfiber cloths that capture dust instead of pushing it around, and surface-safe solutions for floors. A hand-wiping technique reduces the amount of product used in the first place. There is also a fragrance-free option for sensitive households, and you can ask us to use your own preferred products.

The checklist does not shrink. It is the same full room-by-room clean, kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, floors, with a different product list.

The first visit, step by step

  1. Free in-home estimate. Someone from our Brazilian-owned team walks the home with you and asks about sensitivities, pets, babies and anything you want avoided.
  2. Product conversation. You choose ours, yours, or fragrance-free.
  3. Cleaning day. The team works top to bottom, kitchen and bathrooms first, floors last.
  4. Walkthrough. We check the details together and fix anything you spot.

Spring pollen in the Upstate and humid summers keep a lot of Greer households sealed up for months, and that makes strong scents hang around. Families who notice headaches or sneezing after a standard clean are often the ones who switch.

Who gets the most from it

  • Families with babies and young children who crawl on floors.
  • Pet owners worried about residue where animals lie and lick.
  • Allergy and asthma sufferers who react to heavy perfume.
  • Households sensitive to fragrance of any kind.

If one of those fits you, say so at the estimate. We can set up the products before the team arrives, so nobody is guessing on cleaning day.

Gentler products, honest limits

Gentle cleaners work, but they like time. Heavy soap scum on a shower door or baked-on grease around a stovetop may need longer dwell time or extra passes. That is why we ask when the home was last professionally cleaned. If your bathroom has a lot of buildup, we may suggest one deeper first visit and then lighter maintenance, rather than promising a miracle in a single pass. Being straight about that is better than leaving a film behind.

What the team brings and what you keep

Here is a quick list of what to set out before the visit, so the first green clean goes smoothly:

  • Any product you want us to use, with the bottle labeled so nobody mixes things up.
  • A note about allergies, asthma or fragrance sensitivities in the household.
  • Notes on pets, including which rooms they use most.
  • Any surface that needs special care, such as stone counters or finished wood.

Everything else, the cloths, the vacuum and the tools, comes with the team. Microfiber cloths are washed between homes so nothing travels from one house to the next.

Making it a habit

Many Greer families try green cleaning once, notice the air in the house feels different afterward, and move to a recurring schedule. Recurring visits cost less per visit than one-offs, and a regular clean keeps buildup low so gentler products can do the job without extra work. If you are still deciding, start with a single visit and see how your household reacts before committing to anything more.

A note for homes with babies

If a crawling baby is in the house, tell us which floors matter most. We can clean those with the gentlest option, let them dry fully before leaving, and wipe the low surfaces a little more carefully, such as the legs of tables and chairs, play mats, and baseboards at hand height. Parents tend to notice these spots before anyone else, and it takes only a few extra minutes to do them well.

The same goes for pet bowls and sleeping spots. We keep to the products you approve and move around the animals calmly, so a dog or cat is not startled by the visit. If anything in the house is sensitive to a product, a quick conversation before we start is all it takes to adjust.
