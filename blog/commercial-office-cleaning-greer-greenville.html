Daily office cleaning checklist

  • Empty trash and recycling; replace liners.
  • Clean and disinfect restrooms: toilets, sinks, mirrors, floors; restock supplies.
  • Wipe high-touch points: door handles, light switches, railings, shared keyboards and phones.
  • Clean break room counters, sink and table.
  • Vacuum entry mats and spot-clean entry glass.

Weekly checklist

  • Vacuum all carpets; mop hard floors.
  • Dust desks, shelves, window sills and frames.
  • Clean inside the microwave and wipe the refrigerator exterior.
  • Wipe glass partitions and conference room tables.

Monthly checklist

  • Baseboards, vents and light fixtures.
  • Clean inside the break room refrigerator.
  • Detail clean restrooms: grout, partitions, behind fixtures.
  • Dust blinds and high surfaces.

How to choose a commercial cleaning company in the Upstate

  1. Ask what is included in each visit and get it in writing.
  2. Check reviews on Google from real local clients.
  3. Confirm the schedule: early mornings, evenings or weekends around your hours.
  4. Look for consistency: a small local team means the same people learn your space.
  5. Get a walkthrough estimate so the price matches the actual scope.

How often should an office be cleaned?

High-traffic spaces like retail and medical offices usually need daily or several-times-a-week service. Small offices with a few employees often do well with one or two visits per week. Perfect Services recommends a frequency after seeing the space.

High-touch areas most offices forget

Germs spread through the things everyone touches. These spots should be disinfected on every commercial cleaning visit:

  • Entry door handles and push plates.
  • Light switches and elevator buttons.
  • Shared printers, copiers and conference room remotes.
  • Break room faucets, refrigerator and microwave handles.
  • Restroom faucets, flush handles and paper dispensers.
  • Reception counter and payment terminals.

Why professional office cleaning pays for itself

  • First impressions: clients notice clean glass, floors and restrooms immediately.
  • Fewer sick days: regular disinfection of shared surfaces helps limit the spread of colds and flu during winter.
  • Staff focus: employees spend their time on their real job, not on cleaning.
  • Longer-lasting interiors: carpets, floors and furniture last longer when grit and spills are removed regularly.
