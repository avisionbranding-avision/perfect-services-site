Move-out cleaning in Greer: the kitchen, step by step

Move-out cleaning in Greer, SC, kitchen first: the order for oven, fridge, cabinets and floors, plus how the free in-home estimate sets your price.

Perfect Services Team
November 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • The kitchen is the room that decides most rental deposits.
  • Oven and refrigerator come first because they take longest to soak.
  • An empty kitchen is cleaned inside the cabinets as well as outside.

If a landlord in Greer is going to deduct anything from a deposit, the kitchen is where it usually starts. It has the most surfaces, the most grease and the most appliances, and an empty kitchen shows every shortcut. This is the order we follow in a move-out kitchen, and it works just as well if you are doing it yourself.

Start the slow jobs first

The oven gets sprayed or soaked before anything else, because it needs the longest dwell time. The refrigerator is emptied, unplugged if the lease allows it, and its shelves and drawers are pulled out and washed in the sink. Freezers need to defrost a little before the ice lifts. While those sit, everything else can move forward.

Why the fridge matters more than it seems

A refrigerator left with spills tends to carry a smell no air freshener beats, and a property manager notices it at the door. Wipe the gaskets, the door shelves and the drain channel at the back. This is the part people forget.

Inside the cabinets and drawers

This is where move-out differs from regular cleaning. Every cabinet and drawer is emptied and wiped inside, because a landlord opens them. Crumbs in a silverware drawer and sticky rings on a shelf are standard deductions. Shelf liners come out if they are stuck down or dirty. Then comes the outside: fronts, edges, handles and the underside of the cabinets above the counter.

Range hood, stovetop and backsplash

The hood filter is soaked, the hood wiped inside and out, and the backsplash degreased. Burners and drip pans come off if they can. On a gas range, the grates are scrubbed one at a time. The corner where the stove meets the counter is checked with a toothbrush, not a sponge.

Counters, sink and small details

  • Counters wiped and the seams along the backsplash cleaned
  • Sink scrubbed, faucet and handle bases detailed
  • Dishwasher: filter checked, door edge and gasket wiped
  • Microwave inside and out, including the turntable ring
  • Light switches, outlets and the door frame wiped
  • Window sill above the sink cleaned, track vacuumed

Floors last, then the final walk

The floor is vacuumed along every edge and under the toe-kick, mopped by hand and finished at the exit. Then the cleaner walks the room with fresh eyes and checks from three angles: standing, kneeling and with a flashlight along the counters. Light from a window in a Greer apartment hallway can hide a smear that a flashlight shows right away.

How long does it take?

A move-out kitchen is often the longest room in the house, usually a good chunk of the visit. If the oven is clean and the fridge was emptied, much less. If grease has been building for several years, plan for extra time, which is something we spot during the estimate. Booking this week works best when your move date is firm.

An honest note on what cleaning cannot fix

A burned-in stain on a laminate counter, a scratched stainless door or a chipped sink is wear, not dirt. We can make everything as clean as it can be, but we cannot erase damage, and we would rather tell you so at the estimate than promise something we cannot deliver. A landlord expects normal wear in most leases, and clean damage looks very different from dirty damage in a walkthrough. Take photos of anything questionable before you start.

If you are still on the lease and plan to clean ahead of time, work in this order across the week: pantry and cabinets first, then appliances, and finally the floor on the last day once the movers have finished. That keeps you from cleaning the same spot twice. For the full checklist, see move-out cleaning in Greer.

Kitchens in different Greer homes

An apartment kitchen near the I-85 corridor is compact, so everything is within reach and the oven and fridge are the whole job. A larger family kitchen in a house toward SC-101 may have a pantry, a second refrigerator in the garage and an island with drawers on both sides. Each of those adds time. We note them during the estimate so the plan matches the real space and nothing is a surprise on the day.

What not to do

Do not use steel wool on a glass cooktop. Do not leave cleaning products in the cabinets, since they count as items left behind. Do not run the self-clean cycle on the oven right before the walkthrough if the smell lingers; some Greer landlords dislike it. And do not leave the garbage disposal, which smells after the water is shut off. Pour some baking soda and a squeeze of lemon in, and run it once.
