Summer apartment cleaning in Greer: heat, humidity and dust

Summer apartment cleaning in Greer SC: keep humidity, mildew and dust under control in a small unit, with a checklist and a free in-home estimate.

Key takeaways

  • A small apartment holds humidity, so bathrooms, balcony doors and the fridge need attention in summer.
  • Dust shows up faster when the air conditioner runs all day, especially on vents and baseboards.
  • Perfect Services prices apartment cleaning only after a free in-home estimate.

Why does my Greer apartment feel sticky even when it is clean?

Because the air is wet, not because the surfaces are dirty. In July the Upstate is humid, and a one- or two-bedroom apartment has less space for that moisture to go. Steam from the shower, a drying rack inside the unit and an air conditioner that cycles on and off all keep the air heavy, and the first sign is a sticky feel on counters and a smell in the bath. Apartment cleaning in Greer in summer is mostly about dealing with that moisture before it turns into mildew.

Where moisture collects in a small unit

A lot of the apartments and condos we visit have the same weak spots. These are worth checking monthly, and they are what the team looks at first:

  • The bathroom exhaust fan cover and the ceiling around it, which collect lint and spots.
  • The caulk line along the tub and the bottom of the shower curtain.
  • The rubber seal on the refrigerator door.
  • The track of the balcony or patio door, where water and dirt sit.
  • Window sills with condensation from a cold AC unit meeting warm air.
  • The floor behind the bathroom door, where wet towels sit.

Does the air conditioner make more dust?

It moves more of it. A unit that runs from May through September pulls dust through the return vent and pushes it onto the nearest flat surface. Renters near US-29 and I-85 also see a layer of fine grit on the balcony and the sill when the door has been open. Dust the vents, the top of the refrigerator and the baseboards with a damp microfiber cloth. A dry cloth just moves it. If your apartment community changes AC filters on a schedule, ask the office when yours is due.

How do I keep a small apartment fresh between cleanings?

Short routines beat long weekends. Pick two or three things for each day and the unit stays manageable. A simple summer rotation looks like this:

Small things renters miss in July

Ask a renter what they cleaned this month and the answer is usually the floors and the kitchen. What gets missed is the stuff that sits at eye level for the one person who lives there and nobody else notices: the top of the refrigerator, where grease and dust sit together, the shelf above the stove, and the inside of the microwave door. In summer those surfaces pick up cooking steam, and the dust sticks to them instead of floating away.

Closets are another one. In a humid month, shoes and gym bags left on a closet floor can hold damp air, and a few inches of airflow makes a difference. Move the pile off the floor, crack the door and wipe the baseboard inside. If you have a stackable washer in a hallway closet, check the door gasket and leave the door open after each load. These are the same things we mention at the walkthrough, because a tenant who handles them between visits keeps the deposit conversation easy at move-out time.

Pet owners should add one more item: wash the pet bed cover and wipe the floor where the water bowl sits. Hair and dander mix with humidity and make a heavy smell in a small room faster than in a house.
