If you work long days and your apartment or condo in Greer is mostly a place to sleep, the cleaning question is not "how do I get a spotless home." It is "which three hours a week do I want back." That is what most professionals tell us when they ask about apartment cleaning in Greer, and it shapes how we set up the service.

Start with the access, not the checklist

Unlike a house, an apartment has a front gate, a building door, a parking permit or a leasing office between us and your kitchen. Sort that out first and the rest runs on autopilot. Before the first visit, have these ready:

  • The gate code or building entry method, and the parking spot for the team.
  • Whether you want us to enter with a key, a lockbox or a smart-lock code.
  • A note about pets, so they are secured and we plan for hair and dander.
  • Any rooms or drawers that stay closed, such as a home office.
  • A day that fits your week, with a backup if work travel moves it.

Because access is listed as one of the price factors, we ask about it at the estimate rather than discovering a locked gate on the first day.

What a recurring visit covers

For a one-, two- or three-bedroom unit, the standard visit includes kitchen counters, sink, stovetop and appliance fronts, then the bathroom: toilet, shower, tub, mirror and vanity. After that come floors vacuumed and mopped, dusting of surfaces, shelves and furniture, beds made, trash emptied, baseboards and door frames, the glass on the balcony or patio door, and light switches and handles sanitized. We work top to bottom and room by room, so we do not carry dust from one room to the next.

The Brazilian detail-first approach shows in small places: the edge of the shower door, the stovetop knobs, the track of a patio door, the cabinet handles you touch every morning. These are the things a quick wipe misses.

How long it takes, and booking this week

A compact apartment is usually a shorter visit than a house, and we give you a time range after the estimate. That means you can schedule around a lunch break or leave the unit and come back to it done. If you want to be set before a busy stretch, such as holiday guests or year-end deadlines, the estimate can usually be arranged within the week, and you can reach the team by phone for the soonest opening.

What to leave out for the visit

The simplest way to get more from every visit is to make the surfaces easy to reach. Clear the kitchen counter of mail and chargers, put dirty laundry in a basket, and move work papers off the dining table. We do not rummage through desks, and we will leave a laptop setup exactly as it is, but we cannot wipe around a pile for long. Ten minutes of tidying the night before means the visit goes to the bathroom, the floors and the baseboards, which is the work you hired us for. If you need a particular spot covered, such as the inside of the microwave, add it to a short note and leave it on the counter.

A final tip for roommates and couples: agree on a shared list of what the cleaning should cover and what each person still does. A written note stops the common argument over whose dishes were left. If you are in a lease, check the community rules on service visits and guest access, since some complexes require notice. We are used to these details in Greer apartment communities and can work with whatever your property requires.
