One-time cleaning in Greer: the kitchen, step by step

What happens in your kitchen during a one-time house cleaning in Greer, SC: degreasing, appliances, cabinets, counters, floors and how to book a free estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • A one-time kitchen clean is a degreasing job first and a wiping job second.
  • Fridge shelves, the backsplash and the cabinet tops carry the most hidden buildup.
  • Perfect Services gives exact prices only after a free in-home estimate.

Kitchens are why people call for a one-time clean. The living room can wait, the bedrooms can be hidden behind a door, but the kitchen is where guests stand and where a layer of grease tells the truth about the last six months. This is what happens, in order, when our team does a Greer kitchen for a single visit.

The first look

We start by standing in the doorway for a minute. That sounds silly, but it is how the team decides where the time goes. A kitchen that is mostly dusty needs a different plan from one with a stove that has not been scrubbed since spring. If you are home, tell us what bothers you most, whether it is the oven door, the sticky cabinet by the stove or the fridge. Whatever you point at gets time.

Degreasing, the part that takes the longest

Cooking grease does not stay on the stove. It travels: onto the cabinet doors above, the wall behind the stove, the range hood and even the top of the fridge. It mixes with dust and turns into a tacky film. A damp cloth alone just smears it. So we apply a degreaser to the worst zones, give it a few minutes to loosen the film and then wipe it off, one section at a time, with a fresh cloth. This is why a kitchen takes a larger share of a visit than any other room.

  • Cabinet fronts, especially around the stove
  • Range hood and the wall behind the cooktop
  • Tops of the fridge and upper cabinets
  • Backsplash and grout lines
  • Small appliance exteriors

Appliances, outside first

Fridge doors, dishwasher panels and microwave exteriors are wiped, including the handles and seals. The microwave interior gets steamed and wiped. Inside the fridge and inside the oven are optional add-ons, which many Greer households choose before a holiday, since both take significant time and should be asked for ahead of the visit. If the oven has heavy baked-on residue, we will tell you in the estimate whether it is worth including.

Counters and sink

After the vertical surfaces are done, counters are cleared and cleaned with a product suited to the material. We wipe the edge, the back strip against the wall and under the toaster and the canister set. The sink is scrubbed, the faucet descaled where needed and the drain cleared of visible debris. Last, the whole counter gets a dry wipe so there are no streaks.

Floors last

The vacuum takes the baseboards and the crumb line under the cabinets first. Then the floor is mopped from the far corner out. In a Greer house with red clay on the shoes, the doorway to the garage or the back deck is the dirtiest strip and gets an extra pass. A fresh mop pad is used, since a dirty one just redistributes the soil.

The spots most people forget

Every kitchen has a few. The knobs on the stove collect a thick film from fingers. The handle on the oven and the fridge, which hands touch more than any other object in the house, rarely see a cloth. The window sill above the sink holds water spots and dust. The trash can, inside and out, smells long before anyone thinks to wash it. And the small gap between the counter and the stove holds a surprising amount of burnt crumbs. A team goes down this list on every visit, which is why even a tidy kitchen looks different afterward.

Time of year matters as well. In late fall, kitchens in Greer are busy: pies, roasts, the oven running for hours. A one-time clean in early December is mostly grease and sugar, while one in late spring finds pollen on the window sill and sticky film near the back door. We adjust the plan to the season, because the buildup is different.

What a one-time visit does not try to do

It is honest to say what a single visit cannot fix. Years of baked-on residue on a gas range, scratched finishes, stains that have soaked into old grout: we do what a cleaning can do, and tell you if something needs a different solution. That is why the walkthrough comes first. We would rather say it before than after. Heavier work like this is where our guide to what changes the price of one-time cleaning comes in handy.

Getting ready, and what to expect afterward

Clear the counters of anything personal, and run the dishwasher if it is full. You do not need to scrub anything first; that defeats the point. When the visit ends, the kitchen should smell neutral, with no film on the cabinet handles and a floor that is dry within the hour. Neighbors in Duncan book the same visit before holidays.
