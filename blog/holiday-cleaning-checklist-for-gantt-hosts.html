Holiday cleaning checklist for Gantt hosts

One time house cleaning Gantt SC for the holidays: a countdown checklist, what to clean first, and how a free in-home estimate fits a busy December.

November 10, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Start the countdown two weeks out with the big jobs, and keep the last 48 hours for kitchen and guest areas.
  • Guests see the entry, the kitchen, the bathroom they use and the room they sleep in. Clean those most carefully.
  • A one-time clean is priced after a free in-home estimate; holiday weeks book fast, so ask early.

The turkey is the easy part. What makes holiday hosts nervous is the guest bathroom, the oven door and the corner of the living room nobody has looked at since summer. If you are hosting in Gantt this season, here is a countdown that puts the right jobs in the right week.

Two weeks out: the jobs that take time

These are the tasks that do not show on a quick tidy, but guests notice them in good light.

  • Pull out the guest-room bedding and wash comforters and pillow covers.
  • Clean the inside of the refrigerator so there is room for leftovers.
  • Degrease the oven, range hood and the cabinet fronts around the stove.
  • Wipe baseboards and door frames in the rooms guests will use.
  • Clear the entry closet and the hall so coats have a place to go.

If the oven is the part you dread, you can ask for it as an add-on: the inside of the oven, fridge or cabinets can be added to a one-time visit.

One week out: the rooms people see

Focus on the path a guest takes from the front door. Entry floors, the guest bathroom, the dining room and the living room matter most. In a typical house we see around this time, the dining table has become a drop zone for mail and school papers. Clear it now so you are not clearing it on the 23rd.

In the bathroom, scrub the grout lines near the toilet base and the bottom of the shower door, then replace the hand towel and the bath mat. Dusting the ceiling fan above the dining table is a small job that changes how the room looks under holiday lights.

The last 48 hours

Save these for last, because they get messy again quickly:

  1. Vacuum and mop all floors, finishing at the front door.
  2. Clean the kitchen counters, sink and stovetop the day before cooking.
  3. Wipe the bathroom mirror and faucet, and put out fresh soap.
  4. Take out trash and recycling and put in new liners.
  5. Make the guest bed and add a clean towel on the foot.

Do it yourself or book a one-time clean?

Doing it yourself works when the house was cleaned recently and you only need a refresh. A one-time clean is the better call if it has been months since the last professional visit, if there are pets, or if your calendar is already full of school events and travel. Our team handles the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, floors, baseboards and door frames, mirrors and glass, and trash, usually in a single visit.

It is also a good way to try a cleaning service for the first time. If you like the result, you can move to a regular schedule later. Common holiday trouble spots

A few problems come up again and again in houses that host a crowd. Greasy film builds on cabinet doors beside the stove after weeks of cooking, and a damp cloth alone will not lift it. A degreasing product, wiped by hand and followed by a clean cloth, will. Fingerprints collect on glass doors, the dishwasher front and light switches. Pet hair finds its way to the guest bed, so vacuum the mattress edge and shake out the throw pillows before making it.

Think about the floor under the Christmas tree or wherever you place extra seating. Move furniture once, vacuum the spot that has been hidden since last year, and you will not have to do it again before the big day. The same goes for the hall closet: guests need somewhere to put coats, and a closet full of boots and bags defeats the purpose.

After the guests leave

Plan the day after, too. Run the dishwasher, wipe the counters, strip the guest bed and open a window to clear cooking smells. Leave the full reset for a few days later when you have rested. Some Gantt hosts schedule a second visit in early January to bring the house back to normal, and that is a reasonable use of a one-time cleaning.
