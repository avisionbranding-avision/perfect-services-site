What is Brazilian house cleaning? A guide for Lyman homeowners

What is Brazilian house cleaning? A guide for Lyman, SC homeowners on the detail-first method, what it covers and how the free estimate sets the price.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Brazilian house cleaning is a hand-done, detail-first method, not a different product.
  • The extra time goes into corners, door frames, edges and hand-washed floors.
  • Prices come only after a free in-home estimate.

People in Lyman sometimes ask us what "Brazilian house cleaning" actually means, usually with a raised eyebrow. It is a fair question. There is no special machine and no secret product. What it describes is a way of working: slower hands, more attention and a habit of finishing the details most cleaners skip. Here is what that looks like in a Lyman home.

Where the method comes from

Perfect Services is Brazilian-owned, and our team learned to clean in a tradition where the floor is washed by hand, the baseboards are wiped one at a time and a house is not finished until you have checked the corners. In many Brazilian homes, cleaning is a routine with its own order, passed from family to family. We brought that routine to the Upstate, and it is what we mean by Brazilian house cleaning.

What it looks like in the house

The easiest way to explain it is to walk through a visit.

  • Every surface is hand-wiped, not just sprayed
  • Corners, edges and baseboards are cleaned by hand
  • Furniture and small items are moved and cleaned under
  • Door frames, switches and handles are detailed
  • Bathrooms are scrubbed, including grout and the toilet base
  • The kitchen is degreased: stovetop, range hood and cabinet fronts
  • Floors are hand-washed along the walls, not just down the middle
  • A final walk-through compares each room against a checklist

The last step matters more than people expect. Before leaving, someone looks at the room from the doorway, then from the far corner, because light from a window shows streaks and missed spots that a quick glance does not.

How it differs from regular house cleaning

TaskTypical quick cleanBrazilian detail-first
FloorsMop down the middleHand-washed, edges and corners first
BaseboardsWhen time allowsEvery visit, by hand
FurnitureDusted aroundMoved and cleaned under

It is not magic, and it is not always the right fit. If you only want a quick tidy, a lighter house cleaning visit may do. But if you have ever paid for a clean and then found dust along the baseboard the next morning, this method is built for you.

What Lyman homes bring to it

Lyman is a former mill town with a mix of historic mill homes and new construction, about 6 miles from Greer along US-29 and SC-292. The older homes have narrow trim, deep sills and wood floors that benefit from hand work, because a mop head does not follow every edge. Newer homes have larger open rooms and big tile or vinyl areas where the hand wash takes more time but shows more. In both, the baseboards and door frames are the part people notice after a visit.

What the first visit teaches us

On a first visit we pay attention to how the household lives. Where do the shoes pile up? Which bathroom gets the heavy use? Does the dog sleep on the sofa or the bed? Those answers decide where the hand work goes first. By the second or third visit, the team knows the house, and the details that took extra time at the start are already handled. That is also why we like to send the same people each time.

Bring up anything delicate, such as an heirloom table or a hand-painted tile, at the estimate. We would rather hear it before the visit than after.

How long a visit takes

Hand work takes longer than a spray-and-go clean, and a first visit takes the longest. We tell you the time window at the estimate. Once a home is on a regular schedule, visits get shorter because the detail work does not pile up.

How to tell if it is for you

  1. You like to run a finger over the baseboard and find it clean
  2. You have a pet that sheds, or kids who leave marks on door frames
  3. You want the floors to feel different, not just look different
  4. You are fine paying for time instead of speed
