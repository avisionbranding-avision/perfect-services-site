Deep cleaning vs. regular cleaning: which one do you need?

Deep cleaning vs. regular house cleaning explained: what each includes, how long they take, how often to book and how to choose the right service for your Greer, SC home.

Perfect Services Team
October 30, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Regular cleaning maintains a home that is already clean; deep cleaning removes the build-up regular visits do not reach.
  • Book a deep clean for a first visit, after 4+ weeks without professional cleaning, before or after a move, or before guests.
  • Most clients start with one deep clean, then switch to weekly or biweekly maid service.

When you ask Perfect Services for a cleaning estimate, one of the first questions is whether you need a deep cleaning or a regular cleaning. Both keep your home clean, but they are built for different moments. Choosing the right one saves money and avoids disappointment.

What regular house cleaning covers

Regular house cleaning, also called maid service or recurring cleaning, is maintenance. It keeps a home that is already in good shape clean and comfortable. A Perfect Services regular cleaning includes:

  • Dusting reachable surfaces, shelves and furniture.
  • Vacuuming and mopping all floors.
  • Kitchen counters, sink, stovetop and appliance fronts.
  • Bathrooms cleaned and disinfected: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and sink.
  • Beds made and trash emptied.
  • Because we follow the Brazilian method, switches, handles and visible baseboards get attention too.

What deep cleaning adds

Deep cleaning is a top-to-bottom reset. It includes everything in a regular clean plus the areas where dirt builds up over weeks and months:

  • Baseboards, toe kicks and door frames washed by hand in every room.
  • Cabinet fronts, handles and the backsplash.
  • Grout lines in kitchens and bathrooms; soap scum and hard water spots removed.
  • Vents, ceiling fan blades, light fixtures, window sills and blinds.
  • Behind and around the toilet, under furniture that can be moved and inside the microwave.
  • Optional add-ons like inside the oven and refrigerator.

How to choose in 30 seconds

Choose a deep cleaning if:

  • It is your first time with a professional house cleaning service.
  • It has been more than a month since the last professional cleaning.
  • You are moving in or out (see our move-out cleaning checklist).
  • You are hosting guests or the holidays are coming (see our holiday cleaning checklist).
  • You just finished a remodel; in that case consider post-construction cleaning.

Choose a regular cleaning if the home was deep cleaned recently and you want to keep it at that level.

The smart combo: deep clean first, then maid service

Most Perfect Services clients in Greer start with one deep cleaning and then switch to weekly, biweekly or monthly maid service. The first visit removes the build-up; every visit after that is faster and more affordable, and the home stays at the same level all the time.

How the price changes

Deep cleaning costs more than a regular visit because it takes more time and covers more surfaces. Recurring regular cleanings usually cost less per visit than one-time cleanings. Read our full guide to house cleaning prices in Greer, SC to see every factor that affects your quote.

Room by room: where the difference shows

Kitchen

A regular cleaning wipes counters, the sink and appliance fronts. A deep cleaning also degreases the range hood and backsplash, washes cabinet fronts and handles, cleans inside the microwave and gets the crumbs out of the toe kicks under the cabinets. In homes with white shaker cabinets, which are common in Greer, this is where clients notice the biggest change.

Bathrooms

Regular cleaning disinfects the toilet, sink, tub and mirror. Deep cleaning scrubs grout lines, removes soap scum and hard water spots from glass and chrome, cleans the exhaust fan cover and the floor behind the toilet.

Bedrooms and living areas

Regular visits dust reachable surfaces and vacuum. Deep cleaning adds ceiling fan blades, blinds, window sills, door frames, baseboards and vacuuming under beds and cushions. In carpeted bedrooms, it also means slow, overlapping vacuum passes that pull up the dirt that settles at the base of the fibers.

5 signs your home needs a deep cleaning now

  1. You can see a gray line on the baseboards.
  2. The shower glass stays cloudy after you wipe it.
  3. Ceiling fans spread dust when you turn them on.
  4. The kitchen backsplash or range hood feels sticky.
  5. Allergies get worse inside the house, especially after pollen season.

If you checked two or more, start with a deep cleaning from Perfect Services and then keep the results with regular visits.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I get a deep cleaning?

Most homes benefit from a deep cleaning two to four times a year, plus before moving or hosting. With recurring maid service, you need fewer deep cleans.

Is a deep cleaning the same as a move-out cleaning?

They are similar, but move-out cleaning is done in an empty home and always includes inside cabinets, closets and appliances.

Can I add inside the oven or fridge to a regular cleaning?

Yes. Ask for it in your estimate request and Perfect Services will include it in your price.
