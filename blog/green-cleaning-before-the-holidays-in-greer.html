Green cleaning before the holidays in Greer

Green cleaning before the holidays in Greer SC: prepare a pet and kid-friendly home for guests with low-odor products, plus a booking timeline and estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 25, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Book a low-odor visit a few days before guests, so the house is clean without a strong smell on arrival.
  • Tell us about allergies, pets and sensitivities at the estimate so they go in the visit notes.
  • Prices come after a free in-home estimate; holiday weeks fill up, so ask early.

Imagine hosting Thanksgiving with a grandmother who has asthma, a beagle under the table and a toddler who puts everything in their mouth. You want the house clean, and you do not want to walk guests through a cloud of lemon spray. That is the situation where green cleaning in Greer earns its place before the holidays. This article is a planning guide, different from our winter piece because the focus is on guests.

Start with who is coming

Before booking, write down the sensitivities in the house: allergies, asthma, fragrance headaches, pets that chew, a newborn. Those details change the plan more than the square footage does. They tell the team which products to avoid, which rooms deserve a longer visit, and whether to leave certain surfaces for you to handle. Share them when you request your estimate, and they go on the notes for the visit.

A plan for the last two weeks

  1. Two weeks out: decide on your hosting rooms and ask about dates.
  2. One week out: declutter counters and floors so the cleaning is not working around piles.
  3. Two to four days out: the cleaning visit, with a fragrance-free option if guests are sensitive.
  4. Day of: a quick wipe of the bathroom and kitchen counters and a fresh towel.

The visit being a few days before keeps the house fresh without the first-day smell fading away, and it leaves a margin if anything gets moved.

What the team does and what you can skip

The same room-by-room checklist applies as in any service: kitchens and bathrooms with gentler products, dusting with microfiber, floors cleaned with surface-safe solutions and baseboards and door frames checked. You can skip pre-cleaning. What helps is putting away dishes, clearing counters and telling us which rooms are off limits.

  • Guest bathroom: sink, toilet, shower glass, mirror and floor, with unscented options.
  • Kitchen: counters, the sink and appliance fronts, wiped with warm water and a mild degreaser.
  • Dining room: table, chair legs and the floor under them, where crumbs hide.
  • Guest bedroom: dusting, vacuuming, and the handles on closets and drawers.

How much does green cleaning cost in Greer?

Perfect Services gives exact prices only after a free in-home estimate. It is a short visit, with no commitment, and it is how we see your actual rooms rather than guess. Here is what changes the number:

  • Home size in rooms and square feet.
  • Product choice: ours, yours, or fragrance-free.
  • Frequency, since recurring visits cost less per visit.
  • Condition, because heavy buildup takes longer with gentle products.
  • Bathrooms, which are the most time-intensive rooms.

If you plan to host more than once during the season, mention it and we can discuss a plan for the whole stretch.

Choosing products for guests

There is no single right answer. Some households want no scent at all; others only want to avoid ammonia or bleach. A few have a product they trust and want used on one surface, such as a baby-safe spray in the nursery. We keep a short note for each home with those preferences, and the same note follows the team on every visit. The note also lists what to leave alone, for example a hand-washed table or a rug that cannot be wet.

Pets at the party

Dogs and cats add hair to every surface, and holiday guests bring more of it on their clothes. We focus on the places hair collects, like baseboards, rugs, stair edges and the sides of the sofa. If your pet has a special bed or bowl, we leave them where they are and clean around them. For a pet that gets anxious with strangers, tell us so we can plan how the team enters and leaves the house.

The kitchen before a big meal

A kitchen about to be used for ten hours does not need to be spotless in the showroom sense. It needs an empty sink, a degreased stovetop, wiped appliance fronts and a refrigerator with room in it. We focus on those, and on the floor in front of the sink and stove, which takes the most wear during cooking. A fresh, odor-free kitchen also lets the food smell like food, which is what guests came for.

Kids, food and cleanup after

Holiday meals leave sticky handprints and crumbs under the table. After guests leave, a second smaller visit can reset the kitchen and floors, and it is easier than facing it alone on New Year's Day. Ask about adding it when you book the first one.

If something comes up

If a guest arrives early, a flight is delayed or the menu changes the plan, call or text and we will adjust the visit where we can. Holiday weeks are tight, so the earlier we hear, the more likely we can find another slot nearby. And if a stain or spill happens after the visit, a damp microfiber cloth and plain warm water handle most of it while the surface is still fresh.
