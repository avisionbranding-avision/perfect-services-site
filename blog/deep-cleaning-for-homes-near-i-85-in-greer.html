Why winter is a good time for a deep clean near the interstate

Once the heating runs, air moves through the vents all day, and whatever has settled in them gets pushed into the room. Windows also stay closed, so dust that would drift out in spring stays put. The holidays add overnight guests and more cooking. Together, those make the weeks before the first big gathering a natural window for deep cleaning.

The Upstate's mild winters also mean pollen and clay are never entirely gone, so entryways and floors still collect more than people expect. Homes near I-85 are also close to the GSP Airport side of Greer, where many families host out-of-town visitors over the holidays, which makes a clean guest room and bathroom a priority.

The team starts at the top of each room and works down. Fans and vents come first, then window tracks and blinds, then cabinets and counters, then baseboards. Floors are last. That order is why a deep cleaning looks different from a quick tidy: every surface is reached once, in the right sequence, and nothing has to be redone.

The room-by-room checklist

Kitchen

  • Cabinet fronts and handles degreased, especially near the stove
  • Range hood, backsplash and appliance exteriors wiped down
  • Grout lines scrubbed
  • Optional add-on: inside of the oven or fridge

Bathrooms

  • Grout and hard-water spots worked by hand
  • Behind and around the toilet base
  • Mirrors, chrome and shower tracks

Living areas and bedrooms

  • Ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures dusted
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds cleaned
  • Baseboards and door frames hand-wiped
  • Furniture and shelves dusted before floors are done

Everything a regular deep cleaning covers is on that list, and everything in a regular house cleaning is included too. Two spots deserve a mention because they are the ones guests notice: the bathroom they will use and the kitchen where everyone gathers. We tend to spend the most careful time there, so if you only have budget or time for a few areas, tell us at the estimate and we will plan around them.

Guest room and entry

  • Bed made and surfaces dusted, with door frames and light switches wiped
  • Closet doors and handles cleaned
  • Entry floor and door tracks cleaned, since that is where clay and dust collect first

What you can do before the team arrives

  1. Clear counters and floors of small items so surfaces are reachable.
  2. Decide which add-ons you want and tell us at the estimate.
  3. Tell us about guest rooms, since those usually get priority before the holidays.
  4. Close doors on rooms where pets rest.

This does not need to be perfect. The team works around ordinary clutter; it only slows things down when rooms are packed.
