Apartment cleaning checklist for Wellford renters

Apartment cleaning checklist for Wellford, SC renters: room-by-room tasks for small spaces, a weekly rhythm and how the free estimate sets your price.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Small spaces get dirty in the same few places, so a short room-by-room checklist covers most of it.
  • A weekly and monthly rhythm keeps an apartment ready for inspection or guests.
  • Perfect Services prices apartment cleaning only after a free estimate visit.

Apartments are small, and that is the problem. A single bathroom does the work of three, the kitchen is also the dining room and the entry is also the closet. Dirt has nowhere to hide, and it also has nowhere to go. If you rent in Wellford, SC, this checklist breaks the work into rooms and rhythms that fit a smaller space and a busy week.

Start at the door

The entry gets the grit. Shake out the mat, wipe the door and the handle, and vacuum the first few feet of floor. In a small apartment, a clean entry sets the standard for the rest and cuts down on dirt traveling inward. On a rainy day, a tray for wet shoes saves your floor.

Kitchen

  • Counters, sink and stovetop wiped and sanitized
  • Appliance fronts and handles cleaned, since fingerprints collect there
  • Microwave wiped inside, with a bowl of water and a lemon slice steamed for two minutes to loosen splatter
  • Refrigerator shelves checked weekly for old food
  • Floor swept and mopped, including under the edge of the cabinets

Bathroom

  • Toilet cleaned inside, outside and around the base
  • Shower or tub scrubbed, with the grout lines checked
  • Mirror and vanity wiped
  • Floor mopped along the edges
  • Trash emptied, towels swapped

With only one bathroom, you cannot wait for a good day. A quick weekly pass beats a monthly deep scrub that takes an hour you do not have.

Living area and bedroom

Dust surfaces from the top down: shelves, the TV stand, nightstands, then the furniture. Vacuum the floors and the area under the bed and sofa, where dust gathers. Make the bed, since a made bed changes how a bedroom reads. Wipe light switches and door handles. If you have a balcony or patio door, clean the glass and the track, a spot that gets forgotten until a landlord inspection.

Storage and clutter come first

In a small space, clutter is the real cleaning problem. Mail on the counter, shoes by the door and laundry on the chair mean every surface has to be cleared before it can be wiped, and that doubles the time. Give each pile a home before you clean: a basket for mail, a bin for laundry, a hook for bags. Ten minutes of putting things away is worth more than an hour of working around them.

Walls and ceilings matter more in an apartment too. Without a lot of floor to look at, scuffs on the wall near the door and cobwebs at the corners show up. A damp microfiber cloth takes most of it off.

What Wellford renters should know

Wellford is a small town along US-29 and I-85 with quiet residential streets, about 7 miles from Greer, a drive of 15 minutes. A lot of the housing is smaller apartments, townhomes and duplexes, with carpet in the bedrooms and vinyl in the kitchen. Carpet holds dust and pet hair, and vinyl shows streaks, so a vacuum with a good filter and a lightly damp mop are the two tools worth having. Humid Upstate summers also mean bathrooms need a ventilation habit: run the fan, and wipe the shower wall after use to slow down mildew.

Pets, plants and balconies

If you share the apartment with a dog or cat, add a weekly pass with a rubber squeegee or damp glove over upholstery to lift hair the vacuum leaves behind. Plants on a balcony drip dirty water onto the floor and the glass, so wipe the tray and the sill when you water. A patio door track fills with grit and dead insects, and a stiff brush plus a damp cloth clears it in a few minutes.

How long it takes

A typical apartment is a short visit for a team, often under a few hours once on a schedule. We give you the time window at the estimate, so you can plan around work or a lease inspection.
