Apartment cleaning 29651: how to prep in four steps

A little preparation makes the visit go further. These four steps take about ten minutes and change how much of the time goes to cleaning rather than moving things around.

Step 1: Clear the surfaces, not the clutter

You do not need to tidy the whole place. Just clear the kitchen counters, the bathroom vanity and the coffee table, so the team can wipe them. Anything on a surface gets moved and put back, but it takes time, and in a small unit time is the main cost.

Step 2: Put away what you do not want touched

Personal papers, jewelry and anything fragile go in a drawer or a closet. We clean around items, but we would rather not guess what matters to you.

Step 3: Tell us about pets and access

If there is a dog or cat, tell us where it will be during the visit. Hair and dander add time, especially on couches and along baseboards. For access, write down the gate code, the building number and where to park. Apartment communities with gates and visitor parking are common in Greer, and a missing code can eat the first fifteen minutes.

Step 4: Know what is on the list

  • Kitchen counters, sink, stovetop and appliance fronts
  • Bathroom: toilet, shower, tub, mirror and vanity
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Dusting of surfaces, shelves and furniture
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Baseboards and door frames
  • Balcony or patio door glass
  • Light switches and handles sanitized

Beyond the list, the team looks at the places a renter or owner stops seeing after a few months: the top of the refrigerator, the vent above the stove, the inside rim of the toilet and the gap between the stove and the counter. These are small, but they are what make a unit feel clean when you walk back in.

The balcony door is the one residents forget. A sliding door that faces a parking lot or a tree line collects pollen in spring and a film in summer, and the track needs its own pass with a brush.

A small unit is a shorter visit than a house, but a lived-in two-bedroom with a dog is not quick. We confirm the time during the estimate. Schedules fill up around the first of the month, when many leases turn over, so booking early in the week gives you more choices.

A word for owners of condos and townhomes in 29651. Your unit often has more surfaces that need care than a rental apartment: finished wood, a tile backsplash, a glass shower door, a stair rail. Tell us what you use and what you avoid, and we bring the right products. If you use green cleaning products at home because of kids or pets, say so at the estimate and we plan around that.

Pet owners can help by brushing the dog or cat before the visit and keeping a lint roller by the couch. It sounds small, but loose hair that is already off the animal is hair we can vacuum instead of chase around the room, and the floors end up cleaner for it.
