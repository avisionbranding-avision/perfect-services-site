Why managers switch cleaners

Most property managers do not leave a cleaner because of one bad visit. They leave after the third email from a tenant saying the baseboards or the shower were still dirty. The fix is a crew that works by checklist and by hand. Brazilian house cleaning in Greer is that kind of crew, and it fits rental properties well.

The method, briefly

Every surface is hand-wiped, not just sprayed. Corners and baseboards are done by hand. Furniture and small items are moved and cleaned under. Door frames, switches and handles are detailed, bathrooms are scrubbed including grout and the toilet base, and the kitchen is degreased: stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts. Floors are washed by hand along the walls, and a final walk-through checks the room list. That last step is useful for you, because it gives you something concrete to compare against.

What to put in your own checklist

  • Number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and any rooms to skip
  • Pets in the lease, so the team can plan for hair
  • Access: lockbox, key or tenant present
  • Appliances that need interior cleaning
  • Photo needs after the clean, for your records
  • Who pays: owner, manager or tenant

The detail-first method changes what a manager sees after a visit. Instead of a home that looks acceptable from the doorway, you get one that holds up when someone opens a drawer, lifts a rug or runs a finger along a baseboard. That matters at lease renewals, when a tenant is deciding whether to stay, and at inspections, when you may need to prove condition. It also gives you a basis to ask a tenant to maintain their end.

It is worth separating what the owner pays for from what the tenant handles. Some owners schedule a deep clean once a year and ask tenants to keep up in between. Others include a monthly visit for a furnished property. Either arrangement works as long as it is written down and each party knows what the visit includes.

Before the first visit, send us a short list of known problem spots, such as a stained tub, a sticky cabinet or a rental with heavy pet hair. We plan the first visit around them and report back on what we found, so you can decide whether a deep clean is worth adding.

In practice it replaces re-cleans: when the first visit is thorough, you stop paying for the second visit to fix what was missed.
