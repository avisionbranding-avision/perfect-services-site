Airbnb cleaning in Greer: the kitchen, step by step

The kitchen reset a Greer, SC Airbnb turnover team runs between guests: fridge check, appliances, counters, restock list and how to ask for a free estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • A rental kitchen gets reset against a list, not by feel, so every guest finds it the same way.
  • The fridge, the dishwasher and the trash are checked first because guests leave things there.
  • Perfect Services prices turnovers only after a free in-home estimate of the rental.

A family of five just left your Greer rental, and the next guest lands in a few hours. In between, the kitchen has to go from a full weekend of cooking to a space that looks like your listing photo. That is the job of a turnover team, and this is the order a Greer Airbnb kitchen gets done in. Hosts sometimes ask what we actually do in there for the time we are on site, and the honest answer is a lot of small, repeated checks.

Minute zero: look before touching

A turnover cleaner walks the kitchen once before starting. The point is the damage and missing-item check: a broken glass, a chipped plate, a missing pan or a stain on the counter. Anything found is photographed and added to the note the host receives. Doing this first matters, because once everything is wiped and put away, the evidence is gone.

The fridge and the trash come first

Guests leave things. Half a pizza, a bag of vegetables, a bottle of wine they did not finish. The fridge is emptied of anything that is not a host staple, shelves are wiped and the door seals checked, since mold hides there. The trash and recycling are taken out and the bins relined, then the bin lids and the cabinet door in front of them are wiped. A kitchen can look clean and still smell like last night's dinner if the bin is skipped.

Dishes and appliances

The dishwasher is unloaded or run, depending on the state of things, and the filter is checked for food. Everything goes back where the host's list says it goes, so the next guest finds the mugs in the same cabinet as the photos. The coffee maker gets descaled when needed, the toaster crumb tray gets emptied, and the microwave is wiped inside and out. Appliance fronts on stainless steel are wiped with the grain, since cross-wiping leaves streaks that show in guests' photos.

  • Dishwasher unloaded, filter checked
  • Coffee maker emptied, grounds out, carafe rinsed
  • Toaster crumb tray emptied
  • Microwave wiped inside and out
  • Stove top and knobs cleaned, drip pans checked

Counters, sink and the backsplash

With the appliances done, counters are cleared and wiped, and the backsplash is checked for splatter. Rentals near Trade Street and GSP Airport often see short stays with heavy cooking, so the stove splash zone is usually the part that needs a closer look. The sink is scrubbed and the drain strainer emptied. A dry finish on the faucet matters in a listing photo, and it matters more when a guest walks in and sees it shining.

The restock list

This is the host's best friend. After the surfaces are done the team checks the supplies you asked us to track: dish soap, sponges, paper towels, trash bags, coffee filters, salt and pepper. The list goes to you, or the items are replaced if you leave them and give instructions. Nothing runs out silently, which is how a small thing becomes a one-star remark. Our vacation rental cleaning service includes this restocking checklist as part of every turnover.

Floors last, edges first

Crumbs live along the baseboards and under the edge of the cabinets, so the vacuum starts there and moves toward the center. The mop goes in from the far corner toward the door. Rentals that sit near a trailhead or the lake often have sand or red clay by the entry, which gets a second pass. Floors finish the kitchen because nobody should walk on them until the end.

The small things guests photograph

Listing reviews often include phone photos, and kitchens are overrepresented. Four details come up again and again: a sticky spot on the counter by the coffee maker, crumbs inside the silverware drawer, a water ring around the faucet, and the inside of the microwave. These are not hard to fix, but they are easy to forget when you are cleaning alone at the end of a long day. A team that works from a list reaches all four without thinking about it.

Seasonal habits matter as well. In summer, fruit flies show up around a compost jar or a forgotten banana, so the trash and the counter near the window get a closer look. In holiday weeks, rentals near Greenville fill with families who cook large meals, and the oven and stove splash zones need more time. When a host tells us the calendar ahead, we plan the visit around it, adding a longer slot after big-cooking weekends and a shorter one after a quiet couple's stay.
