Apartment cleaning during Upstate pollen season in Greer

Pollen finds its way into Greer apartments through balcony doors and windows. See how apartment cleaning handles it and how the free in-home estimate works.

Key takeaways

  • Small apartments concentrate pollen: it sits on balcony doors, window sills and dark furniture.
  • Cleaning in a set order, high to low and soft to hard, keeps it from resettling.

An apartment has less floor than a house, but pollen does not scale down with it. In a one-bedroom near Greer, a thin yellow layer on the balcony door and the windowsill can end up as most of what you breathe for a month. If you have ever wiped the same bedside table twice in one day in April, you know the feeling.

This guide is for people who rent or own an apartment or condo in Greer and want to know what pollen season changes about cleaning, what you can do yourself, and what a professional visit adds.

Why apartments feel pollen more

Small spaces recirculate air quickly. One open window and a few trips through the door are enough to bring pollen to every room. Add carpet in the bedroom, a balcony door that does not seal perfectly, and an HVAC filter you cannot reach without asking maintenance, and pollen has a lot of places to stay. Upstate pollen season runs from about late February through May, with the worst of it usually in March and April.

The five spots to check first

  • The balcony or patio door, including the track and the rubber seal
  • Window sills and the top of the blinds
  • The vents and the front of the HVAC return
  • Dark furniture, TV stands and nightstands
  • The entry mat and the first two feet of floor inside the door

These are the spots the team also checks at the start of a visit. They are not complicated, but they are the ones that make a unit feel "off" even when it looks tidy.

A routine that works in a small unit

  1. Close windows on high-pollen days. Weather apps and local news give a daily pollen forecast; check it before opening up.
  2. Take shoes off at the door and keep a mat inside.
  3. Wipe with a damp microfiber cloth, not a dry duster. Dry dusters launch pollen back into the air.
  4. Wash bedding weekly in spring. A pillowcase that has sat near an open window will hold more than you think.
  5. Vacuum with a filter that traps fine dust, and empty the canister outside.

What to do about the balcony

Balconies are the one spot most renters forget about, and pollen settles there thickest. If your balcony has a chair or small table, wipe them with a damp cloth before they come inside or before guests sit down. Sweep toward the railing, not toward the door, so what you move does not travel into the apartment on the soles of your shoes. The glass of the balcony door is part of every visit, so you only need to handle the outdoor furniture and floor yourself.

A small habit with a big effect: keep a spare, clean cloth by the door and wipe the handle and frame as you come in during spring. It takes ten seconds and saves a lot of wiping later. Frequently asked questions

Does pollen get worse in apartments?

It can feel that way. Small units recirculate air quickly, and balcony doors and windows are close to the living and sleeping areas.

Should I dust with a feather duster in spring?

No. A damp microfiber cloth holds pollen instead of sending it back into the air.

Do you clean the balcony door track?

Yes. The balcony or patio door glass is part of the apartment checklist, and we pay extra attention to the track in pollen season.
