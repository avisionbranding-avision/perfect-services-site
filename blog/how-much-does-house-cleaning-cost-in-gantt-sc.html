The five things that set the price

Home size and layout

More rooms and more square feet take more time. Layout matters too. An open floor plan with one big kitchen and living room cleans differently from a house with many small rooms and doors.

Bedrooms and bathrooms

Bathrooms are the most time-intensive rooms. A four-bedroom house with two bathrooms is often quicker than a three-bedroom with three, because each tub, toilet and mirror is worked by hand.

Frequency

Weekly and biweekly visits cost less per visit than monthly ones. A house cleaned often stays at the maintenance standard, so the team spends the visit on the details rather than on catching up.

Pets and kids

Extra hair and traffic add a little time. A dog that sheds along the baseboards or a playroom with crumbs under the cushions simply needs another pass.

The first visit

The first clean is usually deeper, to bring the home up to the standard the regular visits will keep. That is normal, and it is why some customers start with a deep cleaning and then switch to recurring house cleaning.

What is included in a regular house cleaning

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized.
  • Appliance fronts and handles wiped.
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome.
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand.
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted.
  • Beds made and trash emptied.
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation, so every room gets detail work over a few visits.
  • Light switches and high-touch spots wiped.

The hand work is the Brazilian detail-first approach the company is built on. It is slower than a quick spray and wipe, which is part of why it looks different afterward.

How long does a house cleaning take?

For an average family home, the visit takes a few hours with a team. A small house or a recurring visit in a home that is already kept up can be faster, and a large or heavily lived-in home takes longer. The estimate visit is where the team gives you a realistic time as well as a price.

Comparing quotes fairly

If you are collecting several quotes for house cleaning services in Gantt, SC, compare the same things: what is included, how often, whether supplies are included, and whether the price was set after someone saw the home. A low number from a listing is not the same as a price from a visit. Ask each company what happens if the house needs more time than expected. Also ask who is cleaning your home and whether it is the same people each time, since familiarity with your house is half of why a recurring service works.

What a visit looks like from your side

You do not need to prepare much. Put away anything fragile or valuable, clear the counters and floors where you can, and let the team know about any rooms to skip or spots that need extra attention, like a stained vanity or a pet's corner. Most Gantt homeowners are out during the visit, and some stay home and work in another room. Either is fine. If you have a key arrangement or alarm code, the team agrees on it with you first.

After the first visit, the team keeps notes on your home, so each clean follows the same pattern and picks up where the last one stopped. That is the reason the second and third visits usually go smoother and faster than the first.
