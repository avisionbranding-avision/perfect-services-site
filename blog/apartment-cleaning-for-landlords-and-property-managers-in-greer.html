Apartment cleaning for landlords and property managers in Greer

Apartment cleaning for landlords and property managers in Greer SC: unit turns, move-in ready standards and how our free in-home estimate sets pricing.

November 22, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • A unit turn goes faster when the cleaning list is the same every time.
  • Door frames, baseboards, appliance fronts and balcony glass are what tenants notice first.
  • Pricing follows the unit and is given after a free in-home estimate.

A unit turn in Greer has a rhythm: the tenant hands over keys, the maintenance list gets worked, and then someone needs to make the place look rentable before showings start. The cleaning step is the one that quietly delays everything, because it cannot be done well until the repairs are finished and cannot wait past the first showing. This post is for owners of apartments and condos and for the managers who run turns, and covers how apartment cleaning in Greer fits into that schedule.

The most common mistake we see is booking the clean before maintenance finishes, then needing a second visit to remove drywall dust. If repair dates are uncertain, say so, and we plan the visit for after.

The standard we clean to

Our apartment and condo checklist is simple and repeatable, which is what a manager needs:

  • Kitchen counters, sink, stovetop and appliance fronts
  • Bathroom toilet, shower, tub, mirror and vanity
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Dusting of surfaces, shelves and furniture left in the unit
  • Baseboards and door frames
  • Balcony or patio door glass
  • Light switches and handles sanitized
  • Trash emptied

For an empty unit between leases, the specialized move-in and move-out cleaning may be the better fit, and we can tell you which one at the estimate.

What tenants notice first

New tenants judge a unit in the first two minutes. They open the fridge, look in the shower, run a finger along the baseboard and check the balcony glass. Those are the places the team slows down. A unit that looks cared for at the start also tends to be returned in better shape, although that depends on the tenant.

How much does apartment cleaning cost in Greer?

We do not publish prices, because a studio, a two-bedroom condo and a townhome unit are not the same job. For landlords, the pricing factors are:

  • Unit size, from studio to three bedrooms
  • Number of bathrooms
  • Frequency, since recurring visits cost less per visit
  • Pets, whose hair and dander add time
  • Access, such as gate codes and parking

How long a unit takes and how to schedule

Time depends on how long the unit has been empty and what the last tenant left behind. A tidy one-bedroom takes a short visit; a neglected two-bedroom with heavy kitchen build-up takes longer. Greer's apartment and condo communities sit mostly along SC-14, SC-101 and US-29, close to the team's base, so travel time is low. Call early in the week if you want a clean to land before the weekend showings.

Next steps

Reports, photos and the paper trail

A turn goes smoother when you have a record of the unit's condition on the day we leave. Our team notes anything we cannot fix by cleaning, such as a cracked tile, a stained countertop or a worn carpet edge, and flags it so you can decide who handles it. That note helps both ends of a tenancy: it helps you set expectations for the next tenant, and it gives you a clear starting point if there is ever a disagreement about condition at move-out.

Recurring service for occupied units

Some owners offer cleaning as part of a longer lease or keep a unit furnished for corporate stays. In that case, recurring visits make more sense than single turns. A fixed schedule keeps the unit steady, the tenant sees the same people, and you avoid the rush before every inspection. Recurring visits cost less per visit, which matters when the unit is booked month after month.

What cleaning cannot do

We want to be direct about limits. Cleaning cannot remove permanent stains, repair damaged flooring or replace worn caulk. If a bathroom needs its grout regrouted or a carpet needs replacing, cleaning will improve the look but not change the cause. During the walkthrough we separate what cleaning will fix from what needs a contractor, so you spend on the right thing first. That honesty saves a second visit, and it protects your reputation with the tenants who will move in next.

Finally, think about the entry and the common touchpoints. Light switches, handles and door frames are where a tenant's hand lands first. We sanitize them on every visit, and we finish at the front door so the first thing a prospective tenant sees is a clean threshold.
