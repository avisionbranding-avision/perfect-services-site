Move-out cleaning checklist for Easley renters

A room-by-room move out cleaning Easley SC checklist for renters: what landlords inspect first, what to leave for the pros and how to book a free estimate.

Key takeaways

  • Landlords usually open the oven, refrigerator, cabinets and bathrooms first, so start there.
  • Work from the top down and leave floors for last, one room at a time.

Easley has a lot of renters who live in newer subdivisions and older homes on the same side of town, and the move-out walkthrough looks about the same in both: the landlord opens the oven door, then the refrigerator, then checks the bathroom. If you are leaving a rental in Pickens County and want the full deposit back, this checklist covers those spots in the order that saves the most time.

If you would rather hand it off, a move out cleaning service in Easley follows the same list, and the team works it room by room until the last floor is mopped.

Start with the room where deposits are lost: the kitchen

Most move-outs we see start losing points in the kitchen. Empty every cabinet and drawer before you wipe anything, because crumbs hide in the corners and shelf-liner edges. Then work top to bottom.

  • Cabinets and drawers, inside and out, including the tops of the upper cabinets if you can reach them
  • Refrigerator and freezer, shelves and crisper drawers washed in the sink
  • Oven interior, racks and the glass in the door
  • Microwave inside, plus the turntable
  • Range hood filter, stovetop and backsplash degreased
  • Sink, faucet and the drain edge

The oven is the one that surprises people. Baked-on grease needs time with a degreaser, so spray it first and do the rest of the kitchen while it sits.

Bathrooms: where the small details show

Bathrooms look clean from the doorway and fail up close. Landlords check the toilet base, the grout lines in the shower, the glass track and the inside of the vanity drawers. Hard water spots on glass and fixtures are common across the Upstate, so a little vinegar solution or a descaler helps, as long as it is safe for your surfaces.

Do not forget the exhaust fan cover, the towel bar and the light switch plate. These are the places a walkthrough inspector's eye lands when everything else looks fine.

Bedrooms, closets and living areas

Closets get skipped more than any other space. Take out the shelves if they come out, wipe the rods and vacuum the floor of the closet, including the corners behind the door. Then move to baseboards, door frames and window sills. In a house with sliding doors or older window tracks, scrape the grit out with a small brush before you wipe, otherwise you only push dirt around.

Ceiling fan blades collect a layer of dust that falls the moment someone turns them on, so wipe them with a damp cloth or a pillowcase before you vacuum the floor below. If you had pets, check the carpet near doors and under the bed for hair and the spots where it settles.

How long does a move-out clean take?

For a renter doing it alone, plan on a full day for an average apartment and longer for a house with several bathrooms. A team usually finishes much faster because several people work different rooms at once, and an empty home cleans faster than a furnished one. The condition the last occupant left matters too: a kitchen with heavy grease needs more time than one that was wiped weekly.
