There is no honest flat rate for house cleaning in Five Forks, SC, and any company that gives you one over the phone is guessing. A 2,000 square foot ranch off Woodruff Rd and a two-story with a bonus room and four bathrooms are different jobs. Here is what really moves the price, so you can judge any quote you receive, ours included.

The five things that set the price

Everything comes down to time. Cleaners are paid for hours of careful work, so anything that adds hours adds cost.

  • Home size and layout. More rooms and square feet take more time. An open plan is quicker per square foot than a house with many small rooms.
  • Bedrooms and bathrooms. Bathrooms are the most time-intensive rooms because of the shower, the grout and the fixtures.
  • Frequency. Weekly and biweekly visits cost less per visit than monthly, since the home never gets far from clean.
  • Pets and kids. Hair, tracked-in dirt and extra traffic add a little time.
  • First visit. The first clean is usually deeper, to reach the maintenance standard that later visits keep up.

Why Five Forks homes vary so much

Five Forks sits between Greer and Simpsonville, in Greenville County, and the housing is mixed: established subdivisions with larger family homes next to newer builds. Many of the homes we see here have a lot of bathrooms, a bonus room over the garage and wide hardwood or tile floors in the main living areas. That is why a square foot rate rarely works. We would rather walk the house.

What a visit includes

When you hire house cleaning in Five Forks, the price covers the full checklist:

  1. Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized
  2. Appliance fronts and handles wiped
  3. Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome
  4. Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand
  5. Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted
  6. Beds made and trash emptied
  7. Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation
  8. Light switches and high-touch spots wiped

You can see the same list on our house cleaning service page. Notice that baseboards are on a rotation, which keeps the regular visit fast. If you want all baseboards done in one visit, say so and we plan the time.

How long does a visit take?

A typical Five Forks home takes one team a few hours, with the first visit running longer. Bigger houses with four or five bathrooms take more. We give you an expected time at the estimate, and it is usually the same every visit once the home is in rhythm. Ways to keep the cost reasonable

Clear counters and floors before we arrive so time goes to cleaning rather than moving things. Tell us about trouble spots up front. Choose a frequency you will keep, because consistency is cheaper than catching up. And ask us to explain what is included instead of comparing only the bottom line, since a cheaper quote often skips the details, like door frames and switch plates. What to ask any cleaner about the quote

Whoever you call, find out what the price includes, whether the same people come each time and what happens if something is missed. A clear answer to those three questions tells you more than the number does. In a larger home, like many in Five Forks, ask how the team handles the second floor and whether the visit is the same length every time. If a company hesitates, that is a sign the details are not on a checklist.

Also think about the add-ons you might want later: the inside of the fridge, the oven or the windows. They are easy to include at the first visit and cost less to plan than to squeeze in afterward. Mention pets and kids at the estimate, too, so the time we plan is the time we need and the first visit does not run over.
