If you search for house cleaning cost in Inman, SC, you will find plenty of numbers and very little explanation. The honest answer is that two houses of the same square footage can need very different amounts of time, and time is what you pay for. So here is how pricing really works, what we look at, and how to get an exact figure without guessing.

What sets the price of a house cleaning

Every estimate comes down to the same five questions. This is what we weigh during the walkthrough:

FactorWhy it matters
Home size and layoutMore rooms and square feet take more time
Bedrooms and bathroomsBathrooms are the most time-intensive rooms
FrequencyWeekly and biweekly visits cost less per visit than monthly
Pets and kidsExtra hair and traffic add a little time
First visitThe first clean is usually deeper, to reach the maintenance standard

Frequency surprises people the most. A home cleaned every week stays near the standard, so each visit is lighter. A home cleaned once a month collects more, so each visit does more work. That is why recurring plans cost less per visit.

What is included in the price

A standard visit with our house cleaning and maid service covers the same checklist every time:

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized, with appliance fronts and handles wiped.
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome.
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand.
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted.
  • Beds made and trash emptied.
  • Light switches and high-touch spots wiped.
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation.

The rotation on baseboards and door frames is deliberate. Doing every edge every week would make a recurring visit last far longer, so we spread that detail work across visits and cover it all over a month or so.

What the walkthrough looks at

People often ask what we are actually checking during the estimate. The short list: how many bathrooms there are and what shape the grout and glass are in, how much of the floor is hardwood, tile or carpet, whether there are pets and where they sleep, and how much furniture sits in the way. In a lake home or a farmhouse-style property near Inman, we also notice things like mudrooms and back entries, because dirt gets tracked in through those first.

We also ask what bothers you most. Some homeowners care about the kitchen, others about bathrooms or the pet hair on the stairs. Knowing that lets the team spend time where you will notice it, and it keeps the estimate honest because nothing is a surprise on the day.

If you are comparing a maid service in Inman against doing it yourself, add up the real hours: a few for bathrooms, a few for floors, a few for dusting and the kitchen. For many families that weekend block is the actual cost, and it is part of why recurring visits appeal to them.

How long does it take?

A first visit to an average family home can take a few hours, depending on size and condition. Later visits tend to be shorter. We always give the expected window at the estimate. If you are looking for a maid service close to I-26 or along US-176, the drive from Greer is about 20 minutes, so scheduling is rarely the obstacle, and booking this week is often realistic.

Ways to keep the price reasonable

You have more control than you think. Pick up toys, laundry and clutter before the team arrives so surfaces can be reached. Choose a recurring schedule if you can. Tell us up front which rooms matter most. And if the house has not been cleaned professionally in a long time, consider that the first visit may be heavier than the ones after it.
