A Brazilian-style weekly cleaning routine for Greer homes

If you want Brazilian house cleaners near Greer on a regular schedule, the best results come from splitting the work: small daily habits from you and a detailed visit from the team. The idea behind a Brazilian-style routine

Brazilian cleaning is built on order and repetition. Each room has a checklist, each surface is wiped by hand, and the floor comes last. The routine below borrows that logic for the days between visits, so nothing piles up. It is not a strict program. Pick the days that fit and skip the rest.

Monday to Friday: one small job a day

DayFocusWhat to do
MondayKitchen resetCounters, sink, stovetop wipe, floor spot-mop
TuesdayBathroomsMirror, sink, toilet seat and handle
WednesdayLiving areasDust surfaces, tidy cushions, vacuum high-traffic paths
ThursdayBedroomsChange linens, clear nightstands
FridayEntry and floorsShake out mats, sweep, wipe door handle

Fifteen minutes a day is enough for most homes. In Greer, red clay tracked in from the yard shows up fast at the entry, and spring pollen settles on window sills and car-side doors, so the Friday entry reset earns its place.

What the weekly team visit covers

This is where the Brazilian method does the heavy lifting. A typical visit includes:

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not only sprayed.
  • Baseboards, corners and door frames detailed.
  • Small items and furniture moved and cleaned under.
  • Bathrooms scrubbed, including grout and the toilet base.
  • Kitchen degreased: stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts.
  • Floors hand-washed along the walls.
  • A final walk-through against the room checklist.

Because the team handles the detail work, your daily jobs stay light. Homes that keep a routine like this tend to need shorter visits over time, since there is less buildup to cut through. A first visit takes the longest, and later ones are faster.

A monthly rhythm for the jobs nobody remembers

Some tasks fit a monthly list better than a weekly one: oven interiors, the fridge shelves, window tracks, ceiling fan blades, and the tops of cabinets. Pick one a week and you will finish the list before the month ends. If you would rather not, ask about deep cleaning as an add-on to a regular visit. Adjusting the routine for the season

In spring, pollen is the main enemy: wipe window sills and the top of the porch door more often, and keep the entry mat clean. In summer, humidity makes bathrooms and the kitchen drain need extra attention. In fall, leaves and red clay come in on shoes, so the entry floor deserves a mid-week sweep. In winter, closed windows mean dust stays in the air longer, so dusting on Wednesday helps more than it does in June.

Holiday weeks are the other exception. Before guests arrive, move the visit earlier in the week and add a short extra pass on the guest bathroom and the kitchen. That small change keeps the house company-ready without a last-minute scramble, and the weekly Brazilian house cleaning in Greer schedule can shift around it.

One more habit helps: keep a short note on the fridge of anything you want the team to focus on next visit, such as a stained spot or a shelf that needs moving. Notes like that cut the back and forth and make each visit faster.

Choosing a schedule that fits your home

Weekly suits families with kids or pets and anyone working from home. Every other week works for smaller households or those who like to do the daily jobs themselves. Monthly is better as a reset than a routine.
