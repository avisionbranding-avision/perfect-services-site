Move-out cleaning for busy professionals in Greer

Move out cleaning Greer professionals can plan around: a week-by-week timeline, what the team cleans, how the free estimate works and how to book.

Key takeaways

  • Book the free in-home estimate at the start of your notice period, not the day before the keys are due.
  • Schedule the clean for after the movers and before the walkthrough, with a day of cushion if you can.
  • The exact price comes after a short visit, so you can plan around a real number instead of guessing.

Your lease ends on the thirtieth, the new job starts on the second, and the only free afternoon is a Wednesday. If that sounds like your calendar, the cleaning is the piece that should be handled by someone else. This is a practical timeline for move out cleaning in Greer written for people who are working full-time through a move.

The only step people skip is the first one. A booking made while there are still weeks left costs nothing extra and gives the team the room to fit a closing-day schedule. A booking made two days out may still work, but the options narrow.

What a move-out clean covers

This is a clean for empty rooms, and the list is what landlords and agents check at a walkthrough. The move-in and move-out cleaning service includes:

  • Inside and outside of kitchen cabinets and drawers
  • Inside the refrigerator, freezer, oven and microwave
  • Range hood, stovetop and backsplash degreased
  • Bathrooms: toilet base, grout, glass and vanity interiors
  • Closet shelves, rods and floors
  • Baseboards, doors, frames and light switches
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped edge to edge

In the apartments and townhomes people rent around Greer, the places that cost deposits are rarely the obvious ones. They are the oven interior, the shower track, the baseboards behind where a couch sat, and the fridge shelves. Those are the places we go first.

What to do yourself and what to leave

You do not need to scrub anything before the team arrives, but a few small steps save time on the day. Take every personal item out of the cabinets, the fridge and the bathroom, since the team cleans surfaces, not belongings. Pull down any shelf liners or adhesive hooks you installed. Put trash and boxes outside, and tell us about anything that was already damaged when you moved in, such as a stained countertop or a cracked tile, so nobody tries to scrub away something that cannot be cleaned.

Photos help you too. A quick set of pictures of each room after the clean is a simple record if a deposit question comes up later. Most people take them on their phone during the final walkthrough, and it takes about two minutes.

How long it takes

A one-bedroom apartment can be done in a few hours, while a three-bedroom house with two bathrooms takes a team most of a day. Heavy buildup extends that, and a team of more people shortens it. Greer is our home base, roughly ten minutes from most addresses off I-85, SC-14 and US-29, so scheduling around a tight Friday handover is realistic.

Deposit protection and the walkthrough

If a deposit is at stake, read the lease clause on cleaning before you book anything. Some leases require professional cleaning, and others only ask for broom-clean condition. We cannot promise a landlord's decision, but the team finishes with a walkthrough against the room checklist, which is the same list a landlord uses. If you can be there for the last ten minutes, you see the result directly.

For renters in nearby towns the process is identical, for example move-out cleaning in Duncan. To see how the checklist looks in practice, our article on move-out cleaning checklist for Inman renters breaks it down room by room.

If the lease and the new place do not line up

A gap of a few days between the old lease and the new one is common. In that case, book the clean for the day the old place is empty, even if the new one is not ready, so the cleaning date is not the thing that slips. If the new home is a house you are buying, a move-in clean before you unpack is worth asking about at the same estimate, since it is easier to clean an empty room than one with boxes stacked in it.

Moving with a deadline from work

Many professionals moving into or out of Greer have a start date set by an employer, and Greer sits close to GSP Airport and the BMW plant, so the move date is rarely flexible. If you are in that situation, say so when you book. A larger team is the usual answer to a hard deadline. Some people also pair the move-out clean with a regular maid service at the new place, and a first-time deep clean there is a common choice before unpacking.

The Brazilian detail-first method we use means each room is done against a checklist and finished by a walkthrough, so nothing gets left because the day ran late. That is useful when you are not standing in the doorway watching.
