Airbnb cleaning for landlords and property managers in Greer

Airbnb cleaning for landlords and property managers in Greer SC: turnover standards, damage reports and how a free in-home estimate sets your price.

Perfect Services Team
November 22, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • A repeatable standard and a photo report matter more to an owner than any single clean.
  • One point of contact and an early calendar keep tight turnovers from slipping.
  • Pricing comes after a free in-home estimate, based on size, linens, restocking and volume.

Ask a property manager what keeps a short-term rental profitable and the answer is rarely the nightly rate. It is the third turnover of the week, when the cleaner is new, the checklist is in someone's head and the guest finds a stained duvet cover. Owners and managers in Greer who want Airbnb cleaning for landlords that stays consistent need a standard on paper, a proof of work and a team that picks up the phone.

Write the standard down once

We start every new unit with a free in-home estimate that doubles as a walkthrough. We look at the layout, the surfaces, how guests actually use the space and what your listing photos promise. From that visit the checklist is written, and every turnover follows it. A good standard has three parts:

  • The reset list: kitchen, bathrooms, beds, floors, trash and bins
  • The finish list: staging that matches the listing, and a supplies check
  • The exceptions list: what the team reports instead of fixing, such as damage, odors or missing items

Having the exceptions list matters most. Cleaners should not quietly repair or hide a problem, and owners should not hear about it from a guest.

What lands in your inbox after each visit

Besides a finished unit, you get a damage and missing-item report with photos. For managers who answer to an owner, that report is the paper trail. For owners who live out of town, it is the nearest thing to being there.

How long it takes and how soon you can start

A turnover takes as long as the unit needs: a small condo moves quickly, a four-bedroom house with three bathrooms takes a team longer. We confirm the window at the estimate. If you need to start this week, call and ask about openings, since holiday weeks fill early and we would rather tell you straight than overpromise.

Seasonal patterns worth planning for

Short-term rentals in the Upstate do not book evenly. Football weekends, graduations, holiday travel and spring events all stack turnovers into a few days, and humid summers bring a damp smell into closed-up units that sat empty. If you keep a calendar, mark the weeks when you expect back-to-back stays and send it to us ahead of time. We will tell you plainly what we can staff, so you can adjust check-in times with guests before the day, not on it.

Keeping several units consistent

The harder problem for a manager is consistency across units, not quality in one. Two units with the same layout can end up with different results if each cleaner works from memory. A shared checklist, the same team where possible and the same report format on every visit mean that a review mentioning a bathroom or a kitchen can be traced back to the visit notes and fixed once for all units.

We also suggest a quarterly look at each unit, outside the turnover rhythm. Turnovers keep the surfaces fresh, but they do not always reach inside oven doors, behind appliances or along the high shelves. A periodic deeper visit catches what the turnover rhythm cannot, and it is a good time to review the checklist with us and update it for anything that changed in the unit.

Questions to ask any cleaner you hire

  • Do you walk the unit before you start, and do you report what you find?
  • Who do I call when a window changes at the last minute?
  • How do you handle linens that arrive damaged or stained?
  • Will I see photos of problems, or only hear about them?

Those four answers separate a team that cleans from a team that manages the turnover with you. We built our process around them. When a problem does show up, such as a broken blind or an odor from a drain, you get the photo and a short note the same day, so you decide what to do while the unit is still empty.
