Airbnb turnover cleaning checklist for Spartanburg hosts

Airbnb turnover cleaning checklist for Spartanburg, SC hosts: kitchen reset, bathrooms, linens, restocking and photo reports, with a free estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • A good turnover follows the same order every time so nothing is missed between guests.
  • Damage and missing-item reports with photos protect you with the platform.
  • Same-day turnovers need tighter scheduling; Perfect Services quotes after a free estimate.

A guest finds one long hair in a shower and the review drops a star. For a host in Spartanburg, turnover day is where the rating is won or lost, and it happens in a short window between checkout and the next check-in. Spartanburg is the hub of the county, with historic homes near Morgan Square, the colleges and large suburban neighborhoods all linked by I-26, I-85 and I-585, so hosts here host everyone from visiting parents to business travelers. This checklist follows the order a turnover crew works in.

1. Walk in and look first

Before cleaning, spend two minutes checking the property. Look for damage, missing items and anything left behind. Note it right away, because later you will not know what happened before cleaning. Our team photographs problems and sends a damage and missing-item report.

2. Strip and start the laundry

Remove all linens and towels and start the laundry first so it runs while the rest is cleaned. Beds are made with fresh linens, which the host provides. Laundry can happen on site or off site, and that choice affects timing.

3. Kitchen reset

  • Dishes washed or loaded and run
  • Counters, sink and stovetop wiped
  • Appliance fronts, microwave and fridge handles cleaned
  • Trash removed and bins reset
  • Coffee station and pantry checked for host supplies

4. Bathrooms

Bathrooms are cleaned and disinfected from top to bottom: mirror, sink, toilet, shower and floor. Check drains for hair, wipe chrome and replace towels. This is where most bad reviews start, so it deserves the longest time.

5. Floors, staging and restocking

Vacuum and mop all floors, then stage the home to match your listing photos: pillows, throws, remote controls, welcome notes. Then restock toilet paper, soap, coffee and other supplies against your list, and tell the host what is running low.

Hosts in Spartanburg often ask what separates a turnover from an ordinary house clean. The answer is the clock and the standard. A guest expects a hotel-level reset with a home feel, and the window is short. That is why a turnover crew works from a written list and does not improvise. Every visit follows the same order, and the host can compare one turnover to the next.

The photo report matters more than most hosts expect. If a guest breaks a lamp or leaves a stain, a dated photo from the turnover helps you report it to the platform. If a bath towel goes missing, the missing-item list tells you before the next guest arrives, not after the complaint. Some hosts use that report to decide when to replace linens or restock a supply.

Staging is the last step and the most overlooked. A rental that matches its listing photos feels right to a guest. That means pillows arranged the same way, remotes in the same spot, and a welcome note where guests expect it. If you send us your listing photos, the crew can stage to match, and you do not have to visit the property between guests.

For hosts who live out of town, a consistent crew solves the biggest problem: you cannot check the property yourself. A crew that knows the house and reports back on every visit becomes your eyes in Spartanburg.

How long does a turnover take?

Most turnovers fit in a few hours for a typical one- or two-bedroom rental, and larger homes take longer. We plan around your check-out and check-in times and tell you if a same-day turn needs more people.

One last tip for Spartanburg hosts: share the house rules and any special instructions with the team before the first turnover. Tell us where the spare linens are stored, how to run the laundry and which supplies to restock. A short written list posted in the utility closet saves a message later, and it keeps every cleaner who visits working the same way.

If your rental has a hot tub, a grill or an outdoor space, tell us now so the plan includes it. Guests notice the porch as quickly as the kitchen.

It helps to be honest about the calendar. Spartanburg hosts see their busiest stretches around graduation weekends, college move-in, holidays and any local events that fill hotels. During those weeks, check-outs and check-ins stack up and the cleaning window shrinks, so tell us your booking calendar early. The more notice we have, the better we can schedule a crew for each turnover, and the less likely it is that a same-day change leaves a guest waiting in the driveway.

Some hosts also ask about a deeper reset between seasons. A monthly or quarterly deep clean, with grout, window tracks, behind appliances and the inside of cabinets, keeps the property from wearing between ordinary turnovers. We can discuss that at the estimate and price it separately, so your regular turnover cost stays simple.
