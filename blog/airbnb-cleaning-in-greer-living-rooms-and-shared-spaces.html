What the living room looks like after a stay

Shared spaces take the most traffic and the least attention from guests. Some things we find again and again:

  • Crumbs, popcorn and pet hair pushed deep into sofa seams
  • Rings and sticky spots on coffee tables and end tables
  • Remote controls and game controllers with a film of hand oil
  • Fingerprints on the TV screen, glass tabletops and sliding door
  • Pillows and throws moved to bedrooms and never returned
  • Items left behind, from earbuds to children's toys

None of this is the guests' fault. It is simply what a lived-in room looks like after three or four days, and it all needs to be gone before the next check-in.

Our order of work in a shared space

1. Walk the room before touching anything

We look first. Damage, stains, a missing lamp shade or a wobbly chair gets a photo before we start, because a damage and missing-item report with photos is part of the service. Doing it first means the photo shows what the guest left, not what we disturbed.

2. Collect and remove

Cups, wrappers, stray dishes and trash go out first. Cushions come off the sofa so we can see what is underneath.

3. Dust high to low

Shelves, picture frames, TV stand, lamps and ceiling fan blades come before the floor, so nothing we knock loose lands on a clean surface. Microfiber is damp enough to pick up dust instead of moving it around.

4. Wipe the touch points

Remotes, light switches, door handles, the thermostat and the arms of chairs get wiped and sanitized. These are the spots a new guest handles in the first five minutes.

5. Floors last

Vacuum under the cushions and along the edge of the sofa, then vacuum and mop the room, finishing at the door so we do not walk back over a wet floor.

6. Stage to match the listing

Pillows go back where the photos show them, the throw is folded, the coffee table is styled the way you listed it, and the host-provided guidebook sits where guests will find it. A room that looks like your photos earns better reviews than one that is merely clean.

A few host habits that make the living room easier
