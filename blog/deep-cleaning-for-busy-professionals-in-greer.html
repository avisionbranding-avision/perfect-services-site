The one-day reset

Think of it as a reset, not a recurring cost. You book one long visit, a team works through the whole house, and you come home to a place that matches the day you moved in. After that, regular house cleaning is much easier to keep up, and for many of our clients that is the plan.

What to do before the team arrives

  1. Clear counters and floors of everyday items.
  2. Put away mail, laptops and anything you do not want moved.
  3. Leave a note about rooms to skip and surfaces to treat carefully.
  4. Arrange entry, and tell us about pets.
  5. Decide on add-ons: inside the oven, fridge or cabinets.

That is the whole job for you. The team does the rest and you can be at work.

Which rooms take the longest

Bathrooms, always. Grout lines and hard water spots are the slowest work in the house, and kitchen cabinet fronts come second, because grease and fingerprints collect near the stove. Window tracks and blinds are the jobs most people have never done at all.
