What is post-construction cleaning, and how is it different from a regular clean?

A regular visit maintains a home that is already clean. Post-construction cleaning removes what a building project leaves behind. The team handles fine construction dust on surfaces, ledges and trim, the inside of cabinets, drawers and closets, windows, glass, tracks and sills, paint specks, sticker residue and adhesive, light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans, bathrooms and new fixtures, and floors that are vacuumed and washed in multiple passes. The last step is a touch-up pass before you move in or start using the room.

Drywall sanding makes the finest dust, and it travels. After a remodel it lands inside closed cabinets and on top of door frames, which is why a quick wipe leaves it behind. Our post construction cleaning in Greer is built around getting that dust out of places a household cloth never reaches.

A typical sign you need it: you wipe a counter, and the cloth comes away gray. Another is a sticker still on a new window, or a line of paint along a glass edge that no household spray lifts. These details are slow, careful work, and they are the difference between a house that looks finished and one that only looks built.

What should I do before the cleaners arrive?

  • Ask the contractor to remove debris, scrap material and tools first. We clean, we do not haul.
  • Finish the punch list if you can. Cleaning before the last touch-up means dust returns.
  • Keep power and water on, since fixtures and appliances are cleaned and checked.
  • Cover or move anything you do not want in the work area.
  • Mention special surfaces, such as new stone or unsealed floors, so the team uses the right method.

It also helps to tell us how many phases you want. A rough clean after the messy work, a final clean after the trades leave and a touch-up can be booked together, and many first-timers choose that route.

Frequently asked questions

Does post-construction cleaning include hauling debris?

No. The contractor should remove scrap and trash first. The team cleans surfaces, glass, fixtures and floors after that.

Can I book rough and final cleans together?

Yes. The rough clean, final clean and touch-up can be booked together, which many first-time customers in Greer prefer.

Is a post-construction clean needed after a small bathroom remodel?

Usually yes. Even a small remodel creates fine dust that settles in vents, trim and drawers, and the estimate visit will show how much work remains.
