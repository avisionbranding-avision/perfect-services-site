Deep cleaning for landlords and property managers in Greer

A tenant who stays four years leaves a house that looks fine from the door and tells another story at the stove. Grease behind the range, grout that has gone gray, window tracks full of grit: none of it appears in a quick turnover, and all of it affects the next listing. For Greer landlords and property managers, a deep clean between long tenancies is how a unit goes back to a clean baseline. Here is how we handle deep cleaning in Greer for rentals.

When a rental is ready for a deep clean

Not every turnover needs one. We suggest it when:

  • The tenant stayed more than a year or two with no professional clean
  • The kitchen shows baked-on grease on the hood, backsplash or cabinet fronts
  • Bathroom grout and glass show hard-water or soap build-up
  • The unit had pets, smoking or heavy cooking
  • You plan to start recurring cleaning for the next tenant or for showings

If a unit was only lightly used, a regular clean may be enough. The estimate helps you decide.

Our scope for a turnover deep clean

A deep clean includes everything in a regular house cleaning, then goes further:

  • Baseboards and door frames hand-wiped
  • Cabinet fronts and handles degreased
  • Grout lines scrubbed in the kitchen and bathrooms
  • Ceiling fan blades, vents and light fixtures dusted
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds cleaned
  • Behind and around the toilet base
  • Appliance exteriors, range hood and backsplash

That is the plain-language scope, so your maintenance person and our crew are working from the same list. If you want the inside of the oven, fridge or cabinets too, ask for them at the estimate, since add-ons add time.

Document the unit before and after

Landlords and managers get the most from a deep clean when they treat it as a record. Take dated photos of each room before we start, and again afterward. Note damage that is not dirt: cracked tile, stained carpet, a torn screen. We clean, we do not repair, and a good photo set separates the two. It helps in a deposit dispute, and it gives your next tenant a unit that matches the listing.

Talk to the outgoing tenant early if you can. A written move-out cleaning standard in the lease, such as the oven, the fridge and the bathrooms, sets expectations and shortens the turnover. When a tenant leaves the unit clean, we may suggest a regular visit instead of a full deep clean, and we would rather tell you that at the estimate than sell you more than the unit needs.

A useful routine is a three-step walkthrough: you or your manager walk the unit with a phone and note the problem spots, we walk it at the free estimate and add what we see, and a final walk after the clean confirms the result. That way nobody is arguing over what the unit looked like.

Keep in mind that a deep clean is not a repair or a paint job. Stains that have soaked into carpet or grout may lighten but not disappear, and we tell you that at the estimate so you can plan for repairs or replacement before the next listing, not after it.

Add one more step for managers who work through a listing agent or a maintenance coordinator: share the cleaning date early, so painters, flooring crews and the cleaners do not collide. A deep clean belongs after repairs and paint and before the photos and the first showing. Done in that order, the unit looks its best in the listing and stays that way for the first walkthrough, instead of picking up fresh dust the day after the cleaners leave.

Deep clean first, then maintenance

For managers with several units, a good rhythm is a deep clean at each long-tenancy turnover and a lighter visit between showings. Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a rental needs a deep clean or a regular clean at turnover?

If the tenant stayed long, had pets, or the kitchen and bathrooms show build-up, choose deep cleaning. For light use, a regular clean may be enough. The free estimate decides.

Can you clean several units in the same week?

Often yes. Send us the addresses and your dates, and we will plan the visits and give an exact price per unit after the estimate.

Do you repair damage found during the clean?

No. We clean and we tell you what we notice, such as cracked tile or stained carpet, so you can document and handle repairs.
