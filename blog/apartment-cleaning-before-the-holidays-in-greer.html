Apartment cleaning before the holidays in Greer

Apartment cleaning before the holidays in Greer SC: a short plan for guests, deposit checks and decorating, and how the free in-home estimate sets your price.

Perfect Services Team
November 25, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • A small unit needs a different holiday plan: clear the clutter first, then clean the entry, kitchen and bathroom.
  • Plan the visit for 2 to 3 days before guests arrive so the place stays fresh.
  • Price is given after a free in-home estimate, and holiday weeks book quickly.

You have a family dinner on Thursday, your sister is sleeping on the sofa, and your Greer apartment has about 800 square feet to hold all of it. That is the situation many renters and condo owners face in December, and the cleaning plan for it is not the same as the weekly one. The goal is a place that looks calm in photos, feels fresh for a few days, and does not take the whole weekend to prepare. Here is how we think about apartment cleaning in Greer before the holidays.

Start with the order of operations

Most people clean first and then decorate, and that is backwards in a small space. Put up the tree, the lights and the extra chairs, then clean around them. Otherwise you pass the vacuum over a clean floor and then drag a stand and boxes across it. The team follows the same sequence when it visits: furniture and surfaces first, floors last.

  1. Clear clutter from counters and the coffee table. Surfaces cannot be cleaned under piles.
  2. Set aside the dishes and the pots you will use for the meal, so the kitchen visit can finish the sink and stovetop.
  3. Take out the recycling and trash so the cleaning day starts with empty bins.
  4. Confirm access: gate codes, parking and the time your building allows service.
  5. Leave a note if there are fragile or sentimental pieces that should not be moved.

What guests notice first

In an apartment, guests are in the entry, the bathroom and the kitchen within a minute of arriving. Those three spots deserve the most attention. The entry floor collects holiday mud and wet leaves, so a good wipe of the door, the handle and the first foot of floor makes a difference. The bathroom needs mirrors, the toilet, the sink and the shower glass finished, with the trash emptied and a fresh towel set out. The kitchen needs the sink empty, the counters clear and the stovetop wiped.

Beyond that, we pay attention to the things people only see when they sit down: the baseboards at sofa level, the legs of the dining table and the light switches near the dining area.

Timing the visit

If you book the cleaning the morning of the party, you risk a rushed finish and fresh cooking mess right after. Two or three days before is a better target for most people, because you can do a quick tidy on the day itself. If you have a holiday trip and are coming back to the unit, book the visit for the day before you return so you arrive to a clean place. Holiday weeks fill up quickly, so ask early.

How much does holiday apartment cleaning cost in Greer?

There is no honest way to give you a number without seeing the unit. Perfect Services gives prices only after a free in-home estimate, and it is a quick visit with no commitment. What matters:

  • Unit size, from a studio to three bedrooms.
  • The number of bathrooms.
  • Whether this is a single visit or part of a recurring schedule, which lowers the per-visit cost.
  • Pets, since hair and dander add time.
  • Access, including gate codes and parking.

A single holiday visit is a fine way to start, and many clients keep a monthly or biweekly schedule after seeing how it feels.

Gifts, boxes and the clutter problem

December adds packages, wrapping paper and gift bags to a space that was already full. A simple rule helps: give every incoming item a temporary home, such as one closet shelf or one corner, before the cleaning visit. Then the team can clean the rest of the floor properly instead of working around stacks. Flatten boxes and take them to recycling as they arrive, and keep the hallway clear so the crew can carry supplies in without squeezing past tree boxes.

Deposit and lease questions

Some people also use the holiday break to prepare for a move at the start of the year. If your lease ends in January, a standard visit is not the same as a move-out clean. For that, look at move-out cleaning in Greer, which covers the inside of appliances and cabinets that landlords check. Ask about it at the estimate and we can combine the plans.

Small spaces and shared walls

If you share a wall or a floor with neighbors, cleaning times matter. We schedule visits for daytime hours so vacuums and moving furniture do not become a complaint from the unit next door. In buildings with a limited parking lot or elevator booking, tell us in advance so the crew arrives with the right access. These small details are part of why we ask a few questions at the estimate before setting a date.

A short list for the last hour before guests

  • Wipe the bathroom sink and put out a hand towel.
  • Run a vacuum or broom over the entry.
  • Light a candle or open a window for five minutes if the day is mild.
  • Clear the kitchen counter by the door where coats and bags land.

Since the team already did the heavy work, this takes less than twenty minutes.
