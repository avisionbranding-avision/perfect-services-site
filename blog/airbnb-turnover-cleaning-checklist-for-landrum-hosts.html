Airbnb turnover cleaning checklist for Landrum hosts

The hardest hour of an Airbnb stay is the one between checkout and the next check-in. A host in Landrum, the foothills town off I-26 and US-176 with historic homes and horse country close by, often has a short window and a guest who finds every missed spot. A checklist that runs in the same order every turn is how you avoid reviews that mention hair in the shower.

Landrum (ZIP 29356) is in Spartanburg County, about 16 miles north of our Greer base. Many rentals here are cottages, farm stays and vacation homes whose owners live elsewhere, so the list below also works for remote hosts handing it to a cleaning team.

Start with the walk-through

Before touching anything, walk the property with your phone. Photograph damage, stains and anything missing. That one step settles most disputes and gives you evidence for platform claims. Strip beds and collect towels at the same time so laundry can start early.

The turnover checklist

  • Full kitchen reset: dishes, counters and appliances
  • Bathrooms cleaned and disinfected
  • Beds made with fresh linens, provided by the host
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash removed and bins reset
  • Supplies checked and restocked
  • Damage and missing-item notes with photos
  • Staging to match your listing photos

The order that saves time

  1. Open windows, start laundry and take out the trash.
  2. Kitchen: empty the dishwasher, wipe counters, appliances and the fridge shelf where guests leave food.
  3. Bathrooms: scrub top to bottom, then finish with the floor.
  4. Bedrooms: make beds, wipe nightstands, check under the bed.
  5. Living areas: dust, straighten and stage.
  6. Floors last, working toward the door.
  7. Final check against your listing photos.

What hosts often miss

The same few spots come up in rentals again and again: the inside of the microwave, the top of the refrigerator, hair on the shower wall, dust on nightstands and the track of a sliding door. A lived-in foothills cabin also collects pollen on porches in spring and leaves in fall, so entries need a sweep in addition to the interior reset.

Linens and laundry

Linens are the biggest time factor in any turnover. Hosts provide the sets, and we make the beds with fresh linens. If the property has a washer and dryer, laundry can run during the visit. If not, linens go off site and return, and the schedule has to allow for that. Keeping two or three sets per bed makes same-day turns much easier.

Restocking and reporting

A turnover checklist should include the small things: toilet paper, soap, trash liners, coffee and whatever else your listing promises. We note anything running low and report it so you can reorder. If something is broken or missing, you get a short report with photos, which helps with platform claims and with keeping the property in good shape for the next guest.

Seasonal notes for foothills rentals

In the fall, leaves collect on porches and in entries. In spring, pollen turns the porch yellow. Guests notice both before they even reach the door, so a quick sweep and wipe of the entry is part of staging the property.

Pricing a turnover fairly

Hosts sometimes expect one flat figure for every stay. In practice, a two-bedroom cottage after a weekend couple and a four-bedroom house after a family reunion are very different jobs. That is why we look at the property, ask about your usual guests and agree on a plan. It also lets us flag the extras, such as a hot tub deck, an outdoor kitchen or a pile of towels, before they become a surprise.

Keeping reviews strong

Cleanliness is the review category hosts lose the most points in, and the comments are almost always about small things: hair, dust on a shelf, a smell in the fridge. A consistent checklist, a staging photo to match and a short report after each turn catch most of them. Hosts who live out of town gain the most from this, since the report is their eyes on the property.

If a guest leaves early or an unexpected booking appears, tell us as soon as you can. Short notice is manageable when we know the property already, and the checklist stays the same whatever the timing.
