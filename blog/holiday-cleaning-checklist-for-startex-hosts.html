Holiday cleaning checklist for Startex hosts

Hosting Thanksgiving or Christmas in Startex, SC? Use this one time house cleaning checklist, timeline and booking tips to be ready before the family arrives.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Start with the rooms guests actually see: entry, kitchen, guest bathroom and the table area.
  • A one-time clean booked two or three weeks ahead beats a last-minute scramble the week of the holiday.
  • Prices come only after a free in-home estimate, never from a quick online number.

If the whole family is coming to your place this year, the cleaning list is longer than the cooking list. This holiday checklist is written for Startex hosts, from the mill village streets along US-29 to the newer homes closer to I-85, and it shows where one time house cleaning in Startex, SC saves the most hours.

Work backwards from the day guests arrive

The hosts who stay calm are the ones who treat cleaning like a countdown. Three weeks out, decide what a professional will handle and what you will do yourself. Two weeks out, declutter. One week out, the deep work is finished. The last two days are only for touch-ups, trash and the bathroom you will use the most.

The rooms guests judge first

People remember the entry, the guest bathroom and the kitchen. In older Startex homes the entry often has a small hall with a heavy front door and a lot of fingerprints around the knob and the glass. Wipe the door, the switch plate and the floor just inside, because that is where red clay and fall leaves come in on every shoe.

The guest bathroom comes next. Cleaning it well means the toilet base, the faucet base, the mirror edges and the floor behind the door. These are the spots a quick wipe skips and a visitor notices while standing there for two minutes.

A kitchen checklist for a holiday week

The kitchen does the hardest work in November and December, so it needs more than a counter wipe:

  • Degrease the stovetop and the wall behind it
  • Wipe cabinet handles and the fronts near the stove
  • Clean the microwave inside and the fridge door
  • Empty the fridge of anything past its date to make room for leftovers
  • Clean the sink, faucet and drain strainer
  • Mop under the table and along the cabinet base

If the oven has not been cleaned in a year, an inside-the-oven add-on before the big cook makes a real difference, since old grease smokes when you run it at high heat for hours.

Small things hosts forget

  • Window sills and tracks, where pollen and dust collected all fall
  • The ceiling fan above the dining table
  • The laundry area, since guests often use it
  • The garage door entry and mudroom
  • Trash cans, inside and out

What to do the day after

Hosting does not end when the last plate is cleared. The morning after a big meal, run the dishwasher, wipe the counters before the grease sets, and take the trash out before the bags sit in the garage. Because the heavy cleaning was done a week earlier, the cleanup is a short job instead of a weekend. Families who stay over also appreciate fresh towels and a quick wipe of the guest bathroom, so keep a small basket of supplies there for the week.

If you are hosting more than once this season, a Thanksgiving clean followed by a December top-up works well. Tell the team at the estimate that more than one date is possible, and they can plan the first visit to make the second one lighter. Some Startex homeowners also like to book the day after the last guests leave, to reset before the new year.
