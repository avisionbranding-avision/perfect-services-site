How we work through a deep cleaning

The team follows a fixed order so nothing gets cleaned twice. Ceilings, fans and vents come first, because that dust falls. Then window sills, tracks and blinds, then cabinet fronts and handles in the kitchen, where grease builds up. Bathrooms get the slowest work: grout lines, hard-water spots and the area behind the toilet. Baseboards and door frames are hand-wiped as we move room to room, and floors are done last.

It is detail work, which is where the Brazilian method of cleaning shows. We would rather spend an extra few minutes on a grout line than leave it for the next visit. Once the work is done, we walk the house with you and point out what we did in each room, so you know where the changes are. If you want to book this week, mention it when you contact us and we will tell you what is open.
