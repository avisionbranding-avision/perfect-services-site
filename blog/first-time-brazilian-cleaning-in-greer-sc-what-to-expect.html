First-time Brazilian cleaning in Greer, SC: what to expect

Booking Brazilian house cleaning in Greer, SC for the first time? Here is the method, the first visit step by step and how a free in-home estimate works.

Most people who call us about Brazilian house cleaning in Greer, SC have the same story: someone came, the house looked fine from the doorway, and a week later they found dust on the baseboards and grime behind the toilet. They are not asking for anything fancy. They want the corners done. This is what to expect from the first visit, laid out as the questions people ask us at the door.

What makes it "Brazilian"?

It is a method, not a product. Brazilian cleaning is detail-first: surfaces are hand-wiped instead of just sprayed, edges and baseboards are cleaned by hand, and small furniture and items are moved to clean under them. Many Brazilian homes are cleaned this way every week, and our team brought that habit to Upstate houses. You can read the full scope on the Greer Brazilian house cleaning page.

The result is not a different smell or a special spray. It is that the places most visits skip get touched every time.

Walking through the first visit

The team starts with a quick look at the house and your priorities, then works room by room. A typical first visit includes:

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not just sprayed
  • Corners, edges and baseboards cleaned by hand
  • Furniture and small items moved and cleaned under
  • Door frames, switches and handles detailed
  • Bathrooms scrubbed, including grout and the base of the toilet
  • Kitchen degreased: stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts
  • Floors hand-washed along the walls
  • A final walk-through against the room checklist

That last step matters. Before the team leaves, someone goes back through each room with the checklist in hand, so you do not discover a missed bathroom shelf on Thursday.

How to get your home ready

You do not need to scrub before we arrive. A short preparation helps the team spend its time cleaning instead of sorting:

  1. Put away jewelry, paperwork and anything fragile.
  2. Clear the counters and floors of loose items.
  3. Tell us which pets are home and where they should stay.
  4. Point out any surface that needs special care, like unsealed wood or natural stone.
  5. Leave a note about your top two priorities.

Pets are common in Greer households, and a calm dog behind a closed door is easier than a nervous one under our feet. Hair along baseboards and in corners is something this method handles well, since those spots are cleaned by hand.

Why the first visit takes longer

First visits usually run longer than later ones. The house has not had this level of detail before, so baseboards, door frames and the edges of floors carry weeks of buildup. After the first clean, the same list becomes upkeep and goes faster. Homes with hardwood, tile and grout benefit most, because those materials show missed spots quickly. In the Upstate, red clay tracked in during wet weeks settles in exactly those floor edges. If you need a heavier reset first, our deep cleaning service can be booked ahead of regular visits.

What the team checks in the first walkthrough

Before any estimate, someone walks the house with you. The things we look at are practical: what the floors are made of, how many bathrooms there are, whether the kitchen has heavy grease on the hood, and how the pets use the space. Hardwood in a dining room needs a different touch than tile in a laundry room. We also look at the spots most services skip, such as the track of a sliding door, cabinet handles and the tops of door frames, so we know how much detail the first clean needs. Ask as many questions as you like at this stage. Nothing is decided until you are comfortable with the plan, the frequency and the date, and the estimate carries no commitment.

This is also your chance to say what bothers you. If a bathroom never feels clean no matter what you do, show it. Often the cause is grout or a hard-water film, and knowing it ahead of time lets the team plan the right tools and time.
