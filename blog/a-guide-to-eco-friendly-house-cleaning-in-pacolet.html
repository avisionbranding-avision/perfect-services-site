What is different about a green clean

Three things change. The products are low-odor and family- and pet-friendly on request. The cloths are microfiber, which capture dust instead of pushing it around. And the technique leans on hand-wiping, which uses less product. For households that are sensitive to scent, we also offer a fragrance-free option. You can ask us to use your own products if you prefer a specific brand.

What does not change is the checklist. The team still goes room by room: kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, floors. Gentler does not mean skipping steps.

Where it makes the biggest difference

  • Nurseries and playrooms, where little hands go straight to the floor and then to the mouth.
  • Kitchens for pet owners, where food bowls and counters share a space.
  • Bathrooms for anyone with asthma or allergies, since strong fumes in a small closed room are the issue.
  • Bedrooms, where you breathe for eight hours straight.

Spring pollen and humid summers in the Upstate also mean dust and damp residue collect on window sills and bathroom corners. A microfiber cloth does a better job there than a spray that evaporates.

What to expect on the first visit

When we walk through a home before a green clean, we ask three questions. Is there a scent you want to avoid? Are there pets or babies at floor level? And are there surfaces, like old wood or natural stone, that need a milder product? The answers set the plan for the visit. Heavy build-up, like soap scum that has been there for a while, can take longer with gentler products. We say so up front and, if needed, plan a second pass rather than reaching for something harsh.

Keeping it going between visits

A few habits stretch a green clean. Keep a dry microfiber cloth near the kitchen and one in the bathroom, and wipe quickly after use. Take shoes off at the door, since outdoor dirt is what finishes a floor. Wash cloths separately from other laundry and skip fabric softener, which leaves a film on the fibers.

Questions to ask any green cleaning service

The word green is not regulated in a way most people expect, so ask directly. What products will you use in my bathroom and on my floors? Can I see the labels? Can you work fragrance-free? Do you use microfiber? Can I provide my own? A good cleaner answers these without hesitation and notes your answers for the next visit.

Ask also what the service does not do. Gentler products are not a promise to remove every old stain in one visit. If a bathroom has years of mineral buildup, a plan that spreads the work over two visits is honest and safer for the surface.

Why a mill-town home benefits

Older houses often have porous wood, original tile and painted trim that react badly to harsh sprays. Wiping by hand with a damp microfiber cloth is slower than spraying everything, but it protects those finishes. It is the same Brazilian detail-first method our team uses everywhere, applied with a lighter touch. We would rather explain this before the visit than surprise you with it afterward, and we write your product preferences down so every visit follows them.

If anyone in the house has asthma or eczema, mention it when you book. We cannot give medical advice, but we can leave out scented products, choose fragrance-free cleaners and keep the visit as light on the air as possible, which is usually what the family is hoping for.
