How often should you book Brazilian cleaning in Greer?

How often should you book Brazilian house cleaning in Greer? Compare weekly, every two weeks and monthly visits and see which rhythm fits your home and budget.

Perfect Services Team
November 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Every two weeks is the most common rhythm; weekly suits big families and pets, monthly suits quiet homes.
  • Recurring visits cost less per visit than one-time cleans and stay quicker because the detail work is already done.
  • The exact price comes after a free in-home estimate, with no commitment.

Say you hired a Brazilian cleaning team for the first time in Greer and the house looks the best it has in years. Now the real question: how soon do you book again? Most of our recurring clients settle on every two weeks. Some do weekly, some monthly, and a few start weekly for two months and then relax the schedule. The right choice depends on who lives in the house, not on how big it is.

The three common rhythms side by side

RhythmBest forWhat it feels like
WeeklyLarger families, kids, pets, people who host oftenThe house stays close to guest-ready all the time
Every two weeksMost households with a normal routineTidy and fresh, with a light reset each visit
MonthlyQuiet homes, couples, people who travelA thorough reset, with daily upkeep done by you

None of these is the wrong answer. What changes is how much the team has to do on each visit. With a longer gap, there is more to clean off the surfaces, in the bathrooms and along the floors. With a shorter gap, we spend our time on detail instead of catching up.

What the Brazilian method does to the schedule

Brazilian house cleaning is a hand-done, detail-first style: surfaces wiped by hand, baseboards and corners cleaned, door frames and switches detailed, floors washed along the walls, and a final walk-through against the room checklist. The first visit does the most work, because we clean to a baseline. After that, the detail is already in place, so later visits tend to feel lighter and the house holds its look longer. That is why a regular schedule often costs less per visit than a one-time clean, and why many homeowners find that every two weeks is enough once the first visit is done.

Which rhythm fits your household

Use the list below as a quick self-check. Count how many describe your home.

  • Two or more kids at home, or a pet that sheds: lean toward weekly.
  • Hardwood or tile floors with a lot of foot traffic near the garage or back door: lean toward weekly or every two weeks.
  • Two adults who work out of the house most days: every two weeks usually works.
  • You travel often or are away for long stretches: monthly may be enough.
  • Guests, dinners or family visits every weekend: weekly makes the week easier.
  • You like to clean the surfaces yourself and want the bathrooms and floors done: monthly or every two weeks.

In Greer, the season also matters. Spring pollen coats windowsills and floors, and humid summers show up in bathrooms. A lot of people go weekly for a few months in spring, then ease back to every two weeks. Tell us, and we will adjust the plan.

What changes with kids, pets and guests

Kitchens and bathrooms are where a longer gap shows first. Pet hair gathers along baseboards and under furniture, kids leave fingerprints on door frames and switches, and guests mean extra towels, extra dishes and more use of the guest bathroom. Our detail method touches all of those spots on each visit, which is why households with a lot going on tend to notice the benefit of a shorter gap. A quiet home with two adults can go longer between visits because there is simply less to catch up on.

Starting out: a simple plan

  1. Book the free estimate and tell us about pets, kids and floor types.
  2. Choose a first visit. If the home has not been professionally cleaned in a long time, we may suggest a deeper first visit.
  3. Set the schedule at weekly or every two weeks for the first two months.
  4. Review it after four to six visits and move up or down.

If you are not sure the first visit is worth it, the article on what to expect from a first Brazilian cleaning in Greer walks through the visit room by room. Many people also pair the first visit with deep cleaning, then keep it up with regular visits.

You can stretch the time between visits by making a few quick habits. Put shoes at the door, wipe the kitchen counters after dinner, and run the bathroom fan after showers. These do not replace a visit, but they make each one lighter. And if you have a busy season ahead, with holidays, guests or a move, add an extra visit and drop it again afterward. Frequently asked questions

Is every two weeks enough for a Greer home with kids and a dog?

For many households it is, but families with young kids and shedding pets often prefer weekly visits. We suggest starting weekly for a couple of months, then reviewing the schedule once you see how the home holds up.

Does the Brazilian method make later visits faster?

Often, yes. The first visit sets the baseline with the detail work on corners, baseboards and grout. Later visits tend to focus on keeping that baseline, so they can feel lighter, though the exact time depends on your home.

Can I skip a visit or switch from weekly to monthly?

Yes. Recurring schedules can be adjusted. Let us know before the visit when you are traveling or when your household routine changes, and we will update the plan going forward.
