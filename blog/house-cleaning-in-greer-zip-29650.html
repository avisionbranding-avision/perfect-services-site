What to do before the team arrives

A little preparation makes the first visit count. You do not need to scrub anything first, since that is our job, but these steps help the time go into cleaning.

  1. Clear the kitchen counters and the bathroom surfaces of loose items, so we can wipe under them.
  2. Pick up toys, laundry and papers from the floors.
  3. Tell us about anything fragile, any rooms you want skipped and where the pets will be.
  4. Leave a note about the spots that bother you most. Many people say the shower glass or the kitchen backsplash, and we make a point of starting there.

On the day, the team will not rearrange your things. Everything goes back where it was, and the bed is made at the end of the visit. If you spot something afterwards that was not what you expected, tell us, and we will come back to fix it.
