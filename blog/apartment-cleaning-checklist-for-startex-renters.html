The weekly checklist for a small space

In a small unit, mess shows up fast because everything is within eyesight. Keep the weekly pass short and in the same order.

  • Kitchen: wipe counters, rinse the sink, wipe the stovetop and the front of each appliance
  • Bathroom: clean the toilet, shower or tub, mirror and vanity
  • Dust shelves, tables and furniture, top to bottom
  • Make the bed and empty the trash
  • Sanitize light switches and handles
  • Vacuum, then mop the hard floors

An apartment in humid Upstate summers also needs one extra habit: leave the bathroom fan on after a shower and wipe the shower door once a week. The dampness shows up as film on glass and grout faster than most renters expect.

Small units also reward a quick reset habit. Ten minutes on Sunday evening, covering the sink, the trash and the bathroom mirror, keeps the weekly pass from turning into a long one.

The lease-end additions renters forget

This is where deposits get argued about. Move-out inspections often look at the same places, and they are the ones a weekly pass never touches.

  • Baseboards, wiped along their full length
  • Door frames and the tops of doors
  • Inside the oven and the microwave
  • The track of a sliding door and the glass of a balcony or patio door
  • The inside of cabinets and drawers
  • The edges of floors where the vacuum does not reach

Deep cleaning or move-out cleaning can be added when the unit is going back to the landlord.

Common mistakes we see in rental units

A few habits cause most of the trouble at inspection time. Renters wipe the stovetop but leave the grease on the wall behind it. They clean the toilet but not the base where it meets the floor. They vacuum the middle of the room and skip the edges, so a gray line stays along the baseboard. With a pet, hair gathers under the sofa and in door tracks, and a cat tree or dog bed leaves a mark on the floor that only shows once it is moved.

The fix is not more effort, just a different order. Start with the places no one looks at, do the visible ones last, and finish with a slow walk through the unit holding the checklist. If the unit has a balcony or patio door, wipe the glass from both sides, since fingerprints show in afternoon light.
