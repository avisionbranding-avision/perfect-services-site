The idea behind the method

In many Brazilian homes, cleaning is not a quick pass with a spray bottle. Surfaces are washed and wiped by hand, floors are cleaned along the walls instead of only in the middle of the room, and furniture and small items are moved to clean underneath. The method came from that habit, and it is why a home cleaned this way feels different the next morning: the edges are clean too.

What a visit includes

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not just sprayed
  • Corners, edges and baseboards cleaned by hand
  • Furniture and small items moved and cleaned under
  • Door frames, switches and handles detailed
  • Bathrooms scrubbed, including grout and the toilet base
  • Kitchen degreased: stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts
  • Floors hand-washed along the walls
  • A final walk-through against the room checklist

What a first visit looks like

The team arrives with the room checklist and works one room at a time. Kitchens and bathrooms get the longest time, because grease, grout and the base of the toilet are where a surface clean and a detail clean look different. Bedrooms and living areas follow, with furniture moved so the floor under it is cleaned too. The last step is a walk-through against the checklist, so nothing is left to memory.

Why it works well in foothills homes

Marietta's older homes often have hardwood, wood trim and deep window sills. Hand-wiping suits those materials better than heavy spraying, which can leave film or dull a finish. Newer homes have tile and grout, which benefit from the scrubbing. In both cases the visible result is the edge of the room: the line where floor meets wall, which is the first place a hurried clean falls short.

It is also worth saying what the method is not. It is not a gimmick, and it does not mean a different set of products. It means taking the time, in the same order, every visit.

Questions homeowners ask us

The most common question is whether the method is slower. It is, a little, on the first visit, since more is done by hand. The second is whether we use special products. We use standard, safe products, and the difference is in the technique. People also ask if they need to be home. You do not; many clients give a key or door code, and the team follows the same checklist each time.

Pets are welcome in the conversation, too. Tell us about them at the estimate so we can plan around feeding areas, litter boxes and shedding.

Starting small

If you are unsure, begin with one visit and judge it by the edges of the room. Look at the baseboards, the door frames and the corners of the bathroom. That is where the method shows, and it is usually enough to decide.
