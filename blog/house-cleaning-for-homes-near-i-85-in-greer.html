Does living near I-85 really change how a house gets dirty?

It changes where the dirt collects more than how much of it there is. Homes near a busy highway corridor tend to show fine dust on window sills, blind slats, vent covers and the tracks of sliding doors, because those are the places air moves through when a door or window opens. Add the red clay Greer is known for, which rides in on shoes and pet paws, and the entry floor takes most of the punishment.

None of that is unusual, and none of it needs a special product. It needs a team that checks those spots on every visit instead of only wiping the flat, obvious surfaces. That is the heart of the Brazilian detail-first method we use at Perfect Services, and it is why clients near the interstate keep the same crew: we work from the top of a room down, and we look at edges and corners before we call a room done.

What does a regular visit cover?

A standard house cleaning and maid service visit covers the rooms people use every day. Here is what the team does in a typical home:

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized, with appliance fronts and handles wiped
  • Bathrooms done fully: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted before any floor work starts
  • Floors vacuumed and then mopped by hand
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Light switches and other high-touch spots wiped
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation, so every room gets them regularly

The rotation matters. Rather than promising every baseboard every week, we move through the house so nothing goes untouched for months. If you want all of it at once, that is what deep cleaning is for, and we explain the difference below.

How often should a house near a highway be cleaned?

Most families we see in Greer settle on weekly or biweekly visits. Biweekly is the common choice for households without pets, and weekly makes sense when there are dogs, small children or someone with allergies. Monthly works if the house is already in good shape and you only want to reset it.
