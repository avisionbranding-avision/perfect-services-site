Office cleaning checklist for small businesses in Duncan

Office cleaning checklist for small businesses in Duncan, SC: daily, weekly and monthly tasks, who does what, and how the free walkthrough sets your price.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Split office cleaning into daily, weekly and monthly tasks so the work is predictable.
  • High-touch points and restrooms matter most to staff and clients.
  • Perfect Services prices commercial cleaning after a free in-person walkthrough of the space.

A small office in Duncan usually has the same problem: nobody owns the cleaning. The front desk wipes the counter, someone takes out the trash when the can is full, and the restroom gets attention only when a client is about to arrive. A written checklist fixes that, whether you clean the space yourself or hire a team. Here is the one we would hand to a small business along the I-85 corridor.

Daily tasks

These are the items that people see and touch every day. They take the least time and make the biggest difference to how the office feels.

  • Desks, counters and workstations wiped clear of crumbs and coffee rings
  • High-touch points: door handles, light switches, keypads and shared phones
  • Restrooms cleaned, disinfected and restocked with soap, paper and towels
  • Break room: sink, counters, microwave and refrigerator exterior
  • Trash and recycling emptied from every bin
  • Floors vacuumed or swept, with entry mats shaken out
  • Glass doors and the entry area wiped, where handprints show first

Weekly tasks

Weekly work catches what daily work does not reach:

  • Floors mopped, including under the desks and along the walls
  • Reception and waiting areas dusted: shelves, magazine racks, frames
  • Interior glass and partitions cleaned
  • Inside the microwave and a quick refrigerator purge
  • Conference table, chairs and whiteboards wiped

Monthly tasks

These are the jobs that vanish from a to-do list unless they are assigned a date:

  • Baseboards and door frames wiped
  • Vents and light fixtures dusted
  • Blinds and window sills cleaned
  • Upholstered seating vacuumed in the waiting area
  • Behind and under furniture that can be moved

What changes in Duncan

Duncan is a fast-growing town along I-85, US-29 and SC-290, with new subdivisions feeding small offices, clinics, contractors' shops and service businesses. Many of these buildings are newer, with carpet in the offices and tile in the entry. Carpet holds more dirt than people realize, and a mat at the door removes a surprising amount before it travels inside. After a rainy week, the floors in the first ten feet of the building are where the work is.

Restrooms: the part clients remember

Nobody writes a review about a clean desk, but people remember a restroom that was not. Check the toilet base, the floor along the wall, the soap dispenser and the underside of the sink, since those are the spots a quick pass skips. Refill paper goods before they run out rather than after a client notices. If the building has a fan, wipe its cover monthly, because the gray fuzz on it shows up in the light.

Break rooms deserve the same attention. A refrigerator with forgotten lunches from last month is the kind of detail that tells staff nobody is looking. A weekly purge on a set day, say Friday afternoon, takes five minutes and keeps the whole room from drifting.

How long it takes

A small office of a few rooms and one restroom is typically a couple of hours per visit once it is on a regular schedule. A larger one takes longer, and we put the time window in writing after the walkthrough so you can plan around staff and client hours.

Seasonal notes for Upstate offices

Spring pollen blows in on shoes and coats and settles on entry glass and windowsills, so door glass and sills need more frequent wiping from March through May. In summer the humidity makes restrooms and break rooms more prone to odors, and in winter salt and wet shoes make floors the priority. Adjust the weekly list with the seasons and the office stays ahead of it.

DIY or hire out

Some small offices clean themselves for a long time, and that is a perfectly good choice. It stops working when the owner is the one wiping the break room, or when the restrooms start to look tired. A recurring team solves the ownership problem: the checklist belongs to someone, and the work is done by hand, which is the Brazilian detail-first method that Perfect Services uses at home and at the office.
