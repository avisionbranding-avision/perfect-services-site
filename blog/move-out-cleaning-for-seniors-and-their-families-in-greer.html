Move-out cleaning for seniors and their families in Greer

Move out cleaning for seniors in Greer: how families handle a downsizing move, what the team cleans, how the free estimate works and how long it takes.

Perfect Services Team
November 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • A downsizing move leaves a house full of years of use, so the cleaning goes after the belongings are out, not before.
  • Adult children can book from a distance: the team visits for a free in-home estimate and the price comes after that visit.
  • Plan the cleaning for the last day or two, once the rooms are empty and the final walkthrough is close.

The moving truck leaves on a Friday, the closing is Monday, and the kitchen still has forty years of cabinet shelves behind the doors. That is the usual shape of a senior move in Greer: the belongings go quickly, and the house itself is what is left over. This guide is for the people who carry that last part, whether that is the homeowner or a son or daughter coordinating from Charlotte or Atlanta.

Who usually books a senior move-out clean, and why

Most calls we see for move out cleaning in Greer from older homeowners fall into three situations. A couple is selling the family house to move into something smaller. A parent is moving closer to an adult child. Or the family is clearing a home after a loss and needs it ready for the listing. In each case the house has usually been lived in for a long time, and long-lived homes carry things a short rental never does: grease that has built up behind the range, hard-water film on glass shower doors, and dust sitting on the tops of door frames that nobody has reached in years.

Climbing a stepladder to scrub a range hood or kneeling to wash the floor along the walls is exactly the work that is hard on the person who has to do it, and it is also what a buyer's agent or landlord looks at first. Handing that part to a team is not a luxury. It is the sensible way to finish a move.

What the team cleans after the furniture is gone

A move-out clean is built around empty rooms, so the order matters. We start where the dirt is heaviest and work toward the exit, so the floors are the last thing touched. This is what the move-in and move-out cleaning service covers:

  • Inside and outside of every kitchen cabinet and drawer, including the shelf liners that are still stuck down
  • Inside the refrigerator, freezer, oven and microwave
  • Range hood, stovetop and backsplash degreased
  • Bathrooms scrubbed: toilet base, grout, glass and vanity interiors
  • Closets, with shelves, rods and floors wiped
  • Baseboards, doors, door frames and light switches
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped edge to edge

The part families underestimate is the inside of the cabinets. A kitchen that has been in daily use for decades has crumbs, sticky rings and the occasional forgotten jar in every corner. Emptying and wiping those is slow, and it is the first thing a new owner opens.

How to split the work between family and cleaners

The cleaners should not be sorting belongings. That job belongs to the family, and it goes better when it is finished before the cleaning day. A simple split works well:

  1. Family clears every room completely, including the garage shelves, the attic access and the pantry.
  2. Anything staying with the house, such as curtain rods or a washer sold with the property, is labeled with tape so nobody has to guess.
  3. Trash and donation bags are out of the house before the team arrives.
  4. Someone confirms how the team will get in, which we arrange when we book the estimate.
  5. The cleaning is booked after the movers leave and before the final walkthrough.

If the homeowner would rather not be at the house on cleaning day, that is fine. Many families in Greer live out of town while a parent's home is being sold, and the walkthrough at the estimate is where the details are agreed.

How long it takes, and when to book

A typical empty three- or four-bedroom house takes a small team most of a day. A smaller home or condo is quicker, and a house with a lot of buildup or a finished basement takes longer. Greer is our home base, so the team is usually about ten minutes away, and that helps when a closing date moves. If you are booking this week, say so when you call, because the schedule fills around the end of the month and the start of the school year.

Details that matter in older Greer homes

Greer includes historic homes near Downtown Greer and Trade Street as well as newer neighborhoods near GSP Airport, and the cleaning is different in each. Older houses tend to have wood trim with many layers of paint, radiators or old vent covers, and tubs with stubborn ring marks. Newer homes have more glass and larger open kitchens. We look at which one we are standing in before we say how long it will take.

Red clay is another local detail. Years of it tracked in at the back door can stain the threshold and the first few feet of flooring, and it needs a slow hand rather than a quick mop. The same care applies to the tracks of sliding doors, which collect grit that looks clean until a finger runs through it.

Frequently asked questions

Can an adult child book the cleaning for a parent who is moving?

Yes. Many senior moves in Greer are arranged by a son or daughter. Book the free in-home estimate on the Greer page, call (864) 399-5168 or text (864) 666-8520, and tell us who will let the team in. The price is given after the visit.

Should the cleaning happen before or after the movers?

After. Move-out cleaning is built for empty rooms, so book it once the movers have left and before the final walkthrough or closing. Cleaning around furniture means rooms get done twice.

Do you clean inside the oven, fridge and cabinets in a long-lived home?

Yes. The inside of the oven, refrigerator, freezer, microwave and all kitchen cabinets and drawers are part of the move-out clean. Heavy grease takes more time, which is one of the things we look at during the estimate.
