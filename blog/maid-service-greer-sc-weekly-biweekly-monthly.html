Maid service in Greer: weekly, biweekly or monthly?

How to choose the right maid service frequency in Greer, SC. Compare weekly, biweekly and monthly house cleaning by lifestyle, pets, kids and budget with Perfect Services.

Perfect Services Team
October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Weekly maid service suits busy families, pets and kids; biweekly is the most popular balance; monthly fits small or low-traffic homes.
  • Recurring service costs less per visit and keeps the home at the same level every week.
  • Start with a deep clean, then pick a frequency that matches how your home is used.

Recurring maid service is the easiest way to stop spending weekends cleaning. The question is how often. Book too rarely and every visit feels like a deep clean; book too often and you pay for cleaning you do not need. Here is how Perfect Services helps clients in Greer, Taylors and Greenville choose the right frequency.

Weekly house cleaning

Best for: families with young kids, homes with pets that shed, people who host often and anyone who wants the house always guest-ready.

  • Floors stay free of pet hair, crumbs and tracked-in red clay.
  • Bathrooms and kitchens never build up soap scum or grease.
  • Lowest price per visit, because each visit is pure maintenance.

Biweekly house cleaning (every two weeks)

Best for: most households. It is the most popular choice among our Greer clients.

  • The home never falls far behind, and you handle quick tidying in between.
  • A good balance between budget and a consistently clean home.
  • Works well for couples, professionals and small families.

Monthly house cleaning

Best for: smaller homes, one or two people, homes without pets and people who keep up with daily cleaning themselves.

  • A monthly visit acts like a light deep clean: baseboards, bathrooms, kitchen detail.
  • Higher price per visit than weekly or biweekly, because there is more build-up.

What every Perfect Services maid visit includes

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, stovetop, appliance fronts, cabinet fronts spot-cleaned.
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, glass, mirrors and floors cleaned and disinfected.
  • Bedrooms and living areas: dusting, beds made, floors vacuumed and mopped.
  • High-touch points: switches, handles and door frames, part of our Brazilian cleaning method.

How to start recurring maid service

  1. Book a first deep clean so the home starts from a clean baseline. Not sure? Read deep cleaning vs. regular cleaning.
  2. Choose your frequency and a regular day and time.
  3. Tell us your priorities: rooms to focus on, products you prefer, pets, entry instructions.
  4. Adjust anytime. Many clients move from weekly to biweekly after a few months.

Benefits of recurring maid service beyond a clean house

  • Time back: most families spend several hours a week cleaning. Recurring service gives those weekends back.
  • Healthier air: regular dusting and vacuuming reduce dust, pollen and pet dander, which matters during the long Upstate allergy season.
  • Less stress before guests: the home is always close to guest-ready.
  • A team that knows your home: with a small local company like Perfect Services, the same people learn your preferences, your products and what matters most to you.

Maid service vs. doing it yourself

Doing it yourself costs nothing in cash, but it costs time and usually skips the detail work. Professional house cleaning follows a checklist every visit, so baseboards, door frames and bathroom grout never fall behind. Many clients split the work: they keep up with dishes and laundry, and Perfect Services handles the deep, physical cleaning.
