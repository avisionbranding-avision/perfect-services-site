Who gets the most out of it

The households that ask for green cleaning most often share a few things in common, and any one of them is reason enough.

  • Families with babies and young children who crawl on the floors
  • Pet owners, since animals lick paws and sleep on the surfaces we clean
  • Allergy and asthma sufferers who react to strong smells
  • Anyone in the house who is sensitive to fragrance

Pets deserve a special mention. A dog that sleeps on the sofa or a cat that walks across the kitchen counter is in constant contact with the surfaces we clean, so a lighter product residue is a real benefit. We also vacuum upholstery edges and pet beds on request, and we work around food bowls instead of moving them without asking. Small habits like these are what make the visit feel safe, not just clean.

What we do when buildup is heavy

This is the part people worry about most, so it gets its own answer.

Gentler products do not mean skipping the hard spots. Soap scum on a shower door, grease behind a stove and mineral spots on a faucet are the three we get asked about. We soften them with dwell time, work them with a non-scratch pad and finish with a hand wipe. It takes a little longer than a quick spray, and that is why the estimate includes a look at the bathrooms and kitchen. If a home is far overdue, we may suggest starting with a deep cleaning and then moving to green maintenance visits.

A short list to prepare

Because every household is different, a few minutes of preparation goes a long way. Think about what bothers your family most: a certain smell, a certain product, a surface that reacts badly. Tell us, and we will build the visit around it. We would rather hear about a sensitivity at the estimate than discover it halfway through the day.

  1. Tell us about any product allergies or sensitivities before the visit.
  2. Decide if you want our products, your own or fragrance-free only.
  3. Point out surfaces you want treated gently, such as natural stone.
  4. Let us know where pets will be during the cleaning.

Frequency is the other lever. A first green visit is usually the longest, because it resets the home. After that, a regular schedule keeps buildup from returning, and each visit gets faster and lighter on product. Ask about it at the estimate if you think you might want to continue.
