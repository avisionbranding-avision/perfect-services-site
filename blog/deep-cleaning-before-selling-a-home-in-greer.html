Deep cleaning before selling a home in Greer

Deep cleaning home sale Greer: when to book before listing photos and showings, which rooms buyers judge first, and how a free in-home estimate works.

Perfect Services Team
November 23, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Book the deep clean before listing photos, not after, so the pictures show the clean house.
  • Buyers judge kitchens, bathrooms, smell, windows and closets first.
  • Prices come only after a free in-home estimate.

Sellers in Greer often spend weeks on paint and landscaping and then forget the thing a buyer notices in the first thirty seconds: whether the house feels clean when the door opens. Photos make it worse, because a camera shows the grime on a baseboard and the film on a window that nobody has looked at in years. A deep clean timed correctly can be the cheapest part of getting a home ready to list, and Greer has a wide mix of homes where that matters, from older houses near Downtown Greer and Trade Street to newer neighborhoods close to GSP Airport and the BMW plant.

Book it before the photographer arrives

The order matters. Listing photos are the first showing, and they stay online for as long as the home is for sale. If you clean after the photos, the buyers see the dirty version first. So the sequence we recommend is: declutter, deep clean, photos, then showings. A short touch-up before open houses keeps it fresh after that.

If you are listing during the holiday stretch, plan around it. Buyers still tour in December, and winter light is flat and shows every smudge on glass. Book a few weeks ahead so the date is not squeezed against a photo session.

Smell is the part sellers cannot judge in their own homes, because they stop noticing it. Pets, cooking, a damp basement or a closed-up laundry room all leave something behind. A deep clean removes the sources: grease on the range hood, residue in sink drains, dust in the vents, and pet hair on stairs. Do not cover smell with strong air fresheners, since buyers often read that as hiding something. A clean, neutral house is what you want.

A pre-listing checklist

  • Remove personal items and extra furniture before the clean, so the team can reach everything.
  • Fix small things that cleaning will reveal, like cracked caulk or a loose handle.
  • Have the carpets looked at separately if they are stained, since stains need a different service.
  • Open closets and cabinets that you want cleaned inside.
  • Decide whether the oven and refrigerator interiors should be done.
  • Leave a clear path to all rooms, including the garage entry and laundry area.
  • Plan to be away or out of the way on the day of the clean.

How much does deep cleaning cost before selling in Greer?

Price follows the house, not the listing price. These factors decide the time:

  • Home size: square feet and number of rooms set the base time.
  • Condition: heavy build-up takes longer to remove.
  • Add-ons: inside the oven, refrigerator or cabinets adds time.
  • Bathrooms: grout and hard water spots are the slowest work.
  • Access: clutter-free rooms clean faster.

Plan for the days between the clean and the showings. If you cook every night and have pets, a few quick habits protect the work: wipe counters after dinner, keep shoes in a basket by the door, and run a vacuum over high-traffic floors before a showing. You do not need to repeat the deep clean; you only need to protect it for a couple of weeks.

Deep clean now, move-out clean later

A deep clean before listing is not the same as a clean at closing. The first prepares a lived-in home to show well. The second, a move-out cleaning, happens after you have left, so the buyer receives an empty home in good shape. Many sellers book both. If you are still living in the house during showings, a short touch-up visit before open houses is easier than a second deep clean.

Our deep cleaning in Greer uses the Brazilian detail-first method: the team walks the house first, works top to bottom, and finishes each room with a look from the doorway, the way a buyer would see it. Those last checks are where smudges on a mirror or a missed switch plate get caught. A Greer home sits in either Greenville or Spartanburg County, and the sale schedule moves quickly, so we try to fit visits into the same week where possible.

If a home sits empty while you relocate, dust returns faster than people expect, especially when the HVAC runs. In that case, plan the deep clean closer to the listing date and ask about a short touch-up before the first open house. Vacant homes also pick up a stale smell, so opening windows for a few minutes on a mild day before showings helps.

Working with your agent

Ask your agent for the photo date and the first showing date, then work backward. The clean should end at least a day before photos, so dust has not settled. Frequently asked questions

When should I schedule a deep clean before listing my Greer home?

Before the listing photos, ideally a day or two earlier. That way the photos show the clean home and dust has not had time to settle.

Should I also clean the inside of the oven and fridge?

Buyers do open them. We can include the oven and refrigerator interiors as add-ons, priced at the free in-home estimate.

Is a deep clean the same as a move-out clean?

No. A deep clean prepares a lived-in home to show well, while a move-out clean happens after you leave and before the buyer takes the keys.
