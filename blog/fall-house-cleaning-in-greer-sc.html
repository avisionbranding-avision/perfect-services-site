Fall house cleaning in Greer, SC

Fall house cleaning in Greer, SC: leaves, red clay and heating season, a late-November checklist, and how pricing works after a free in-home estimate.

Key takeaways

By the last week of November, the front door of a Greer house tells the story of the season. Leaves are stuck to the mat, red clay is dried into the grooves of the boots by the door, and the heat has just come on for the first time since spring. That mix is what makes fall cleaning different from the spring reset, and it is what this checklist is built around.

What fall brings into Greer homes

Three things show up in almost every home we clean this time of year. The first is leaves and dirt tracked in from yards, especially in neighborhoods with mature trees such as those near Downtown Greer and Trade Street. The second is red clay, the Upstate classic, which turns to a fine orange dust once it dries on tile and hardwood. The third is the heating system: the first weeks of heat blow settled dust out of the vents and across every flat surface.

A late-fall checklist, room by room

  • Entry: shake out the mat, sweep the threshold and wash the floor where boots land.
  • Vents and returns: wipe covers and vacuum the dust that gathered over the summer.
  • Windows and sills: clean the tracks, where leaves and grit collect.
  • Kitchen: degrease the stovetop and hood before holiday cooking starts.
  • Bathrooms: scrub grout and wipe fixtures before relatives use the guest bath.
  • Bedrooms: swap bedding, vacuum under beds and dust ceiling fans.
  • Baseboards and door frames: wipe the scuffs that show up in holiday photos.
  • Floors: vacuum and mop last, along the walls as well as the open areas.

Do it before the holidays, not after

Hosting changes how a house gets used. More people come through the entry, the kitchen runs for hours, and the guest bathroom suddenly matters. Cleaning two or three days ahead lets you enjoy the visit. If you wait until the week of, the cleaning competes with cooking and shopping. Because many people think the same way, the calendar fills up in the last weeks of the year. If you want to book house cleaning in Greer before the holidays, contacting us early helps.

What the team does differently in fall

When our team walks into a Greer home in November, the first look goes to the entry and the vents. We work top to bottom so that dust from the vents and fan blades lands on surfaces we have not cleaned yet, and floors always come last. The method is Brazilian and detail-first, which means hand-wiped surfaces, baseboards and door frames, and floors washed by hand along the edges. Homes near the BMW plant and GSP Airport tend to be newer, with larger open floor plans, and the same order of work applies there too.

Pets, boots and fall allergies

Fall also brings a change in how people live in the house. Pets spend more time inside as the temperature drops, so hair and dander build up on couches and along the baseboards. Kids come home with sports gear and school bags that land in the same few spots. Some people notice allergies flaring in the fall, with ragweed in the air outdoors and dust blowing from the vents indoors. A thorough vacuum with a good filter and wet wiping of surfaces take dust out of the house instead of moving it around.

If you want to keep the house in shape through the holidays, a simple routine helps. Keep a mat outside and one inside. Take shoes off at the door or keep a boot tray. Wipe the counters and handles daily, and let a recurring visit handle the details every week or two. That way the deep work happens on a schedule, and the days before guests arrive are only about touch-ups.
