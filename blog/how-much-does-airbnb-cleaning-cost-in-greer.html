The five things that set the price

FactorWhy it matters
Property sizeBedrooms and bathrooms drive most of the time. Bathrooms take longer per room than anything else.
Turnover windowA same-day turnover needs tighter scheduling and sometimes more than one person.
LinensLaundry on site means waiting for machines; off site means pickup and drop-off.

None of these is a surprise to anyone who has hosted for a season. What changes from host to host is the weight each one carries, which is why a walkthrough beats a calculator.

What a standard turnover should include

When you compare quotes, ask each company to say what the price covers. Ours includes the full kitchen reset (dishes, counters, appliances), bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, beds made with fresh linens you provide, floors vacuumed and mopped, trash removed and bins reset. We also do a restocking checklist for host supplies, a damage and missing-item report with photos, and staging to match your listing photos.

  • Does the price include laundry, or only making the bed with clean linens?
  • Is there a photo report at the end of each visit?
  • Who checks the supplies, and who buys what is missing?
  • What happens if a guest checks out late?
  • Is there a charge when the property is left in rough shape?
  • Does the same person or team come each time?

If a quote is cheaper because it leaves three of those out, you are paying for the rest in your own time.

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Greer, in practice?

Because we price after seeing the space, the practical answer is a process. You book the free estimate, we visit, we walk through the bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen and the laundry setup, and we ask how many turnovers you expect. Then you get a price in writing. Hosts are sometimes surprised that the estimate visit asks about guests as well as rooms. How many people stay, whether pets are allowed and how the house looks after a typical checkout change the time more than the square footage on the listing.

Why the cheapest turnover can cost the most

A low per-visit number looks good until you count what it does not include. Three costs hide behind a cheap turnover.

Missed items. If no one checks the supplies, you find out the toilet paper ran out when a guest messages you. A restocking checklist at each visit prevents that.

No evidence. A damage report with photos is how you handle a claim with the platform. Without it, you are relying on memory.

Rework. A turnover that misses the baseboards or the oven means a review comment, and the review costs more than the cleaning would have.

A good rule: judge a quote on what happens when something goes wrong, not on what happens when everything goes right.

How to budget across a month

Hosts in Greer tend to run into two patterns. Some have steady weekend bookings, so turnovers come in clusters. Others have scattered stays through the week. The number of turnovers per month is a factor in how we plan your property, and bookings that come in a predictable rhythm are easier to schedule than ones that arrive a day ahead.

If your rental is near I-85 or SC-14, you may get short notice from guests passing through. Tell us at the estimate visit how often you expect last-minute bookings. We can then agree on what the process is when a booking lands the day before.

It also helps to budget for a periodic deeper visit. Our guide on deep cleaning a Greer short-term rental covers when that makes sense. The routine turnover stays cheaper when the deeper work happens on schedule instead of piling up.
