Questions to ask before hiring move-out cleaning in Greer

Hiring move out cleaning in Greer, SC? Here are the questions to ask about deposits, ovens, closing dates and the free in-home estimate before you book.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Ask what is inside the oven, fridge, cabinets and closets before you compare quotes.
  • Ask who handles a missed spot after the landlord or buyer walk-through.
  • Pricing comes only after a free in-home estimate, never from a phone guess.

A landlord's walk-through takes about ten minutes, and in Greer it usually goes room by room with a flashlight and a lease in hand. Whoever you hire for move out cleaning has to survive that inspection, so the questions you ask before booking matter more than the brand name on the invoice. Here are the ones we would ask if we were the ones moving.

Question 1: What exactly is on the list?

"Move-out clean" means different things to different companies. Ask for the list in writing, or ask them to read it to you. A real move out cleaning service in Greer should cover the inside of every kitchen cabinet and drawer, the inside of the oven, fridge, freezer and microwave, the range hood, closet shelves and rods, window sills and tracks, door frames, and floors edge to edge. If the answer is "the usual", keep asking.

Here is a short version you can hold a company to:

  • Cabinets and drawers cleaned inside and out
  • Oven, fridge, freezer and microwave cleaned inside
  • Range hood and stovetop degreased
  • Bathroom grout, toilet base, glass and vanity interiors
  • Closet shelves, rods and floors
  • Baseboards, doors, frames and light switches
  • Window sills, tracks and blinds
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped to the walls

Question 2: Will someone look at the home first?

This is the question that separates a real estimate from a guess. A three-bedroom in the newer streets near the BMW plant that was lived in for two years is a different job from a rental near Trade Street that had a dog and a decade of grease on the stove. Anyone who names a number without seeing the space is either padding it or planning to renegotiate on the day.

Perfect Services offers a free in-home estimate. Someone visits, opens the oven, checks the shower glass and the tracks, and then gives you the price. There is no commitment. You can book the visit with the form on our Greer move-out cleaning page, call (864) 399-5168 or text (864) 666-8520.

Question 3: What happens if the landlord or buyer finds a spot?

Every company says it is thorough. Ask what the plan is when a property manager points at a dusty door track the next morning. A good answer is short: tell us within a day or two, and the team comes back and fixes it. A bad answer involves fine print about "reasonable standards". Since your deposit may be on the line, get the answer before you book, not after.

Question 4: Does the schedule fit your closing or lease date?

Greer has a steady stream of moves tied to the GSP Airport area jobs and the BMW plant, and lease endings cluster at the end of the month. Both mean the last days of a month fill up quickly. Ask how much notice they need, whether the home must be empty, and whether they can work around a closing at the end of the day.

Empty homes clean faster, because nobody is working around boxes. If you are still carrying things out, tell them. A larger team can sometimes take a tight timeline, and the estimate visit is the right moment to explain it. Booking early in the week you need the clean is realistic in many cases; booking the day of a closing is where things go wrong.

Question 5: Who is in my home?

Ask whether the people cleaning are employees or a rotating group of subcontractors, and whether the same team that estimated will do the work. Perfect Services is a Brazilian-owned local team, and the detail-first method we follow means every surface is hand-wiped rather than only sprayed, and baseboards, frames and handles get their own pass. That matters in an empty house, where there is nothing to hide the corners.

How much does move out cleaning cost in Greer?

Nobody can answer that honestly without seeing the home. The price depends on the size and number of rooms, how the last occupant left the place, how much baked-on grease the oven and hood carry, whether the house is empty or still furnished, and how tight the timeline is. We do not quote dollar amounts online or over the phone; the exact price comes after the free in-home estimate. If a company gives you a flat figure for "a house", ask what is left out of it.

A few things to settle before cleaning day

Make sure the water and power are still on, since a crew cannot mop without water or see inside a closet in a dark house. Leave keys or a lockbox code arranged. Take photos of the clean home once finished if you are a renter; they help if there is a dispute later. If you also need help with a place you are leaving after a long stay, a move-in and move-out cleaning visit can be paired with deep cleaning of heavily used rooms.
