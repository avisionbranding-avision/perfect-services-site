How much does office cleaning cost in Greer, SC?

How office cleaning is priced in Greer, SC: square feet, frequency, restrooms, hours and floors, plus how to compare quotes and get a free estimate.

Key takeaways

  • Square feet, frequency, restrooms, after-hours work and floor types decide the price.
  • Compare quotes by scope and schedule, not by one headline number.

Two offices in Greer can have the same square footage and get quotes that look nothing alike. One has a single restroom and mostly hard floor. The other has three restrooms, carpet throughout and a kitchen that gets used all day. If you are asking how much office cleaning costs, the useful answer is a list of the things that move the price, which is what follows.

We will not name a figure here, because a figure without seeing your space would be a guess. Perfect Services gives prices only after a free on-site estimate, and below is what we look at when we do one.

The five factors behind every office cleaning quote

  • Square feet. The total area to clean. More floor and more surfaces mean more time on site.
  • Frequency. Daily, weekly or monthly. A set schedule lets a crew plan the work and keep the space from building up grime, which is why recurring service usually costs less per visit than a one-time clean.
  • Restrooms. The number, and how much traffic they see. Restrooms are the most labor-intensive rooms in any office.
  • Hours. Cleaning during the day, after hours or on a weekend can change the staffing and the price.
  • Floor types. Carpet, tile and hard floors need different tools and different care.

How the same office can be quoted two ways

Take a hypothetical 2,000 square foot office. Quote A is a nightly clean with trash, restrooms and vacuuming. Quote B is a weekly clean that adds dusting, glass and a break room deep wipe. These are different services, and comparing them as if they were the same is the most common reason a quote seems high or low. The table shows where scope usually differs.

Questions to ask before you accept any quote

  1. Is the price per visit or per month?
  2. How many visits per week does it assume?
  3. Are restrooms disinfected and restocked, or just wiped?
  4. Does it include trash, recycling and liners?
  5. Who brings supplies and equipment?
  6. What is the process if something is missed?

Ask for the same list from every company you compare, and put the answers side by side. The cheapest line on a page is rarely the cheapest result if restrooms are skipped or floors are only vacuumed. A good answer to the last one is a quick re-clean of the missed item. Our approach is the detail-first method of Brazilian cleaning: hand-wiped surfaces, edges and corners, and a walk-through at the end of every visit.

Ways to get more from the same budget

Pick a schedule that matches how the office is used. A space with a full break room might benefit from three visits a week, while a quiet office might do well with one. Clear desks before the crew arrives. Let us know about the areas that matter most to clients, like the lobby. And consider adding a periodic deep cleaning on top of the regular schedule, rather than turning the weekly service into a bigger one.
