The short definition, and what the team does differently

Brazilian house cleaning is a detail-first approach where each room is worked from its edges toward its center, surfaces are wiped by hand, and the team checks the room against a written list before moving on. It is not a special product or a secret formula. It is order, patience and attention to small spots.

  • Corners, edges and baseboards are cleaned by hand, not just reached with a vacuum attachment.
  • Small furniture and items are moved, cleaned under and returned to place.
  • Door frames, switches and handles get their own pass.
  • Bathrooms include grout and the base of the toilet.
  • Kitchens are degreased: stovetop, range hood and cabinet fronts.
  • Floors are hand-washed along the walls, where mops usually leave a gray line.
  • A final walk-through compares every room to the checklist.

The order we work in

  1. Walkthrough with you, noting anything fragile or off limits.
  2. Bathrooms first, so products can sit while we work elsewhere.
  3. Kitchen, starting with the stovetop and hood so grease does not drip onto a finished counter.
  4. Dusting from high to low through the living areas and bedrooms.
  5. Floors last, ending at the door.
  6. Checklist inspection before we leave.

Who it is for, and when to start

Start by looking at your own home for the clues. Dust lines along baseboards, gray edges on floors, sticky cabinet handles, grout that never looks quite clean after a mop: these are the signs that a hurried method has been used. Many Taylors families tell us they simply want the same standard every time, without having to point out the same corner twice. That consistency is the real selling point, and it comes from the room checklist rather than from any single cleaner. People who tend to choose this method are homeowners disappointed by rushed services, families who want the corners done on every visit, and owners of homes with hardwood, tile and grout. If your house has not had a thorough clean in a while, a deep cleaning first can make recurring visits easier to keep up. Families with pets may also like green cleaning for product choice.
