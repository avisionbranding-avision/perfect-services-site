The deposit fight almost never starts in the living room. It starts with a greasy oven, a gray shower track or a fridge shelf nobody pulled out. If you rent in Greer and the keys are due soon, this page walks through what a landlord's walkthrough looks at and how move out cleaning for renters in Greer covers it.

What the walkthrough actually checks

Most leases in Greenville and Spartanburg counties say the unit must be returned "broom clean" or "in the condition received, normal wear excepted." Those phrases leave room for argument, and the argument is usually settled with a flashlight and a checklist. Walk the home the way a property manager would. Start in the kitchen, then bathrooms, then closets, then floors on the way out.

The spots that cost renters money tend to be the same ones, and we see them in apartments and townhomes from Downtown Greer out toward the GSP Airport area:

  • Inside the oven and under the stovetop drip pans
  • Range hood filter and the wall behind the stove
  • Refrigerator shelves, drawers and the rubber door seals
  • Shower tracks, grout lines and the base of the toilet
  • Baseboards and door frames, especially where furniture sat
  • Window sills and the tracks of sliding doors
  • Closet shelves and the floor under them

What our team does in an empty home

We work top to bottom so dust falls onto surfaces we have not cleaned yet. The kitchen gets the most time: cabinets inside and out, oven, microwave, freezer, stovetop and backsplash. Bathrooms come next, with grout, glass, vanity interiors and the toilet base. Then closets, baseboards, doors, frames, light switches, window sills and blinds. Floors are last, vacuumed and mopped edge to edge so we are not walking dirt back over finished rooms.

This is the Brazilian detail-first method applied to an empty unit: hands and cloths on the corners instead of a quick spray. It is also why the team asks you to leave the rooms empty and the utilities on, so water and power are available.

One habit saves renters more trouble than any product: take dated photos before you start. Shoot every room once it is empty, plus close-ups of the oven interior, the shower, the inside of the fridge and any existing marks on walls or floors. If the landlord later disagrees about condition, those pictures settle it faster than memory. Keep your move-in inspection sheet too, since it shows what was already worn when you arrived. Normal wear, such as light carpet traffic patterns or faded paint, is a different category from damage or dirt, and knowing the difference helps in a conversation with a property manager.

A common mistake is cleaning too early. Renters wipe the kitchen on Monday, then cook, pack and carry boxes through it until Friday, and the work is undone. The sensible order is to pack everything, move out, then clean the empty rooms once. The only exception is the fridge and pantry, which are worth emptying a day or two earlier so food does not complicate move day.

Frequently asked questions

Will move out cleaning guarantee my full deposit back in Greer?

No cleaner can promise that, because the landlord makes the decision. A thorough clean built around what inspectors check, plus photos of the empty home, gives you the best position.

Should I clean before or after the movers leave?

After. Cleaning an empty home is faster and nothing gets tracked back in by boxes and furniture.

Do you clean inside the oven and fridge?

Yes. Inside the oven, refrigerator, freezer and microwave are part of the move-out checklist, though heavy grease can add time.
