First hour: remove and rescue

  • Open windows for fresh air and run the kitchen fan
  • Collect trash, cups, bottles and paper plates in large bags
  • Gather dishes and glasses; soak anything with dried food
  • Check couches, chairs and floors for spills and treat stains right away
  • Find and return borrowed items, and secure anything fragile

Handle stains first

Fresh stains come out much more easily than set ones. Blot, do not rub. For red wine or juice on fabric, blot with cold water and a little mild dish soap. For grease, lightly cover with baking soda, let it sit and then vacuum. Always test a cleaner on a hidden spot first, and follow the care label on upholstery and rugs. If a stain does not lift on the first try, stop and try again later rather than scrubbing harder.

Kitchen reset

  • Run the dishwasher and hand-wash large pots and platters
  • Wipe counters, the stovetop and the sink
  • Clean the microwave if used for reheating
  • Empty the refrigerator of leftovers and old items, then wipe shelves
  • Wipe cabinet fronts and handles, which often get sticky
  • Mop the floor last

Bathrooms and living areas

Guest bathrooms take heavy use during a party. Scrub the toilet and sink, wipe the mirror, replace towels and empty trash. In living areas, dust surfaces, wipe tables and coffee tables, vacuum floors and furniture, and check corners and behind cushions for crumbs and stray items.

Planning ahead for the next gathering

A little preparation before the party makes the cleanup much shorter. Set up clearly labeled trash and recycling bins, put out a damp cloth and a roll of paper towels for quick spills and use washable table covers for food areas. Move rugs and light-colored throws out of the way, and keep a bottle of stain remover where you can reach it quickly. If you are hosting a large group, consider disposable plates for part of the event and a designated spot for coats and bags.

  • Clean the guest bathroom thoroughly the day before, then touch up
  • Stock extra toilet paper, hand soap and trash bags
  • Run the dishwasher before guests arrive so it is ready to load
  • Close doors to rooms you do not want used
