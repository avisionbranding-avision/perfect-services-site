Airbnb turnover cleaning checklist for Easley hosts

Airbnb turnover cleaning checklist for Easley hosts: what to reset between guests, how to handle linens and restocking, and how to get a free estimate.

Key takeaways

  • Work the same room order every turnover so nothing gets skipped under a tight check-in window.
  • Linens, restocking and a damage check matter as much as the bathrooms.
  • Pricing for turnovers is set after a free in-home estimate based on size, window, linens and frequency.

An Airbnb turnover in Easley has a clock on it: the last guest leaves at 11, the next one checks in at 4, and the review that follows depends on what the bathroom looks like at 4:05. This checklist is the order we use when we reset a short-term rental, written so a host can run it or hand it to a cleaner.

Start with a walk-through, not a spray bottle

Before any cleaning, spend five minutes looking. Open every cabinet door you would expect a guest to open, check under the bed, and glance at the closet floor. You are looking for damage, missing items and anything left behind. Photograph problems right then, because the report you send the host the same day is only useful if it is specific.

Then strip the beds and start the laundry. Linens take the longest of any task, so they go in first and run while everything else is cleaned.

The turnover checklist, in order

  • Strip beds, start the first laundry load, collect used towels
  • Empty trash and recycling, reset bins with fresh liners
  • Kitchen: dishes, counters, sink, stovetop, microwave, fridge shelves
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirror, vanity, floor, restock paper and soap
  • Dust surfaces top to bottom, including lamp shades and the TV stand
  • Vacuum, then mop hard floors, finishing at the door
  • Make beds with fresh linens, set towels and pillows to match your listing photos
  • Final walk-through against your restocking list, then lock up

The reason for working top to bottom, and back of the unit to the front door, is simple: dust falls, and you do not want footprints on a freshly mopped floor.

Where Easley rentals tend to need extra attention

Easley is a growing Pickens County city, with established neighborhoods and plenty of new subdivisions along US-123 and SC-93. Guests who stay here often drive in from I-85 or the Clemson side, bringing in pollen in spring and leaves in fall. A back door track, a mudroom floor or a screened porch can need a second look. If guests bring pets, expect hair along the baseboards and under the couch, and check the sofa cushions for it.

Kitchens in rentals get used differently from a family kitchen. Guests cook one big meal, leave the pan to soak, and move on. The stovetop and the inside of the microwave are the two places that most often get missed under a time limit.

Linens, laundry and the restocking list

Whether laundry happens on site or off site changes both your timeline and your cost. On-site washing means the cleaner stays longer. Off-site means a spare set of linens for every bed so the swap is quick. Either way, linens come from the host.

Keep a short restocking list taped inside a cabinet: toilet paper, hand soap, dish soap, coffee supplies, trash bags. A cleaner can check it against what is on the shelf and tell you what is running low before a guest notices.

Staging to match the listing

Guests judge a rental against the photos they booked from. That means the throw pillows, the folded towels and the position of the chairs matter more than they would in your own home. Keep one reference photo per room on your phone and set the room back to it at the end of every turnover. It takes a minute and it saves you the review that says the place looked different from the pictures.

The same goes for small comforts: a clean welcome mat, a trash can with a fresh liner, a bathroom that smells like nothing at all. Guests rarely mention what is right, but they always mention what is missing.

How long a turnover takes, and booking this week

A one- or two-bedroom turnover is often a few hours with a full reset, and a larger house takes longer. A deep clean between busy seasons will take more time than a standard turnover. If a new listing opens soon, ask for the estimate early this week, since weekend check-ins fill the calendar first.
