What is Brazilian house cleaning? A guide for Landrum homeowners

What is Brazilian house cleaning? A guide for Landrum, SC homeowners: the hand-detail method, what is covered and how the free in-home estimate works.

Perfect Services Team
November 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Brazilian house cleaning means every surface is hand-wiped and corners are done by hand each visit.
  • It suits older homes with hardwood, tile and grout and anyone tired of rushed cleans.
  • Prices come after a free in-home estimate, never before.

If you ask a Brazilian cleaner what makes the method different, the answer is usually a single word: hand. In a typical rushed visit, a surface is sprayed and wiped once and the corners are skipped. In Brazilian house cleaning, the team works the house the way many families in Brazil clean their own: every surface wiped by hand, floors washed along the walls and detail checked against a room list. Landrum homeowners, with their older homes and historic character, often notice the difference in the first visit.

What the method actually is

It is not a secret product or a style of mop. It is an order of work and a standard of attention. The team starts at the top of a room and finishes at the floor, moves small items and cleans under them, and finishes each room against a checklist before moving on. The final walk-through makes sure nothing was left unchecked.

What gets done by hand

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not just sprayed
  • Corners, edges and baseboards cleaned by hand
  • Furniture and small items moved and cleaned under
  • Door frames, switches and handles detailed
  • Bathrooms scrubbed, including grout and the toilet base
  • Kitchen degreased: stovetop, hood and cabinet fronts
  • Floors hand-washed along the walls

Why it fits Landrum homes

Landrum is a foothills town along I-26 and US-176 in Spartanburg County, known for historic homes and horse country nearby. Older homes tend to have hardwood, tile and grout, and plenty of trim, all of which collect dust at the edges. Houses near horse country also see more dust and red clay carried in on boots. A hand-detail clean gets into the places a quick pass leaves behind. It is a good fit for families disappointed by rushed cleaning services.

The biggest difference people notice is not any single task, it is the consistency. Because the order of work is the same every visit, the house comes out the same way every visit. The team does not decide in the moment which corner to skip. It checks the room against a list. That is also why homeowners who return to the same crew each week see the house improve over time, as detail work such as baseboards and door frames gets done on a rotation.

The method also takes into account how the house is used. With a dog or children, the team pays more attention to floors, doorways and the bottom of upholstered furniture. In kitchens, the emphasis is on grease: the stovetop, the hood and the cabinet fronts nearest the stove, where a film builds quietly. In bathrooms, grout and the base of the toilet get hand scrubbing, since those are the places where a quick spray does the least.

Landrum's older homes and the rural properties nearby can also bring surprises: wide baseboards, deep window sills, wood floors with gaps where dust settles. Hand work reaches these in a way a machine pass does not. If your house has features like that, mention them at the estimate so we can plan the time.

How long does a visit take?

Because the detail level is higher, a Brazilian clean takes a team longer than a quick pass over the same house, and the first visit takes the longest. Your estimate gives you the time. Ask for a slot this week and we will work to fit one.

Finally, a note about expectations. A Brazilian clean is thorough, but it is not magic. A house with years of build-up will not look new after one visit, and we say so at the estimate. What you can expect is steady progress: after the first visit, the house is at a clean baseline, and every visit after that keeps it there while the detail work rotates through baseboards, door frames, cabinet fronts and the track of each sliding door.

That honesty is part of the method. We would rather show you the house and explain the plan than promise something we cannot deliver. If you would like a look at how it works in a home near Landrum, the free estimate is the best way to start.
