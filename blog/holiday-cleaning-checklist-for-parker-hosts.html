Holiday cleaning checklist for Parker hosts

Hosting family in Parker SC this season? A week-by-week holiday cleaning checklist and how one time house cleaning fits in. Book a free in-home estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Spread the work over three weeks, and book the detail-heavy rooms for a professional one-time clean.
  • Holiday weeks fill up fast, so book the free in-home estimate before the second week of the month.
  • Price depends on home size, condition, add-ons, date and pets, and it is given only after the free estimate.

Thanksgiving is a few weeks off, and in Parker that often means older mill-neighborhood homes with smaller kitchens, a full house of relatives, and one oven doing a very large job. Guests will notice the guest bath, the kitchen and the entry long before they notice anything else. This checklist shows what to do yourself, what to leave for a one-time house cleaning in Parker, SC, and how to line it up so the cleaning is finished before the cooking starts.

Three weeks out: the things that take time

This is the stretch for jobs you can do in pieces without ruining a weekend.

  • Clear the guest room closet and the surfaces in the guest bathroom
  • Wash bedding and the throws guests will use
  • Declutter the entry and hall, where coats and shoes will pile up
  • Run a self-cleaning cycle on the oven, or plan to have it done by a cleaner
  • Empty the refrigerator of anything past date to make room for holiday food
  • Wash the front door glass and check the doormat

None of this is exciting, but it removes most of the stress from the final week.

One week out: the deep work

This is where a professional one-time clean pays for itself. The part of a house that takes the longest is not the floors. It is the baseboards, door frames, cabinet fronts, inside of the oven and the grime on the kitchen backsplash. Our team works room by room, in a fixed order: kitchen first, then bathrooms, then bedrooms and living areas, with floors last. That way nothing gets walked over after it is done.

A one-time visit covers full kitchen cleaning and degreasing, bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, bedrooms and living areas dusted, floors vacuumed and mopped, baseboards and door frames, mirrors and glass polished, and the trash emptied. Add-ons include inside the oven, the fridge or the cabinets, which are the three requests we hear most before a big dinner.

How much does a one-time cleaning cost in Parker?

The honest answer is that it depends on the house. A small older home and a larger two-story house are not the same amount of work. The factors are home size (rooms and square feet), how long it has been since the last professional clean, add-ons such as the oven, fridge, cabinets or windows, the date you pick (holiday weeks book quickly), and whether pets add hair and dander. What the team looks at during the walkthrough

Older homes like many of the mill-area houses on the west side of Greenville have their own quirks: smaller closets, original trim with more edges to dust, and kitchens where every inch of counter matters on the big day. We plan the visit around those details instead of a generic floor plan.

At the estimate we walk the house with you and look at the same spots every time. We check the kitchen first, because that is where grease and build-up show up. We look at the bathrooms for hard water on the glass and grout, at the baseboards in the main halls, and at the floors to see whether they are carpet, hardwood or tile and how each one is treated. If you have a pet, we ask where it sleeps. If someone has allergies, we can talk about fragrance-free options and our green cleaning service.

The walkthrough also lets you tell us what matters most. If your mother-in-law always checks the top of the refrigerator, tell us. We would rather know than guess.

Think about the guest bathroom as well. It is the room every visitor sees and every host forgets until the last minute. A fresh toilet, a wiped mirror, clean towels and a spare roll of paper in sight take care of most of it. If you only do one extra thing the day before guests arrive, do that one.

A one-time clean takes longer than a regular visit because it is a full reset. A small home may be done in the first part of a day, while a larger house with add-ons takes most of one.
