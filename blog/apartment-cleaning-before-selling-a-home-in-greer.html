Apartment cleaning before selling a home in Greer is about making a small place read as light, clear and cared for, and it is a different job from a standard apartment and condo cleaning in Greer visit, because the standard is higher and the schedule is tied to showings.

Why smaller homes are harder to show

In a large house, a dusty corner in a back bedroom gets lost. In a two-bedroom condo, the whole home is visible from the front door. The balcony slider is right there, the kitchen is within three steps of the sofa, and the bathroom is usually the only one. A fingerprint on the stainless steel is seen by every visitor.

That is why we spend more time per square foot on sale prep. A good clean makes the room look larger, because light moves across clean glass and bare counters. It also helps a buyer's agent, who usually has a short window to make a case for the unit.

Steps in order: a room-by-room plan

  1. Declutter first. Put away personal items, extra small appliances and papers. The cleaning team should not have to decide what to keep.
  2. Start high. Dust ceiling corners, vents and light fixtures, then shelves and door frames.
  3. Kitchen. Degrease the stovetop and the range hood filter, wipe cabinet fronts and handles, clean the sink and shine the appliance fronts.
  4. Bathroom. Scrub the tub or shower, the glass, the grout and the vanity, then polish the mirror and fixtures.
  5. Bedrooms and living area. Wipe baseboards, dust furniture, clean mirrors, and make beds so the rooms look finished.
  6. Balcony or patio door. Clean the glass on both sides and the track.
  7. Floors last. Vacuum and mop, working toward the door.

What buyers and agents notice first

  • Smudged stainless steel and a greasy range hood.
  • Soap scum on the shower glass and discolored grout lines.
  • Dust along baseboards and on door frames.
  • Streaky patio door glass and a dirty track.
  • Light switch plates and cabinet handles with hand marks.
  • Pet smells, especially around entry mats and bedroom corners.

Fitting the clean around showings

Ideally the main clean happens after the unit is decluttered and before the photographer arrives. If you are still living there, schedule it for a day you are out. A condo owner who lists in November, with holidays coming, might want a second, lighter visit before the first weekend of showings. If you moved out already, ask about adding move-out cleaning after the furniture is gone, since empty rooms show every mark on the floor and walls. For a long vacant unit, a deep cleaning before the listing photos is usually the better first step.
