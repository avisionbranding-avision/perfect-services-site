Summer house cleaning in Greer: heat, humidity and dust

How Upstate heat and humidity affect your home and what to clean in summer: a Greer, SC guide to bathrooms, floors, AC vents and a plan to book ahead.

Perfect Services Team
November 23, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Humidity makes bathrooms, window sills and closets the summer trouble spots.
  • Open doors and AC filters bring dust indoors, so vents and floors need more attention.
  • Prices come only after a free in-home estimate.

July in the Upstate does two things to a house: it makes everything damp, and it makes everyone open and close doors a lot. Bathrooms grow mildew faster, floors collect grit from yards, and AC vents push dust into rooms that were clean last week. Summer is eight months away from today, but the best time to set a summer cleaning plan is before the busy season, so here is how we think about it for Greer homes.

Where summer humidity shows up first

We see the same few spots in homes across Greer every year once the weather turns humid.

  • Shower corners, caulk lines and grout, where mildew starts as gray or pink film.
  • Bathroom exhaust fan covers, which fill with dust and slow down.
  • Window sills and tracks, where condensation gathers dust.
  • Closets and the back of dressers on exterior walls.
  • The inside of trash cans and the kitchen sink drain area.
  • Pet bowls and dog beds, which hold smell in warm weather.

Heat, dust and air conditioning

Greer summers also mean pollen leftovers, cut grass and the occasional storm that blows grit against the house, so floors and sills see more dirt than in winter. When the AC runs most of the day, dust moves through vents on a loop. Wiping vent covers and ceiling fan blades helps, and so does vacuuming the floor beneath them. Change your filter on the schedule your system calls for. Dust that settles on furniture in summer is usually dust that came out of the vents.

What the team looks at on a summer visit

On a humid-season visit we pay extra attention to the places moisture and dust meet: the tub surround, the sink overflow, the rim of the toilet, the shelf under the bathroom mirror and the sills of any window that fogs in the morning. In the kitchen we check the area behind the faucet and the rubber seal on the fridge door, since both hold dampness. Bedrooms get a look under the bed and along the wall behind the headboard. It is a short list, but it is where summer cleaning is won or lost, and we mention anything unusual so you can act on it.

Planning ahead, step by step

Even in November, a short plan now saves a scramble in June. Most people wait until the first week of real heat, find the bathroom fan caked with dust and the shower corners dark, and then try to fit everything into a single Saturday. Here is the calmer way to do it.

  1. Book a free estimate in winter and choose between weekly and biweekly visits.
  2. Have the first deeper clean done in early spring so summer can be a maintenance season.
  3. Tell the team about pets, allergies or rooms with moisture trouble.
  4. Check windows, vents and closets in the first warm week and add whatever you find to the list.
  5. Keep a basic daily routine: wipe the bathroom mirror and counter, run the fan, put shoes away.

One more point about summer: mud and grass come in with kids and dogs, and the entry floor takes the beating. Keep a mat outside and a mat inside, and vacuum the mat as often as the floor. When we clean here, we wash floors by hand along the walls, which removes the gray edge that a quick mop leaves. In humid weather that edge is damp and holds dirt even longer. Our house cleaning in Greer team also checks the bathroom fan cover and the shower caulk on each visit and tells you if it spots early mildew, so you can deal with it before it needs a deeper treatment. If your home has a basement or a closet on an exterior wall, run a small dehumidifier there, since a closed damp space is where odors start. And keep summer towels moving through the wash, because a towel that stays damp on a hook picks up smell within a day or two. These habits cost little and save you a surprise in August.

Frequently asked questions

Why do bathrooms get dirtier in the Upstate summer?

High humidity keeps surfaces damp, so mildew and soap film build faster. Running the exhaust fan and wiping the shower after use slows it down.

Should I clean vents before summer?

Yes, wipe vent covers and ceiling fans before heavy AC season and change filters on schedule, since dust moves through the air system.

Can I book summer cleaning now?
