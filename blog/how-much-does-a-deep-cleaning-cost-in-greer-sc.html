What is a deep cleaning?

A deep cleaning goes beyond a regular maid visit. It spends time on the places that build up between professional cleans and that a weekly routine does not cover. For a full list, see the deep cleaning service page. Typical items include:

  • Baseboards, door frames and window sills wiped by hand
  • Inside the oven, microwave and refrigerator when requested
  • Scrubbing grout, tub and shower glass
  • Cabinet fronts, handles and switch plates
  • Ceiling fans, vents and light fixtures
  • Detailed floor edges and corners

Is deep cleaning worth it?

For many homes, yes, especially at the right moments. A deep clean is a reset. After it, regular maid service takes less time and holds up better. It is worth considering:

  • Before starting recurring cleaning for the first time
  • In spring, when pollen and red clay have built up
  • Before or after hosting during the holidays
  • After illness, a new baby or a long stretch of busy weeks
  • Before listing or after buying a home

It is not necessary every month. Many households do one or two a year and keep up with regular house cleaning in between.

How long does it take?

A deep clean typically takes about double the time of a standard visit, since it covers extra tasks. A team of two or three can often finish a mid-sized home in half a day to a day, depending on condition. Larger homes or homes with heavy buildup may need more time.

How to compare quotes

Ask every company the same questions: what is included, how many people come, how long they plan to stay and whether the price is fixed. A low number with a vague scope is not a deal. How to get the most from your deep clean

A few simple steps make the visit more effective and can even lower the time needed:

  • Clear counters and floors of clutter so the team can reach every surface
  • Point out problem areas, such as a stained shower or a greasy range hood
  • Decide ahead of time whether you want the oven and refrigerator included
  • Plan to be out for a few hours if you can, especially with pets

Greer homes in particular tend to collect red clay near entries, pollen in spring and humidity-related grime in bathrooms during summer. A deep clean before the holidays or after pollen season is a common time to book, and following it with a maintenance schedule helps the results last.
