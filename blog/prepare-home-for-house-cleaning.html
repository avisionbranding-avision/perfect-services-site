How to prepare your home for a professional house cleaning

How to get ready for your first professional house cleaning in Greer, SC: what to pick up, what to tell your cleaners, pets, access and valuables. Tips from Perfect Services.

Perfect Services Team
October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Pick up clutter so cleaning time goes to cleaning, not organizing.
  • Tell your cleaners about priorities, fragile items, pets and access before the visit.
  • You do not need to pre-clean. That is what you are paying for.

Booking your first professional house cleaning is exciting, and many people wonder: should I clean before the cleaners come? The short answer is no. But a few minutes of preparation lets the Perfect Services team spend every minute on the details that matter most to you.

1. Pick up clutter, do not pre-clean

  • Put away toys, clothes, mail and dishes so surfaces and floors are reachable.
  • Clear kitchen counters as much as you can.
  • Leave the dirt. Scrubbing, dusting and floors are our job.

2. Tell us your priorities

Every home has a room that matters most: the kitchen before guests, the bathrooms, the playroom floor. Write it in the estimate form or text us. We will start there.

3. Secure valuables and fragile items

  • Put away jewelry, cash and important documents.
  • Let us know about antiques, special finishes or items you prefer we do not touch.
  • Tell us about natural stone counters, unsealed wood or delicate floors.

4. Plan for pets

Many clients have pets, and that is fine. Let us know in advance if a pet should stay in a room or crate, and if there are areas with heavy shedding. Pet hair is one of the reasons people choose weekly maid service.

5. Access and parking

  • Leave a key, lockbox or door code if you will not be home.
  • Share gate codes for neighborhoods or apartment complexes.
  • Tell us where to park.

6. Supplies

Perfect Services brings the products and equipment. If you prefer a specific product, fragrance-free options or have allergies, tell us before the visit.

What to expect on the first visit

The first cleaning usually takes longer, especially if it is a deep clean. We follow a room-by-room checklist based on the Brazilian cleaning method, so baseboards, switches and corners are included. After the visit, walk through and tell us what you love and what you want adjusted. That feedback shapes every visit after.

Quick pre-cleaning checklist

  1. Clutter off floors and counters.
  2. Dishes in the sink or dishwasher.
  3. Valuables put away.
  4. Pets planned for.
  5. Access and parking shared.
  6. Priorities sent to the team.

Mistakes to avoid before your first house cleaning

  • Spending the morning cleaning. You are paying for it. Save your energy.
  • Not mentioning problem areas. If the shower glass has hard water spots or the oven needs attention, tell us in advance so we plan time for it.
  • Forgetting about access. A locked gate or a missing code delays the visit.
  • Leaving laundry piles on beds. We cannot change sheets or vacuum around piles easily.

After the cleaning: keep it clean longer

  • Use a squeegee on shower glass after each use.
  • Keep two door mats to stop dirt and Upstate red clay at the entry.
  • Wipe the kitchen counters every night.
  • Run a quick vacuum in high-traffic areas mid-week.
  • Book recurring maid service so the home never falls far behind.

Preparing for a move-out cleaning

For a move-out or move-in cleaning, the rule is different: the home should be empty. Remove all furniture, boxes and personal items so we can clean inside closets, cabinets and appliances. See the full move-out cleaning checklist for Greer renters.
