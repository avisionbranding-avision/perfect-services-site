What to do yourself first

The cleaning team cleans an empty home. Anything still sitting on counters or shelves slows the work and can be missed. Run through this short list before the visit.

  • Move all furniture, boxes and trash out of the rooms, or at least away from the walls.
  • Empty the refrigerator and freezer and throw out what is left in the pantry.
  • Take down curtains and anything hanging on closet rods.
  • Remove nail-hole fillers or wall decorations that are yours to handle, since paint touch-ups are not part of cleaning.
  • Keep power and water on until the clean is finished, because the oven, dishwasher and fixtures are checked as they are cleaned.
  • Tell us about any lease or closing checklist. We would rather match a written list than guess.

What happens on cleaning day

Teams clean from the top down and the back to the front, so dust never lands on a surface that is already finished. In a typical move-out the order looks like this:

  1. Ceiling fans, light switches, door frames and window sills, plus blinds and window tracks.
  2. Closets: shelves, rods and floors.
  3. The kitchen, which takes the longest: cabinets inside and out, drawers, the inside of the refrigerator, freezer, oven and microwave, then the range hood, stovetop and backsplash.
  4. Bathrooms: toilet base, grout, glass and the inside of vanities.
  5. Baseboards and doors.
  6. Floors last, vacuumed and mopped edge to edge, so the team does not walk over a finished floor.

That is the Brazilian detail-first method in plain terms. We look at the places an inspector opens or bends down to check: the track of a sliding door, the back of a cabinet door, the base of a toilet. First-timers are often surprised that the cleaners spend so much time on spots they never thought about.

Two things surprise people in Greer homes in particular. Red clay tracked in over years settles into floor edges and door thresholds, and it takes patient work along the baseboards rather than a quick pass with the mop. In older homes near Downtown Greer, painted trim and window tracks collect layers of dust that only show when the room is empty and the light hits from the side. We point these out at the estimate, so nothing comes as a surprise on the day.

Another common question is whether the cleaning replaces your own effort entirely. It does not need to. Most renters do the quick things, such as taking out trash and removing personal items, and leave the scrubbing to the team. That split keeps the job focused on what a professional crew does faster and better.
