Fall one-time cleaning in Greer, SC: clean before you decorate

Fall one-time cleaning in Greer SC: why to clean before the holiday decorations go up, what a single visit covers and how our free in-home estimate works.

Perfect Services Team
November 24, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Cleaning before holiday decorations go up saves you from working around boxes, lights and a tree.
  • A one-time visit can cover the kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting and details you rarely reach.
  • Perfect Services gives prices only after a free in-home estimate.

Most Greer families pick one weekend in late November to haul the decorations up from the attic. The house gets filled with boxes, the tree goes up in the corner and nobody wants to clean around any of it. The smarter order is the opposite: clean first, decorate second. A one-time cleaning in Greer before the season starts means the dust under the sideboard and behind the couch is gone before the tree covers it up for six weeks.

Why clean before the decorations

Think about what stays put in December. The tree sits on one spot of the floor for a month. The mantel carries garland and candles. The dining table is set for guests that you cannot move. Anything you skip now stays skipped until January. This is also the time of year when the house is closed up, the heat is running and the air is dry, so dust settles quickly on surfaces you just decorated. A clean start keeps the next six weeks easier.

What a one-time visit covers in a fall home

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, stovetop, appliance fronts, cabinet fronts and a degreasing pass.
  • Bathrooms: shower, tub, toilet, vanity, mirror and floors, plus the guest bath that holds the towels.
  • Bedrooms and living areas: dusting from the top down, including ceiling fans and frames.
  • Floors: vacuumed and mopped, with edges and under the furniture you can easily move.
  • Baseboards, door frames, mirrors and glass.
  • Optional add-ons: inside the oven, the fridge or cabinets, ideal if you will cook a big meal.

The order we work in

People ask what happens in the first hour. The team starts with a walkthrough so everyone knows which rooms matter most, then opens the kitchen and bathrooms because those need product time to work. While the product sits, the crew dusts in the bedrooms and living areas from high surfaces down. The floors come last, starting in the farthest room and ending at the front door. In fall that last pass matters because leaves and clay come in with every visitor.

Fall dust and what hides it

Some areas show the season. Window sills collect a gray ring from cool glass and warm indoor air. Heating vents blow out the dust that sat there since spring. And the entry floor holds a line of grit from leaves, mud and mulch. If you have pets, the couch and the stair carpet hold a lot of hair. We vacuum upholstered edges and the stair treads where it is practical. If your house has a fireplace, tell us before the visit so we can handle the hearth area.

A short pre-visit list

  1. Clear counters and tables of papers, mail and kids' projects.
  2. Put away what is fragile or valuable.
  3. Decide which add-ons you want, such as the oven.
  4. Tell us about pets, gate codes and parking.
  5. Leave the decorations boxes in the garage until we are done.

The rooms guests actually see

If the budget or the time is tight, the visit can focus where it counts. In most Greer homes that means the entry, the kitchen, the guest bathroom and the room where people will sit. The bedrooms matter if someone is staying over, but the family room and dining table are where holiday guests spend their time. We can plan the visit around those rooms at the estimate and add the rest if you want.

Think too about the day after. A host who knows the kitchen will be a disaster after a big meal can book a second, smaller visit for the days after the holiday. It is a common pattern in late November: one clean to start the season and one to reset when the guests leave. We can look at the calendar together and decide whether one visit is enough.

What stays the same in every home

Whether the house is a historic home near Downtown Greer or a newer place near GSP Airport, the checklist is the same. Our crew works in a fixed order so nothing is skipped, and ends with a look at corners, door frames and handles. That is the detail work people notice a week later, long after the vacuum lines have gone. Tell us what you expect to see and we will tell you honestly what a single visit can and cannot do.

Frequently asked questions

Should I clean before or after putting up holiday decorations?

Before. The tree, garland and boxes get in the way, and dust under and behind them stays until January. A one-time clean first keeps the season easier.

Can the team clean around a Christmas tree?

Yes, but it is faster and more thorough if the tree is not up yet. Tell us at the free in-home estimate what will be in place.

Is a one-time cleaning enough before guests arrive?

For most homes, yes. The visit covers kitchen, bathrooms, floors and dusting, and you can add the oven or fridge if you will cook for a crowd.
