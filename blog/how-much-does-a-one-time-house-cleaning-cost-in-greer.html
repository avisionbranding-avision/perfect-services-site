How much does a one-time house cleaning cost in Greer?

One time cleaning cost Greer homeowners ask about: how home size, condition, add-ons and the calendar shape a price, and why we quote after a free estimate.

Key takeaways

  • A one-time price depends on home size, how long since the last professional clean, add-ons, the date and pets.
  • The more time since the last clean, the longer the visit, so two same-size homes can have different prices.
  • Perfect Services gives prices only after a free in-home estimate; call (864) 399-5168 or text (864) 666-8520.

Ask three people in Greer what a one-time house cleaning costs and you will hear three answers, and they may all be true. A home cleaned six weeks ago and a home that has not seen a professional in two years are different jobs, even if the floor plan is identical. That is the reason a good cleaner will not give you a figure over the phone. This article explains what drives the price so that the estimate, when it comes, makes sense.

Perfect Services is a Brazilian-owned cleaning company based in Greer. We do not publish a price list. Prices come after a free in-home estimate, a short visit with no commitment. If you are wondering how a one-time clean compares with recurring cleaning, we cover it near the end.

The five things we look at

FactorWhat it means for the visit
Home sizeRooms and square feet set the baseline time.
ConditionTime since the last professional clean. Build-up in the kitchen and bathrooms takes longer to remove.
Add-onsInside the oven, fridge, cabinets or windows each add work.
DateHoliday weeks book quickly, so availability matters.
PetsHair and dander add time on floors and soft furniture.

The first and the second are the ones that surprise people. We have seen smaller homes take longer than larger ones because of what had accumulated in the kitchen and the shower.

What the base visit includes

The base visit covers the work most people picture when they say "cleaned": full kitchen cleaning and degreasing, bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, bedrooms and living areas dusted, floors vacuumed and mopped, baseboards and door frames, mirrors and glass polished, and the trash emptied. The inside of the oven, fridge and cabinets are optional extras. If a quote you receive does not list what is covered, ask for it in writing.

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, stovetop, appliance fronts, degreasing
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, vanity and mirror
  • Bedrooms and living areas: dusting, furniture, surfaces
  • Floors: vacuumed and mopped
  • Details: baseboards, door frames, glass
  • Optional: oven interior, fridge, cabinets

Why the estimate is free and in person

A phone call cannot tell us how high the shower grout is stained or whether the floors are tile, hardwood or carpet. Photos are no better, because they do not show the corners. A visit does. The team member walks the home, usually in about the time it takes to have a coffee, asks about pets and add-ons, and notes what you care about most. Then you receive a price.

It has no cost and no obligation. If the number does not suit you, you can say so. You can book through the form on our one-time cleaning page for Greer, by calling (864) 399-5168 or by texting (864) 666-8520.

What to have ready for the estimate visit

You do not need to clean before the estimate. It helps to have a few things in mind so the visit is short and the price is accurate.

  • The date you want, or the event it is for, so we can check the calendar.
  • Which add-ons you are considering: oven, fridge, cabinets or windows.
  • Any pets in the home and where they stay during a visit.
  • The rooms that matter most, or any you want left alone.
  • How we get in: you at home, a key or a gate code.

With that, the walkthrough takes little time, and you leave it knowing exactly what the price covers. Our one-time house cleaning in Greer team will confirm everything in writing.

How long it takes. It varies with the size and condition of the home, and we do not promise a duration before seeing it. Expect more time for a first visit than for later ones, because there is more to lift. Greer is our home base, with homes along SC-14, near GSP Airport and around downtown, so we can usually offer a visit within the week you ask for. Holiday weeks fill up first, so book early for those.

One-time or recurring: which costs less over time?

A one-time clean is a reset. Recurring visits cost less per visit because the home never gets to the stage that needs a heavy reset. If you only need help before an event, one-time is the right fit. If you are tired of catching up, ask for a recurring estimate after the first visit.
