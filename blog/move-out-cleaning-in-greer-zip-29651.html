Two days out: empty what can be emptied

Cleaners cannot clean inside a cabinet that is still full. The best thing you can do two days ahead is clear the spaces that hide grime.

  • Pantry and kitchen cabinets: empty them completely, including the back corners.
  • Refrigerator and freezer: toss or move the food, and take out loose shelves if you can.
  • Closets: shelves, rods and floors need to be visible.
  • Garage or storage nooks that are part of the lease or sale.
  • Wall hangings, curtain rods and anything you plan to patch. Nail holes are a repair job, not a cleaning job.

One day out: the walkthrough and the crew

The day before handover is when a cleaning crew does its best work, because the furniture is gone and the floors are open. The order matters. We start high and finish low: ceiling fans, light fixtures and vents first, then walls and door frames, then cabinets, appliances and bathrooms, and floors last so nothing gets walked over after it is done.
