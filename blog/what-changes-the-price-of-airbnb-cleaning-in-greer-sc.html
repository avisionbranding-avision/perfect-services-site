What changes the price of Airbnb cleaning in Greer, SC

Airbnb cleaning cost in Greer, SC comes down to size, turnover window, laundry, restocking and volume. See what moves the price and how the estimate works.

Key takeaways

  • Laundry on site versus off site and the length of the turnover window move the price more than most hosts expect.
  • A steady monthly volume usually makes scheduling easier for both sides.

Two Airbnb listings in Greer can both say "two bedrooms, one bath" and still be very different jobs. One is a tidy condo where guests strip the beds and take out the trash. The other has a garage fridge, three sets of towels per guest and a checkout at noon with check-in at two. How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Greer?

We do not publish a number, and we do not give one over the phone or from photos. Perfect Services gives an exact price after a free in-home estimate: a short visit where someone walks the property, checks the layout and the laundry setup, and asks how your guests usually leave the place. There is no commitment. The reason is simple. The factors below stack on top of each other, and the combination is different for every listing.

The five things that move the price

1. Property size and layout

Bedrooms and bathrooms set the baseline, and bathrooms count for more than bedrooms because they take the most hands-on time. A glass shower, a double vanity or a second tub all add minutes. So do stairs, a sunroom or a finished basement that guests actually use. Square footage matters less than how many surfaces the team has to touch in every room.

2. The turnover window

A turnover with a full day between checkout and arrival can be done calmly by a small crew. A same-day turnover with a two-hour gap usually needs more people to finish at once, and that shows up in scheduling and cost. Many Greer rentals sit close to GSP Airport and the BMW plant, which means back-to-back stays are common. If your calendar is often tight, tell us at the estimate so the plan is built around it.

3. Laundry

Beds are made with host-provided linens, so the real question is who washes them. If the crew can run a load in your machines while they clean, the laundry fits inside the visit. If sheets and towels have to leave the property, or the machines are slow, the visit changes. Some hosts use a linen service and some prefer on-site laundry. Either is workable, but the choice belongs in the estimate.

4. Restocking

A restocking checklist is part of the service: the team checks soap, paper goods, coffee and the other items you provide against your list. A short list takes a minute or two. A long one, with welcome baskets, kids' items and a pet bowl setup, takes longer. The more you want checked and refilled, the more time the visit needs.

5. Volume per month

A listing that turns over a handful of times a month is a different arrangement from one that turns over almost daily. Regular volume helps us plan crews and keep the same people on your property. It does not mean a discount is guaranteed, but it does mean the schedule can be built properly instead of squeezed in.

What stays the same in every estimate

The work itself does not shrink to fit a budget. Every turnover includes the kitchen reset, bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, beds made, floors vacuumed and mopped, trash removed, the restock check, a damage and missing-item report with photos and staging to match your listing photos. If a quote leaves out the damage report or the restock check, it may look lower because the job is smaller.

  • Ask what is included in writing.
  • Ask whether laundry is part of the visit or billed separately.
  • Ask how a late checkout is handled.
  • Ask who sends the damage photos and how quickly.

Ways hosts keep turnovers predictable

Most of what you can control happens before the crew arrives. Keep supplies in one labeled spot so the restock check is fast. Leave a clear checkout time in the house rules and in the listing. Tell us about delicate surfaces, such as a soft marble counter or an unsealed wood table, because those need different products. If you live out of town, share the lockbox or smart lock process early. These small things shorten the visit, and a shorter visit is easier to price fairly.
