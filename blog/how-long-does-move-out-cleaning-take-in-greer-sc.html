How long does move-out cleaning take in Greer, SC?

How long does move out cleaning take in Greer SC? See what sets the time, how the crew works room by room and how to plan around a closing or lease date.

Perfect Services Team
November 13, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Time depends on home size, kitchen condition and whether the home is empty, not a fixed number.
  • A bigger crew shortens the day without lowering the standard.
  • Price and schedule both come from the free in-home estimate.

There is no honest single answer to "how long does move out cleaning take in Greer," because the clock depends on the home, not the calendar. An empty one-bedroom apartment that was kept tidy is a short visit. A large house with a greasy oven and years of buildup in the bathrooms is a much longer one, and we handle that by adding people to the crew rather than stretching the job over several days. Here is what actually moves the time, and how to plan around it.

What sets the length of a move-out clean

When a Perfect Services team member walks a Greer home for the free estimate, these are the things being weighed. They are the same things that decide how many hours the crew needs on the day.

What the team looks atEffect on time
Rooms and square feetMore rooms means more baseboards, closets and floor
Kitchen conditionCabinets inside and out, oven, refrigerator, hood and backsplash take the longest
BathroomsGrout, glass and vanity interiors need scrubbing, not just wiping

Crew sizeA larger team shortens the clock, it does not change the standard

The kitchen alone often decides whether a job feels quick or slow. The inside of the oven, the refrigerator and freezer, and the range hood are included in move out cleaning in Greer, and heavy grease adds time.

How the day runs, in order

We do not clean room by room at random. The sequence is built to avoid redoing work:

  • Start high: fans, light switches, door frames, window sills, tracks and blinds.
  • Move to closets and cabinets, because the debris they hold falls to the floor.
  • Spend the middle of the day on the kitchen and bathrooms, the two heaviest rooms.
  • Finish with baseboards, doors and then floors, vacuumed and mopped edge to edge, so nobody walks over wet work.

Floors are last for a practical reason. Once they are done, the crew leaves through a clean path and the home is ready to inspect.

One more point we explain at almost every walkthrough: a quicker clean is not a lighter clean. If a landlord or buyer will open cabinets, run a hand along the baseboards and check the tub corners, the crew needs the time to do those spots properly. We would rather add a person to the team than skip the back of a drawer. That is why the estimate comes first. Seeing the home lets us decide the crew size honestly, instead of promising a time that the house cannot support.

Families moving into a new home sometimes ask the same question for the move-in side. The timeline is similar, and often a bit shorter, since the previous owner usually left the place fairly tidy, but we still wipe cabinets and shelves before you unpack.

How to shorten the job before we arrive

You control more of the timeline than you might think. A few steps save real time on the day:

  1. Be fully moved out before the cleaners arrive. Boxes in the corners hide baseboards.
  2. Empty the refrigerator, freezer and pantry, and defrost if needed.
  3. Run the dishwasher and washer empty ahead of time, so the inside is not full of residue.
  4. Tell us about stuck-on spills or heavy grease, so we send enough people.
  5. Make sure power and water are on.

Greer homes bring one local factor. Red clay dust and pollen settle into window tracks and door thresholds, so those spots can take longer in homes near open land or construction. Renters in apartments off US-29 or SC-14 tend to see a shorter timeline than owners clearing out a large house, but only because there are fewer rooms, not because the standard is lower.

Frequently asked questions

Can move-out cleaning be done in one day in Greer?

Usually yes. Larger or heavily soiled homes get a bigger crew so the work still finishes on the scheduled day, which helps with closing and lease deadlines.

Should the cleaning happen before or after the movers?

After. Once the last box is out, the crew can clean top to bottom and finish with the floors without anyone tracking dirt back in.

Does a dirty oven make the clean take longer?

Yes. The inside of the oven is included, but heavy grease adds time, and we note it at the free estimate so the crew is sized correctly.
