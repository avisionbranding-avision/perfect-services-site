Apartment cleaning in Greer: living rooms and shared spaces

Apartment cleaning living room Greer renters and condo owners trust: how we clean small shared spaces, balcony doors and floors in Greer, SC.

Key takeaways

In a one- or two-bedroom apartment, the living room does a lot of jobs. It is the TV room, the dining table, the laptop desk and the place everyone drops their keys. That is why apartment cleaning in the living room works differently in Greer than a big family room in a house: less square footage, more stuff, and a lot less space to move things around.

Here is how our team approaches a living area in an apartment or condo, and a short routine you can use between visits.

The small-space problem

We clean units around Greer in all kinds of layouts, from studios to three-bedroom condos. The living room usually has a pattern: a sofa pushed against a wall, a TV on a stand with cables behind it, a coffee table covered with mail and a balcony or patio door that gets fingerprints from every angle. Dust collects on the top of the TV, the window blinds and the cable bundle. Crumbs fall between the sofa cushions and the arm.

The challenge is that nothing has a lot of room to hide, so when the surface is cluttered, a cleaner either works around it or the surface stays dirty. Clearing the flat spots before we arrive makes a big difference.

Before we arrive: a ten-minute reset

  • Pick up cups, plates and anything that belongs in the kitchen.
  • Put loose mail and papers in one pile or a tray.
  • Move charging cords off the floor so the vacuum can run freely.
  • Take valuables and fragile decor off open shelves or tell us what to leave alone.
  • Leave gate codes and parking instructions for visitors. Access is part of how we plan the visit.

You do not need to tidy the whole place. Think of it as clearing a path.

What we do in an apartment living area

Our standard apartment and condo cleaning includes dusting of surfaces, shelves and furniture, vacuuming and mopping floors, baseboards and door frames, balcony or patio door glass, and sanitizing light switches and handles. In practice, that looks like this:

  1. Dust from the top down. Blinds, the top of the TV, shelves, then the coffee table.
  2. Glass last among the dusting. The balcony door gets cleaned inside and the handle gets sanitized.
  3. Soft furniture. Cushions are lifted and the seams are vacuumed.
  4. Touch points. Light switches, the thermostat and door handles are wiped.
  5. Floors. Vacuum edges and under furniture we can move, then mop.

The shared spaces outside your door

Many Greer apartment communities have a shared hallway, an entry stoop or a mailbox area. We clean inside your unit, not common areas that belong to the community, but it helps to know how they affect your floors. Grit from a breezeway comes in on shoes. A mat inside the door and a shoe rack catch most of it before it reaches the carpet.

A weekly rhythm that keeps a small living room under control

Between visits, a few habits make a big difference in a small space. Wipe the coffee table when you clear the dishes. Run a dry microfiber cloth over the TV stand once a week. Shake the entry mat outside. Vacuum the sofa seams when you vacuum the floor. Then let the team handle the baseboards, door frames, blinds and the balcony door glass on a regular schedule.
