The three phases of post-construction cleaning in Hendersonville

The three phases of post-construction cleaning in Hendersonville, NC: rough, final and touch-up, what each covers and how the free estimate sets your price.

Perfect Services Team
November 16, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Post-construction cleaning runs in three passes: rough clean, detailed final clean and a touch-up before move-in.
  • Fine drywall and sanding dust is the main reason a one-pass clean never works.
  • Perfect Services gives a price only after a free walkthrough of the site.

Builders in Hendersonville tend to say the same thing at the end of a job: the house is done, but it is not ready. Fine drywall dust is in the vents, stickers are on the new windows, there is paint on the hinge of a door nobody remembers painting. Post-construction cleaning is how a finished build becomes a home someone can live in, and it works best in three phases rather than one big pass.

Why three phases and not one

Construction dust is finer than household dust, and it moves. If you clean a room, then a painter or flooring crew walks through, the room is dirty again. If you clean everything in one visit, you end up cleaning top surfaces that settle dust back down an hour later. A phased clean matches how a job really finishes, trade by trade, and keeps you from paying twice for the same room.

Phase 1: the rough clean

The rough clean happens after the heavy work is done, usually once drywall, trim and most of the messy trades have finished. The goal is to clear the big stuff so the next crews can finish cleanly:

  • Debris, scraps and packaging removed from every room
  • First vacuum of floors, subfloors and stair treads
  • Dust knocked down from windowsills, ledges, trim and cabinet tops
  • Stickers, tape and protective film lifted off windows and fixtures where it is safe to do so

It is not pretty, and it is not supposed to be. It is a reset that makes the electricians, painters or flooring installers cleaner to work around.

Phase 2: the detailed final clean

This is the main event, and it is what most people picture when they ask what a post-construction clean includes. It happens after all trades have left. Our post-construction cleaning service works through each room top to bottom:

  • Fine construction dust removed from all surfaces, ledges and trim
  • Inside cabinets, drawers and closets, where sanding dust likes to collect
  • Windows, glass, tracks and sills
  • Paint specks, sticker residue and adhesive removed
  • Light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans
  • Bathrooms and new fixtures polished
  • Floors vacuumed and washed in multiple passes, since a single pass moves dust around

Because this is detail work done by hand, it fits the Brazilian method Perfect Services was built on: cloths and attention in the corners, not a spray and a quick wipe. A new home still has corners, tracks and door frames that need to be touched, and that is where the time goes.

Phase 3: the touch-up before move-in

A day or two after the final clean, dust that was suspended in the air has settled. Furniture deliveries, a last punch-list repair or an inspection can also leave marks. The touch-up is a short visit that wipes the settled dust, spot-cleans floors and fixes anything the final walkthrough turned up. It is the pass that makes a home feel finished instead of nearly finished.

How much does post-construction cleaning cost in Hendersonville?

People look up "post construction cleaning rates" expecting a per-square-foot number. We do not publish one, because square footage is only part of it. The price depends on the size of the home, the number of bathrooms and windows, how many trades worked in it, how much paint and adhesive needs removing, whether it is a new build or a remodel, and how many phases you want. Perfect Services gives a price only after a free walkthrough of the site: no commitment, and the number you get is the number you pay.

What owners can do to help

A few small things make the final clean faster and the result better. Keep the utilities on, since we need water and power. Remove leftover materials, paint cans and tools before we arrive. Give us a short list of anything that needs special care, such as natural stone, specialty finishes or new appliances still in protective film. And tell us whether a final inspection is coming, so we can plan the touch-up for the right day.

How long it takes

A final clean on a typical home takes a full day or more with a team, and the rough clean and touch-up are shorter. We give you a time window at the walkthrough so your closing date, inspection or move-in is not at risk.

Questions to ask any cleaner

  1. Do you offer all three phases, or only one pass?
  2. Is paint, adhesive and sticker removal included?
  3. Do you clean inside cabinets, vents and fixtures?
  4. Do you wash floors in more than one pass?
  5. Will you see the site before quoting?

Do I need all three phases of post-construction cleaning?

Not always. A small remodel may only need the final clean. Larger builds benefit from a rough clean during the project and a touch-up before move-in.

Why is construction dust so hard to remove?

It is very fine and settles into vents, cabinets, tracks and trim. It spreads when disturbed, so we wipe surfaces by hand and wash floors in several passes.
