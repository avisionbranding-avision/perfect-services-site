What gets cleaned on every visit

A regular commercial cleaning in Greer follows a repeating list, and it should be written down for you:

  • Desks, counters and workstations wiped.
  • Door handles, light switches and keypads, the places every customer touches.
  • Restrooms cleaned, disinfected and restocked.
  • Break room, sink, microwave and fridge exterior.
  • Trash and recycling removed.
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped.
  • Glass doors and entry areas.
  • Reception and waiting areas dusted.

When the kids come to the office

For a family business, the office is sometimes also the after-school waiting room. That is a different cleaning job. Backpacks leave dirt along the wall, snack crumbs gather under the desk, and a craft project spreads across the conference table. If kids are part of your workday, mention it during the estimate. The team can schedule the visit after pickup, pay attention to the floors under the desks and the break room, and leave the personal items where they were.
