Most Greer hosts who call us for the first time are not worried about the cleaning itself. They are worried about the part they cannot see: what happens between a guest leaving and the next one walking in. Step one: the free in-home estimate

Before anything is scheduled, someone from the team visits the property. We walk through the bedrooms, bathrooms and kitchen, check where the linens live, ask how guests usually leave the place and look at the entry, since a lockbox or smart lock changes how we get in. The visit is free and there is no commitment. You can book it with the form on our Greer vacation rental cleaning page, by calling (864) 399-5168 or by texting (864) 666-8520.

This is also when we agree on what a normal turnover looks like for your listing. A one-bedroom near GSP Airport used by traveling workers is a different job from a four-bedroom family house in the newer streets near the BMW plant, even though both are listed on the same platform.

What is on the turnover list

Every Airbnb cleaning service in Greer should be able to tell you exactly what it covers. Ours follows the same order each time, so nothing depends on memory:

  • Full kitchen reset: dishes, counters and appliances
  • Bathrooms cleaned and disinfected
  • Beds made with fresh linens that you provide
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash removed and bins reset
  • Restocking checklist for host supplies
  • Damage and missing-item report with photos
  • Staging to match your listing photos

The last two items are the ones hosts tend to value most after a few weeks. The report arrives while you are still free to message a guest about a broken lamp, and the staging step means the throw pillows and towels look the way they do in your pictures.

How a first turnover day unfolds

StageWhat the team does
ArrivalQuick look at the whole place before touching anything, noting damage, left-behind items and anything unusual.
Linens and trashBeds stripped, trash out, laundry started if it is done on site.

We work from the top down and finish with the floors. It is a small habit, but it is the Brazilian detail-first method in practice: dust never lands on a surface we have already done.

What to prepare before the first visit

A little preparation makes the first turnover smoother. Tell us how the property is accessed and whether any rooms are off limits. Leave clean linens and towels in a spot we can find, and write down which supplies you stock, for example coffee, soap and paper goods, so the restocking checklist matches your setup. Mention anything fragile or particular, such as a decorative item that must stay on a certain shelf.

We usually can fit a first visit into the same week you ask, depending on the calendar. After the first turnover, most hosts settle into a rhythm: we sync with your bookings, you receive the report after each clean and you adjust the checklist as you learn what guests leave behind. Questions hosts ask before the first clean

The most common one is about trust. Hosts want to know who is entering the property and what happens if something is broken. Perfect Services is a Brazilian-owned team based right here in Greer, and the photo report exists partly for this reason: it creates a record of the condition of the home after every visit, in both directions.

The second question is about supplies. We bring our own cleaning products and equipment, and we check your guest supplies against the list you give us. If a stock item runs low, it appears in the restocking notes, so you can reorder before the next check-in rather than finding out from a review.

The third is about changes. Listings evolve: you add a sofa bed, install a hot tub or open a basement room. Tell us, and we will update the checklist and look at the new space during the next visit. For a host in Greer, where the same property can serve weekend visitors, contractors and families in town for the holidays, a checklist that grows with the listing matters more than a fixed one.
