What actually sets the price of a house cleaning

Cleaning is priced by time and team size. Every factor below changes how many hours it takes to reach a clean you would be happy with.

FactorWhy it matters
Home size and layoutMore rooms and square feet take more time. A two-story layout with long hallways adds steps too.
Bedrooms and bathroomsBathrooms are the most time-intensive rooms, so a four-bath house is a different job than a two-bath one.
FrequencyWeekly and biweekly visits cost less per visit than monthly, because the home never gets far from clean.
Pets and kidsExtra hair and traffic add a little time, mostly on floors and around doors.
First visitThe first clean is usually deeper, to bring the home up to the maintenance standard.

Notice what is not on the list: a flat rate per square foot. Square footage is only one input, and a rate that ignores the others tends to be wrong for someone, either you or the cleaner.

What is included in a regular visit

Knowing what the price covers makes comparing companies much easier. A standard visit with our team includes:

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized
  • Appliance fronts and handles wiped
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation
  • Light switches and high-touch spots wiped

The baseboard and door frame work is done on a rotation, so over a few visits every room gets its turn. That is part of the Brazilian detail-first method we work with: floors are washed by hand along the walls, and corners are not skipped because they are hard to reach. If you want the whole house detailed at once, our house cleaning and maid service can start with a deeper first visit, or you can book deep cleaning first and move to a recurring schedule after.

How long does a cleaning take?

It depends on the factors above, but a typical Boiling Springs family home with a recurring plan is usually handled by a small team in a few hours. The first visit runs longer. If your home has not had professional attention in months, expect the team to spend extra time on bathrooms and kitchen buildup before settling into the normal routine.

Our team comes from Greer, about 16 miles and 25 minutes away, so we plan the route and the arrival window ahead rather than squeezing visits in. If you are hoping to get on the calendar this week, call or text and ask what is open; the estimate visit is the first step either way.

Ways to keep the cost reasonable

  • Choose a recurring schedule. Biweekly is the most common choice for families, and the home stays closer to clean between visits.
  • Clear toys, laundry and papers from counters and floors before the team arrives, so the time goes to cleaning.
  • Tell us about pets, allergies or products to avoid when we do the walkthrough.
  • Skip add-ons you do not need. The estimate lists them separately, so you can add the oven or fridge only when it matters.
