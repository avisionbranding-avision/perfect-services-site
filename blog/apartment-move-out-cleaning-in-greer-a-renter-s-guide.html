What the move-out walkthrough usually looks at

Property managers differ, so read your lease and any move-out sheet they gave you. In a typical walkthrough, though, the spots that draw attention are the same ones we see ourselves when we walk into an emptied apartment: the inside of the oven, the refrigerator shelves and drawers, the bathroom grout, the tub edges and the floors along the baseboards. Smaller items get missed because they hide in plain sight, like the tracks of a sliding patio door, the tops of door frames and the inside of the cabinet under the kitchen sink.

A tenant is rarely judged on one dusty shelf. What costs people part of a deposit is a pattern: grease on the stovetop, a film on the shower glass, and hair along the floor edges all add up to a home that was not cleaned for the next renter.

What apartment move out cleaning in Greer should cover

Here is the room-by-room list our team works from. You can use it to clean yourself or to check what a cleaner has included.

  • Kitchen: inside and outside of every cabinet and drawer, inside the refrigerator and freezer, inside the oven and microwave, range hood, stovetop and backsplash.
  • Bathrooms: toilet base and behind it, grout, glass doors, tub and shower walls, and inside the vanity.
  • Bedrooms and closets: shelves, rods and closet floors, plus wiped doors, frames and light switches.
  • Windows: sills, tracks and blinds.
  • Floors: vacuumed and mopped edge to edge, which is the very last step.

The order matters more than the effort

Renters who clean on their own often work hard and still end up redoing the floors. The reason is sequence. Dust and crumbs fall, so you start high and finish low. Lights, vents and the tops of door frames come first. Cabinets, appliances and counters come next. Bathrooms follow, and floors always go last, backing out toward the front door so you are not walking on a wet floor.

The oven is the other place people lose time. If it has baked-on grease, a few minutes of spraying will not do it. Our Brazilian detail-first method is to let the product sit while we move on to another room, then come back and finish. That is a small habit, but it is the difference between one pass and three.

Some renters find that a place left in rough shape needs a deep cleaning level of effort before the move-out checklist even starts. We will tell you that during the estimate rather than after.
