How much does house cleaning cost in Wellford, SC?

What sets the price of house cleaning in Wellford, SC: size, bathrooms, frequency and pets. See how the free in-home estimate works before you book.

Perfect Services Team
November 28, 2026 · 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Price depends on size, bathrooms, frequency, pets and how the house looks on the first visit, not on a flat rate.
  • Perfect Services quotes only after a free in-home estimate, so the number matches your actual home.
  • Weekly and biweekly visits cost less per visit than monthly ones because the home stays at maintenance level.

The honest answer to "how much does house cleaning cost in Wellford?" is that it depends on your house, and any company that gives you a firm number over the phone without seeing the place is guessing. A quiet three-bedroom off US-29 with two adults and no pets is a very different job from a four-bedroom with two dogs and three kids, even if the square footage looks the same on paper. Here is what actually moves the price, so you can judge any quote you get for house cleaning in Wellford.

The five things that set the price

When our team walks through a home in the 29385 ZIP code, we look at the same handful of things every time. Together they tell us how many people and how many hours the job needs.

  • Home size and layout. More rooms and more square feet take more time. A long ranch with a lot of hallway floor is different from a compact two-story with the same footage.
  • Bedrooms and bathrooms. Bathrooms are the slowest rooms to clean properly: shower glass, grout, the base of the toilet, chrome, the vanity. A fourth bathroom adds more time than a fourth bedroom.
  • Frequency. Weekly and biweekly visits cost less per visit than a monthly one, because the house never gets far from clean.
  • Pets and kids. Hair, tracked-in dirt and the usual family traffic add a little time on each visit.
  • The first visit. The first clean is usually deeper, because we bring the house up to the standard we then maintain.

Why we do not quote by photo or over the phone

Perfect Services gives prices only after a free in-home estimate. A teammate comes to the house, walks through every room, asks what matters most to you and sees the real condition of kitchens, bathrooms and floors. There is no commitment and no pressure. Wellford is only about 7 miles east of Greer, roughly a 15 minute drive, so getting someone out to you is simple.

That visit is also where we catch the things a photo hides: a glass shower door with hard water film, a kitchen with built-up grease on the cabinet fronts, a lot of hardwood or tile that needs hand mopping. Once we have seen it, the number we give you is the number you pay for that service, with no surprises on the day.

Wellford is a small town along US-29 with quiet residential streets, and many of the homes we see there are single-level houses with long hallways and a lot of hard flooring. Floors take real time in those homes, and they are one reason a quote based only on bedroom count often misses. A family with a big yard also tracks in more red clay and grass, which shows up on mats and entry floors by the end of the week. None of this is a problem, it just needs to be seen once so the schedule and the price are right from the start.

What a regular visit includes

Price only makes sense next to what you get for it. A standard visit with our house cleaning and maid service covers:

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized, with appliance fronts and handles wiped
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Light switches and other high-touch spots wiped
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation, so every part of the house gets attention over a few visits

When you compare quotes from other cleaning companies in the Upstate, ask whether these items are in the standard visit or sold as add-ons. A low price that leaves out the bathrooms' grout or the floors' edges is not really lower.
