What the method means for a house with kids

Brazilian cleaning is not faster, it is more thorough. Every surface is hand-wiped, not just sprayed. Corners, edges and baseboards are cleaned by hand, small items are moved and cleaned under, and door frames, switches and handles are detailed. For a family home that means fewer spots where crumbs and sticky fingerprints can sit between visits.

It also means the work is on a checklist. The team finishes with a walk-through against the room checklist, so what is done today is what was done last time. Parents who have been disappointed by rushed cleaning services tell us this consistency matters more than any single feature. A Brazilian house cleaning visit in Greer is the same careful job every week.

Fitting visits around school and work

The team can work while the house is empty, which for many families means the hours between drop-off and pickup. It can also work while you work from home, with a few ground rules. Plan one of these arrangements:

  • Weekday mornings after drop-off, with the house empty and key access arranged.
  • Early afternoon visits before pickup for families with one parent at home.
  • Saturday visits for households with two long workweeks.
  • A fixed day every week or every other week, so the family knows when to expect a clean house.

Greer families commute on I-85, SC-14 and US-29, and school pickup lines can change a plan quickly. If you need to reschedule, tell us early so the team can adjust.

How to prepare in ten minutes

Do not clean before the cleaners come. A basic reset is enough:

  1. Pick up toys and clothes from floors so every surface is reachable.
  2. Clear counters of mail, backpacks and school papers.
  3. Put away anything fragile or sentimental that you do not want moved.
  4. Tell us about allergies, products to avoid, or rooms to skip.
  5. Leave a note about pets, alarms or the best door for access.

The team does not touch homework, art projects or anything that looks like it is in progress, and a labeled bin for those items is the easiest way to keep them safe.
