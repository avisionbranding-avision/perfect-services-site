Getting your Greer apartment ready for an inspection

Getting your Greer apartment ready for an inspection: a day-by-day plan, the spots inspectors notice and when to call a pro.

November 10, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Start a week ahead and work room by room, saving floors and glass for the last day.
  • Baseboards, door frames, appliance fronts and bathroom grout are what most often get missed.

Seven days out: decide what you can do and what you cannot

A notice slid under the door says the property manager will walk through on Thursday. Most renters then spend Wednesday night scrubbing until midnight. A better plan starts earlier and is honest about what needs help. Check your lease and any checklist your community gave you, because what they inspect for varies. What follows is the order we would work in, and where our apartment cleaning service in Greer tends to step in.

  • Read the notice and note the date, time and what it says is being checked
  • Walk each room with your phone and take photos of anything already worn or damaged
  • Make a short list of jobs: oven, fridge, bathroom grout, balcony door, carpet
  • Decide which jobs you will do and which you will hand off
  • Gather supplies so you are not shopping on the night before

Three days out: the jobs that take longest

Start with the things that need soak time. Put cleaner on the shower glass and tub, and let it work while you do something else. Wipe the inside of the microwave. Pull everything off the stovetop and degrease around the burners. Pull out the fridge shelves if they are dirty.

These are the jobs an inspector's eye goes to, because they show how the unit was used. Rushed on the last evening, they get half done. Done three days ahead, there is time to go back to anything that still looks dull.

The places inspectors actually notice

SpotWhat goes wrong
BaseboardsDust and scuffs along every wall, especially behind the couch
Door frames and light switchesFingerprints and smudges that build up over months
Appliance frontsGrease around the stove, streaks on the dishwasher
BathroomSoap film on glass, mildew at the caulk line, hair in the drain
Balcony or patio doorSmudged glass and a dirty sliding track
FloorsCrumbs in corners and a dull mopped finish

In a Greer summer the sliding door track collects pollen and dust fast, and in spring the yellow film shows on every dark surface. Wipe tracks with a damp cloth and a thin tool, not just a swipe on top.

Two days out: the rooms people forget

Closets, the hall, the laundry closet and the entry are where clutter hides, and an inspector opens doors. Clear the floor of each closet and wipe the shelf. Check behind the bathroom door, under the sink, and the back of the front door. Run a damp cloth over the vents you can reach, and replace any light bulb that has burned out, since a dead bulb looks like neglect even in a clean room.

Take a second round of photos once each room is done. If the property manager disagrees with anything later, you have a time-stamped record of how the unit looked.

The day before: finish in the right order

Work from top to bottom and from the back of the unit to the front door. Dust shelves and the tops of cabinets first, then wipe counters and handles, then do mirrors and glass, and leave floors for last so you do not walk through finished work. Empty the trash and take it to the dumpster, since a full can is the first thing noticed.

If you have pets, check corners where hair gathers and run a lint roller over upholstered pieces. A dog bed in the middle of a clean living room is fine; hair on the baseboard behind it is not.

On the morning itself

Open the blinds so the light shows the room as it is. Do a last pass for dishes in the sink, wet towels in the bathroom and trash by the door. Put away shoes, bags and anything on the floor near the entry. Leave the thermostat at a comfortable temperature and the lights on in rooms without windows. Do not try a new product on the day of the inspection. A cleaner that streaks glass or leaves a heavy scent can undo an evening of work, so stick to what you used earlier in the week.

If you need to be at work, leave a short note with the date and a contact number in case someone has questions. A tidy, calm unit and a clear note go further than a perfect floor and no communication.

How early should I start cleaning for an apartment inspection?

About a week, so the longest jobs (oven, bathroom, fridge) are done early and the last day is only dusting, glass and floors.

What do property managers usually notice first?

Baseboards, door frames, appliance fronts, bathroom grout and the balcony door track. Check your lease and community checklist for specifics.
