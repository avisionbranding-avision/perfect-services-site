What is Brazilian house cleaning? A guide for Woodruff homeowners

What is Brazilian house cleaning? A guide for Woodruff, SC homeowners: the hand-wiped, detail-first method, what it covers and how the free estimate works.

Perfect Services Team
November 11, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Brazilian house cleaning is a detail-first method: surfaces are wiped by hand and corners, edges and floors along the walls are cleaned on every visit.
  • It suits homes with hardwood, tile and grout, and people who felt rushed by previous cleaners.
  • Perfect Services gives prices only after a free in-home estimate.

Ask someone who has switched to a Brazilian cleaner what changed and the answer is usually small and specific: "She moved the couch." "The corners of the floor were clean." Brazilian house cleaning is not a secret technique. It is a habit of working slowly enough to do the parts of a house that most quick services skip.

For Woodruff homeowners, where a single street can mix older houses with new rural subdivisions, that habit matters. Different floors, different ages of grout and different ways of living in a home all need hands, not just a spray bottle. Here is what it means in practice.

The method in one sentence

Every surface is wiped by hand, in a set order, and checked against a room checklist at the end. That is it. The rest is the discipline to do it the same way each visit. Our Brazilian house cleaning in Woodruff follows these principles.

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not just sprayed
  • Corners, edges and baseboards cleaned by hand
  • Furniture and small items moved and cleaned under
  • Door frames, switches and handles detailed
  • Bathrooms scrubbed including grout and toilet base
  • Kitchen degreased: stovetop, hood, cabinet fronts
  • Floors hand-washed along the walls
  • Final walk-through against the room checklist

What a visit looks like, step by step

  1. Walkthrough. The team looks at the rooms, the floor types and anything you want avoided or emphasized.
  2. Top first. Dusting begins high, on fans, shelves and frames, so nothing falls on a finished surface.
  3. Kitchen and bathrooms. These get the most time. Stovetops and hood are degreased, and shower grout and the area behind the toilet are scrubbed.
  4. Furniture and small items. Lamps, decor and small furniture are lifted, cleaned under and put back.
  5. Floors last. Hard floors are washed by hand along the walls, which is where dirt collects and a mop often misses.
  6. Final check. The team walks each room against the checklist before leaving.

None of this makes the other approach wrong. It just means the Brazilian way takes more care per room. For examples of what that looks like, read about the details Brazilian cleaners never skip, or how we care for hardwood and tile floors.

One honest note on expectations: detail takes time. A Brazilian clean of the same house will usually take longer than a quick pass, and that is the reason it looks different afterward. Speed is not the goal. A finished room that matches the checklist is. If your schedule is tight, tell us how many hours you can have the team in the house, and we will plan which rooms get the most attention during that window and what to cover on the next visit.

Who it is a good fit for

This service tends to suit homeowners who felt disappointed by rushed visits, families who want a detailed clean and anyone with hardwood, tile and grout that need careful treatment. If you want the corners done on every visit, it is the right choice. For homes that have not been cleaned in a long time, a first visit may be deeper, and you may prefer starting with deep cleaning before moving to regular service.

Woodruff is about 17 miles and 30 minutes southeast of Greer, reached along I-26 and SC-101, and our team schedules visits for the 29388 area with that drive in mind.

Small things homeowners notice after the first few visits

The changes are rarely dramatic. The door frame by the kitchen stops showing handprints. The baseboard under the dining table is white again. The stovetop knobs are clean, and so is the little gap between the stove and the counter. In homes with hardwood, the floor along the wall no longer has a gray line, because that strip is washed by hand and not left for the mop to skip.

Families with kids and pets often feel it most, since hair and crumbs collect under furniture and along edges. If that is your house, mention the rooms where the family lives most, and the team will give them extra attention. For households sensitive to dust or strong smells, say so at the start, so the plan can fit.
