What to expect from your first house cleaning in Greer

Booking your first professional clean can feel like inviting someone to judge your house. It is the opposite: a good team has seen every kind of home and is there to make yours easier to live in. Step 2: Prepare the house (10 minutes)

  • Pick up clothes, toys and papers so surfaces and floors are clear
  • Put away valuables and anything fragile you do not want moved
  • Tell us about pets, alarm codes and any areas to skip
  • Leave a note about priorities, such as a cluttered kitchen or a particular bathroom

You do not need to wash dishes or tidy deeply. Tidying is part of the reason you are hiring help.

Step 3: The visit

A team arrives with supplies and equipment and starts at the top of each room. A typical visit includes:

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, stovetop, cabinet fronts, appliance exteriors and floors
  • Bathrooms: toilet, tub or shower, vanity, mirror and floors
  • Bedrooms and living areas: dusting, surfaces, vacuuming and mopping
  • Detail work: baseboards, door frames, switch plates and corners

Perfect Services follows a Brazilian detail-first method. That means we finish one room completely before moving to the next, and we check the details that are easy to miss. See the full house cleaning and maid service page for what is included.

How long does it take?

A first visit in a mid-sized home commonly takes a few hours with a team of two or three. If the home has not been professionally cleaned in a while, we may recommend a one-time deep cleaning first, then regular visits to keep it fresh.

Step 4: After the clean

Walk through the home or review photos if you were out. If anything needs attention, tell us and we will fix it. Many Greer households then choose a weekly, every-other-week or monthly schedule, since regular visits are quicker and keep the house from needing a big reset. Common first-visit questions

New clients tend to ask the same things, so here are quick answers before you book.

  • Do you bring supplies? Yes. The team arrives with its own products and equipment. If you prefer specific products, or need green cleaning, tell us when you book.
  • Will the same people come each time? We aim to send the same team for recurring clients, so they learn the home and your preferences.
  • What if I am not happy with something? Tell us right away and we will go back and fix it.
  • What about pets? Let us know in advance. We work around pets and can ask you to secure them in one room if needed.

Choosing a frequency

Once your first clean is done, the best frequency depends on your household. Weekly visits suit busy families with pets and children, since messes never build up. Every two weeks is the most popular balance of price and results. Monthly visits work well for smaller households or as a reset between your own cleaning. If you are not sure, start with every two weeks for the first month and adjust. Our guide on weekly, biweekly and monthly maid service goes deeper on this choice.
