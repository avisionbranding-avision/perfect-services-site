What actually moves the price

When we price a regular clean, these are the factors that matter, in roughly this order of importance.

FactorWhy it matters
Home size and layoutMore rooms and square feet take more time, and a house with many small rooms takes longer than an open plan of the same size.
Bedrooms and bathroomsBathrooms are the most time-intensive rooms in the house.
FrequencyWeekly and biweekly visits cost less per visit than a monthly one, because the house never gets far from clean.
Pets and kidsExtra hair and foot traffic add a little time to floors and surfaces.
First visitThe first clean is usually deeper, to bring the house up to a standard the regular visits can maintain.

Notice that frequency sits in the middle of that list. A lot of people assume the biggest saving comes from a smaller house, but in practice a steady biweekly rhythm does more for the cost per visit than almost anything else.

What we ask at the walkthrough

The questions are practical. How many people live in the house, or how often is it used? Are there pets? Which rooms matter most to you, and which can wait? Is there a part of the house you have been avoiding, such as the inside of the oven, the glass shower door or the window tracks? Your answers change the plan more than the square footage does, and they stop us from spending your money on rooms you do not care about while skipping the one that bothers you every morning.

We also look at the surfaces themselves. Natural stone, wood floors and older finishes each need different products, and a stone counter in a Flat Rock kitchen is not cleaned the way a laminate one is. Better to learn that before the first visit than during it.

Second homes and vacation rentals in Flat Rock

Flat Rock is a historic mountain village, and many of the houses here are second homes or vacation rentals. That changes the cleaning plan. A house that sits closed for a few weeks collects dust on every flat surface, and in a humid mountain climate it can also pick up a stale smell that a quick wipe does not fix. Owners who arrive for a weekend want to walk into a house that is already ready, not spend the first evening cleaning it.

If that is your situation, tell us when the house is empty and when you arrive. A clean timed to finish just before you get there is a different job from a clean on a fixed weekly day, and the estimate takes that into account. For rentals with guest turnovers, our house cleaning and maid service can be paired with turnover scheduling, and we will talk through the details at the walkthrough.

What a regular visit includes

Price makes sense only when you know what you are buying. A regular visit from our team covers:

  • Kitchen counters, sink and stovetop cleaned and sanitized
  • Appliance fronts and handles wiped
  • Bathrooms: toilet, shower, tub, mirrors and chrome
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped by hand
  • Surfaces, shelves and furniture dusted
  • Beds made and trash emptied
  • Baseboards and door frames touched up on a rotation
  • Light switches and high-touch spots wiped

The Brazilian detail-first method is mostly about that rotation line. We do not wipe every baseboard every week, but we do not skip them either. Each visit gets a few extra details, so over a month the whole house has been touched.

How long does a visit take?

A regular clean in an average home usually takes a small team a few hours, and the first visit takes longer than the ones after it. A larger house, several bathrooms or a lot of pet hair stretch that. We would rather tell you a realistic window at the estimate than promise something short and rush the work.
