The three phases of post-construction cleaning in Pickens

Post construction cleaning Pickens SC explained in three phases: rough clean, final clean and touch-up. See what each covers and get a free in-home estimate.

Perfect Services Team
November 22, 2026 · 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • Post-construction cleaning works in phases: a rough clean, a final clean and a touch-up pass.
  • Drywall sanding creates the finest dust, and it settles in vents, ledges and cabinets long after the crew leaves.
  • Perfect Services prices the job after a free in-home estimate, because project size and dust level decide the time.

The crew leaves, the dumpster goes away, and the house still looks like a work site: a pale film on every surface, paint specks on the glass, stickers on the new windows. In Pickens, whether the project was a kitchen remodel, an addition or a new build near the foothills, the cleanup is not one job. It works best in three phases, and that is how our team plans post-construction cleaning in Pickens.

Phase one: the rough clean

The rough clean happens while the work is nearly done, often before the final fixtures go in or the flooring is finished. The goal is to take out the bulk: debris, packaging, loose dust and the heavy layers that would otherwise get ground into new surfaces. We clear floors and ledges, wipe down the inside of cabinets and closets and remove the worst of the drywall dust.

Doing this first matters because fine dust is abrasive. If a crew installs trim or a painter works over a dusty floor, the dust ends up in the finish. A rough clean gives the finish trades a cleaner surface to work on, and it makes the final clean faster.

Phase two: the final clean

The final clean is the one homeowners picture. It starts at the ceiling and ends at the floor, one room at a time, and it is slow because it is detail work. The list looks like this.

  • Fine construction dust removed from all surfaces, ledges and trim
  • Inside cabinets, drawers and closets
  • Windows, glass, tracks and sills, with stickers and paint specks removed
  • Adhesive and sticker residue removed from glass, appliances and fixtures
  • Light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans
  • Bathrooms and new fixtures polished
  • Floors vacuumed and washed in multiple passes

Multiple passes on floors is not a figure of speech. The first vacuum lifts what you can see, the wash picks up what is left, and the last pass catches what resettles. A single mop over a freshly sanded house leaves a haze.

Phase three: the touch-up before move-in

Even after a careful final clean, a house keeps shedding dust for a day or two. Air has been moving through vents, doors have opened, and the last trades have walked through. A touch-up pass right before move-in, or before a closing or inspection, catches fingerprints on new glass, dust that settled on the countertops and footprints on the floors. It is short, and it is the difference between a house that is ready and one that is nearly ready.

A word on what the clean does not include. We do not haul away construction debris, dispose of leftover materials or repair finishes damaged during the project. If a countertop was scratched or a floor finish was marred, the clean can show it but not fix it, so it is worth a final walk with your contractor before and after. Handing the cleaners a list of known trouble spots, such as a window with stubborn paint or a tub with a protective film, helps the crew spend time where it matters.

How long does it take?

A rough clean can take part of a day. A final clean of an average home takes a team most of a day or longer, since it is slow, room-by-room work. The touch-up is usually the shortest. Larger additions, whole-house remodels and homes with heavy sanding dust take more time, which we plan at the estimate. If you have a closing or inspection date, tell us at the start so we can keep room for the final pass.

If you are a builder or a homeowner managing the project, a short plan helps: book the rough clean as the finish trades begin, the final clean once the work and punch list are done, and the touch-up in the days before move-in. Our post-construction cleaning service can book all three together. Once you move in, ask about regular house cleaning to keep the new space that way.
