In Brazil, a clean house means every surface was touched by hand, not just the ones you see. That is the standard our Brazilian cleaning team brings to every home: edges, corners, baseboards and the spots behind and under things.

Brazilian house cleaning is not a product or a trend. It is a way of working: slower where it matters, hand-wiping instead of spraying and moving on, and finishing every room on the checklist before leaving.

Families who switch to Brazilian cleaners usually say the same thing: they did not know their home could feel this clean. The difference is in the details a typical rushed clean leaves behind.

What's included

  • Every surface hand-wiped, not just sprayed
  • Corners, edges and baseboards cleaned by hand
  • Furniture and small items moved and cleaned under
  • Door frames, switches and handles detailed
  • Bathrooms scrubbed including grout and toilet base
  • Kitchen degreased: stovetop, hood, cabinet fronts
  • Floors hand-washed along the walls
  • Final walk-through against the room checklist
