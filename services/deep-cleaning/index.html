Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between deep cleaning and regular cleaning?

A regular cleaning maintains a home that is already clean. A deep cleaning removes build-up from baseboards, grout, vents, fans, cabinet fronts and appliances, the places regular visits do not reach every time.

How often should a home in Greer and the Upstate get a deep cleaning?

Most homes in Greer and the Upstate benefit from a deep cleaning two to four times a year, or once before switching to regular maid service.

How long does a deep cleaning take?

It depends on the size and condition of the home. We tell you the expected time together with your free estimate.

Do you clean inside the oven and fridge?

Yes, as an add-on to any deep cleaning. Just mention it when you request your estimate.
