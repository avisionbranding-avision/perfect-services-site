Post-Construction Cleaning in Greer and the Upstate

Fine dust, debris and residue removed after a build or remodel, so you can move in right away.

Get my free estimate (864) 399-5168

Construction dust does not stay where the work happened. It settles on every ledge, inside cabinets, in light fixtures and in window tracks. Our post-construction cleaning removes it in phases, top to bottom, so it does not come back a week later.

After a remodel, the last thing you want is to clean drywall dust out of a brand-new kitchen. We handle the final clean for homeowners, builders and contractors, leaving the space ready for photos, inspection or move-in.

New construction and renovation projects need a different kind of cleaning: residue on glass, paint specks, sticker glue and dust inside every cabinet. Our team brings the tools and the patience for it.

What's included

  • Fine construction dust removed from all surfaces, ledges and trim
  • Inside cabinets, drawers and closets
  • Windows, glass, tracks and sills
  • Paint specks, sticker residue and adhesive removed
  • Light fixtures, vents and ceiling fans
  • Bathrooms and new fixtures polished
  • Floors vacuumed and washed (multiple passes)
  • Final touch-up pass before move-in

Who it's for

  • Homeowners finishing a remodel or addition
  • Builders and general contractors
  • New construction closings
  • Kitchen and bathroom renovations

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with builders and contractors in Greer and the Upstate?

Yes. We clean new construction and remodels in Greer and the Upstate for homeowners, builders and general contractors, including final cleans before closing.

How soon after construction can you clean?

Book us after the trades are finished and the heavy debris is removed. We can also do a rough clean mid-project and a final clean at the end.

Do you remove paint and sticker residue from windows?

Yes. Removing residue, paint specks and stickers from glass and fixtures is part of our post-construction cleaning.

Why does construction dust keep coming back?

Fine dust settles slowly and hides in vents, fixtures and cabinets. We clean top to bottom in phases so it is removed, not just moved around.
